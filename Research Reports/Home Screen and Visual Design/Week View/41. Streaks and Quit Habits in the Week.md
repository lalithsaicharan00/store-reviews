# Week View, Question 41: Streaks and Quit Habits in the Week

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**The feedback.**
- Streaks are missing from the week view. Are they needed there, and where?
- How do quit habits show in a week, when the live counter is the main thing? They have to look good next to the other cards.

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:**
  - Loop's 50 reviews on streaks and the main screen;
  - the first 70 of Days Since's 152 reviews mentioning its calendar.
- **Reused:**
  - Question 31 (streak units) and Question 36 (the live counter);
  - Notion C024 (streaks) and C095 (never shame).

---

## What to do

**Line 1 of every week card ends with the habit's running record, right-aligned:**
- a normal habit shows its **streak**: 🔥 23 days, or 🔥 4 weeks;
- a quit habit shows its **live counter**: 12 days 04:23:07, ticking.

It sits in the same place on every card, so the eye finds it the same way.

**A quit habit's line 2 is one continuous bar across the seven days:**

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [icon] Smoking                12 days 04:23:07│
│ ████████████████████████████████████████▒▒▒ │  ← clean days in the habit's colour
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Clean days are one unbroken bar in the habit's tint.
- **Today's segment fills as the day goes by.** That is the live counter drawn as a line.
- **A slip breaks the bar,** with a small neutral dot on that day. There is no red, and no cross.
- Before the quit date, that part of the bar is empty.

**A cut-back habit** (a count with a maximum) uses normal day cells (Question 40). A day over the maximum shows red.

## Key findings

**1. Yes, the streak belongs on the week screen, next to the name.**
- **14 Loop reviews ask for it** on the main table, which is Loop's week-style screen:
  - "it should go right after the name of the habit and before the days listed" `P3#1645`
  - "I wish to be able to see the streaks for all habits next to the habit name in the home page, after all the streak is what defines a habit." `P3#1860`
  - "It would be nice to see the current streak right next to the habit on the home screen." `P3#1905`
- **One even asks for the streak in place of the tick:** "when we check mark it should show number of steak day instead of just check mark" `P3#3051`
- **Notion C024:** removing the streak from the main list drew 52 complaints in one app.
- **Why the right end of line 1:** line 2 is taken by the days, and the right end of line 1 is the one free, fixed place. The quit counter uses the same spot.

**2. Quit habits in a week: a line through the clean days is what people love.**
- **Days Since's calendar draws exactly this,** and its reviewers describe it:
  - "It lays out how many days you’ve been without a reset by showing a straight line in the colour of the tracker" `A3#1300`
  - "It is very easy to track n see your progress with that line under the dates in calendar." `A3#3535`
  - "I like how the calendar shows your streaks. So even if you do give in, it’s not like all your progress and achievements are erased" `A3#1305`
  - "Using the calendar which shows your streak and relapses, this helped me identify that I actually relapse 90% of the time between Tuesday and Thursday." `A3#2390`
- 11 of the reviews read praise the line in particular.
- **2 find it hard to read:** "The calendar with the bar thing is hard to read (I only track one addiction). I suggest changing it for something like a green dot on clean days and a red otherwise." `A3#4175`
  - Ours is one habit per card, not several bars stacked in a month, and aligned to the date row. That is the case that reviewer found hard.
  - We keep neutral colours for slips (C095), not red.

**3. Why the quit card still matches the others (reasoned from first principles).**
- Every week card has the same shape: title, the record on the right, and the days across line 2.
- For a quit habit "the days" is a continuous bar rather than separate cells, because an unbroken run is what quitting means. The visual difference says "counter, not checklist".
- **The live part stays live:**
  - the counter on line 1 ticks;
  - today's segment grows.

**Limits.**
- **Reasoned, not reviewed:**
  - today's segment filling through the day;
  - the neutral slip dot.
- **Days Since's bar is monthly, not weekly.** Whether a 7-day slice reads as clearly needs a test.
