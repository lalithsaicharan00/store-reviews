# Week View, Question 40: What a Day Cell Shows — Ticks, Fills and Numbers

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**The feedback.**
- Does every day need a checkbox? Loop shows ticks for yes/no habits and numbers for measurable ones. Which approach is better?
- Where do "5/8 glasses" or "0/20 min" go in a week without clutter?

**Basis.**
- **Read in full, from Loop's 27,196 reviews:**
  - all 127 on measurable (numeric) habits;
  - 12 on tick and cross marks;
  - 17 on its percentage score.
- **Reused:** Question 34 (the card fills as progress; the text gives the exact number) and Question 36 (cut back = a count with a maximum).

---

## What to do

**Every day cell works the same way the Day card does: it fills in proportion to that day's progress.** No checkboxes.

| That day | Cell |
|---|---|
| Nothing logged (past day) | empty, thin outline |
| Part done (count, timer, checklist) | fills in proportion, with the **amount** small inside ("5", "12", "5k") |
| Done (any type) | solid habit colour with ✓ |
| Skipped | dashed outline with "–" |
| Not scheduled that day | no cell, just a faint dot (non-scheduled-days report) |
| Cut back, over the maximum | red, with the amount ("3") |
| Today | the same, plus a thin ring |
| Later this week | faint |

**No units in the cells, and no goals.**
- The unit and goal belong to the habit, not the day. Day view shows them ("5/8 glasses").
- In the week, the fill says how far and the number says how much.

**Tapping a cell does the next step for that day,** as the Day button does (+1, done, next item). Hold for the menu (enter a number, skip, note).

---

## Key findings

**1. Loop users ask for exactly this: the number, or a fill, in the day's cell.**
- 5 reviews ask Loop to show the amount or shading per day:
  - "Can the widget for measurable habits have the number displayed please!! Or atleast some color grading or cup filling visual which clearly shows low, high and target reached days (full cup / darkest color)." `P3#4068`
  - "please show numerical value in circle that is used to show intensity of any habit" `P3#8700`
  - "measurable habits have a small number written on each day on Calendar" `P3#4815`
  - "the widgets of 'measurables' are shaded after meeting the target" `P3#6850`
- Fill plus a small number answers all of them in one cell.

**2. A checkbox for everything loses the numbers; two kinds of cell split the model.**
- **Loop added numeric habits late,** and its reviews show both halves of the problem:
  - people wanted numbers ("I needed a numeric tracking like" `P3#15482`, and many more);
  - then they wanted them visible and quick to log.
- **17 ask for one tap to add, instead of typing:** "Typing numbers manually is tedious. Adding '+' & '-' buttons on the home screen would fix this." `P3#1671`
- **Reasoned:** a filling cell is one visual rule for every type. A yes/no day is simply 0% or 100%, which is the same "fills as you go" model as the Day card (Question 34). Nobody has to learn two kinds of cell.

**3. "Not logged" and "No" must look different.**
- "In Yes/No type habit No should be distinguished from No Log." `P3#5677`
- Notion C256 says the same: done, skipped, missed and not-yet-logged are distinct states.
- **Hence:**
  - empty outline for nothing logged;
  - dashed with "–" for skipped;
  - faint for days still to come.

**4. Keep the marks simple.**
- When Loop added outlined ticks beside filled ones, users revolted: "outlined check marks along with the filled-in check marks is confusing, distracting and clutters up the interface" `P3#7440`. 4 reviews say this.
- **So each state has one look,** and ✓ appears only on done cells.

**5. No percentage anywhere in the grid.**
- Loop's smoothed score confuses its own users: "The way percentage for the chart/graph is calculated doesnt make sense." `P3#4203` (6 such here, and 111 across the Home Screen Cards corpus).
- The cells already show each day plainly.

---

**Limits.**
- **Space:** a number inside a 46 pt cell stays small; "5k" is the longest. Test legibility at large text sizes.
- **Reasoned:** tapping a past day's cell to add one. Backfill is wanted (Notion C010), but a mistaken tap on an old day is possible. The Undo bar covers it.
