# Today Screen, Question 6: The "Now" Button

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** A **Now** button sits next to the view switcher. The Afternoon section header also carries a small "Now" badge.

**The question.** What is this button for? Do people need it? If they do, where should it live?

**What it does today.** It is the optional "Now focus" from the Day Structure report.
- When on, Home shows only the current part of the day (for example Afternoon) and hides the other sections.
- It is off by default and remembered.
- It came from a small group of reviews praising apps that offer "either the habits due now or the whole day".

**Basis.**
- **Direct screen:** all 1,487,223 reviews for "only show what's due now", time-of-day tab or view, focus view, and hiding or moving completed habits. 154 matched; all 154 were read, 44 relevant.
- **Flags from other questions:** 16 reviews coded under Questions 1 to 5.
- **Earlier coding:** the Day Structure report's hand-coded set of 88 reviews on views split by time of day.
- **External:** Apple's guidance on toolbar controls.

---

## What to do

1. **Remove the "Now" button from the top.** Its meaning isn't clear from the label; even our own team had to ask what it does. The need behind it is met better in three quieter ways (below).
2. **Keep the badge, and use it to guide the eye.**
   - The current section keeps its **Now** badge.
   - When Home opens, the list scrolls so that section is at the top.
   - Finished earlier sections are collapsed. Unfinished ones stay visible, marked "earlier today".
   - Nothing is ever hidden because the clock moved on.
3. **Put "Due now" inside the one filter button from Question 5.**
   - The menu reads: **All habits · Due now (Afternoon) · Hide done · [groups]**.
   - When anything other than All is on, the button fills and the period line says so, for example "showing 3 of 11".
   - The app always opens on All.
4. **Offer "Hide done" in the same menu.** This is asked for far more often than a Now view, 29 reviews against 7.
   - Leave it off by default. Some people like to see what they have finished.
   - By default, done habits sink to the bottom of their section.
5. **Never switch the view by itself.** No automatic jump to the current period, and no starting on anything other than All.

With these changes, the filter button now appears whenever day sections are on, which is the default. It is still one small control at the end of the period line, and costs no row. This refines Question 5: the button appears once there is anything to choose.

---

## Key findings

**1. A "now only" view helps a few people.**
- 7 direct reviews, plus 5 in the Day Structure set, praise or ask for a current-period view:
  - "I love the way you only see the habits for the current part of the day, because it really makes it look like theres less to be done which makes it a lot easier to get started." `A13#736`
  - "particularly the new slider at the top to switch between the current part of the day versus the entire day." `A13#14645`
  - "there’s no way to see only what’s currently due." `A43#218`
- So the option is worth keeping, but only as an option.

**2. Forcing it, or hiding behind it, hurts many more.** The Day Structure coding of 88 reviews found:

| Complaint | Reviews |
|---|---|
| No single overview: habits are split across time-of-day pages | 25 |
| Want to open on the whole day / have the view remembered | 12 |
| The app jumps to the current period by itself | 10 |
| Unfinished habits disappear when their time passes | 4 |

These are mostly Productive's 2019 redesign:
- "It is very easy to miss something then, when you autoshift, for example, from Morning to Afternoon view." `A13#12184`
- "At least let the default view be “all day” so I don’t have to swipe back and forth" `A13#13702`

A time-of-day filter can also silently hide habits: "some habits weren’t showing up on the list. I then found out that it was because of the selected time of day filter on the top of the screen." `A33#1164`

**3. The bigger wish is "show me what's left", met by hiding or sinking done habits (29 for, 7 against).**
- "without an ability to hide completed habits for the day looks too cluttered." `A13#11175`
- "I find it very odd that anyone would want to stare at completed to-do items." `P2#12803`
- **But not everyone wants it:** "I don't like the update that hide the completed habits" `P125#4284`

So "Hide done" is a choice, and sinking done habits to the bottom is the default.

**4. A button called "Now" breaks a basic guideline.** Apple: "Make sure the meaning of each control is clear. Don't make people guess or experiment to figure out what a toolbar item does."
- Next to a view switcher, "Now" reads as a jump to today, a timer, or a mode. People won't guess that it hides sections.
- A named option in a menu ("Due now (Afternoon)") explains itself.

## What other apps do

| App | How it handles "now" | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **Habitify** | Area bar at the top that switches to the current time block by itself ([help centre](https://intercom.help/habitify-app/en/articles/7990118-manage-the-time-of-day)) | Praised by some; the review above says it silently hid habits |
| **Productive** | Opens on the current part of the day | The 2019 complaint wave |
| **Streaks, Loop, HabitKit** | No now view at all | — |
| **Do Habits** | Time of day kept as labels inside one list | Praised: "still view the whole list together" (N66 in the Day Structure report) |

---

**Limits.**
- **Few direct reviews:** the direct screen is small (44 relevant), and most of the weight comes from the earlier Day Structure coding.
- **Nearby wording:** "Hide done" counts include some to-do apps (5 of the 29). The habit-app share alone (25) still clearly outnumbers the Now requests.
