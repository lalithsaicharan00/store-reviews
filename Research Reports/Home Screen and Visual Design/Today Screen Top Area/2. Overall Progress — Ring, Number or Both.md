# Today Screen, Question 2: Overall Progress (Ring, Number or Both)

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** Row 1 has an overall ring with "5/11". Row 2, the date strip, already gives every day its own ring, including today (Thu 24).

**The question.** The header ring and today's ring in the strip show the same thing. Is that redundant? Do people want a ring, a number, or both? And if both, how do we show them without clutter? Can progress merge neatly into the date strip?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews, in two passes: progress-display wording, then ring, percentage and "how many left" wording in habit apps.
- **Read in full:** 1,255 matches (generic "progress" in other languages was kept only when a ring, bar or percentage was mentioned).
- **Relevant:** 316.
- **Competitor check:** the same 49-app Home census used in Question 1.

Citations follow the usual `A`/`P`/`N` + line format.

---

## What to do

1. **Show today's progress once, not twice.** Delete the header ring. Today's cell in the date strip is the ring.
2. **Put the number under the selected day in the strip.** A small caption under that day's ring reads **"5 of 11"**. The ring and the number now sit together, and each appears only once.
3. **Prefer "5 of 11" to "45%" on Today.** A count is concrete, says how much is left, and doesn't read as a failing grade at 2 pm.
   - Keep percentages for the Progress tab and for longer periods.
4. **Rings fill in proportion to what's done.** 5 of 11 fills 45% of the ring, never all-or-nothing. A complete day gets a full ring plus the done mark.
5. **Count only habits that are due that day.** Weekly, "x times a week", off-day and skipped habits must not drag the number down (see the Non-Scheduled Days report).
6. **Everything else stays quiet.** No 0% shown in red, no "you failed today" wording. When all habits are done, one small celebration.

```
Now:     Today  Thu 24 Sep · 14:30          ◔ 5/11   (+)
         Fri  Sat  Sun  Mon  Tue  Wed  Thu
         (18) (19) (20) (21) (22) (23) [24]

Better:  THURSDAY 24 SEPTEMBER
         Today                                      (+)
         Fri  Sat  Sun  Mon  Tue  Wed  Thu
         (18) (19) (20) (21) (22) (23) [◔24]
                                        5 of 11
```

## Key findings

**1. People do want an overall "how am I doing today" figure (105 reviews, 35 apps, mean 4.10★).** Most of these are requests from apps that don't have one. Loop users ask for it constantly:
- "only missing feature would be overall score at the top as an extra motivation" `P3#1800`
- "to see our daily score in the main tab itself" `P3#2147`

Only 3 reviews call the overall figure unwanted. So the element stays; the question is only its form.

**2. Showing it twice is noticed and disliked.** A Habitify user described exactly our duplication:
- "Redundancy: The current day feedback, a vertical line in place of the histogram DUPLICATES information already available" `A33#1248`

12 more reviews complain about the same thing shown twice (the same menu in two places, a tick plus a colour that says the same). In the competitor census, 7 of 49 apps put a ring on each strip day. **None of them also puts a ring in the header.**

**3. The number matters more than the shape.**

| What people ask for | Reviews |
|---|---|
| The number (count or %) | 47 |
| What's left ("2 left") | 19 |
| Ring and number together | 15 |
| A ring | 22 habit + 28 Apple Fitness |
| A bar | 20 |
| Against rings | 4 |
| Against numbers | 3 |

The ring is liked as a feeling ("I get satisfaction as the circle fills" `A23#6782`). The number is what people read.

The "both" requests all ask for the number placed **on or inside** the circle, not beside it:
- "Show the count inside the circle" `A55#606`
- "if I did 2/4 habits that day it would say 2/4 and the status circle would be half filled" `A1#47380`

**4. A count beats a percentage on Today.** 19 reviews say percentages or ring messages feel like judgement, and 29 find percentages confusing ("how is it calculated?").
- "It was a bit of a downer to see on the third day 66% when I don't do that thing until evening. I'm conditioned to see that as a failing grade." `P24#14101`
- An app was praised for *not* showing a day score: "it used to bum me out to see that the days weren’t at “100%” if I forgot to do something" `A10#46703`

A count ("5 of 11") answers what people actually want to know, which is what's left: "I'd like to be able to estimate quickly how many habits left." `P2#22994`

**5. Day rings must fill in part (13 + 23 reviews).** All-or-nothing circles are a named complaint:
- "If you complete all of your habits, the circle in the day fills up. If don’t complete all your habits, the circle gets outlined. I’d prefer if the circle filled a little with each habit I complete." `A13#11415`

23 more reviews want each day's progress visible in the week or month, which is exactly what the strip rings do.

**6. The denominator must be honest.** 29 reviews say the figure is wrong or unclear, mostly because it counts weekly habits, skipped days or unscheduled days.
- "there is this big circle showing a percentage of over all daily task completion. The problem is, that weekly tasks are somehow not relevant for that percentage." `A41#220`

**7. Per-category rings are a niche request (8 reviews).** For example, "multiple different colored rings … Each ring can be a category" `A1#2448`. This belongs to group chips (Question 5), not to the header.

## What other apps do

**Header progress:** 16 of the 49 competitor Home screens show overall progress near the top:
- a count ("4 of 6", "0/1 done"): 9
- a ring: 4
- a bar: 2
- text: 1

**Two apps that come close to our layout:**
- **Blossom:** a date strip, plus a separate "TODAY 4 of 6" card with a 66% ring.
- **Habit Tracker Daily Goals:** a thin progress bar directly under the strip.

**Strip rings:** in all 7 apps that ring each strip day, the strip itself carries the progress. None adds a header ring on top.

**Apple Fitness** (28 reviews): the ring motivates people, but the notifications that go with it are the most-cited source of shame ("you didn’t close your ring").

---

**Limits.**
- **Mostly requests:** most support for an overall figure comes from apps without one, so the count measures demand, not tested satisfaction.
- **Concentration:** 35 of the per-habit progress-bar reviews come from one app, so they are counted separately and not used here.
- **Unfamiliar layout:** no reviewer described a caption under the selected strip day, because no app reviewed does it. It is a design inference from findings 2 to 4.
- **One coder:** Claude coded every review. Codes are in the evidence folder.
