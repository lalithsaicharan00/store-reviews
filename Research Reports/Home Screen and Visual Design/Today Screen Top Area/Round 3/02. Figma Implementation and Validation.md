## Consistency Corrections During Implementation

- The retained Round 2 active-filter example displayed Anytime, Morning and Evening despite selecting Afternoon + Health. Round 3 now shows only Walk and Meds, with “1 of 2” completion. The Learning zero-result example says “0 due.” These are logical consistency corrections, not additional research claims.
- The illustrative fixture totals are 18 assigned and 11 scheduled, in both the group and section breakdowns. The exact fixture is saved in [Illustrative Design Fixture](<Evidence/Illustrative Design Fixture.json>).
- Contextual creation returns to the existing filter without automatically selecting the new group or section. Back/Cancel preserve context. Inventory browsing does not log, reschedule or add an off-day habit to Today.
- Group deletion keeps habits in Ungrouped; section removal moves them to Anytime and preserves history. These are protective design specifications consistent with the missing-habit evidence, not experimentally established preferences. Confirmation details are outside this iteration.
- Night shift is 22:00–06:00 in this example. Tracking-day attribution changes at 04:00 independently. The 03:59/04:00 example explains the exact boundary; no end-time field is added.

## Validation

**Round 2 preservation:** Before and after snapshots of 4,960 nodes matched: fingerprint `a33a6c23`, serialized snapshot size 1,997,827 characters. The snapshot covers hierarchy, IDs, names, geometry, visibility, paint, typography, auto-layout properties and reactions. No Round 2 node was intentionally edited. See [Figma Validation](<Evidence/Figma Validation.json>).

**Review evidence:** All 83 saved evidence records—32 prior-source verifications and 51 targeted candidates—resolve to their exact review IDs at their saved one-based physical source lines. No mismatches. These are selected evidence records, not a representative sample or prevalence estimate. See [Source Verification](<Evidence/Source Verification.json>).

**Design checks:**

- All rendered text uses the existing DM Sans family.
- Final structural audit found no collapsed text or text extending outside its auto-layout parent in the 35 screens.
- No obsolete “Times of day,” combined “views and dates,” old phrase-frequency justification or old layout/calendar instruction remains in visible Round 3 text.
- Main headers, calendar, Filter, Your day, layout chooser, inventory, zero states and representative forms were inspected as full-screen renders. Targeted visual fixes were followed by new renders. The whole section was also reviewed for board spacing and annotations.
- New action-control instances use at least 44 pt height. Existing inherited controls were retained except where this iteration changed their sizing or role.
- Two local component families (Action and Chip) provide reusable controls; the section has 149 instances. Foundations include one local variable collection with 16 variables and three text styles. No unrelated library was imported.
- Representative navigation is wired: 84 destination links, plus Back/Cancel/Done actions; 121 nodes have reactions. Destination audit found no links leaving Round 3.

**Prototype limits:** This is a design iteration with representative clickable navigation and illustrative data. Form values, layout options, filter choices and schedule calculations are not a complete stateful application simulation. Save/Create actions illustrate returning to the previous context; they do not persist real habit data. Large-type, long-name, localization and usability testing remain unperformed, as stated in the research report. No production behavior or winning terminology is claimed to have been validated by those checks.
