# Today Screen, Question 9: Naming the Screen and Tab — Is "Today" Still Right?

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** The first tab is called **Today**. The screen now also has Week, Month and Year views (Question 4).

**The question.** If the screen can show a whole month, is "Today" still the right name? If not, what should it be called?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for how people name an app's main screen, "Today" used as a label, and wanting only today or more than today.
- **Read in full:**
  - every habit-app review that uses "Today" as a label (151);
  - every one that names the main screen as a today, daily, habits, main, summary, journal, overview or checklist screen (595);
  - random samples of 150 "home screen / home page" mentions (from 1,775) and 100 "dashboard" mentions (from 245).
  - That is 1,006 reviews in all, 605 of them relevant.
- **Also used:** the 49-app census and Apple's tab-bar guidance.

---

## What to do

1. **Keep the name "Today"** for both the tab and the screen.
   - It names where the screen always starts and what people use most.
   - Week, Month and Year are views of the time *around* today. They open on the current week, month and year.
2. **Make the tab keep its promise.**
   - Tapping **Today** while already on it jumps back to today: Day view, today's date, top of the list.
   - Opening the app on a new day always lands on today.
3. **Never let the word "Today" sit on anything that isn't today.**
   - The period line names what is shown: "21–27 September", "September 2026", "Wednesday 23 September" (Question 4).
   - The same rule applies to widgets.
   - The big "Today" heading is already gone (Question 1), so the only "Today" left is the tab.
4. **Don't rename it "Home".** Most people use "home screen" to mean the phone's home screen, which is where our widgets live.
5. **Don't rename it "Habits".** People expect "Habits" to mean the full list of every habit they manage, not today's checklist. Keep that word for that list.
6. **Keep "Dashboard" and "Summary" for the Progress tab.** When reviewers ask for a dashboard, they usually mean stats across habits and time.

---

## Key findings

**1. People have no shared name for this screen. They use whatever the app calls it.**

| What reviewers call the main screen | Reviews | Apps |
|---|---|---|
| "main screen / page / view / list" | 254 | 48 |
| "Today" tab / page / view | 92 | 20 |
| "dashboard" (64 of the 100 sampled) | 66 | 29 |
| Journal / summary / overview / tracker page | 47 | 20 |
| The app's own "home screen / page" (32 of the 150 sampled) | 36 | 16 |
| "habits" tab / page | 33 | 15 |
| "daily view" | 24 | 14 |

- "Main screen" is simply the default phrase.
- "Today" shows up mostly where the app itself says Today. HabitNow alone gives 34 of the 92.
- So people pick up the label they are shown. That frees us to choose the label that describes the screen best.
- The Today page is the one people live on: "this page is the one you use most." `A1#53903`

**2. When people complain about "Today", the day is wrong, not the name.** There are 15 such complaints:
- **7 are about midnight or the day boundary:** "after midnight, I might want to check some habits that are technically from yesterday, but in my mind it's still" today `P19#80`.
- **4 are about a label that kept saying Today on another day:** "you can use the arrows to go to different dates, but the date shown at the top quickly gets replaced with" Today `P2#2429`.
- **4 are bugs** where "Today" showed the wrong date.
- **None** objects to the tab being called Today while showing a week or a month.
- The fixes are the day-start setting (Question 8) and a period line that always names the date (Questions 1 and 4). A new name would not help.

**3. People want both views: a focus on today, and a look beyond it.**
- **20 value a screen that shows just today:**
  - "only shows the current day habits on the interface instead of the whole week." `P3#3682`
  - "The Today view perspective makes it even easier to use." `A86#1464`
- **54, in 23 apps, want to see further** than one day, mostly the week:
  - Habitify "doesn't have weekly view on the main screen like loop" `P37#452`
- One screen that opens on today and switches to Week, Month or Year serves both groups. Naming it after where it opens is accurate.

**4. The name makes a promise, and people hold apps to it.**
- "I would like that the Today tab would show today whenever I open it" `P2#4732`
- So re-tapping the tab must return to today (What to do, point 2).

**5. "Home" and "Habits" already mean something else to people.**
- **Home:** in the random sample, 118 of 150 "home screen" mentions (79%) are not about the app's own screen. Nearly all mean the **phone's** home screen, usually about widgets:
  - "I have it set up so that each of my habits is a check mark on my home screen" `P3#8389`
  - Calling our tab "Home" would make every "add it to your home screen" instruction ambiguous.
- **Habits:** 10 people treat "Habits" as the full list, separate from today's:
  - "Add the option to show 'Habits' page on app open instead of 'Today' page." `P2#6923`
  - "the main view only shows “today” habits" `A53#713`

**6. Competitors and Apple point the same way.**
- **Competitors:** "Today" is the most common title in the 49-app census, used by 15 apps. No other single word comes close.
- **Apple:** a tab label helps people navigate "by clearly describing the type of content or functionality the tab contains. Use single words whenever possible."
  - Today is one word and describes the default content.
  - Apple's Calendar app uses the same idea: a **Today** control that works from Day, Week, Month and Year views and always brings you back to the current date.

---

**Limits.**
- **Nobody reviews a tab name directly.** The recommendation rests on how people talk, the complaints above, the census and Apple's guidance. A quick first-click test can confirm it: "Where would you tick off this morning's habits?"
- **Some counts come from samples.** The "home" and "dashboard" shares come from random samples of 150 and 100 (seed 20260924). The main-screen and Today counts come from reading every match.
- **HabitNow dominates the "Today" vocabulary** with 34 of the 92.
