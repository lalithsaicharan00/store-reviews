# Today Screen, Question 26: The Calendar's Today Button, and Two Small Fixes

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3 follow-up 2.

## 1. The calendar sheet's Today button

**The feedback.**
- **Today** sits next to the ‹ › arrows, so it pushes them around when it appears.
- It also shows even when today is already selected.

**What to do.**
- **Today appears only when you are away:** another month is showing, or another day is selected.
- **It lives in the sheet's title row, left of ✕:** "Pick a day · [Today] ✕". That spot is outside the month row.
- **The month row never changes shape.** It is always "September 2026 … ‹ ›", with the arrows pinned to the right edge.

**Why.**
- **Reviews:** people need a quick way back.
  - Round 1 found 9 reviews asking for an obvious way back to today.
  - One reviewer: "there's no way to quickly return to today's date with a single tap" `A25#962`
- **Reasoned from first principles:**
  - Someone paging months taps the arrows again and again at the same spot, so the arrows must never move.
  - A control that appears must not shift controls people are about to tap.
  - The main header already shows a **Today** button in its top row only when you're away (Question 14). Using the same place and the same rule in the sheet makes one habit people learn once.

## 2. View sheet subtexts: simpler

**The feedback.** Drop the count explanations. Give each heading a short, plain subtext.

**The user's decision:**

| Heading | Subtext |
|---|---|
| Layouts | How your cards look |
| Day sections | Parts of your day |
| Groups | Areas like Health or Work |

- This supersedes Question 21's smart count line.
- The numbers stay: today's habits, never "due".
- Totals stay in Edit.

## 3. One "New day starts at" card everywhere

**The feedback.** The card must look the same wherever it appears.

**What to do.** Every Day sections screen shows the same card, including the empty one:
- **New day starts at [04:00]**;
- "Anything you tick before 04:00 counts for the day before.";
- the example "Thu 04:00 → Fri 03:59 = all Thursday".

**Why (reasoned).** Question 19 found that people are unsure which day a late tick counts for. The explanation is what answers that, so it can't be dropped on some screens.
