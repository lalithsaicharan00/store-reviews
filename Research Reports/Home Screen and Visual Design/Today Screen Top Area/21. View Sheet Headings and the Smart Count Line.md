# Today Screen, Question 21: View Sheet Headings and the Smart Count Line

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

> **Revised by Question 26:** the smart count line was dropped at the user's request. Subtexts are now short: "How your cards look", "Parts of your day", "Areas like Health or Work".

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3 follow-up.

**Screen.**
- In the View sheet, "Day sections" and "Groups" are 16 px bold headings, but LAYOUT and SHOW are small grey labels.
- The only explanation of the numbers is "Numbers = due Thu 24", in the corner.

**The feedback.**
- All three parts get the same heading style (**Layouts, Day sections, Groups**), each with a subtext line.
- The subtext explains that the numbers are *today's* habits, not the section's or group's total.
- No "due".
- The line only speaks up when it matters, and names the sections or groups that differ.

**Basis.**
- **Counted:** how people word it in 876,180 habit-app reviews.
- **Reused:** Question 18's count rules, and Baymard's guidance on filter counts.

---

## What to do

1. **Three headings, one style:** 16 px bold, with a 13 px grey subtext beneath.

   | Heading | Subtext |
   |---|---|
   | **Layouts** | How your cards look |
   | **Day sections** | Show one part of your day. *[count line]* |
   | **Groups** | Show one area, like Health. *[count line]* |

2. **The count line changes with the data:**

   | Situation | Line |
   |---|---|
   | Every chip's number equals its total | *(no count line: the numbers can't be misread)* |
   | 1–2 differ | "Numbers are today's habits. **Morning** has 4 in all." / "…**Health** has 12 and **Mind** 4 in all." |
   | 3 or more differ | "Numbers are today's habits, not each group's total." |
   | Some group has none today | Covered by the same line; its chip reads "0" |

3. **Never "due".** Use "today's habits". When the selected day isn't today, use that day: "Numbers are Tuesday's habits".
4. **Totals stay in Edit** ("Health · 12 habits"), so the line only needs to point there when it helps.

---

## Key findings

**1. People say "today's habits", not "due".**

| Phrase in habit-app reviews | Reviews |
|---|---|
| "today's habits / tasks / list" | 43 |
| "habits for today" | 36 |
| "habits due today" | 4 |
| "scheduled today" | 4 |

**2. A number needs its meaning next to it, but only when there is something to misread.**
- Baymard: showing the number beside each option "gives users confidence before they click".
- If today equals the total everywhere, a line explaining the difference is noise.
- When only Morning differs, naming Morning answers the one question someone might have ("why 3? I have 4"). That is faster to scan than a general rule.

**3. Same style, same weight.**
- The three parts are siblings: how things look, which part of the day, which area.
- Giving them identical headings makes the sheet read as three equal sections.

**Limits.**
- **Unproven wording:** the exact strings ("in all", "today's habits") are proposals. Check them with a quick first-read test.
