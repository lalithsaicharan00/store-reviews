## What the user asked for (their points, in order)

1. **Tapping "Today" opens the calendar.** Keep this. It's good.
2. **Take Day · Week · Month · Year out of the calendar sheet.** They are layouts of the habit cards on Home, not dates. Find them an intuitive home: tucked away (they're rarely used), but not in the wrong place.
3. **The filter is almost good.**
   - Put **Edit** right next to the time-of-day heading/options, not far away.
   - Same for **Groups**.
4. **Adding from the filter:**
   - Should there be an **Add a new group** option inside the filter? Research whether it's intuitive.
   - Same question for **Add a new day section**.
5. **Better copy for "Time of day".**
   - Go through the Home Screen and Visual Design reports and the Day Structure and Organization reports; the better name should come from them.
   - The sheet must clearly say: this is a filter, and from here you can also edit and add.
6. **What the numbers mean** (on sections: Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening; and on groups):
   - Total habits in the group or section, versus what's scheduled today. Make the difference clear.
   - If a group or section has nothing today, should it show? How?
   - How do people reach the habits in a group or section that aren't on today?
7. **Start of the day.**
   - Only the start is needed: the minute before it is the end of the previous day. Say that clearly.
   - It is related to times of day, so it may belong with them. Research this.
8. **Overall:** improve the filter and find a better place for the layout control. It changes the cards' layout; it is not a calendar thing.
