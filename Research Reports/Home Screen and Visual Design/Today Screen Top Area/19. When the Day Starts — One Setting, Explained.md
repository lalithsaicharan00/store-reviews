# Today Screen, Question 19: When the Day Starts — One Setting, Explained

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3.

**Screen (round 2).** The editor has a "Day starts at 04:00" card: "Evening runs until then. A 01:00 tick counts for the day you started."

**The feedback.**
- Only the **start** is needed, because the minute before it is the end of the previous day. Say that clearly.
- It relates to day sections, so it may belong with them.

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 149 new day-start matches.
- **Reused:**
  - round 1's 154 midnight-rollover reviews;
  - the Day Structure report's day-start model.

---

## What to do

1. **One setting: New day starts at [04:00].** No end field.
2. **Explain it with the user's own situation, right under the time:**
   - "Anything you tick before 04:00 counts for the day before."
   - A one-line example: **"Thu 04:00 → Fri 03:59 is all Thursday."**
3. **Place it at the top of the Day sections editor.** That is where people shape their day. Also list it in Me → Settings, leading to the same screen.
4. **Tie it to the sections visually:**
   - the sections are listed in time order below it;
   - the last section reads "**Evening · 18:00 – 04:00**", so the day's end is visible without a second input.
5. **Default a few hours after midnight** (04:00 here), as the Day Structure report suggests. The exact default is a product call.

---

## Key findings

**1. The need is real, and mostly night owls and shift workers.**
- 51 reviews in 22 apps ask to move the day boundary. Round 1 adds 154 midnight-rollover reviews.
- "I work until 3am, so I don't want a new day to start at midnight" `A1#45753`
- "its so annoying that everything resets midnight and I still wants to fill up some habits" `A53#1284`

**2. People mostly think in "start", and the "end" framing is the same setting.**
- Of 54 reviews that name the setting, 28 say start, begin or new day, 15 say reset, 14 say end, and 4 say rollover.
- One review uses both framings for one thing: its title asks to "select what time the day ends", and the body says "choose when the next day starts" `A41#296`
- So one input, worded as a start and explained as an end, covers both ways of thinking.

**3. An unexplained setting confuses people, even when it works.**
- About Streaks' "start of the day" setting, one reviewer wrote: "feature is really not intuitive and I still don't understand how it works" `A23#0`
- One reviewer describes exactly the rule proposed here, saying that delaying the "day starts" time should "extend the preceding day" `A23#5057`
- A concrete example ("Thu 04:00 → Fri 03:59 is all Thursday") answers the question people actually have: which day does my late tick count for?

**4. It belongs with the day sections, but it is its own line.**
- The Day Structure report: "Day starts at" "is when yesterday closes and today opens. It is not the same as Morning's start". The last section "runs until the day start".
- Putting both on one screen makes that relationship visible. Keeping it as a separate first row stops people confusing it with Morning's start time.

---

**Limits.**
- **Placement is a design inference.** Reviews ask for the setting but don't say where it should live.
- **Test the example line:** with night-shift users.
