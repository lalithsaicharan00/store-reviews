# Today Screen, Question 37: Section Headers — Name Only

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 5.

**The feedback.** Each section header carries a grey subtext. Is it needed?
- **Quitting:** "live · not in the day count"
- **Anytime:** "any time"
- **Morning / Afternoon / Evening:** the start time ("06:00", "12:00", "18:00"), which the person already knows.

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 12 new matches on when a part of the day starts, or on habits landing in the wrong section.
- **Reused:** the Day Structure verification (1,574 hand-coded reviews on sections, including 248 hand-sub-typed complaints) and Questions 25 and 35–36.
- **Screenshots:** Productive (App Store 13) and Habitify (App Store 33), two of the apps whose sections draw the most praise.

---

## What to do

**Headers show the name only**, plus the **Now** badge on the current section, and the count and Start where they apply.

| Header | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Quitting | Quitting · live · not in the day count | **Quitting** |
| Anytime | Anytime · any time | **Anytime** |
| Morning | Morning · 06:00 | **Morning** |
| Afternoon | Afternoon · 12:00 · Now | **Afternoon · Now** |
| Evening | Evening · 18:00 | **Evening** |

Section times stay where they are set and changed: the Day sections editor and Edit section (Question 25).

---

## Key findings

**1. Nobody asks to see section times on the list; they ask to set them.**
- The Day Structure verification found 125 reviews wanting to customise sections (boundaries, day start, more sections) and 22 complaining about fixed boundaries.
- Every one of these is about **changing** the times ("I can change the times for morning, afternoon and evening to fit my life" `A1#53099`). None asks to **see** them on Today.
- The whole-corpus screen found no review confused about when a section starts. Its 12 matches were about habits hidden or sitting in the wrong section.
- So the time is set once in the editor, and people know it because they chose it (or accepted the default).

**2. What the time was doing is already done elsewhere (reasoned from first principles).**
- "Where am I in my day?" is answered by the **Now** badge on the current section.
- "When is this habit?" is answered by the habit's own time on its card (Question 28: 10 reviews asked for it).
- A section's start time answers neither question. It repeats a setting.

**3. The subtexts under Anytime and Quitting only restate the header.**
- "Anytime · any time" says the same thing twice.
- "live" is shown by the ticking counters themselves (Question 36).
- "Not in the day count" is visible: the Quitting header has no count, while every other header shows one ("1/3").
- Stay-minimal is the strongest praise theme in this category: Days Since's "radical simplicity" is 3,647 reviews (34.3%), and the Notion card C006 says the same. A line that restates what is visible is exactly what that asks us to cut.

**4. The best-praised section apps show names only.**
- In their current listing screenshots, Productive shows "Evening · All Day · Morning" and Habitify shows "Morning", both with no times.
- Productive and Fabulous account for 402 of the 656 reviews praising sections in the Day Structure verification.
- Names only is therefore consistent with what people already praise.

---

**Limits.**
- **Based on absence:** the case rests on no one asking for the times, plus reasoning. It is not direct evidence that times on headers hurt.
- **Test:** whether someone who has just renamed a section to something like "Before work" still knows where it falls. The order of the sections and the Now badge should carry it.
