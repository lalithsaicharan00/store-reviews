# Today Screen, Question 38: Section Counts — Only When Collapsed

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 5.

**The feedback.**
- Every section header shows done/total ("1/3", "3/3"), which makes it look important.
- Is the count needed at all? If so, which part matters: done, total, or how many are left?
- If it stays, it should sit beside the name.
- If the assumption is wrong, change nothing.

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:**
  - 13 matches on "how many left";
  - 51 on "X of Y";
  - 17 on progress per section or group;
  - 35 on counts feeling cluttered or stressful.
- **Reused:** Question 22 (the day's count lives in the selected day's card in the week strip: "5/11") and Question 37 (headers show the name only).

---

## What to do

1. **Drop done/total from every section header.** It repeats what the cards already show when a section is open.
2. **A collapsed section shows, beside its name, what its hidden cards would tell you:**
   - **"2 left"** if some are still to do;
   - **"All done"** if none are.
3. **An open section shows only its name** (plus Now and Start where they apply).
4. **The day's overall count stays** in the week strip's today card ("5/11").

| Header | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Anytime (open) | Anytime · 1/3 · Start | **Anytime** · Start |
| Morning (collapsed) | Morning · 3/3 | **Morning · All done** |
| Afternoon (open) | Afternoon · Now · 1/2 · Start | **Afternoon · Now** · Start |
| Evening (collapsed, 1 of 3 done) | Evening · 1/3 | **Evening · 2 left** |

Your assumption was half right. The count is not needed on open sections. When a section is collapsed, the useful number is how many are **left**, not the total.

---

## Key findings

**1. Nobody asks for a count per part of the day.**
- The 17 "progress per section or group" matches are about Fabulous's routine screens and progress by group or category:
  - "how many tasks of each group has been completed" `P2#22962`
  - "see the overall percentage for each category" `P30#3622`
- None asks for a done/total per time of day. Groups are a filter here, so a group's progress belongs in Progress, not on Today's headers.

**2. When people ask for a number, it is "how many are left".**
- 9 reviews want the remaining count, mostly on the app icon or a widget:
  - "I love that the number of habits left for the day shows up on the icon" `A13#2628`
  - "I'd like to be able to estimate quickly how many habits left." `P2#22994`
  - "a view of habits remaining today" `P31#418`
- One also praises the moment it reaches zero: "something about no goals left for today is so satisfying" `A10#29587`

**3. Done/total is asked for at the level of the whole day, and we already show it there.**
- 6 reviews ask for the day's total:
  - "if I did 2/4 habits that day it would say 2/4 and the status circle would be half filled" `A1#47380`
  - "if I could see “5/7 tasks completed today”" `A86#1309`
  - "Today’s Progress: 100% (10/10 habits completed)" `P92#7`
- Question 22 put exactly this in the selected day's card in the week strip ("5/11"). Repeating it per section splits one number into four.

**4. Why the collapsed exception (reasoned from first principles).**
- An open section's cards already show what's done and what's left, each with its own fill and button.
- A header count there only repeats them.
- A **collapsed** section hides its cards, so the header must speak for them.
- The question someone has about a hidden section is "is there anything still to do in there?". "2 left" or "All done" answers it; "1/3" makes them do the subtraction.

**5. Fewer numbers is what people praise.**
- "Info on the main page doesn't overwhelm you with percentages" `P3#14012`
- A badge number "stressed me out" until it could be turned off `A23#4472`
- Stay minimal (Notion C006) points the same way. Every number on Today should answer a question the screen doesn't already answer.

---

**Limits.**
- **Mostly reasoned:** the collapsed-only rule. The reviews show what number people want ("left"), not where.
- **Small counts:** 9 and 6 reviews.
- **Test:** whether people notice the "2 left" on a collapsed section, and whether anyone misses the per-section fraction.
