# Q47 codebook — day parts as top tabs, swiping between them
TAB_POS          likes filtering/tabbing by part of day (seeing one part is less overwhelming)
TAB_OK           used to dislike the tabs, now fine
TAB_NEG          dislikes day-part tabs or pages: no single overview, has to swipe or tap between parts, can't turn them off
AUTOSWITCH       the app jumps to the current part on open, even when it is empty or when the user chose All
HIDDEN_PART      a habit from another part (a missed morning one, or an all-day one) is hidden
HIDDEN_FILTER    thought habits were missing because a time-of-day filter was on
ALLDAY_DEFAULT   wants "all day" to be the default page
ANYTIME_ALWAYS   wants Anytime habits visible in every part
ACCIDENT         swiping between parts and ticking a habit are the same motion, so taps go wrong
TAB_BUG          a bug in part tabs (not synchronised, wrong content, can't select)
WANT_TABS        asks for a tab or page per part of the day or routine
WANT_HEADERS     asks for headers or groups inside one list, not filters
HEADERS_POS      likes day parts shown as sections or headers in one list
PARTS_REMOVED_BAD  complains that the app removed day parts (Me+)
WANT_CURRENT     wants the app or widget to show only the current part
CURRENT_ONLY_POS likes seeing only the current step (routine player)
PAGES_USE        uses pages (Streaks) or phone pages to separate parts of the day
PAGES_NEG        dislikes flipping or swiping across pages; wants everything on one page
WANT_SWIPE       wants swipe left/right between pages, lists or tabs
SWIPE_POS        likes swiping between lists or categories
SWIPE_CONFLICT   swiping to change list collides with swipe-to-delete or scrolling
TOPTABS_NEG      dislikes horizontal top tabs for many lists (Google Tasks 2026)
LISTTAB_POS      likes tabs for lists (to-do apps)
WANT_ALL         wants to see everything at once, not one list/page at a time
DAYSWIPE         wants swipe left/right to change the day
SHIFT            fixed day parts don't fit night/shift work
Screened: habit apps (App + Play) plus Google Tasks (N6). Families: D1_PARTCTRL 154, D4_PARTHIDE 114, D3_SWIPELIST 74, D2_ONLYPART 3; 341 unique. All read. 119 codes on 115 reviews; every quote verified by script.
