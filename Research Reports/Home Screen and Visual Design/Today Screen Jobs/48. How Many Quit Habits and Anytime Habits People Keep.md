# Today Screen, Question 48: How Many Quit Habits and Anytime Habits People Keep

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 25 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision. Data only; nothing is designed here.** Decisions live in Notion. This follows Question 46.

**The question.** Question 46 showed how many habits people track in total:
- **1–3:** 37%
- **4–10:** 44%
- **11–20:** 14%
- **21+:** 6%

Two parts of the Today screen are exceptions to the usual habit list: **Quitting** and **Anytime**. Out of 100%:
- if someone uses quit habits, **how many** do they keep at once?
- **how many Anytime habits** do people have?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** every App Store and Play Store review of habit and quit apps for:
  - numbers next to "counters", "timers", "trackers", "addictions", "vices", "bad habits", "habits I'm quitting";
  - numbers next to "quit", "kick", "break" or "sober from";
  - numbers next to "anytime" or "all-day" habits.
- **Read by hand:** 540 matches, after removing 544 "I tried 5 habit trackers" false hits.
- **Merged** with Question 43's counter statements.
- **Result:** 163 people say how many quit habits they have: **74 give an exact number** and **89 say "a few", "several", "many" or "multiple"**.
- **External:**
  - Loggd's published habit data (6,700+ habits from 3,600+ users; the 40 most-tracked habits by name);
  - searches for per-user data from I Am Sober, Quitzilla and Nomo, and from habit apps. None of them publish it.

Citations such as `A3#2066` point to line 2066 (from 0) of `App Store Reviews/3. …/reviews.jsonl`. `P` means Play Store.

---

## The short answer

**Quit habits (people who use quit tracking):**
- **1 or 2 is most people:** 64% of exact counts.
- **Median 2, mean 3.1.**
- **About 4 in 5** have **4 or fewer**. **9 in 10** have **6 or fewer**.
- **Rare:** 10+ (about 5%), up to about 20.
- **Including the vague answers, 87% have more than one.**

**Anytime habits:**
- **No review, study or app maker publishes a per-person count.** This is the one number here that isn't measured.
- **Best estimate, from a model:** about **2 anytime habits** for the middle person.
  - about **1 in 3** have 0–1;
  - about **4 in 5** have 5 or fewer;
  - about **1 in 15** have more than 10.
- **The range depends on one unknown:** what share of a person's habits are anytime. External data puts it between about **30% and 70%**.

## 2. Anytime habits: what exists, and an estimate

**What the reviews give.**
- 18 matches, **none with a per-person count**. Nobody writes "I have 4 anytime habits".
- Question 43 showed that anytime habits **exist and matter**:
  - 48 reviews describe habits with no set time;
  - hiding them rated 2.11★;
  - forcing a time rated 2.75★.
- One reviewer set **every** habit to all-day: "I set all my habits to be all day" `A13#6092`. Nobody reports the typical share.

**What outside data gives.** Loggd's 40 most-tracked habits (2,555 habit entries; one person can have several), sorted by whether the habit is tied to a time of day:

| Kind | Examples | Share of entries |
|---|---|---|
| **Time in the name** | Morning water, Morning stretch, In bed by 10pm, Morning routine | 9% |
| **Usually done at a set time** | Brush teeth, Take vitamins, Journal, Sleep 8 hours, Skincare, Make bed, Plan tomorrow, Meditate / Meditation | 14% |
| **Quit habits** | No alcohol, No junk food, No social media | 4% |
| **No natural time (anytime by nature)** | Gym, Exercise 30 min, 10k steps, Drink water, Read, Run, Study, Practice language | **73%** |

**Caveats:**
- Loggd's list is shaped by its one-tap suggestions: Loggd says eight of its top ten are onboarding picks.
- "Anytime by nature" is not the same as "the user files it under Anytime". Many people schedule the gym for the evening.
- So the true share of habits in Anytime is **somewhere between about 30% and 70%**.

**The estimate.** The Question 46 habit counts, with each habit anytime at 30%, 50% or 70%:

| Anytime habits | If 30% of habits are anytime | **If 50%** | If 70% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 22% | **10%** | 4% |
| 1 | 28% | **21%** | 14% |
| 2 | 19% | **20%** | 18% |
| 3 | 11% | **14%** | 15% |
| 4–5 | 10% | **16%** | 18% |
| 6–10 | 7% | **14%** | 19% |
| 11+ | 3% | **7%** | 12% |
| **Median** | **1** | **2** | **3** |
| **80% have at most** | **3** | **5** | **6–10** |

**Reading it:**
- Whatever the share, **most people have a handful of anytime habits: 0–5**.
- More than 10 is uncommon: 3–12%.
- This is a model, not a measurement. The one thing that would pin it down is our own usage data after launch.

## 3. How the pieces fit together (the Question 46 groups, split)

Using the medians: quit 2 (in mixed apps, 1–2), anytime about 2 at 50%.

| Group | Total habits | Typical quit | Typical anytime | Typical in timed parts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light (37%) | 1–3 | 0–1 | 1 | 1–2 |
| Core (44%) | 4–10 | 1–2 | 2–5 | 2–6 |
| Heavy (14%) | 11–20 | 2–4 | 5–10 | 5–10 |
| Extreme (6%) | 21+ | up to 6+ | 10+ | 10+ |

- This table is arithmetic on the medians above, not a separate measurement.
- It assumes someone uses quit habits at all. Many don't: quit habits are **4%** of Loggd's most-tracked habits.

## How far to trust these numbers

1. **Quit counts are measured but small:** 74 exact and 89 vague.
   - Three quarters come from one app (Days Since), whose reviewers choose it *because* it allows many counters. That pushes the tail up.
   - In mixed apps like ours the counts are lower: 1–4.
2. **Some counts are "quit so far" rather than "tracking right now"**, like "helped me quit two habits". The two usually match in these reviews, but not always.
3. **Anytime is modelled, not measured.** Treat the 50% column as the middle case and the 30% and 70% columns as the bounds.
4. **External per-user data doesn't exist publicly.** I Am Sober, Quitzilla and Nomo advertise multiple counters but publish no averages.
