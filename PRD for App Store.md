## 0. Rules for this document (read before adding anything)

These rules apply to every person and every AI model that edits this file.

1. **Plain English.** Write like you are explaining to a friend.
2. **One bullet per point, 2–3 lines max.** Say the point, the count, and link the source — nothing else.
3. **Every bullet has a count** (how many reviews, out of how many) and a **link to the report** it came from, inline in the same bullet. No separate sources section.
4. **Not a normal PRD.** Ignore the usual PRD definition. Only the sections below.
5. **Sections:** 1 Features — Free · 2 Features — Paid · 3 Features — Not decided yet · 4 Things we should do (marketing, screenshots, listing, launch, support…) · 5 Things we should NOT do.
6. **Bullet template:**
   `- **Short title.** Plain-English point. Count: N of M reviews (X%), <app/scope>. Source: [Report N → section](<relative link to report>).`
7. Add new bullets at the bottom of their section. Never delete one; strike it and say why.

## 5. Things we should NOT do

- **Never give free Premium for a review.** It can buy a #1 rank, but Apple treats it as a violation and the penalty is losing the whole developer account, not just the app — and the users it brings in don't pay anyway. Count: 1,340 of 40,991 China reviews (3.3%) openly say they reviewed only for the membership; just 0.86% of China reviews show any purchase. Source: [Report 1 → Part 0 and 6.4](App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md).
