# PRD for App Store

What we will build, what we will do around it, and what we will avoid — all taken from the store review reports in this repo.

---

## 0. Rules for this document (read before adding anything)

These rules apply to every person and every AI model that edits this file.

1. **Plain English.** Write like you are explaining to a friend.
2. **One bullet per point, 2–3 lines max.** Say the point, give a short plain reason, and list where it was seen — nothing else.
3. **No statistics in the bullet.** A point usually comes from many reports, so one report's numbers would be wrong for the rest. If we ever need numbers, we open the linked reports and read them there.
4. **Every bullet ends with "Repeated in:"** — the report numbers where the same point shows up, each number linking to that report. This is the counter. Only the owner says when to add a number — do not add one on your own. A research report can be linked the same way, by name.
5. **Not a normal PRD.** Ignore the usual PRD definition. Only the sections below.
6. **Sections:** 1 Product rules (never break these) · 2 Features — Free · 3 Features — Paid · 4 Features — Not decided yet (we want it; free or paid is open) · 5 Features to research (might want it; understand it first) · 6 Must-haves (not about free or paid) · 7 Must never break (test these to death) · 8 Things we should do (marketing, screenshots, listing, launch, support…) · 9 Things we should NOT do.
7. **Bullet template:**
   `- **Short title.** The point, then one or two lines of plain reasoning. Repeated in: [N](<link>), [M](<link>).`
8. Add new bullets at the bottom of their section. Never delete one; strike it and say why.

## 1. Product rules (never break these)

- **Free stays free, forever.** Think hard before putting anything in the free plan, because there is no way back. Moving a free feature behind the paywall later is the single worst thing we can do — it produces a flood of "it used to be free" 1★ reviews. Our app lives or dies by its store rating, so this is never allowed. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **One-time purchase, priced fairly.** The product is sold as a one-time purchase first; that is the main reason people buy this kind of app — they are tired of subscriptions. The price must also feel fair; if it looks like we are charging too much, people say so in reviews. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 2. Features — Free

- **Unlimited habits.** Never cap the number of habits a user can create. People rarely upgrade just to get more habits — instead the ones who hit the cap leave a 1★ or 2★ review, and that drags the whole app's rating down for years. Charge for extra capabilities, not for quantity. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>), [Feature Gating vs Quantity](<Research%20Reports/Feature%20Gating%20vs%20Quantity.md>).
- **One reminder per habit.** Every habit gets one basic reminder for free. Reminders are the core of a habit app; locking the first one would feel like a broken app. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Emoji per habit.** Users pick an emoji for each habit. We use emojis, not our own icons — there is already an emoji for almost everything, and we could never draw icons for every use case. Custom icons can come in a later version. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Colour per habit.** Users pick a colour for each habit. Small, cheap, and the kind of thing people love and mention in 5★ reviews. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Data export.** Users can export their own data for free. It is their data; charging for it feels like holding it hostage. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 3. Features — Paid

- **Multiple reminders per habit.** More than one reminder on the same habit is a paid feature. The first reminder is free; the extra ones are a convenience worth paying for. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Use on more devices.** Desktop and Apple Watch are paid. This is about running the app on more devices, not about cloud backup (that is a separate, undecided point). Tablet is undecided — see section 4. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 4. Features — Not decided yet (we want it; free or paid is open)

- **Home-screen widgets.** We will have them; the question is which ones are free. Report 1's app gives basic widgets away for free and users love them. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Cloud backup (iCloud / Google Drive).** Automatic, seamless backup of the user's data — not real-time sync. We want it; free or paid is open. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Tablet.** Running the app on an iPad / tablet. Desktop and Watch are already paid; tablet could go either way. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Skip / holiday mode.** Pause a habit without breaking the streak. We will have it; free or paid is open. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 5. Features to research (might want it; understand it first)

- **Weekly / monthly / yearly reports.** The #1 reason people pay in Report 1's app. Research first: why exactly do people pay for this, and how do we build a version worth paying for. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Shared / group habits.** Doing a habit together with friends or family. Sounds good and drives purchases elsewhere, but we need to understand what it really is and how to build it before we commit. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Passcode lock.** First decide whether we need it at all; if yes, then decide free or paid. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **App icon themes.** Alternate app icons. Decide whether it is worth having. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Quit-habit mode.** Tracking habits you want to stop (days since…). Include it only if it keeps showing up across reports. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Apple Health sync.** Same as quit-habit: decide whether to include it once we see how often it comes up. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Family plan.** Research whether we should offer one at all. If we do, it must not break, and it must be obvious how to use it — how to add members, what the limits are. A broken family plan is a top source of angry paying users. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 6. Must-haves (not about free or paid)

- **Interactive widgets.** Whatever widgets we ship, users must be able to act from the home screen itself — mark a habit done, and any other action the widget shows — without opening the app. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Account system, never forced.** Have accounts so purchases, entitlements and data follow the user to a new phone — most lost-purchase and lost-data complaints come from apps with no account at all. But never force sign-up; the app must work fully without one. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 7. Must never break (test these to death)

These are the bugs that turn paying users into 1★ reviews. They should not happen at all; if one ever slips through, it must be rare. Everything here gets the heaviest testing in the app.

- **Billing.** Never charge the wrong amount, charge twice, or renew someone by surprise. This is the #1 reason paying users turn angry. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Crashes.** The app must not crash randomly. Test it extensively before every release — the most important thing on this list. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Sync / backup.** If someone pays for it, it has to just work — between devices and when they move to a new phone. Test every edge case; a paid sync that fails is a guaranteed bad review. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Restore purchase / entitlements.** A paid user must always get what they paid for, on a reinstall or a new phone, with a restore that works first time. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Data loss.** Never, not even once. The whole point of the app is the user's data; lose it and the app is useless to them. Store it securely and reliably so it is safe no matter what. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 8. Things we should do

- **Run a scholarship program.** Let people who can't afford the app ask for it free or at a discount. In Report 1's app this was the cleanest 5★ review source in the whole dataset — people who got it wrote glowing reviews. We must do it; how to run it is still to be worked out. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Understand the payment-rails problem.** In Russia, Argentina, Turkey, Algeria and Pakistan, cards often fail at checkout, so people who want to pay can't. First understand what exactly breaks there, then solve it (the scholarship program was one fix). Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Localise the app.** The app must be in the user's own language, with a language option in the app — not English everywhere. Outside English-speaking countries, "not in my language" is the top reason a happy user won't pay. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
- **Have a support channel.** At least one clear, upfront, easy-to-find way to reach us. Paying users with no way to get help go straight to a 1★ review. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).

## 9. Things we should NOT do

- **Never give free Premium for a review.** It can buy a #1 rank, but Apple treats it as a violation and the penalty is losing the whole developer account, not just the app — and the users it brings in don't pay anyway. Repeated in: [1](<App%20Store%20Reports/1.%20Habit%20Tracker%20-%20Goal%20Tracker%20%26%20ADHD%20Planner%20%28REPORT%29.md>).
