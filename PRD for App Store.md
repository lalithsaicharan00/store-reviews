# PRD for App Store

What we will build, what we will do, and what we will avoid. Decisions only.

Every bullet is distilled from the review analysis of 70 apps. The evidence, the counts and the
conditions behind each one live in [Feature Ledger.md](<Research Reports/Feature Ledger.md>);
"Repeated in" lists the reports that point the same way.

> be written from the ledger.

## 1. Product rules

Rules that hold across everything else. Break one and the rest stops working.

- **We are building the quiet one, and we are staying with it.** This category splits into two products that both work: the gamified one that sells a pet, points and a world, and the quiet one that sells restraint. We are picking quiet — no pet, no avatar, no points, no leaderboard, no social feed, no nagging, no infantilising copy and no ads. Quiet does not mean thin: we keep the streak and the full capability of a serious tracker, and take away the performance rather than the features. The failure in the research is never the choice, it is reversing it or landing in the middle, so every feature request is measured against this line before it is measured against anything else.
