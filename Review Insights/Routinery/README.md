# Routinery — Review Insights

Written by Claude (Claude Code), 26 November 2026, at the user's request.

What Routinery's store reviews tell us about **who uses a routine app, why, and what they pay for**, as research for a
new product. Routinery Corp.'s routine timer app ("Routine Planner, Habit Tracker"; it began in Korea in 2019 as
굿모닝, "Good Morning"). App Store `1450486923`, Google Play `com.alt.goodmorning`, 9,644 reviews collected 8 September
2026. The data is the checked labels in [`Review Labels/Routinery/`](<../../Review Labels/Routinery/App.md>).

### The trend so far (share of the people who name a pain point)

| Group | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADHD, autism, OCD, depression, anxiety | 15% | 24% | **45%** |
| Can't start or keep going | 27% | 28% | 28% |
| Lose time | 24% | 21% | 20% |
| Mornings go badly | **31%** | 19% | 10% |
| Tried everything else | 16% | 28% | 10% |
| Forget steps | 8% | 6% | 6% |
| Parents and carers | 3% | 3% | 6% |
| Lost their structure (COVID and other) | 11% | 3% | 2% |
| Overwhelmed | — | 4% | 4% |
| Follow a method | 6% | 2% | 0.5% |

**What holds every year:** people use it because it **walks them through a routine one step at a time with a timer**,
so they don't have to decide, remember or find willpower. **Payers mostly pay for the whole app**, not a feature;
when they name one, it's **more routines** (the free plan allows two). Objections are **subscription instead of a
one-time price**, and price. **Korean reviewers pay at 3–4× the English rate.**
