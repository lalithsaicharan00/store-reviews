# MyRoutine — Review Insights

Written by Claude (Claude Code), 27 November 2026, at the user's request.

What MyRoutine's store reviews tell us about **who uses a routine app, why, and what they pay for**, as research for a
new product. Minding. co., Ltd.'s routine app from Korea ("MyRoutine: Organize your day" on the App Store, "MyRoutine:
Routine Habit Goal" on Google Play): a daily check-off list of routines with a social side (other people's routines,
copying them, following "routiners") and a reflection diary, on the web, PC and phone. App Store `1518956326`, Google
Play `com.minding.myroutine`, 5,818 reviews collected 24 November 2026. The data is the checked labels in
[`Review Labels/MyRoutine/`](<../../Review Labels/MyRoutine/App.md>).

The groups are MyRoutine's own, made from its 2020 labels; four were added from 2021's words.

**2020 in one line:** the phone apps' first half-year, all in Korean. The 18 people who name a pain know what to do
but don't do it, give up within days, or found their old way of keeping track didn't fit; **each group values what its
pain lacked** (a reminder; encouragement and other people; a record easier than paper), and 11 of the 18 name other
people's routines. 6 say they paid, all because they liked the whole app, and no review says what premium holds.
**41% of reviews report a bug**, with a crash wave after an update on 14 December, and two people were startled that
strangers could see their routines.

**2021 in one line:** three times the reviews, two thirds now from Google Play. Beside people who don't act and people
whose old tracking didn't fit, **new groups say time and the shape of their days are the problem** (pressed for time,
shift work), and they're the most critical. Each group still values what its pain lacked (a forgiving, encouraging
tracker; a gentler bar; time saved), while **other people's routines fall from 16 likes to 8 and simplicity leads
(57)**. **A paywall appears:** Premium monthly or yearly, a three-week trial, and a free plan of 10, then 15, routines,
the commonest reason to refuse. 20 say they paid, for the whole app, and half of them report a bug. **38% report a
bug, and the widget is the year's most-asked-for thing (41)**: broken on Android, missing on the iPhone until 26
December.
