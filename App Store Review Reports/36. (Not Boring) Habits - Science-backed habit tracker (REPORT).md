# (Not Boring) Habits — App Store ID 1593891243 — Full Review Analysis

**App:** (Not Boring) Habits — *Science-backed habit tracker*
**Developer in the corpus metadata:** Not Boring Software LLC · **Bundle:** `com.andyworks.streaks`
**What reviewers call the maker:** "Andy" / "Andy Works" / "the Not Boring team" (27 reviews praise the developer, e.g. `8202026507`, `9715331511` "Well done Andy!", `12800428077` "Love Andy's toys", `11734980268` "Thanks Not Boring Team"). The bundle ID `…streaks` suggests the project began as a streak app; no reviewer mentions a rename.
**Corpus:** 540 written reviews · 60 storefronts · 15 December 2021 → 4 September 2026
**Corpus mean rating:** 4.0389 · **Extracted:** 8 September 2026 (`itunes MZStore userReviewsRow`)
**Analysis date:** 11 September 2026
**Country scope:** global analysis of all 540 reviews plus one country section, **United States only** (252 reviews). No other storefront reaches the 50-review threshold (next largest: gb 42, cn 38, in 32), so the prompt's eligibility rule yields the US only. gb / cn / in / ca / de / fr are reported as clearly-labelled limited-evidence snapshots.
**Business model as reviewers experience it:** free download. A **"Not Boring" membership subscription** — reviewers name tiers as *Believer* (`信赖者`, `8969889060`, `9076528710`), *Plus* (`13572606322`), *Super* (`12339917492`), *S2* (`11980712489`) — at roughly **$15/£15/€18 per year for Habits alone** and **~$30/year for the 5-app suite** (`10713355484`), rising to **$59–$70** by 2026 (`14174554457`, `14465422602`) and **¥398 as a one-time unlock in China** (`14450598724`). Gated: skins/themes, widgets (from October 2023), and — **from around December 2025 — habits beyond the free limit**, which reviewers report falling from unlimited → 2 → 1. No ads. Data is local to the device; there is no account and no iCloud sync.

## How to read this

- Every quantified finding gives **count, percentage, denominator, scope, period, signal label and review IDs**. Every number traces back to the exact record via `review_id` in `reviews.jsonl`. **Part 11.6 lists every review ID behind every theme; Part 11.7 lists all 540 reviews with the codes each one received.**
- **Every review was read in full and hand-coded**, in every language (English, Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Vietnamese, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian). No theme count comes from keyword matching alone; a 33-pattern multilingual keyword sweep was run **afterwards**, to find misses (Part 11.3).
- Theme counts are **non-exclusive**: one review can carry several themes. Every review carries at least one code.
- Signal thresholds: `<0.1%` ignore · `0.1–0.5%` weak · `0.5–1%` emerging · `1–3%` meaningful · `3–5%` very strong · `>5%` high-priority.
- **Facts** are what reviewers wrote. **Interpretation** is labelled as such.
- The global denominator is **540** unless stated. Era denominators: E1 114 · E2 68 · E3 167 · E4 83 · E5 108. US denominator: 252.

### ⚠️ Seven warnings before any number here is used

1. **This corpus is unusually negative for a habit tracker, and that is the point.** 60.6% is 5★, but **12.2% is 1★ and 17.6% (95 reviews) is 1–2★**. Compared with the other apps analysed in this project, negative themes here rest on real counts, not on two or three records. The corpus mean is **4.039** against a 4.5–4.6 norm elsewhere in the set.
2. **The corpus is small enough that thresholds are easy to clear.** One review is 0.19% of 540 (already "weak"); three are 0.56% ("emerging"); six are 1.11% ("meaningful"). **Treat any theme with n < 5 as anecdotal**, whatever label the table prints.
3. **There is no `version` field.** Every release event below is inferred from what reviewers wrote and is bracketed by the first and last review that mentions it. The October 2023 widget gate and the December 2025 habit cap are each attested by multiple independent storefronts on near-identical dates, which is why they are stated as events; nothing else is.
4. **The free tier changed twice during the window, so "the free tier" is not one thing.** A 2024 reviewer praising "unlimited habits" (`11499453960`) and a 2026 reviewer raging about "ONE habit" (`14183623397`) are both accurate about their own moment. Any cross-period comparison of free-tier sentiment must respect this.
5. **"Payer" evidence is self-selected and small.** 34 reviewers (6.30%) state in the first person that they paid or subscribed. They are not a sample of customers, and **no conversion, renewal or refund rate can be derived from review text.**
6. **Votes are sparse and cannot be used as weights.** `vote_count` is non-zero on 143 of 540 records; the top record has 45 votes (`9750074711`, a 5★ feature request). Helpful-vote counts are reported as context only.
7. **Single-rater coding.** One analyst read and coded all 540 reviews against a 102-code codebook (Part 11.2) plus 10 derived unions. The recall sweep produced 11 corrections (Part 11.3). Borderline calls are disclosed in Part 11.4; no second rater validated them.

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**1. The product wins on craft, and people say so in unusually specific terms.**
**Core praise: 331 reviews (61.30%, high-priority), mean 4.59.** The components are not interchangeable compliments:
- **Design / visuals: 151 (27.96%, high-priority), mean 4.54** — "the best designed app i've come across in 18 years" (`13678372106`), "Monument Valley meets habit tracking" (`8905583877`), "有一种纪念碑谷的感觉" *"it feels like Monument Valley"* (`13191623782`).
- **Haptics, sound and music: 57 (10.56%), mean 4.61** — a differentiator almost no competitor is credited with: "very satisfying to hold down a button … and get actual physical feedback from my phone … Much nicer than just tapping a 'complete' button" (`8139278712`); "the music is absolutely gorgeous, please upload it to Spotify" (`12323195954`).
- **The 60-day monument build: 74 (13.70%), mean 4.69** — "I can't stop because I'm curious about what the next reward is the next day" (`10492156673`); "I needed to finish the campsite or the summit and see today's design" (`13127746442`, quitting alcohol).
- **Concrete outcomes: 58 (10.74%), mean 4.95 — zero reviews below 4★.** Flossing 60 days (`8974979165`), quit smoking (`11412952836`), quit alcohol (`13127746442`), reading 30 min/day (`9519619698`), cold showers (`12072516110`), 62-day morning routine (`13462331422`).
- **Not punishing you: 11 (2.04%) explicitly praise the absence of streak shame** — "As an easily discouraged perfectionist, I LOVE that if you miss a day (or a week) that it doesn't become the app of shame" (`9122428340`, 20 helpful votes). With **notification restraint: 12 (2.22%)** — "the #1 reason I've deleted other habit apps was that they spammed me" (`13967987039`).

**2. The single most consequential event in the corpus is the December 2025 free-tier habit cap. It broke the ratings.**
**`CAP` (free habit-count limit as friction): 30 reviews (5.56%, high-priority), mean 2.30 — and 29 of the 30 fall in E5 (Dec 2025 → Sep 2026), where it is 26.9% of all reviews.** Before December 2025 it is essentially absent (0 of 114 in E1, 0 of 68 in E2, 0 of 167 in E3, 1 ambiguous record in E4).
- **2026 rating collapse:** 2026 mean **3.636** with **27.3% 1–2★**, against 2024's **4.312 / 9.9%**. E5 mean 3.722, 25.0% 1–2★ — the worst era in the corpus, worse even than the rough launch period.
- **The cap also tightened:** reviewers report **2 habits** in Dec 2025 – Feb 2026 (`13577681414`, `13594173870`, `13604432669`, `13710833763`) and **1 habit** from March 2026 onward (`13871290077`, `13971171093`, `14183623397`, `14302616144`, `14433892949`). One reviewer remembers three (`13625458345`), another five (`13871290077`).
- **It reads as a withdrawal, not a price.** `REGRESS` (a capability taken away from the free tier): **19 reviews (3.52%), mean 1.95 — zero 5★, nine 1★**. "Never expect rewards for loyalty … I've been using the app for 3 years and all my history is on there" (`13780647190`); "Bait and switch" (`13770142251`); "Redução das features do plano free" (`13777493217`).

**3. The same mechanism had already been run once, in October 2023, with the same result.** Three 1★ reviews from three storefronts inside 36 hours: `10436306704` (US, "the widget … now shows 'Tap to Unlock' … I am deleting the widget and the app"), `10438409618` (EG, "After making the Home Screen widgets paid (they were on the free version) you deserve an uninstall"), `10439795484` (IN, "Widget Removed. Pretty Useless Now"), followed by `10672228882` (CN, "原来小组件免费现在收费" *"widgets used to be free, now they cost"*). **`WIDGETGATE`: 14 reviews (2.59%), mean 2.64, 10 of them in E3.** **Interpretation:** this is a repeated pattern — ship free, build the habit, then gate what people already use — and it produces the corpus's worst-rated reviews both times.

**4. Monetisation friction is the largest negative cluster in the corpus and it is concentrated in the low stars.**
**`U_MON_FRICTION`: 121 reviews (22.41%, high-priority), mean 2.53. It appears in 41 of 66 1★ reviews (62.1%) and 20 of 29 2★ (69.0%), but only 10 of 327 5★ (3.1%).** Inside it:
- **Subscription-as-a-model rejection: 35 (6.48%), mean 2.60** — separate from price level. "Nothing about this app that should require a subscription. I would be more than happy to pay for it outright, but that is not an option. So, I'm not gonna be paying anything at all" (`11598093250`).
- **Asks for a one-time / lifetime option: 24 (4.44%), mean 3.42 — and only 1 of the 24 is 1★.** This is the *constructive* half of the objection: "I'd love to pay the creator £5 for it" (`9800669659`); "I'd absolutely pay a flat price to own the app, even $20 or 30 bucks" (`9647917670`); "flat fee + micro transactions = hell yeah" (`13384969940`); "ich würde 5€ oder auch 7,99€ einmalig zahlen" (`14249690007`).
- **Price level: 36 (6.67%), mean 2.31.** The objection is mostly about *ratio to perceived substance*, not absolute money: "$15 for permission to buy a $10 skin. Scam!" (`9271651117`); "就这点功能还要订阅？" *"this little functionality needs a subscription?"* (`9373070993`).
- **Launch upsell screen: 24 (4.44%), mean 2.67** — a steady drumbeat across every era. "everytime i open the app its asking me to subscribe. i said no already, please respect that" (`12200644346`); "Can't use the app on my iPhone 13 mini because of a freaking pop up" (`11870684099`, rated 5★).

**5. Usability — not price — was the original problem, and most of it was fixed.**
**`U_USABILITY`: 68 reviews (12.59%, high-priority), mean 2.96.** Its share of each era falls sharply: **21.9% of E1 → 8.8% E2 → 12.0% E3 → 12.0% E4 → 6.5% E5**. `UX-` alone runs 14.9% of E1 down to 2.8% of E5; `ONBOARD-` 7.9% → 1.9%. The 2021–22 reviews are brutal and precise: "Ambiguous icons without labels, unintuitive swipes … no consolidated list / calendar view of all of your habits" (`8182905997`, 8 votes); "Is there no manual? Or user guide? I see no way to add new items like a + sign" (`8777347712`).
- **The one unfixed piece is navigating between habits.** `SWITCH-` 20 (3.70%) + `NOLIST` 12 (2.22%) + `REACH-` 6 (1.11%). "I have to swipe through 16 habits … then every time you swipe to a different habit, it brings you back to the checkmark screen" (`13670415847`). A December 2025 update appears to have improved it — `13572606322` (5★, payer): "there were a few improvements that I thought could be made (like easier habit switching …) and literally that same week they rolled out an update with those exact features."
- **Interpretation:** the cap arrived in the same window as the navigation fix, so the ratings drop is not explained by usability. `U_USABILITY` is at its *lowest ever* in the era with the *worst* ratings.

**6. iCloud sync is the #1 feature request and the #1 complaint of paying customers.**
**`R_SYNC`: 34 reviews (6.30%, high-priority), mean 3.59 — present in every era (7.0% / 8.8% / 7.2% / 6.0% / 2.8%).** It is the only request that has been asked continuously for 4.7 years: `8210628761` (Jan 2022) → `14450598724` (Aug 2026). **Within the 34 explicit payers, 9 raise it — a 26.5% segment rate, the highest of any issue in that group.** "I did end up buying the 1 year subscription, but the app was largely unused … it doesn't sync across devices in iCloud … I do not plan to [renew]" (`10771936163`); "Comprei no IPad … não sincroniza com o iPhone" (`13681998290`); "单个软件买断价格都已经398块了，为什么连多设备间数据同步都没有" *"the one-time price for a single app is already ¥398 — why is there no multi-device sync?"* (`14450598724`).

**7. Paying customers are the *least* satisfied identifiable group in the corpus.**
**34 explicit payers (6.30%), mean 3.794 — below the corpus mean of 4.039.** Only 17 of 34 are 5★; **8 of 34 rate 1–2★ (23.5%)**. Compare the 24 reviewers who praise the free tier (`FREE+`): mean **4.88**, zero below 4★. **Interpretation:** the money is not buying satisfaction — it is buying exposure to the app's real limits (no sync, entitlement not travelling across devices `9506691039` / `8757837312`, billing problems `9580841951` / `12524757331` / `10658186053`, bugs `8969889060` / `13631929530`). Paid status is also where support failures land: both `SUPPORT-` records are payers.

**8. The suite is a halo and a liability at the same time.**
`SUITE+` 34 (6.30%), mean 4.41 — the cross-sell works: "I purchased the S2 for the weather app, but Habits is really the star for me" (`11980712489`). But `SUITE-` 9 (1.67%), mean 3.22, says the bundle is why the price feels wrong: "Theoretically it includes 3 other apps, but this is the only good one … the calculator app is great until it lags while you type a 3 digit number" (`9504356434`); "我只想买这一个" / "Me gustaría que vendieran el premium de solo esta app" (`11084742467`). One Chinese reviewer reports being charged **five** annual fees after buying the bundle membership and recovering only two refunds (`12524757331`, 1★).

**9. Localisation demand was an early, sharp, and then-answered signal.**
`R_LANG` 9 (1.67%) — **8 of the 9 are in E1 alone (7.0% of that era)**, six of them Chinese-market (`8660174556`, `8772660358`, `8787860020`, `8804471876`, `8815511952`) plus `8744602982`, `8778158271`, `8771578171` (MX, 1★ "solo esté en inglés me hace perder el interés"). After September 2022 it almost stops (`10911777454`, Feb 2024, pt-BR). **Interpretation:** localisation probably shipped; the corpus cannot confirm it, only that the complaint disappears. China remains the second-worst-rated significant storefront (cn n=38, mean 3.605) on price and sync, not language.

**10. Cheapest wins, in evidence order:**
1. **Restore a usable free tier (3+ habits).** 30 `CAP` reviews at mean 2.30, all but one since December 2025; multiple reviewers volunteer "three would be reasonable" (`14109551956`, `14236390341`, `12556296093`).
2. **Never retract what is already in use.** Both retractions (widgets 2023, habits 2025) produced the lowest-rated clusters in the corpus. Grandfather existing users.
3. **Ship iCloud sync.** 34 requests over 4.7 years, 26.5% of payers, and the stated reason for at least two non-renewals (`10771936163`, `9745264915`).
4. **Sell a one-time/lifetime unlock alongside the subscription.** 24 reviews ask for it, mean 3.42, only one 1★ — these are willing buyers currently converting to nothing.
5. **Disclose the limit on the store page and cap the upsell to once.** `DISCLOSE` 6 (mean 1.67) and `POPUP` 24 (mean 2.67).
6. **Finish habit navigation:** an all-habits overview and reliable swipe (`NOLIST` + `SWITCH-` + `REACH-` = 27 distinct reviews, mean 3.56).
7. **Then the roadmap:** Apple Watch (9), interactive widget (4), multiple completions per day (5), progression beyond 60 days (6), statistics (10).

## 1.2 Schema

Every record carries: `review_id`, `app_id`, `app_name`, `country`, `country_name`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `author`, `date` (ISO 8601 UTC), `vote_count`, `vote_sum`, `is_edited`. **There are no nulls and no empty strings in any field.**

- **Fields used:** `review_id`, `country`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `date`, `is_edited`, `vote_count`.
- **Fields ignored:** `author` (personal data, no analytical value); `app_id` and `app_name` (constant across all 540 records).
- **No `version` field exists.** Release events are inferred only from review text (Part 8).
- **Votes:** `vote_count` is non-zero on 143 records. Top: `9750074711` (45 votes, 5★, US, Mar 2023, a feature request), `9122428340` (20), `8664069478` (17, CN, "希望可以登陆" — a login request), `8148224036` (16), `8757837312` (14), `8828400666` (12, "I will not subscribe…"). Votes are reported as context, never as a weight.
- **`is_edited` = true on 5 records only:** `8632947214`, `8852296975` ("Sorry for misunderstanding, impressive design and work!" — a rating revised upward), `9849611173`, `12417677947`, `13577681414` (the 4★ habit-cap complaint). All are treated as normal records at their stated dates.

## 1.3 Coverage and reconciliation

| Check | Result |
|---|---|

| Date range | 2021-12-15 → 2026-09-04 (4.72 years) |
| Storefronts | 60 |

## 1.4 Processing method

1. **`prep.py`** — parse `reviews.jsonl`; run every reconciliation check in 1.3; double-HTML-unescape and whitespace-collapse `title` and `body` **without truncating**; emit 6 readable batches of 90 records each in date order.
2. **Read every batch in full** and hand-code each review at the time of reading → `codes.txt`, one line per `review_id`.
3. **`load.py`** — verify every `review_id` is coded exactly once with valid codes; apply `patches.txt`.
4. **`recall.py`** — 33 multilingual keyword patterns across 9 scripts; list every untagged candidate; read all of them; patch real misses (Part 11.3).
5. **`stats.py`** — aggregate by theme, star band, year, half-year, era, US vs non-US, per-storefront and payer segment; emit `ids.json` and the Part 11.6 / 11.7 appendices.

## 1.5 Known limitations

- **Written reviews only.** The App Store's 540 written reviews are a small, self-selecting slice of all ratings. People write after a delight moment or a billing shock; routine users are under-represented.
- **No version, no cohort, no revenue data.** Release dates, prices, tier contents and any conversion or churn rate are outside this corpus. Where reviewers disagree about a price or a limit, the disagreement is reported, not resolved.
- **The free tier changed twice.** Comparing free-tier sentiment across 2022 and 2026 compares two different products (warning 4).
- **Small per-storefront samples.** Only the US clears 50. Every other storefront claim is labelled limited evidence.
- **Single-rater coding**, no second-rater validation (Part 11.4).
- **No external source was consulted.** No product website, App Store listing, press material or third-party spend data. High-spend market membership is therefore **not** used (Part 7.1).

## 3.4 What the product genuinely does well

Ranked by strength of evidence, with the reason the praise is credible.

1. **Sensory feedback as the reward.** `HAPTIC+` 57 (10.56%), mean 4.61. This is the most *specific* praise in the corpus and the hardest for a competitor to copy. Reviewers describe the physical act, not the feature: "It is very satisfying to hold down a button … Much nicer than just tapping a 'complete' button on a reminder list" (`8139278712`); "a small dose of dopamine every time you check something off" (`13670415847`, a 3★ critic); "手机也会给你不错的震动回饋" (`14045271461`). A 2★ reviewer still says "I love the sounds" (`14334631638`).
2. **Curiosity as the retention loop.** `GAME+` 74 (13.70%), mean 4.69. The monument is a *reason to return tomorrow*: "I can't stop because I'm curious about what the next reward is the next day" (`10492156673`); "I almost want to keep my habits just for the app" (`12219094304`); "I needed to finish the campsite or the summit and see today's design" (`13127746442`).
3. **It converts into real behaviour change.** `OUTCOME` 58 (10.74%), mean 4.95, **zero reviews below 4★** — no other theme in the corpus is that clean. Flossing to 60 days (`8974979165`), quit smoking (`11412952836`), quit alcohol (`13127746442`), reading (`9519619698`), 5am wake-up (`12112297898`), exercise (`11870489422`), cold showers (`12072516110`), litter box (`12043647047`).
4. **Kindness after a missed day.** `NOSHAME` 11 (2.04%) + `FLEX+` 2. Small counts, disproportionate weight — `9122428340` (20 helpful votes) is the corpus's clearest articulation of a competitive wedge: "As an easily discouraged perfectionist, I LOVE that if you miss a day (or a week) that it doesn't become the app of shame. I never know if I should start over or quit. So I put my tail between my legs and delete it." Also `9261590460`, `11943524242`, `12937403561`, `11085313395`.
5. **Notification restraint.** `NOTIF+` 12 (2.22%), mean 4.67. Explicitly framed as *why they left other apps*: "One underrated excellent feature is that they give you fine-tuned control over the notifications you receive — probably the #1 reason I've deleted other habit apps was that they spammed me with a billion distracting notifications" (`13967987039`).
6. **Writing quality.** `QUOTES+` 13 (2.41%), **mean 5.00 — every single one is 5★**. "It's easy to overlook how well written the text is in this app. The motivational phrases are encouraging but never guilt inducing" (`9464371564`). Note the counter-signal in 3.5 (`MSG-`).

## 3.6 The one structural criticism of the core concept

`BORING-` 8 (1.48%), mean 2.50, plus `R_BEYOND60` 6 (1.11%) and `GAME-` 4 (0.74%) describe the same ceiling: **the monument is the same journey for every habit, and it ends at 60 days.**

- "the story is repeated for all habits … if you have one habit that's 'ahead', you know the story and you gain nothing by going through it as you complete other habits, which I found to be pretty boring" (`9098509320`, 3★, 8 votes — the most analytically precise negative review in the corpus).
- "the haptics is quite fun at first but it's the same for every habit you set up so the novelty decreases over time" (`13594356619`).
- "希望可以增加不同的旅途，因为好像每一个习惯都是一样的旅途和过程" *"please add different journeys — every habit seems to be the same journey and process"* (`14471085224`, 5★).
- "it's a shame that it lasts only 60 days … I could have built something infinitely" (`13572306369`).
- "the art only goes up to 60 … I feel like it wouldn't hurt to add more especially when you're paying money" (`10043311417`).
- Questioning the mechanic outright: "focusing the entire app experience on building a 3D model (how does this help?)" (`8182905997`); "The UI is more about playing with random shapes then actually figuring out your habits!!" (`9128391997`).

**Interpretation:** the thing that makes people return on day 3 is also what makes them leave around day 60–120. This is the corpus's clearest *retention* finding, and it is independent of price.

## 4.4 Cluster 4 — Habit navigation: the one usability problem that was never fully fixed `[very strong]`

**`SWITCH-` 20 + `NOLIST` 12 + `REACH-` 6 = 27 distinct reviews (5.00%), mean 3.56.** Note the mean: these are mostly 4★ and 5★ reviewers asking for one thing.

- **The mechanism:** one habit fills the screen; habit names sit in small text at the very top; you switch by tapping or swiping that strip.
- **Asked for continuously from week two to the last month of the corpus:** `8185571470` (Dec 2021) → `8253563602` → `8258517590` → `8562867284` → `9189055798` → `9750074711` (45 votes, Mar 2023) → `10016530116` → `10622250811` → `10662024022` → `10713355484` → `10738443241` → `10968040090` ("Please allow to switch between habits by swiping" — entire body: ".") → `11276612751` → `12079206553` → `12102232868` → `12153499058` → `12294811087` → `13572606322` → `13670415847` → `13948551648` (Apr 2026).
- **The specific failure:** the centre check button swallows horizontal swipes. `9750074711` "Right now most anywhere on the screen that we touch 'presses' the center checkmark button"; `13948551648` "Sometimes the swipes don't respond as it's quite a reach to get to the top of the phone and swipe in a small area".
- **It gets worse with more habits** — i.e. precisely for the paying user: `13670415847` "At the end of every day, when I want to restart back at habit one, I have to swipe through 16 habits. That alone is enough for me to not want to use this app anymore"; `10622250811` "switching between habits and having a lot of them is painful".
- **The missing view is an all-habits overview** (`NOLIST` 12): "no consolidated list / calendar view of all of your habits" (`8182905997`); "a way to see your habits on a list that you can check off one by one" (`11659987897`); "A multi-habit view mode would go a long way" (`12153499058`); "an aggregate view with all my progressions across all habits" (`13948551648`); "view all the habits in a single page to check or uncheck" (`14510762767`).
- **Partially shipped, December 2025:** `13572606322` (5★, payer) — "there were a few improvements that I thought could be made (like easier habit switching and more in depth tracking) … and literally that same week they rolled out an update with those exact features." `13681211961` confirms "The recent upgrades only made it even more useful!". But `13670415847` (Jan 2026) and `13948551648` (Apr 2026) still complain, so the fix is incomplete.
- **Adjacent, smaller, unresolved:** cannot delete/rename a habit (`DELETE-` 10, mean 3.00: `8757312048`, `8964135829`, `9021270886`, `9098509320`, `10738443241`, `10945684076`, `11224445709`, `11245244275`, `11280034510`, `12339917492`); habit-name character limit (`NAMELEN` 3: `8774281298`, `13010314496`, `13670415847`); habits shown on days they are not scheduled (`SCHEDDAY-` 3, all 4–5★: `10945684076`, `11815224356`, `12152046240`).

## 4.5 Cluster 5 — The 60-day ceiling `[meaningful, and the main retention risk]`

Covered in 3.6. Summary: `BORING-` 8 + `R_BEYOND60` 6 + `GAME-` 4 = 17 distinct reviews, mean 2.76. Do not treat these as positive or negative:

- **`R_SYNC`** — 10 at 5★, 11 at 4★, 7 at 3★, 1 at 2★, 5 at 1★. Mostly asked by fans; occasionally a reason to quit.
- **`PAYER`** — 17 at 5★ but also 5 at 1★ and 3 at 2★. Paying does not predict satisfaction (Part 6).
- **`POPUP`** — 4 at 5★, 8 at 1★. The same behaviour that irritates a fan drives a detractor's rating.
- **`HAPTIC+`** — 3 of the 57 are at 2★ (`14334631638`, `9098509320`, `8182905997`): people who like the feel and still rate low.
- **`COMP`** — 27 at 5★ ("this is the one I kept") and 9 at 1–2★ ("Dayrise is better at this price range", `13902720788`; "Ich bin jetzt zu Onrise gewechselt", `13689787553`). Comparison shopping cuts both ways.
- **`MISRATE` 9 reviews** where the star rating contradicts the text, in both directions: `11870684099` (5★ titled "Unusable"), `11280034510` (5★ titled "UX is on the low level"), `13652246359` (5★ reporting a UI bug), `10498701164` (5★ complaining sync is missing), `14334631638` (2★ saying "I love the app too"), `11125368439` (3★ titled "This is fabulous!"). **Star ratings in this corpus are noisy at the individual level; the theme counts are the reliable signal.**

# PART 6 — PAID-USER AND UPGRADE ANALYSIS

## 6.1 Framing this correctly

**34 reviewers (6.30% of 540) state in the first person that they paid, subscribed, or hold a membership.** This is the narrowest reliable denominator in the report and every percentage in this Part is labelled as a **segment rate**.

What this group is **not**: a sample of customers. People write reviews after a delight moment or a billing shock, so payers who are quietly content are systematically missing. **No conversion rate, renewal rate, refund rate or ARPU can be derived from this corpus, and none is stated below.**

Inclusion rule: an explicit first-person statement of payment or membership in any language — "I bought a paid subscription" (`8742019793`), "I'm on the yearly subscription" (`9091830635`), "开通了信赖者" *"activated Believer [tier]"* (`8969889060`), "I bough the Super membership" (`12339917492`), "Bought the 5 app package" (`11938623754`), "subscribed to 'plus'" (`13572606322`), "Comprei no IPad" (`13681998290`). Conditional statements ("I'd buy if…") are coded `BUYIF` (10 reviews) and excluded. Borderline calls are listed in 11.4.

**Interpretation (labelled as such):** paying moves you from the app's best surface (a beautiful free tool that asks nothing) onto its worst (no sync, entitlements that don't travel, billing edge cases, and the expectation that £15/yr buys more than skins). The free tier is the product's marketing; the paid tier is where the disappointments live. This is the opposite of the usual pattern in this project's corpora, where payers rate *above* average.

## 6.7 The most dangerous customer in this corpus

**The multi-year free user who upgraded their device or ran out of habits in 2026.** They carry three codes at once — `CAP` + `REGRESS` + long tenure — and they write the lowest-rated, most specific, most quotable reviews: `13780647190` (3 years of history, "stuck"), `13871290077` (5 habits on the old phone, 1 on the new), `13625458345` ("I've used this app for years"), `13594356619` ("I preferred the old version"), `13624027434` ("I loved this app but no more"), `13777493217` (pt-BR, "péssima mudança"). **`LOCKIN` 1 review (`13780647190`) is the canary:** their data is in the app, there is no export and no sync, so they cannot leave cleanly and cannot continue without paying. # PART 9 — WHAT A COMPETITOR LEARNS FROM THIS CORPUS FOR FREE

1. **Feel is a moat and almost nobody builds it.** 57 reviews praise haptics, sound and music specifically, and several say it is the reason they kept *this* app after abandoning others (`8139278712`, `13678372106`, `12957083253`). Competitors are described as "gunk", "too busy", "overwhelming", "spam" (`8868694307`, `12189025964`, `12948428968`, `13967987039`).
2. **"No streak shame" is an unclaimed positioning.** 11 reviews praise it explicitly and one (`9122428340`, 20 votes) explains exactly why streak-reset mechanics make people delete habit apps. Separately, 7 reviews *ask for* streaks — so the right answer is optional, not absent.
3. **Notification restraint wins switchers.** `13967987039` names notification spam as the #1 reason they deleted other habit apps.
4. **Cosmetic gating is tolerated; functional gating is not.** 3 reviews say so in one breath (`11016580901`, `11486217313`, `14174554457`). A competitor can charge for skins and give away widgets and habit counts, and this corpus predicts the reaction.
5. **There is a live market of one-time buyers with money in hand.** 24 reviews (mean 3.42, only one 1★) state they would pay a flat fee and will not pay a subscription — several naming their price (£5, $20–30, €5–7.99, ¥50, ~$100).
6. **Local-only storage is simultaneously an asset and the top churn cause.** 2 reviews praise privacy; 34 want sync; 3 lost their data. Private end-to-end sync captures both.
7. **Gamification has a 60-day half-life.** 17 reviews describe the identical-journey / hard-stop problem. Any competitor shipping varied or endless progression addresses a documented complaint.
8. **Habit-count caps below 3 are a rating liability.** Three separate reviewers volunteer three as the fair number (`14109551956`, `14236390341`, `12556296093`).

# PART 11 — EVIDENCE APPENDIX

## 11.1 Counting rules

- **Denominators.**
  - Every global percentage uses **540** (all records, no removals). A secondary "substantive" series uses **376** (body > 60 characters) and is always labelled.
  - Era denominators: **E1 114 · E2 68 · E3 167 · E4 83 · E5 108**. Half-year denominators are in the 1.6 table.
  - Year denominators: 2021 **8** · 2022 **122** · 2023 **78** · 2024 **141** · 2025 **92** · 2026 **99**.
  - Country: **us 252**, non-US **288**. All other storefront figures use that storefront's own count, stated inline.
  - Segment denominators are always labelled as segment rates: explicit payers **34**; 5★ **327**; 4★ **68**; 3★ **50**; 2★ **29**; 1★ **66**.
- **Themes are non-exclusive.** Every review received at least one code; one review can carry many (maximum 12, `13670415847`).
- **Unions** are computed on distinct review IDs from their components:
  - `U_CORE_PRAISE` = DESIGN HAPTIC+ SIMPLE GAME+ MOTIV OUTCOME BEST USEFUL NOSHAME QUOTES+ VIZ+ DEV+ SUITE+ NOTIF+
  - `U_MON_FRICTION` = SUB- PRICE- CAP PAYWALL WIDGETGATE POPUP ONETIME DISCLOSE TRIAL REGRESS REGIONAL BILLING AFFORD VALUE- LOCKIN ENT
  - `U_MON_POSITIVE` = PRICE+ FREE+ NOADS
  - `U_USABILITY` = UX- ONBOARD- SWITCH- NOLIST DELETE- NAMELEN SCHEDDAY- REACH- CONTRAST- ACCESS- SOUND-
  - `U_RELIABILITY` = DATALOSS WIDGETBUG NOTIF- UIBUG CRASH BUG
  - `U_UNMET` = every `R_*` code
  - `U_SYNC_DEMAND` = R_SYNC R_ACCOUNT ENT
  - `U_PLATFORM_DEMAND` = R_WATCH R_IPAD R_LANDSCAPE
  - `U_WIDGET_DEMAND` = R_IWIDGET R_WIDGET
  - `U_GENERIC_ONLY` = reviews whose *only* codes are GEN+ / GEN- / JUNK / MISRATE / MISPLACED
- **"Payer"** requires a first-person statement of payment, subscription or membership in any language. Conditional statements are `BUYIF` and are excluded from `PAYER`.
- **`CAP` vs `PAYWALL`.** `CAP` is specifically the habit-count limit. `PAYWALL` is any other gated capability or a general "have to pay". `WIDGETGATE` is a labelled subset of the widget case and is counted in both `WIDGETGATE` and `PAYWALL` only where the reviewer also makes the general complaint — the two codes are applied independently, so `PAYWALL` is **not** the sum of its parts.
- **`SUB-` vs `PRICE-` vs `VALUE-`.** `SUB-` objects to recurring billing as a model; `PRICE-` objects to the amount; `VALUE-` objects to the ratio of capability to price. A review can hold all three (`9895154481` does).
- **`REGRESS`** requires the reviewer to state that something they previously had was taken away. Mere dissatisfaction with the current tier is not `REGRESS`.
- **Rounding.** Percentages to 2 dp globally and 1 dp in era, half-year and band tables; means to 2–3 dp as printed.
- **Qualitative splits** (Part 3.3) are labelled as such and are **not** additional quantified themes.

## 11.4 Validation approach and known error risk

- **Control:** all 540 records were read in full, in date order, in six batches of 90, and coded at the time of reading. `codes_final.txt` is the audit trail; every review's codes are listed in 11.7.
- **Structural checks:** every ID coded exactly once; every code in the codebook; union and segment counts recomputed from the codes file, never entered by hand.
- **Recall check:** 33 multilingual patterns, 161 candidate rows read, 11 corrections (11.3).
- **Borderline calls, disclosed:**
  - **`PAYER`, included despite ambiguity:** `8882707067` ("decided to purchase for all the other fab bits and bobs" — purchase stated, object slightly unclear); `9503462497` ("Kann auf jeden Fall den Kauf des vollständigen Software-Bundles empfehlen" — recommends the purchase, implies having made it); `11344820966` (title "Waste of Money" + "before you blow $15" — reads as a buyer's regret); `13462331422` (uses widgets daily, which are paid in Dec 2025 — inferred, not stated); `10703725832` ("Absolutely worth the money"); `12660421508` ("I tried out the paid version").
  - **`PAYER`, excluded (coded `PRICE+` or `BUYIF` only):** `8210628761` (argues *for* paying 15€/yr without stating he did); `8736830661` (recommends buying a skin); `8344721589` ("Definitely worth the subscription"); `11848748540` ("Unbedingt kaufen!"); `13706176527` ("at a reasonable price"); `13967987039` ("Price is perfectly reasonable").
  - **`CAP`, borderline:** `12556296093` (Apr 2025, "I would like it if I could create more then one habit at a time … 3 is a good number") — eight months before the first unambiguous cap report. Could be a request to create habits in bulk. Coded `CAP` and flagged; it is the single E4 `CAP` record and removing it does not change any claim.
  - **`CAP` vs `BUG`:** `14235507906` ("App is extremely bugged. I cannot add a second habit", de, Jun 2026) carries both — the reviewer has hit the cap and interpreted it as a defect. Counted in both.
  - **`WIDGETGATE` on `11178506784`** is an inference from simultaneity (11.3), not a statement.
  - **`SCIENCE-` on `8192214490`** ("Vague science and no specifics") is a complaint about *explanation*, not about the research itself.
  - **`MISPLACED` on `12195758016`**: the body reviews the weather app but also praises Habits; kept in the corpus, coded `MISPLACED` + `SUITE+` + `GEN+`.

- **Direction of residual error:** under-coding is most likely for secondary themes in long multi-issue reviews; over-coding risk is highest for `SIMPLE` and `DESIGN` in short non-English praise, where "好看" / "bonito" may mean either.
