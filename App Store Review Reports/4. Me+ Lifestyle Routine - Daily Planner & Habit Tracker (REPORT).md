# PART 0 — READ THIS FIRST: A 4.80-STAR APP WHOSE WRITTEN REVIEWS AVERAGE 3.93

**Executive summary in one line:** Me+ is a genuinely well-liked routine planner — 60.1% of written reviews are 5★ and 17.8% describe a life change — but **16.8% are 1★, and 57.1% of those 1★ reviews are about money, not the product**. The single largest destroyer of value is a **"7-day free trial" that a large group of users say charged them immediately**, and a **paywall screen whose dismiss button users cannot find**.

### 1. The gap between the store rating and the written corpus is the story

| | Value |
|---|---|
| App Store rating (US, all ratings incl. tap-only) | **4.80** (247,686 ratings) |
| Mean of all 20,465 **written** reviews | **3.93** |
| 5★ share of written reviews | 60.14% (12,308) |
| 1★ share of written reviews | **16.80% (3,438)** |
| 2★ share | 4.94% (1,010) |

People who tap five stars say nothing. People who write do so because something happened. In this corpus, what happened was overwhelmingly a charge.

## 1.7 Monetization mistakes, in priority order

1. **The trial/plan-selection screen bills people who believed they had 7 days.** 789 reviews, mean 1.35. Nothing else is close.
2. **The paywall's dismiss affordance is invisible.** 1,903 "must pay" reviews vs 75 reviews explaining the X exists.
3. **The mid-2025 free task cap** turned tenured free users into detractors.
4. **Cancellation and support are dead ends**, converting recoverable disputes into permanent 1★ and legal threats.
5. **Price is inconsistent and undisclosed until after a 10–20 minute quiz.**
6. **No one-time / lifetime option** — only 20 reviews (0.10%) ask for one, but the ask is unanimous where it appears (`10930935136`, `12009622937`, `11483126752`).

- **Billing cluster: 13.0% (2023 Q2) → 3.8% (2026 Q3).** Real, sustained improvement — with the 2024 Q1 relapse.
- **Localisation:** ES/PT/FR/DE complaints effectively eliminated (Part 6.5).
- **"Can't edit future days": 1.35% of 2023 reviews → 0.22% of 2026.** Fixed.
- **Scheduling flexibility: 2.49% (2023) → 0.67% (2026).** Substantially improved.
- **Onboarding quiz complaints: 4.8% (2023 Q2) → 0.3% (2026 Q3).**

## 7.4 Themes that worsened
- **Free task cap:** 0.03% (2022–24) → **1.32–2.04%** (2025 Q3 onward).
- **"It used to be better / used to be free":** 0.6% of 5★ reviews but **3.2% of 3★ and 3.6% of 2★**, concentrated post-2025.

## 7.5 Themes that persisted, unchanged, for five years
- Notification/alarm reliability (0.9–1.3% every year since 2022).
- Backup / iCloud / account portability (0.28–1.12% every year).
- Upsell pop-ups (1.0–6.3% every quarter, never absent).

# PART 8 — PRODUCT IMPLICATIONS

## Immediate fixes — these determine whether the rating recovers
1. **Rebuild the trial/plan-selection screen so that "7-day free trial" and "charged now" cannot be confused.** Show the exact charge date and amount on the confirmation. → Parts 0.3, 1.1, 6.2, 7.2. Addresses 789 reviews (3.86%) directly and the 1,247-review (6.09%) billing cluster.
2. **Make the paywall's dismiss control unmissable** — a full-width "Continue with the free version" button, not a corner X. → Part 0.4. Up to a material share of the 1,903 paywall-block reviews (9.30%) are users who never found the free tier.
3. **Fix Brazilian (and LatAm/APAC) trial provisioning as a P0.** → Part 6.2. BR runs 7–11× the global billing-complaint rate.
4. **Put in-app cancellation and a working support path in the app.** → Part 1.5. 169 cancellation + 48 no-response reviews, mean ~1.2, several escalating to lawyers and denied Apple refunds.
5. **Fix notification and alarm delivery, including sound and app-closed behaviour.** → Part 4.1. 212 reviews, mean 2.82, and it breaks the core promise for paying and ADHD users alike.
6. **Ship iCloud backup / account restore on new device.** → Part 4.4. 154 + 115 + 55 reviews; disproportionately hits payers.

## Pricing and packaging
7. **Show the price before the quiz, not after it.** → Part 0.6. The quiz theme has a 1.98 mean and 71.9% 1–2★; every one of those reviews is a wasted acquisition.
8. **Reconsider the free daily-task cap, or raise it well above 6.** → Part 0.5. It reversed a two-year rating recovery and produced the "got greedy" vocabulary that now appears in 286 reviews (1.40%).
9. **Stabilise the price and retire the spin-wheel.** → Part 1.1. Users report $19.99–$59.99 for the same thing; that inconsistency is what "scam" language attaches to.
10. **Add a one-time / lifetime SKU.** → Part 1.7. Small explicit demand (20 reviews) but it is the standard objection format in this category and would convert the "I'd pay, just not monthly" segment.
11. **Introduce regional pricing for LatAm, India, Turkey, SE Asia.** → Part 6.2, 6.4. Chile's 6.2% price-complaint rate and India's 41.2% 1–2★ rate both point here.

## Product — converts 3–4★ into 5★
12. **Full task editing: rename, reorder, drag, duplicate.** → Part 4.3. 399 reviews (1.95%), and it is the top complaint among *paying* users.
13. **Finish flexible scheduling: weekly, monthly, every-N-days, X-times-per-week, time windows.** → Part 4.2. Improving but still 0.67% of 2026 reviews at mean 2.77.
14. **Ship the widget properly and let users check off from it.** → 151 reviews (0.74%), mean 3.79, 39.1% 5★ — currently a *positive* theme with a clear ask: `10220454829` (CA, 5★, 35 votes, *"make you cross off the tasks directly from the widget"*), `12010228356` (CA, 4★).
15. **Apple Watch.** → 34 reviews (0.17%, weak) but mean 3.68 and asked for by payers: `14294898131` (BR, 3★), `10778781877` (US, 4★, paying).

## Positioning
16. **Lead with ADHD / executive-function support.** → Part 5.1. 4.51% of the corpus, 62.4% 5★, and it is the segment that pays for outcomes (`10874402938`).
17. **Keep the content library** — workouts, meditation, soundscapes, sleep. → Part 3.6. It is what separates Me+ from "a fancy Reminders app", the comparison that shows up in 3★ and 1★ reviews alike.
18. **Review the 4+ age rating against the weight-loss and shame framing.** → Part 4 safety note. 455 kid/teen reviews and 89 shame/ED reviews, with a 46-vote 1★ leading on it.

## Research questions the corpus cannot answer
- Is the immediate charge a billing defect or the intended behaviour of a non-trial plan? (Reviews describe both; only instrumentation can settle it.)
- What is the actual free-tier cap today, and did it change again after mid-2025?
- Does the discount wheel increase net revenue, or does it mostly generate the price confusion that drives refunds?
- Why did review volume fall ~80% from its 2024 Q1 peak?

## 9.3 Method

1. Parsed `reviews.jsonl` line-by-line in Python; HTML-unescaped title and body; concatenated title + body into one text field; produced a lowercase copy and a Unicode-NFKD accent-folded copy so that `pagué`/`pague` and `türkçe`/`turkce` both match.
2. Built a **62-theme taxonomy** of regular expressions covering English plus Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Turkish, Russian, Polish, Dutch, Danish, Swedish, Norwegian, Finnish and Vietnamese vocabulary. Themes are **non-exclusive**; the mean is 1.44 themes per review, max 16.
3. Ran the taxonomy over all 20,465 records; wrote per-review theme assignments to a machine-readable file; aggregated globally, per star band, per storefront, per year/quarter/month, and per cohort.
4. Reconciled counts against `manifest.json` and `by_country/` (Part 9.2).
5. **Validation:** manually inspected ~260 matched reviews across the 18 highest-impact themes plus 12 stratified random samples, and separately inspected unclassified reviews by language group. Estimated precision is **≥90% for the monetization, billing, onboarding and localisation themes** (these use narrow, distinctive vocabulary) and **~85% for the broad topic themes** `praise_organized`, `req_missing_feature`, `feat_customization` and `feat_notifications`, which capture *topic mention* rather than sentiment — which is why every table reports the theme's mean rating and 1–2★ share rather than assuming direction.
6. External context (version, release date, language list, listed prices, store rating) came from Apple's public lookup API for storefront `us`, accessed **2026-09-09**. It is kept separate from all review-derived evidence.

## 9.4 Known limitations and biases

- **Written reviews are not users.** The store rating is 4.80 across 247,686 US ratings; this corpus averages 3.93. Written reviews over-represent people with a grievance — most visibly a billing grievance. Every conclusion here describes *what people write*, not *what people experience*.
- **Non-English recall is lower than English recall.** After the multilingual pass, unclassified share is 26.5% in Anglophone storefronts vs 34.4% (ES), 36.4% (PT), 33.9% (DE), 45.3% (Nordic/NL). Non-English theme rates in Part 6 are therefore **conservative floors**, not ceilings. The Brazilian and Chilean billing rates are, if anything, understated.
- **Non-EN/FR/DE/PT/ES markets are structurally under-sampled** — only 1.69% of reviews are in a non-Latin script, because people who cannot read the app rarely write about it.
- **The confirmed-payer cohort (n=319) is heavily selection-biased** toward angry payers; it is reported as a segment rate and must never be read as a subscriber satisfaction rate. No conversion rate is claimed anywhere in this report, and none can be derived from review text.
- **Rating is not a feature preference.** Where a theme's mean rating is quoted it describes the reviews containing that theme, not a causal effect of the feature.
- **Star ratings and dates are Apple's; text is the reviewer's.** No sentiment model was used; direction is inferred from vocabulary plus the rating distribution of the matched set, and is stated as mixed wherever the evidence is mixed.
- **The 2025 Q3 and 2024 Q1 events are inferred from review timing**, not from release notes. Apple's public release notes for v2.8.9 say only "bug fixes and performance improvements", so no version can be tied to either change.
- **Fake-review accusations exist but are below threshold** — 13 reviews (0.06%), mean 1.38 (`10996599524`, `10311098663`, `11581025275`, `10448050448`, `10586349632`, `10772526893`, `11307389569`, `10333145690`, `10427324788`). They are recorded here only because the 4.80-vs-3.93 gap invites the question; the corpus cannot confirm or refute them, and the gap is fully explained by ordinary written-review selection bias.

## 9.6 Counting rules

- A review belongs to a theme if any of that theme's patterns matches its title-plus-body text, case-insensitively, in either the raw-lowercase or accent-folded form. Matches are **not** weighted by how many patterns hit.
- Percentages always use the stated denominator: 20,465 globally, the country's own total for country rows, the star band's total for star-band rows, the quarter's total for time rows.
- "Mean" is the arithmetic mean of the 1–5 star ratings of the reviews in that set.
- "1–2★" is the share of that set rated 1 or 2. "5★" is the share rated exactly 5.
- No review was excluded for length, language, age or low information. The single exact-duplicate text pair was retained.
- Segment rates (payer cohort, star-band rates, dismissible-paywall hint) are labelled as such and are never substituted for the global threshold classification.
