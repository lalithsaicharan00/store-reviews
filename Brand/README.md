# Often Enough — Brand and Logo

Written by Codex, 11 October 2026. The user finalized this identity and requested the guide, decision history and exported assets at the repository root.

**Small actions, repeated often enough, build something lasting.**

Three rounded tiles stack upward, tilted 12° clockwise. The tonal progression is part of the logo: darkest at the top on light surfaces, brightest at the top on dark surfaces. The nested artwork occupies 52 pixels of a 60-pixel icon, scaled proportionally at every size.
