# 01. Accounts and Identity

*Written by Claude (Claude Code), 26 Sep 2026. Topic 1 of the [architecture list](README.md). A proposal, not yet built.*

**Scope:**
- who a user is;
- how they sign up and sign in, or never do;
- how several sign-in methods join into one account;
- sessions, sign-out, recovery and deletion.

**Not in scope:**
- **Billing:** topic 2. It reuses the account ID defined here.
- **Backup for people without an account:** topic 3.
- **Phone migration:** topic 4.

**How each rule is backed:**
- **Users show**: backed by review evidence.
- **Platform rule**: an Apple or Google requirement, with a link.
- **First principles**: reasoned from how the system works.

**Evidence:**
- **A fresh corpus screen** for this topic: 1,487,223 reviews, 18 account patterns, 2,643 matches.
- **445 read in full and coded,** with every quote verified.
- **Reused:** the account cards from the [Data Safety report](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/Data Safety — Every Way Users Lose Data, and the Rules That Prevent It.md>).
- **Files:** [`Accounts Evidence/`](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/Accounts Evidence/>).

## 1. The design on one page

1. **No account needed, ever.** The app opens straight into Today. Signing in is offered, never required.
2. **An account is our own ID** (a UUID). Apple, Google and email OTP are *keys* that open it. An account can hold several keys.
3. **Our UUID is the account identity.** A contact email and an email sign-in key have separate roles. Changing either never changes the account ID; a new address must be verified before use.
4. **Signing in never creates an account silently, and never deletes data.** If a key is unknown, we ask: "Create a new account, or sign in another way?" Local data is merged, and anything set aside is kept.
5. **No silent guest accounts on our server.** People without an account are local-only, with their own backups (topic 3). An anonymous server account they cannot reclaim after a reinstall is the "I thought it was backed up" trap.
6. **Sessions never expire on their own.** An update never signs anyone out or reruns onboarding. If a session does fail, the app keeps working offline and shows "Sign in again to keep syncing".
7. **At least one working key, ideally two.** After sign-in we suggest adding a second method, because sign-in providers disappear. Facebook Login was switched off for apps, and users were locked out.
8. **Sign in through the system:** Apple's native sheet on iOS, Credential Manager on Android, the system browser everywhere else. Never inside a web view.
9. **Deletion is complete:** in the app and on the web, Apple tokens revoked, our mailing list cleared, and the store subscription clearly flagged.
10. **Support can always find you:** by account ID (shown in Settings), by any linked email, *including* Apple relay addresses, or by a purchase.

## 3. The design in detail

### 3.1 Identity model

```
Account  (our UUID, e.g. 4F2A…)          ← the only identity; everything hangs off this
 ├─ Keys (sign-in methods)               ← 1..n; at least 1
 │    apple   sub=001234.abcd…  (team-scoped; relay or real email; is_private_email)
 │    google  sub=10987…        (verified email)
 │    email   code-verified address
 ├─ Contact email  (verified for purchase confirmations/support; can differ from any key)
 ├─ Devices        (id, platform, name, last seen, push token)   ← device ID never backed up
 ├─ Sessions       (per device: rotating refresh token)
 ├─ Entitlements   (topic 2, linked by account ID — never by email)
 └─ Data           (one Durable Object per account — sync)
```

- **Keys table on the server:** (provider, provider_sub) → account ID. It is unique, so one Apple ID can never open two accounts.
- **Apple's `sub` is scoped to our developer team** (Platform rule). If the app ever moves to another team, Apple's transfer process (TN3159) must be followed within 60 days. **Publish from the account we intend to keep.**
- **Apple returns the user's name only on the first authorization** (known behaviour; confirm at build time), so store it then. The email may be a relay address.

### 3.2 People who never sign in
- **The app is fully usable,** with all data on the device.
- **No server account and no hidden guest ID on our servers.** The identity is local only: an install ID kept in `no_backup` or equivalent, and used only for diagnostics.
- **Their protection:** OS backup, their own iCloud or Google Drive backup (topic 3), export, and phone transfer (topic 4).
- **Their status is visible:** a small "Not backed up" or "Backed up to iCloud, 2 h ago" marker on the avatar and in Settings.
- **When we suggest an account** (decided 26 Sep 2026: both timings, **at most once a week**; never blocking):
  - after 3 days of use;
  - when they add a 5th habit;
  - **always after a premium purchase**, to protect it (decided);
  - before an iPhone ⇄ Android move.

**Why no silent guest account (first principles + users show):**
- On iOS, a guest credential in the Keychain usually survives a reinstall. On Android it doesn't, and after an OS restore it may be restored to the *wrong* device.
- So a guest server account works *sometimes*, and "sometimes" produces the "I thought it was backed up" story (`A86#87`, `A33#3525`).
- A clear local-only state is more honest.

### 3.3 Sign-up and sign-in
- **Where:** Settings → Account, the avatar, the suggestion prompts, and the fresh-install screen.
- **The fresh-install first screen:** "New here" or "I've used this before" (sign in / scan from my old phone / import a file).
- **Buttons:**

  | Platform | Buttons | How |
  |---|---|---|
  | iOS | **Sign in with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Continue with email** | Apple's native sheet; Google via its SDK (system browser session) |
  | Android | **Continue with Google**, **Sign in with Apple**, **Continue with email** | Credential Manager bottom sheet **plus** a Google button, as Google recommends; Apple through its web flow in a Custom Tab back to our domain |
  | Web / Windows / Mac (later) | Apple, Google, email OTP | Apple JS / Google Identity Services; desktop uses the system browser |

- **Apple is always listed first on iOS and Google first on Android** (users show: `P70#443`, `P70#621`).
- **Email OTP is included at launch** (decided 26 Sep 2026): enter email → receive a 6-digit code → verify → sign in. No password and no forgot-password flow. Copy: “We’ll email you a sign-in code. No password needed.” It works through our backend on every platform and does not require Workers Paid when using an external sender. See [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>) for challenge handling, quota failures and provider choice.
- **Unknown key:** "We couldn't find an account for this Google account." The options are **Create account**, **Try Apple instead** and **Cancel**. We never create an account silently.
- **Known email, different key:** if a verified email matches an existing account's contact email: "An account uses this email. Sign in with Apple to link them." We never auto-merge on email alone.

### 3.4 Linking and merging
- **Settings → Account → Sign-in methods** lists every key, with **Add** and **Remove**. The last key can't be removed.
- **After the first sign-in:** "Add a second way to sign in, in case one stops working."
- **Merging two accounts** (for example, someone accidentally created two):
  1. The user signs in to both, proving they own each.
  2. We show both summaries (habits, dates, purchases).
  3. We merge the data, move the keys and entitlements, and archive the empty account for 30 days.
- **First sign-in on a phone that already has local data:**
  - **Empty account:** upload the local data.
  - **Account has data:** show both summaries and let the user pick "Merge", "Keep account's" or "Keep this phone's". Whatever is set aside is saved as a snapshot.

### 3.5 Sessions and sign-out
- **Tokens:**
  - a short access token (about 1 h) plus a **rotating refresh token**;
  - kept in the iOS Keychain (this-device-only) and Android Keystore-encrypted storage;
  - excluded from OS backups.
- **Never expired for inactivity.** Refresh tokens slide for a year; reuse of an old refresh token revokes the device (theft detection).
- **When a session fails** (revoked, or a server problem): the app keeps working locally and shows a banner, "Sign in again to keep syncing". **Data is never cleared.**
- **Updates** never touch the session or the onboarding state. Both live in the database, and the migration tests check both.
- **Sign-out** asks: "Keep a copy of your habits on this phone?" The default is yes.
- **Apple credential state:** check it on launch (`getCredentialState`). On "revoked", sign out *without* deleting local data.

### 3.6 Recovery
| Situation | Path |
|---|---|
| **Lost the phone** | Sign in on the new one with any key |
| **Provider gone or broken** | Use another linked key, including email OTP. A contact email alone is not a recovery key; otherwise use support |
| **Email changed or lost** (work email, relay turned off) | Use another linked key or an authenticated session to verify and link a replacement email key; otherwise use support. Updating contact email alone does not change sign-in keys |
| **Can't remember which method they used** | The sign-in screen offers "Not sure? Try the other one". Support can look up by any email or purchase |
| **Apple on Android fails** | Fallback: scan a QR code from the old iPhone (topic 4), which joins the account without Apple's web flow |

### 3.7 Deletion
- **In the app:** Settings → Account → Delete account. On the web: a page that works without the app, as Google requires.
- **Before deleting:**
  - offer an export;
  - show any active **store subscription**, with a link to manage it (`showManageSubscription` on iOS, the Play subscriptions page on Android), stating that deleting does **not** cancel it (Apple's guidance);
  - optionally offer "delete when my subscription ends".
- **Deleting removes:**
  - the Durable Object data and R2 snapshots;
  - keys, sessions and devices;
  - contact email and mailing list entries;
  - **Apple token revocation** through the REST API (Apple's guidance).
- **Kept only where the law requires:** purchase records.
- **After deletion:** a confirmation screen with the date, and Restore Purchases still works on a fresh account.
- **Server-to-server notifications from Apple:**
  - `account-deleted` and `consent-revoked`: end the sessions for that key;
  - `email-disabled` and `email-enabled`: update whether we can email the user.

### 3.8 Email we send
- **Transactional email:** email OTP login, verified purchase confirmations and deletion confirmation. Marketing requires a separate opt-in and unsubscribe control; requested login codes do not subscribe the user to marketing (users show: `A33#306`).
- **Purchase confirmations:** only to a verified address we have, after server verification; no email requirement for guest purchases, no duplicates on restore, and delivery failure never blocks Plus.
- **Delivery is replaceable:** backend-owned templates, OTP logic and delivery records behind a provider adapter. Resend/Brevo selection remains pending; Cloudflare can replace the sender later. See [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>).
- **Apple relay addresses:** register our sending domain with Apple's Private Email Relay, with SPF and DKIM passing and exactly matching domains. Otherwise mail to relay users bounces.

### 3.9 Security
- **Verify every ID token on the server:** signature (Apple and Google public keys), issuer, audience, expiry and **nonce**.
- **Rate limits** on sign-in and code endpoints.
- **No passwords stored, ever.**
- **Device list** in Settings, with "Sign out this device".

### 3.10 Support
- **Settings shows the account ID** (a short, copyable form).
- **The support tool looks up by:** account ID, any key's email (including relay addresses), contact email, or a purchase transaction ID.
- **Support can:**
  - see keys, devices and the last sync time;
  - restore data to a point in time (30-day recovery);
  - merge accounts after verification.

### 3.11 Later
- **Web and desktop:** sign in with Apple or Google, or **scan a QR code from the phone** (like WhatsApp Web), so no password is ever needed.
- **Watch:** uses the phone's session through the watch–phone channel; details in topic 7.
- **China:** Apple sign-in works there. Google is blocked, so a WeChat or phone-number key would be the addition, only if we target the market.
- **Profiles on a shared iPad:** requested (`A10#66823`, `P36#194`), but not v1.
- **Age:** no account means no personal data collected from children. If accounts are opened to minors later, check COPPA and GDPR-K.

## 4. Platform rules

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Apps with account creation must offer **deletion inside the app**. A website may finish it, with a direct link. Tell users that store billing continues, and link to manage or refund | [Apple: Offering account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/) |
| Apps using Sign in with Apple **should revoke user tokens through the REST API** on deletion | same page |
| **5.1.1(v):** no login required unless there are significant account features | [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) |
| **4.8:** third-party login requires an equivalent private option (Sign in with Apple) | same |
| **Server-to-server notifications:** `email-enabled`, `email-disabled`, `consent-revoked` and `account-deleted` arrive as signed JWTs at our endpoint. Native apps get no client callback when an Apple account is deleted. A registered endpoint is mandatory for Korea-based developers from 1 Jan 2026, and advised for everyone | [Apple Developer Forums (DTS)](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/804270), [summary](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/sign-in-with-apple-server-notifications) |
| **Apple user IDs and relay emails are team-scoped.** An app transfer needs `transfer_sub` migration within 60 days | [Apple: Transferring apps and users](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/transferring-your-apps-and-users-to-another-team), [TN3159](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3159-migrating-sign-in-with-apple-users-for-an-app-transfer) |
| **Private relay email** needs registered sending domains, with SPF and DKIM matching exactly. New relay addresses use `@private.icloud.com` | [Customer.io guide](https://docs.customer.io/messaging/channels/email/deliverability/authenticating-for-apple-private-email-relay/), [Apple forum](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/798226) |
| **Android:** use Credential Manager's Sign in with Google, with *both* the bottom sheet and a persistent button | [Android: Sign in with Google](https://developer.android.com/identity/sign-in/credential-manager-siwg) |
| **Google OAuth must not run in an embedded web view.** Use the system browser | [Google OAuth policies](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies) |
| **Google Play:** in-app deletion **and** a web deletion link | [Play account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111) |

## 5. Test checklist (edge cases to automate)

1. **Fresh install:** "New here" goes straight to Today with no network needed. "I've used this before" offers all three paths.
2. **Sign-in with an unknown Google account** shows the "Create account?" dialog; no account is created without consent.
3. **Sign-in with Apple using Hide My Email:** the account is created and support can find it by the relay address.
4. **A second device signs in with the other provider** (after linking) and reaches the same account.
5. **Local data + an account with data:** merge shows both summaries; the set-aside copy is kept as a snapshot.
6. **An app update with an expired access token:** no sign-out, no onboarding, silent refresh.
7. **A revoked refresh token:** the app keeps working offline, shows the banner, and no data is lost.
8. **Apple `consent-revoked` notification:** the server ends the sessions, and the app shows "Sign in again" with data kept.
9. **Removing the last key is blocked;** removing one of two works.
10. **Deletion with an active subscription:** the warning and link are shown; data is gone on the server; the Apple token is revoked; no further emails.
11. **OS backup restore on a new Android phone:** tokens and device ID are not restored; the phone asks to sign in and registers as a new device.
12. **The Apple web flow on Android** in a Custom Tab (not a web view) returns to the app. When it fails, the QR fallback works.
13. **Offline sign-in attempt:** a clear message, and nothing breaks.
14. **A China network without a VPN:** Apple sign-in works, and the Google button explains it may be unavailable.
15. **Email OTP:** expired/reused/superseded codes fail; resend and attempt limits apply; contact-email matches cannot silently link accounts. Quota/delivery failures preserve local use. See [email acceptance checks](<Email Delivery Decision.md>).

## 6. Open questions for you

1. **Email sending provider:** OTP login and purchase confirmations are accepted for launch. Choose the initial sender after quota/onboarding/delivery checks; see [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>).
2. **Prompt timing:** after 3 days of use, or only at meaningful moments (5th habit, purchase, phone move)? My suggestion is both, capped at one prompt a week.
