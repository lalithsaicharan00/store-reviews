# 02. Billing and Entitlements

*Written by Claude (Claude Code), 26 Sep 2026. Topic 2 of the [architecture list](README.md). A proposal, not yet built.*

**Scope:**
- how people pay (App Store, Google Play);
- how a purchase becomes "premium" on every device and platform;
- restore, family sharing, trials, refunds, price changes and cancellation;
- how our server verifies and acknowledges purchases.

**Products assumed:** from the [business model report](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Habit Tracker — Business Model, Free Baseline and Moat.md>). That report is a recommendation, not a decision.
- **Plus:** a one-time purchase, about US$14.99, with regional prices.
- **Companion:** an optional subscription, about US$2.99 a month or US$19.99 a year. **Not planned for now** (26 Sep 2026): our apps sell lifetime purchases only. The subscription parts of this doc (§3.7, Companion rows) apply only if a subscription is added later.
- The design works for any mix of one-time products and subscriptions.
- **Decided (26 Sep 2026): native store billing plus our own server check.** No RevenueCat. See §3.11.

**How each rule is backed:**
- **Users show**: backed by review evidence.
- **Platform rule**: an Apple or Google requirement, with a link.
- **First principles**: reasoned from how the system works.

**Evidence:**
- **A fresh screen:** 1,487,223 reviews, 21 billing patterns, 14,567 matches.
- **477 read in full and coded** (at most 3 per app per pattern, so no single app dominates), with every quote verified.
- **Reused:** ledger cards C033 (restore, 45 apps), C065 (payers, 59 apps), C037 (family) and C251 (cross-platform), plus the Data Safety report.
- **Files:** [`Billing Evidence/`](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/Billing Evidence/>).

## 1. The design on one page

1. **Store billing only:** App Store and Google Play. No web checkout, no card details held by us, no subscriptions users can't find in their phone's settings.
2. **Premium is a server-side entitlement tied to our account ID,** built from the store's own signed records. Each purchase carries our account ID (`appAccountToken` on Apple, `obfuscatedAccountId` on Google).
3. **Checked automatically on every launch.** A Restore button exists too, but nobody should need it.
4. **Never downgrade on doubt.** If the store or our server can't be reached, keep the last known entitlement. **Plus never expires,** and nothing (updates, business-model changes, reinstalls) takes it away.
5. **One purchase, every device and platform,** once signed in: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, web.
6. **Paying never means seeing ads or offers for what you already own.** Entitled users never see a paywall for it.
7. **Data is never tied to payment.** When Companion ends, its extras stop. Every habit, backup and export stays.
8. **Native billing, checked by our server.** The stores handle payment, restore, family sharing and refunds. The app acknowledges Google purchases at once (Google refunds anything unacknowledged after 3 days). For signed-in users, every purchase is also sent to our server, verified and recorded on the account, so it unlocks on iPhone **and** Android. Refunds and cancellations reach the server through store notifications and remove it everywhere.
9. **Honest trials and prices:**
   - the exact charge date is shown up front;
   - our own reminder goes out 2 days before a trial ends;
   - no countdowns;
   - existing subscribers keep their price.
10. **A clear "Your purchases" screen:** what you own, where it was bought, when it renews, and one tap to manage or cancel in the store.

## 3. The design in detail

### 3.1 Products in the stores

| Product | Apple | Google | Family sharing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plus** | Non-consumable | One-time product | **On for Apple** (automatic, up to 5 family members). Google Play can't share in-app purchases through Family Library, so on Android: none at launch |
| **Companion** | Auto-renewable; one subscription group, monthly + yearly | One subscription, two base plans (monthly, yearly) | Off (AI cost per user) |

- **No consumables.** Nothing like paid streak freezes; users show those "repairs" failing (`A10#325`).
- **No web purchases at launch.**
- **Listing text** states exactly what is shared on each platform (users show: `A53#1673`, `A13#14660`).

### 3.2 How a purchase flows

```
 App (StoreKit 2 / Play Billing)                         Cloudflare Worker  (billing)
 ───────────────────────────────                         ─────────────────────────────
 buy(product, appAccountToken=accountId)  ──►  store  ──►  signed transaction / purchaseToken
        │                                                         │
        └─ POST /billing/verify  { JWS | purchaseToken } ───────►  verify signature / call Play API
                                                                    record purchase → entitlement(account)
                                                                    Google: ACKNOWLEDGE now
                                   ◄─────────────────────────────  { entitlements, signed }
 finish() / show "Plus is yours"                                    
                                                                   Apple ASSN V2  ──► /billing/apple/notify
                                                                   Google RTDN (Pub/Sub) ──► /billing/google/rtdn
                                                                   → update entitlement (renew, grace, refund, revoke)
```

- **Grant only after verification,** and atomically with the purchase. An upsell may appear only *after* the grant (ledger C033: an upsell screen dropped the entitlement for 16 months).
- **Apple:**
  - listen to `Transaction.updates` from app launch (renewals, Ask to Buy approvals, refunds and other devices all arrive there);
  - use `Transaction.currentEntitlements` for the current state;
  - `finish()` after the server has recorded the transaction.
- **Google:**
  - grant only when the state is `PURCHASED`; `PENDING` shows "Waiting for payment";
  - acknowledge on the server right away, with a client fallback;
  - call `queryPurchasesAsync()` on every launch and resume.
- **Signed-out buyers:** the purchase still works, verified through the store on the device. At purchase time we suggest signing in: "Keep Plus on every phone you own, one tap". It is never forced (users show: `P15#81`, `P35#154`).

#### Purchase confirmation email (decided 26 Sep 2026)

After a new completed purchase is verified and recorded on the server, schedule a confirmation to an available verified contact email. The store receipt remains separate. Guest buyers need no email or account; skip our email when no verified address is available. Sending never blocks purchase acknowledgement or Plus access. Deduplicate by store + purchase ID + message type; restores, account linking and repeated notifications do not send another new-purchase confirmation. See [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>) for retries, content and provider migration.

### 3.3 The entitlement model

```
entitlement(account) =
  plus      : any valid, un-refunded Plus purchase on ANY linked store account  (or promo / goodwill grant)
  companion : any subscription in {active, grace_period, cancelled-but-not-expired}
```

- **Stored on the server** per account, with source (apple, google or promo), IDs (`originalTransactionId` or `purchaseToken`), status, expiry and a family-shared flag.
- **Cached on the device:** a signed entitlement token from our server, plus the store's own local check.
  - **Plus:** cached with no expiry.
  - **Companion:** cached until its expiry, plus 3 days of grace for offline use.
- **Never downgrade on an error:** a network failure, store outage or StoreKit error keeps the last known state. Only a confirmed expiry, refund or revocation removes access (users show "flapping": `A1#51475`, `P124#73`).
- **Store states map exactly:**
  - Apple grace period and Google `IN_GRACE_PERIOD`: keep access;
  - on hold, paused or expired: no Companion;
  - refunded or revoked: remove at once.
- **What refunds and expiry remove:** extras only (Companion features, Plus themes and extras). **Data is never removed.**

### 3.4 Restore, new devices and platforms
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Reinstall or new phone, same OS, signed in** | Entitlements load on sign-in. Nothing to press |
| **Reinstall or new phone, same OS, signed out** | On launch the app reads the store (`currentEntitlements` or `queryPurchasesAsync`), and Plus appears automatically |
| **iPad, Mac or second phone** | Same as above. Apple and Google entitlements cover all of the user's devices on that store |
| **iPhone ⇄ Android, signed in** | The server honours the other store's purchase. Allowed by Apple 3.1.3(b), since Plus is also sold in the app; Google allows "consumption-only" access to content bought elsewhere |
| **iPhone ⇄ Android, signed out** | The move flow (topic 4) says "Sign in to bring Plus with you" (one tap Apple or Google), then links the old store purchase to the new account on the old phone first. The QR transfer does this automatically |
| **Restore button** | Settings → Plus → "Restore purchases": visible, not greyed out. It shows a real result ("Plus found, bought 12 Mar on App Store", or "No purchase found for this Apple ID"), never a fake "restored" |

### 3.5 Buying the "wrong" thing
- **Someone with an active Companion trial or subscription buys something that replaces it:** we can't cancel store subscriptions for them. We show "You still have Companion renewing on 3 Oct. Cancel it here", with a link (users show: `A1#45533`).
- **Someone taps Buy on something they already own:** we show their status instead of the store sheet.
- **A second Plus purchase from a different store account:** the server flags it, and support can refund.

### 3.6 Family
- **Apple Family Sharing turned on for Plus.** Family members get it automatically. Revocation (leaving the family) arrives as a `REVOKE` notification, which removes the extras only.
- **Android:** stated plainly on the listing. A "share Plus with family" feature for Android would need our own account-based family groups: **later, only if asked for.**

### 3.7 Trials, prices and cancelling (Companion)
- **Store introductory offers only:** a free trial or first-month discount. The purchase sheet shows the price after the trial and the **exact date**.
- **Our own reminder, 2 days before a trial ends:** "Your Companion trial ends Fri 3 Oct; you'll be charged $19.99. Keep it or cancel." Tapping it opens the store's cancel page (users show: 33 trial complaints).
- **Price changes:** existing subscribers keep their price (both stores support keeping legacy prices). There are no countdowns and no "offer ends in 10:00" (users show: `A20#672`).
- **Regional prices:** set per country with purchasing-power pricing, not a converted US price (users show: 18 reviews, `A10#16425`).
- **Monthly ⇄ yearly:** one subscription group or base plan set, so the store handles the change. Our screen links to it.
- **Cancel:** "Your purchases" → **Manage subscription** opens `showManageSubscriptions()` on iOS or the Play subscription page on Android. It's never more than 2 taps from Settings.

### 3.8 "Your purchases" screen (Settings → Plus)
- **Plus:** "Yours forever, bought 12 Mar 2027 on App Store" (or "via Family Sharing").
- **Companion:** plan, renewal date, price, status (active, grace, cancelled until 3 Oct) and **Manage** (opens the store).
- **Buttons:** Restore purchases; Contact support (includes the account ID and transaction IDs automatically).
- **No purchase buttons for things already owned** (users show: `A34#83`).

### 3.9 Errors, in plain words
| Store error | Message |
|---|---|
| Apple `storeProblemError` or network | "The App Store didn't respond. Nothing was charged. Try again in a minute." |
| Apple purchase not allowed (Screen Time) | "Purchases are turned off on this device (Screen Time)." |
| Ask to Buy / Play `PENDING` | "Waiting for approval. Plus unlocks as soon as it's approved." |
| Google wrong account | "This will use the Google Play account ja…@gmail.com. Switch accounts in the Play Store." |
| Already owned | Show the status, not an error |

### 3.10 Refunds and support
- **Refunds go through the stores:** Apple's `beginRefundRequest` and the Play refund flow. We never argue refunds (users show: 21 "refund refused", mean 1.10).
- **Goodwill:** support can grant Plus by promo code (Apple offer codes, Play promo codes) or by a server-side grant on the account.
- **Support tool:** look up an account by transaction ID or purchase token, see every purchase, notification and grant, and re-run verification.
- **No promo claims** that can't be redeemed in the app (users show: `P18#1197`).

### 3.11 Native billing plus our server check (decided 26 Sep 2026)

**Decision:** use the stores' own billing (StoreKit 2 on Apple, Play Billing on Google). Our server only verifies and records purchases, so they work across platforms. **No RevenueCat.**

**Why:** our apps sell lifetime purchases, not subscriptions. For a one-time purchase, the stores already handle payment, proof of purchase, restore on any device with the same Apple ID or Google account, Family Sharing, pending payments and refunds. RevenueCat's real value is subscription machinery and cross-store charts, which we don't need, so its 1% fee buys very little. **Revisit only if a subscription is ever added.** RevenueCat can import existing purchases then, so starting native doesn't close that door.

**The flow:**

| Step | Signed out | Signed in |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | store purchase sheet; our account ID attached (`appAccountToken` / `obfuscatedAccountId`) when there is one | same |
| Check it's genuine | the store's own signed record, checked on the device (StoreKit 2 verification; Play purchase state) | **also** sent to our server: `POST /v1/purchases/verify` with the signed transaction (Apple) or purchase token (Google) |
| Server verifies | — | Apple: check Apple's signature chain (Apple Root CA G3), no call to Apple needed. Google: one call to the Play Developer API to confirm the purchase and its state |
| Grant Plus | at once on this device | recorded on the **account**, then synced to every device the person signs into, **on iPhone and Android** |
| Acknowledge (Google) | the app acknowledges right after granting | same; the server acknowledges too as a backup |
| Restore | ask the store on every launch (`Transaction.currentEntitlements` / `queryPurchasesAsync`) | sign in; the account already has it |

**Refunds, cancellations and failures:**
- **Apple refund or Family Sharing revoked:** App Store Server Notifications V2 (`REFUND`, `REVOKE`) reach `/v1/hooks/apple`. The server removes Plus from the account on every platform. On the phone, StoreKit's `Transaction.updates` reports the revocation too.
- **Google refund or cancelled order:** Real-time developer notifications for one-time products reach `/v1/hooks/play` through Pub/Sub. As a backstop, a daily job reads the **Voided Purchases API** in case a notification was missed. The app also sees the purchase disappear from `queryPurchasesAsync`.
- **Payment pending or failed** (Google pending, Apple Ask to Buy): nothing is granted until the store says it's paid. The app shows "Waiting for payment", and grants the moment the store confirms.
- **Our check fails** (server down, store API timeout): **never remove Plus.** The store's on-device record stands, and the server retries (rule 4, "never downgrade on doubt"). Only a confirmed refund or revocation removes it.
- **A purchase that doesn't verify** (forged or wrong app): not recorded on the account. The device keeps whatever the store itself says.
- **What removal takes away:** Plus extras only. Data is never touched (rule 7).

**What we give up:** revenue charts across both stores (App Store Connect and the Play Console each report their own sales) and remote paywall testing. Neither is needed for a lifetime purchase.

**Maintenance:** the store's one-time purchase flow rarely changes. The recurring job is Google's yearly required Billing Library update (for example, version 8 was required for updates from 31 Aug 2026). That update would be needed with RevenueCat too.

**Implementation notes:**
- **Apple verification in a Worker:**
  - verify the JWS signature chain against Apple Root CA G3 with WebCrypto (ES256);
  - the App Store Server API (an ES256 JWT signed by our in-app purchase key) is only needed for lookups, such as finding a purchase from an order ID a user sends to support.
- **Google:**
  - a service-account JWT (RS256) to get an OAuth token, then `purchases.productsv2` to verify, and the Voided Purchases API for the daily refund check;
  - RTDN via a Pub/Sub push subscription to our Worker (Pub/Sub has a free tier).

## 4. Platform rules

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledge Play purchases within 3 days** or they are refunded and the entitlement revoked. Grant only when `PURCHASED`; the 3 days start then | [Play Billing: integrate](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/integrate) |
| Call `queryPurchasesAsync()` on launch and resume, to catch purchases made elsewhere or while the app was closed; use `obfuscatedAccountId` to link a purchase to our user | same |
| Subscription states: grace period = access; on hold and paused = none; cancelled = until expiry; revoked = remove now. Use RTDN plus `subscriptionsv2.get` as the source of truth; `linkedPurchaseToken` links plan changes | [Play subscription lifecycle](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/lifecycle/subscriptions) |
| **Apple 3.1.1:** have a restore mechanism for restorable purchases | [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) |
| **Apple 3.1.2(a):** subscriptions must work on all of the user's devices. Don't take away what users already paid for when moving to subscriptions | same |
| **Apple 3.1.3(b):** content bought on other platforms may be unlocked, if it is also sold as an in-app purchase | same |
| **Google Play:** apps may be "consumption-only" for content bought elsewhere; in-app sales must use Play Billing | [Play Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en), [FAQ](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2020/09/commerce-update-faqs.html) |
| **Apple Family Sharing** for non-consumables and auto-renewable subscriptions, chosen per product in App Store Connect | [Apple news](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=4zbvn7u9), [Tech Talk](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/tech-talks/110345/) |
| **Google Play Family Library:** in-app purchases can't be shared | [Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7007852?hl=en) |
| **App Store Server Notifications V2** report renewals, failures and grace, expiry, refunds and revocation to our endpoint | [ASSN V2](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreservernotifications/app-store-server-notifications-v2) |
| **Account deletion must tell users that store billing continues** and link to manage it (topic 1) | [Apple: account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/support/offering-account-deletion-in-your-app/) |
| **RevenueCat pricing (not used, see §3.11):** free to US$2.5k monthly tracked revenue, then 1% | [pricing summary](https://costbench.com/software/subscription-billing/revenuecat/) |

**StoreKit 2 practice** (Apple's docs weren't re-fetchable today; confirm at build time):
- start `Transaction.updates` at launch;
- use `Transaction.currentEntitlements`;
- `finish()` after recording;
- set `appAccountToken` to our account ID;
- `AppStore.showManageSubscriptions` and `beginRefundRequest` for manage and refund.

## 5. Test checklist

1. **Buy Plus signed out:** unlocked at once. Reinstall: unlocked on first launch with no button pressed. Airplane mode: still unlocked.
2. **Buy Plus signed in on iPhone:** sign in on Android and Plus is there. Sign in on the web later: Plus is there.
3. **Refund Plus** (sandbox): the notification arrives, extras are removed, data untouched, and a kind message is shown.
4. **Family Sharing:** an organiser buys, a member gets Plus. The member leaves: `REVOKE` removes the extras only.
5. **Play purchase left `PENDING`:** "waiting" is shown; it unlocks on `PURCHASED`; it is acknowledged within minutes, and the alert fires if not.
6. **Companion trial:** our reminder fires 2 days before the end. Cancel in the store: access continues to the end date, then stops. Data untouched.
7. **Renewal fails:** the grace period keeps access; recovery keeps access; on hold removes Companion only.
8. **Server down and store slow on launch:** the last known entitlements are kept; no paywall is shown to a payer.
9. **Buy Plus during a Companion trial:** the "cancel your Companion here" notice appears.
10. **Monthly to yearly switch** through the store: the entitlement is continuous and the renewal date is updated.
11. **Update from any past build:** the entitlement is unchanged (added to the migration tests).
12. **Account deletion with active Companion:** the warning and manage link are shown; Plus remains restorable from the store on a new account.
