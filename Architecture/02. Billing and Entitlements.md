## 1. The design on one page

1. **Store billing only:** App Store and Google Play. No web checkout, no card details held by us, no subscriptions users can't find in their phone's settings.
2. **Premium is a server-side entitlement tied to our account ID,** built from the store's own signed records. Each purchase carries our account ID (`appAccountToken` on Apple, `obfuscatedAccountId` on Google).
3. **Checked automatically on every launch.** A Restore button exists too, but nobody should need it.
4. **Never downgrade on doubt.** If the store or our server can't be reached, keep the last known entitlement. **Plus never expires,** and nothing (updates, business-model changes, reinstalls) takes it away.
5. **Free is one device; Plus is every device** (decided 27 Sep 2026; since 1 Oct 2026 the one free device may be a phone or a tablet). Free: 5 habits on one phone or tablet, local only, no account, no sync. Plus: unlimited habits, phones and tablets together, Apple Watch and Wear OS, sync across devices and platforms, and automatic backup, through an account created right after purchase. **Plus Family** covers the buyer and up to 5 invited people, each on their own account and platform (§3.6).
6. **Paying never means seeing ads or offers for what you already own.** Entitled users never see a paywall for it.
7. **Data is never tied to payment.** A refund removes Plus extras only. Every habit, backup and export stays.
8. **Native billing, checked by our server.** The stores handle payment, restore, family sharing and refunds. The app acknowledges Google purchases at once (Google refunds anything unacknowledged after 3 days). For signed-in users, every purchase is also sent to our server, verified and recorded on the account, so it unlocks on iPhone **and** Android. Refunds and cancellations reach the server through store notifications and remove it everywhere.
9. **Honest prices, no trials:** the price is on the Plus screen before the store sheet opens; no trials, no countdowns, no fake discounts; regional prices per country (§3.7).
10. **A clear "Your purchases" screen:** what you own, where it came from, and Restore.

## 3. The design in detail

### 3.1 What is free and what is Plus (decided 27 Sep 2026)

| | Free | Plus (one-time, lifetime) |
|---|---|---|
| Habits | **5**, fixed (decided 29 Sep 2026; never changed) | Unlimited |
| Widgets | **Interactive home-screen and lock-screen widgets**, showing and ticking every free habit | More widget designs and sizes, and more than 5 habits on a widget |
| Devices | **Any one device** (phone or tablet), no sync (decided 1 Oct 2026; was one phone) | Every device together, kept in sync: phones, tablets, Mac and web later |
| Apple Watch / Wear OS | – | ✓ |
| Sync across devices and platforms | – | ✓ (needs the account) |
| Backup | The phone's own backup, on-device snapshots, export/import (topic 3) | Plus automatic backup to our server (needs the account) |
| Apple Health / Health Connect | – | ✓ (first update after launch; topic 5 §9) |
| Account | **None.** Free users can't create one | Created in the purchase flow (§3.2) |

- **Never locked, on any tier:** checking in, history, reminders, widgets and export for existing habits. Other extras (themes, deeper stats) are set with the pricing work.
- **Widgets are never moved into Plus later** (users show: paid basic widgets average 1.72★, and one app's 1★ share doubled when it gated them; [Free Plan Design §6](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Free Plan Design — Habit Cap, Widgets and an Honest Listing.md>)).
- **How the limit is shown in the app: as information, never as pressure** (decided 29 Sep 2026).
  - The habit list shows a quiet line such as "3 of 5 free habits", in secondary text. No colour change, badge, banner or pop-up as it fills.
  - No upgrade prompts before the limit. Adding a 6th habit opens one calm Plus screen: "Plus: unlimited habits, every device, sync and backup. One payment, yours forever. [Get Plus] · [Not now]". Existing habits keep working either way.
  - Users show why: nagging averages 1.44★, and surprise at the wall averages 2.33★ (Free Plan Design §3, §9).
- **Store description** (decided 29 Sep 2026): the first lines say what is free, e.g. "Free forever: 5 habits on your phone, with reminders, interactive home-screen and lock-screen widgets and full history. No ads, no account." and "Plus, one payment: unlimited habits, iPad, Apple Watch, sync and backup." No price words in the title or icon (Google Play metadata policy). Apple 2.3.2 requires the listing to say what needs a purchase.
- **A Free vs Plus comparison screenshot:** not decided. It needs its own research (Backlog).
- **A tablet on its own is free; using it together with a phone is Plus** (decided 1 Oct 2026; replaces "Tablets are Plus", 27 Sep). A free tablet works like a free phone: 5 habits, no account, no sync. Onboarding says once that habits stay on this device (topic 7 §7.1). This avoids a paywall on first launch for tablet-only people (users show: an iPad user's 1★ said the app should say from the start that you have to pay, `A4#8418`).
- **Why:** payers cluster in multi-device and sync, lifetime is the top purchase reason, and nobody in the reviews objects to a tablet or Watch being paid ([evidence](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus Scope and Account at Purchase.md>)).

### 3.2 How a purchase flows

```
 App (StoreKit 2 / Play Billing)                         Cloudflare Worker  (billing)
 ───────────────────────────────                         ─────────────────────────────
 buy(product, appAccountToken=accountId)  ──►  store  ──►  signed transaction / purchaseToken
        │                                                         │
        └─ POST /billing/verify  { JWS | purchaseToken } ───────►  verify signature / call Play API
                                                                    record purchase → entitlement(account)
                                                                    Google: ACKNOWLEDGE now
                                   ◄─────────────────────────────  { entitlements, signed }
 finish() / show "Plus is yours"                                    
                                                                   Apple ASSN V2  ──► /billing/apple/notify
                                                                   Google RTDN (Pub/Sub) ──► /billing/google/rtdn
                                                                   → update entitlement (refund, revoke)
```

- **Grant only after verification,** and atomically with the purchase. An upsell may appear only *after* the grant (ledger C033: an upsell screen dropped the entitlement for 16 months).
- **Apple:**
  - listen to `Transaction.updates` from app launch (Ask to Buy approvals, refunds and other devices all arrive there);
  - use `Transaction.currentEntitlements` for the current state;
  - `finish()` after the server has recorded the transaction.
- **Google:**
  - grant only when the state is `PURCHASED`; `PENDING` shows "Waiting for payment";
  - acknowledge on the server right away, with a client fallback;
  - call `queryPurchasesAsync()` on every launch and resume.
- **The purchase flow: buy first, then one tap to turn on sync** (decided 27 Sep 2026):
  1. **The Plus screen** lists what Plus includes, including "Sync with your iPad and other phones, iPhone or Android" and "Automatic backup". A footnote says: "Sync and backup use a free account; you'll set it up in one tap after buying." **[Get Plus]** opens the store sheet. There is no sign-in before paying, so a broken sign-in can never block a sale.
  2. **Straight after payment**, as the last step of the same flow: "Plus is yours 🎉 · **One last step: turn on sync and backup** · your habits on your iPad and any phone, and safe if you lose this one". It shows **[Continue with Apple]** and **[Continue with Google]**, and a visible link *"Not now: use Plus on this device only"*.
  3. **The skip link must stay** (Apple 5.1.1(v): registration can't be required for a purchase that isn't account-based). Plus works on that device at once, and sync and backup show as off where they live.
  4. **If the app closes between paying and signing in,** the step appears once on the next launch. After that, only the features ask (topic 1 §3.2).
  5. **Signing in** links the purchase to the account on the server, and the phone's habits upload as the first sync.
- **Plus Family:** the same order, but the last step is "Sign in to invite your family", with no skip. A family plan is account-based, which Apple allows (§3.6).

#### Purchase confirmation email (decided 26 Sep 2026; narrowed 27 Sep 2026)

This is the **only email we ever send.**
- **When:** after a new Plus or Plus Family purchase is verified, and only if the buyer has an account (the address comes from Apple or Google). A buyer who taps "Not now" gets it when they sign in later, if the purchase is under 30 days old.
- **Separate from the store receipt.**
- **Never blocks anything:** sending never holds up acknowledgement or Plus.
- **Sent once:** deduplicated by store + purchase ID. Restores, account linking and repeated notifications never send it again.
- See [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>).

### 3.3 The entitlement model

```
entitlement(account) =
  plus      : any valid, un-refunded Plus or Plus Family purchase on ANY linked store account  (or promo / goodwill grant)
              OR active membership of a family whose owner's Plus Family purchase is valid
```

- **Stored on the server** per account, with source (apple, google or promo), IDs (`originalTransactionId` or `purchaseToken`), status, expiry and a family-shared flag.
- **Cached on the device:** a signed entitlement token from our server, plus the store's own local check. Plus is cached with no expiry.
- **Never downgrade on an error:** a network failure, store outage or StoreKit error keeps the last known state. Only a confirmed expiry, refund or revocation removes access (users show "flapping": `A1#51475`, `P124#73`).
- **Store states map exactly:** pending: nothing yet; purchased: grant; refunded or revoked: remove at once.
- **What refunds remove:** Plus extras only. **Data is never removed.**

### 3.4 Restore, new devices and platforms
| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Reinstall or new phone, same OS, signed in** | Entitlements load on sign-in. Nothing to press |
| **Reinstall or new phone, same OS, signed out** | On launch the app reads the store (`currentEntitlements` or `queryPurchasesAsync`), and Plus appears automatically |
| **iPad, Mac or second phone** | Plus is recognised from the store on the same Apple ID or Google account. The first screen asks to sign in to bring the habits (sync needs the account). A tablet or second device **without** Plus shows the Plus screen (§3.1) |
| **iPhone ⇄ Android, signed in** | The server honours the other store's purchase. Allowed by Apple 3.1.3(b), since Plus is also sold in the app; Google allows "consumption-only" access to content bought elsewhere |
| **iPhone ⇄ Android, signed out** | The move flow (topic 4) says "Sign in to bring Plus with you" (one tap Apple or Google), then links the old store purchase to the new account on the old phone first |
| **Restore button** | Settings → Plus → "Restore purchases": visible, not greyed out. It shows a real result ("Plus found, bought 12 Mar on App Store", or "No purchase found for this Apple ID"), never a fake "restored" |

### 3.5 Buying the "wrong" thing
- **Someone taps Buy on something they already own:** we show their status instead of the store sheet.
- **A second Plus purchase from a different store account:** the server flags it, and support can refund.

### 3.6 Family (decided 27 Sep 2026)

**What we sell:** **Plus Family**, a one-time purchase for the buyer plus **up to 5 people they invite**. It works like Duolingo's family plan, not like Apple Family Sharing. Our server holds the family, so it works on **iPhone, iPad, Android and web**, and the invite happens **inside our app**. Evidence: [Family Sharing — Backlog 1](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Family Sharing — Backlog 1.md>).

**Why our own groups, not Apple Family Sharing** (users show + platform facts):
- Apple Family Sharing reaches only people in the buyer's Apple family, and never Android ([Google Play can't share in-app purchases](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7007852?hl=en)). Families are mixed: “Cool app, but don’t buy if your family has android” (`A1#51952`).
- The biggest family failure in the corpus is sharing that happens in iOS Settings with no screen in the app: 77 reviews, mean 2.6★, “there is no way in the app to share it with my family” (`A1#46798`). Buyers expect **an invite**: “There’s NO option to invite others using emails” (`A1#323`).
- Apple Family Sharing **can't be turned off** once on. Keeping it off on both products leaves that door closed. It can still be added later if a real need shows.

**Each person keeps their own account and their own habits.**
- Nobody shares a login. Every member signs in with **their own** Apple or Google account (topic 1), so their habits, streaks, backups and devices are theirs.
- **Private by default:** the owner sees members' names and "joined" dates, **never** their habits. Shared habits between people are not planned for launch.
- A family therefore needs accounts. The buyer pays, then signs in as the last step: "Sign in to invite your family", one tap, with no skip. Plain Plus keeps its skip link (§3.2).

**Buying**
- Plus Family is on the same paywall as Plus: "Plus · just you" and "Plus Family · you + 4 people, any phone". **US$59.99 for 5 people, the buyer + 4** (decided by the user, 10 Oct 2026, §3.7). Reviews suggest families accept "the cost of about 2" for "all of us" (`A10#37023`). No trial, no renewal, lifetime like Plus.
- **Already own Plus?** The paywall shows **Upgrade to Family** for the difference (the Plus Family upgrade product, shown only to accounts that own Plus). The server links the two purchases.

**Inviting** (Settings → Plus → Family)
- The owner taps **Invite** and shares a link (`https://<domain>/join/<code>`) by message, email or QR code. There's also a 6-character code to type in. A code is single-use, expires after 7 days, and can be cancelled. The screen shows **5 seats**, each "empty", "invited · expires 3 Oct" or "Sam · joined 12 Oct".
- **Nothing** happens in iOS or Android settings, and the invite never depends on being in an Apple or Google family.

**Joining**
1. The member opens the link. It opens the app, or the store page if the app isn't installed. After installing, "I have an invite" is on the first screen and in Settings, and it reads the code from the link or lets them type it.
2. They sign in with their own account, or create one (one tap, Apple or Google). Joining a family is how someone without Plus gets an account.
3. **"Join Maria's family? You'll get Plus on all your devices. Maria won't see your habits."** → Join.
4. Plus unlocks at once on every device they're signed into. Existing habits are untouched, and signing in merges local data as in topic 1.
- **Someone who already bought Plus** can still join. Their own purchase stays theirs and matters again if they leave.
- **One family at a time.** Someone who owns a Plus Family can't join another one.

**Leaving and removing**
- A member can **Leave** at any time, and the owner can **Remove** anyone. The seat frees up at once.
- The ex-member keeps every habit. Plus extras stop, unless they own Plus themselves. The message says why: "You left Maria's family. Your habits are all here."
- **Abuse limit:** a family can add at most **10 new members per rolling 12 months**, which covers normal life changes and stops resale. Support can lift it.

**When the owner's purchase goes away**
- **Refund or revocation** (store notification, §3.11): Plus extras stop for the owner and every member, with a message. Data is never touched (rule 7).
- **The owner deletes their account:** the purchase is tied to it, so the family ends. Members get a notice, keep Plus for **30 days** as goodwill, and can buy their own (their habits are unaffected).
- **Our server or a store API is down:** never remove access (rule 4). A member's last known family Plus stays cached like any Plus.

**How the server stores it**
```
family(familyId, ownerAccountId, purchaseRef, seats = 5, createdAt)
family_member(familyId, accountId, joinedAt, leftAt?)
family_invite(codeHash, familyId, expiresAt, usedByAccountId?, cancelledAt?)
entitlement(account).plus = own purchase  OR  member of a family whose purchase is valid
```
- The member's entitlement records its source as `family:<familyId>`, so support and the "Your purchases" screen can say "Plus from Maria's family".
- Every invite, join, leave and removal is logged for support (§3.10).

**Store rules to confirm at submission:** the purchase is made with in-app purchase on each store (Apple 3.1.1 and 3.1.3; Play Billing). Unlocking it for invited accounts is the same account-based access as cross-platform Plus (Apple 3.1.3(b), Play "consumption-only"), and Duolingo and Headspace sell family plans this way. Note it in App Review notes.

**Kids:** members need their own account. Children with their own phones sign in with their own Apple or Google child account. **Profiles for kids without a phone**, inside a parent's app, are a separate later feature. **Family members must be 13 or older** (decided 28 Sep 2026; the join screen says so), which keeps children's-privacy rules (COPPA, GDPR-K) out of scope.

### 3.7 Prices
- **No trials and no subscriptions.** Plus and Plus Family are one-time purchases (users show: trial charges and forgotten cancellations are among the angriest billing reviews, 1.08–1.33★).
- **No countdowns and no "offer ends in 10:00"** (users show: `A20#672`).
- **Regional prices:** set per country with purchasing-power pricing, not a converted US price (users show: 18 reviews, `A10#16425`).
- **The price itself (decided by the user, 10 Oct 2026, [Price and Size from the Extreme Case](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus, Price and the Server — How We Decided/Plus and Plus Family — Price and Size from the Extreme Case.md>)):** Plus never
  below US$24.99 (US$29.99 recommended, still to choose); **no price anywhere, regional, student or a sale, below
  US$19.99** (working floor US$20.99, before that country's tax); Plus Family US$59.99 for 5 people, at most US$10 off.
  Discounts are real and never stacked. Every sale stays profitable with a good margin at the extreme case with
  inflation, which holds only with the server cost changes in Server Cost §7 (free accounts cheap by design).
- **Student price:** a one-time-use offer code after the student is verified (offer codes cover one-time purchases
  since 2025, iOS 16.3+); the discount and how students are verified are still open.

- **No purchase buttons for things already owned** (users show: `A34#83`).

### 3.10 Refunds and support
- **Refunds go through the stores:** Apple's `beginRefundRequest` and the Play refund flow. We never argue refunds (users show: 21 "refund refused", mean 1.10).
- **Goodwill:** support can grant Plus by promo code (Apple offer codes, Play promo codes) or by a server-side grant on the account.
- **Support tool:** look up an account by transaction ID or purchase token, see every purchase, notification and grant, and re-run verification.
- **No promo claims** that can't be redeemed in the app (users show: `P18#1197`).

### 3.11 Native billing plus our server check (decided 26 Sep 2026)

**Decision:** use the stores' own billing (StoreKit 2 on Apple, Play Billing on Google). Our server only verifies and records purchases, so they work across platforms. **No RevenueCat.**

**Why:** our apps sell one-time purchases, not subscriptions. For a one-time purchase, the stores already handle payment, proof of purchase, restore on any device with the same Apple ID or Google account, Family Sharing, pending payments and refunds. RevenueCat's real value is subscription machinery and cross-store charts, which we don't need, so its 1% fee buys very little. 

**The flow:**

| Step | Signed out | Signed in |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | store purchase sheet; our account ID attached (`appAccountToken` / `obfuscatedAccountId`) when there is one | same |
| Check it's genuine | the store's own signed record, checked on the device (StoreKit 2 verification; Play purchase state) | **also** sent to our server: `POST /v1/purchases/verify` with the signed transaction (Apple) or purchase token (Google) |
| Server verifies | — | Apple: check Apple's signature chain (Apple Root CA G3), no call to Apple needed. Google: one call to the Play Developer API to confirm the purchase and its state |
| Grant Plus | at once on this device | recorded on the **account**, then synced to every device the person signs into, **on iPhone and Android** |
| Acknowledge (Google) | the app acknowledges right after granting | same; the server acknowledges too as a backup |
| Restore | ask the store on every launch (`Transaction.currentEntitlements` / `queryPurchasesAsync`) | sign in; the account already has it |

**Refunds, cancellations and failures:**
- **Apple refund or Family Sharing revoked:** App Store Server Notifications V2 (`REFUND`, `REVOKE`) reach `/v1/hooks/apple`. The server removes Plus from the account on every platform. On the phone, StoreKit's `Transaction.updates` reports the revocation too.
- **Google refund or cancelled order:** Real-time developer notifications for one-time products reach `/v1/hooks/play` through Pub/Sub. As a backstop, a daily job reads the **Voided Purchases API** in case a notification was missed. The app also sees the purchase disappear from `queryPurchasesAsync`.
- **Payment pending or failed** (Google pending, Apple Ask to Buy): nothing is granted until the store says it's paid. The app shows "Waiting for payment", and grants the moment the store confirms.
- **Our check fails** (server down, store API timeout): **never remove Plus.** The store's on-device record stands, and the server retries (rule 4, "never downgrade on doubt"). Only a confirmed refund or revocation removes it.
- **A purchase that doesn't verify** (forged or wrong app): not recorded on the account. The device keeps whatever the store itself says.
- **What removal takes away:** Plus extras only. Data is never touched (rule 7).

**What we give up:** revenue charts across both stores (App Store Connect and the Play Console each report their own sales) and remote paywall testing. Neither is needed for a lifetime purchase.

**Maintenance:** the store's one-time purchase flow rarely changes. The recurring job is Google's yearly required Billing Library update (for example, version 8 was required for updates from 31 Aug 2026). That update would be needed with RevenueCat too.

**Implementation notes:**
- **Apple verification in a Worker:**
  - verify the JWS signature chain against Apple Root CA G3 with WebCrypto (ES256);
  - the App Store Server API (an ES256 JWT signed by our in-app purchase key) is only needed for lookups, such as finding a purchase from an order ID a user sends to support.
- **Google:**
  - a service-account JWT (RS256) to get an OAuth token, then `purchases.productsv2` to verify, and the Voided Purchases API for the daily refund check;
  - RTDN via a Pub/Sub push subscription to our Worker (Pub/Sub has a free tier).

## 5. Test checklist

1. **Buy Plus and tap "Not now":** unlocked at once on that device. Reinstall: unlocked on first launch with no button pressed. Airplane mode: still unlocked. Sync and backup show as off.
1a. **Buy Plus and tap Continue with Apple:** the account is created, the purchase is linked on the server, local habits upload, and exactly one confirmation email is sent.
1b. **Tablet without Plus:** opens to the Plus screen; "I already have Plus" restores or signs in. **Tablet with Plus on the same Apple ID:** unlocks automatically and asks to sign in to bring habits.
1c. **Free tier:** the habit list shows "n of 5 free habits" as plain secondary text, with no prompt before the limit; a 6th habit shows the Plus screen once, with Not now; existing habits and all widgets keep working; no sign-in exists anywhere.
2. **Buy Plus signed in on iPhone:** sign in on Android and Plus is there. Sign in on the web later: Plus is there.
3. **Refund Plus** (sandbox): the notification arrives, extras are removed, data untouched, and a kind message is shown.
4. **Plus Family:** the owner buys on iPhone, invites by link; a member on Android signs in, joins, and has Plus at once on Android and the web. The owner sees the member's name, never their habits.
4a. **Invite edge cases:** an expired code, a used code, a cancelled code and a sixth person each show a clear message; nobody is charged or changed.
4b. **Leave and remove:** a member leaves; Plus extras stop, and every habit stays. A member who owns Plus keeps it. The owner re-invites someone else into the freed seat.
4c. **Owner refund** (sandbox): extras stop for the owner and all members, with a message; data untouched.
4d. **Upgrade:** a Plus owner buys the upgrade and gets 5 seats; nobody is charged twice.
4e. **Owner deletes the account:** members get the notice, keep Plus for 30 days, then fall back to free with all data.
5. **Play purchase left `PENDING`:** "waiting" is shown; it unlocks on `PURCHASED`; it is acknowledged within minutes, and the alert fires if not.
6. **Server down and store slow on launch:** the last known entitlements are kept; no paywall is shown to a payer.
7. **Update from any past build:** the entitlement is unchanged (added to the migration tests).
8. **Free widgets:** a free user adds home-screen and lock-screen widgets and ticks habits from them; nothing asks for Plus.
