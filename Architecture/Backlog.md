## Decided so far

| Date | Decision | From |
|---|---|---|
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Suggest signing in both after 3 days of use *and* at key moments** (5th habit, a purchase, a phone move), **at most once a week** | 01 Accounts §3.2 |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **After a premium purchase, always suggest linking an account** ("Keep Plus on every phone you own"). Never forced | 01 Accounts, 02 Billing §3.2 |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Native billing (StoreKit 2, Play Billing), no RevenueCat.** Our apps sell lifetime purchases only. Signed-in purchases are sent to our server, verified and recorded on the account, so they unlock on iPhone and Android. Refunds, cancellations and failed payments reach the server through store notifications (plus a daily Google voided-purchases check) and remove Plus everywhere. Revisit RevenueCat only if a subscription is ever added | 02 Billing §3.11 (was #6) |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Kotlin Multiplatform shared core + native UI + documented behavior + shared fixtures.** One Kotlin implementation for days, schedules, streaks, progress and merge rules. SwiftUI stays native; web and the initial Windows PWA use Kotlin/JS. Platform services remain adapters. Follow established production references and dedicated KMP support paths | [Accepted decision](<Shared Core Decision.md>), [research](<Shared Core Research.md>), 07 Surfaces §9 (was #15) |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Email OTP login and purchase confirmations at launch.** No passwords; guest purchases require no email; confirmation follows server verification and never blocks Plus. Templates and authentication stay in our backend, with a replaceable sender for future Cloudflare migration. Initial provider remains open | [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>), 01 Accounts §3.3/3.8, 02 Billing (resolves launch scope from #3) |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **One sync system: ours. No CloudKit sync for people without an account.** Signed-out users keep the full app, offline, with nothing on our server and automatic backups to their own iCloud or Google Drive; live sync between devices needs a one-tap sign-in. Reviews: iPad reviews are 0.56% of App Store reviews but mention paying ×2.1 (×2.6 when about sync), and the top iPad complaint is iPhone ⇄ iPad not syncing (106); refusal of servers is small and aimed at *forced* sign-up (86, 1.42★) and data selling; only 6 of 1,712 reviews want iCloud *instead of* an account, while iCloud can't cover different Apple IDs or Android + iPad and iCloud-synced apps drew 52 sync-failure reviews. Follow-ups: backups kept per device (03 §3.8), iPad treated as a first-class synced surface and a sign-in line that answers the privacy objection (07 §7). Revisit only if many iPad installs pick "Use on its own" and never sign in (measure with #23) | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/iPad Sync and Server Trust — Backlog 5.md>), 03 Backup §6 (was #5) |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **Plus Family through our own family groups, not Apple Family Sharing.** A one-time Plus Family purchase covers the buyer plus up to 5 people they invite by link or code, each on their own account and their own habits (private), on iPhone, Android and web. Plain Plus stays one person; owners can upgrade for the difference. Apple Family Sharing stays off on every product (it can't be turned off once on). Reviews: families without a family option stay free (18) rather than buy per person (3); 86 bought because of a family option; the failure to avoid is sharing with no in-app invite (Habit Tracker, 77 reviews, 2.6★) | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Family Sharing — Backlog 1.md>), 02 Billing §3.6 (was #1) |
