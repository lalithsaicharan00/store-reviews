## Decided so far

| Date | Decision | From |
|---|---|---|
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Native billing (StoreKit 2, Play Billing), no RevenueCat.** Our apps sell lifetime purchases only. Signed-in purchases are sent to our server, verified and recorded on the account, so they unlock on iPhone and Android. Refunds, cancellations and failed payments reach the server through store notifications (plus a daily Google voided-purchases check) and remove Plus everywhere. Revisit RevenueCat only if a subscription is ever added | 02 Billing §3.11 (was #6) |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **Kotlin Multiplatform shared core + native UI + documented behavior + shared fixtures.** One Kotlin implementation for days, schedules, streaks, progress and merge rules. SwiftUI stays native; web and the initial Windows PWA use Kotlin/JS. Platform services remain adapters. Follow established production references and dedicated KMP support paths | [Accepted decision](<Shared Core Decision.md>), [research](<Shared Core Research.md>), 07 Surfaces §9 (was #15) |
| 26 Sep 2026 | **One sync system: ours. No CloudKit sync.** Live sync between devices goes through the account. Reviews: iPad reviews mention paying ×2.1 (×2.6 when about sync), and the top iPad complaint is iPhone ⇄ iPad not syncing (106); refusal of servers is small and aimed at *forced* sign-up (86, 1.42★) and data selling; iCloud can't cover different Apple IDs or Android + iPad, and iCloud-synced apps drew 52 sync-failure reviews | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/iPad Sync and Server Trust — Backlog 5.md>) (was #5) |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **Free is one phone; Plus is everything else.** Free: 5 habits (fixed forever) on an iPhone or Android phone, local-only, **no account**. Plus (one-time, lifetime): unlimited habits, iPad and Android tablets, Apple Watch and Wear OS, web and Mac later, sync across devices and platforms, and automatic backup on our server. Reviews: lifetime wording is in 15.8% of "I paid" reviews (3.7× the next reason); payers cluster in multi-device and iPad sync; nobody objects to a tablet being paid (58 complain their paid Plus didn't reach the tablet); Watch as Plus draws 1 objection in 88 | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus Scope and Account at Purchase.md>), 02 Billing §3.1 |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **Accounts are for Plus only, created right after purchase.** Buy first, then "One last step: turn on sync and backup" with Continue with Apple / Google and a visible "Not now: use Plus on this device only" link (Apple 5.1.1(v) forbids requiring registration for a non-account purchase). Plus Family: sign in to invite, no skip. **No sign-in nudges.** After "Not now", only features ask (tablet first screen, Settings → Backup). Sign-in is **Apple and Google only**; email codes removed | 01 Accounts §1, §3.2; 02 Billing §3.2 (was #4) |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **No backup to the user's own cloud.** No iCloud (CloudKit) or Google Drive copies, folder auto-export, reinstall marker or backup card. Free users: on-device snapshots, the phone's own backup and export/import. Plus: our server. Reviews: 87 refuse servers (6 say they paid); 131 ask for Drive backup (1 says they paid) | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus Scope and Account at Purchase.md>), 03 Backup §6 |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **One email, ever: the purchase confirmation,** to a Plus buyer with an account (the Apple or Google email). No sign-in codes, marketing, reminders, deletion or incident emails; family invites are shared by the owner as a link, QR or code | [Email Delivery Decision](<Email Delivery Decision.md>), 01 Accounts §3.8 |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **Email provider: Resend.** Official Cloudflare Workers example; its free tier (3,000/month, 100/day) far exceeds one email per purchase. Cloudflare's own sending stays a later option | [Email Delivery Decision §4](<Email Delivery Decision.md>) (was #3) |
| 27 Sep 2026 | **Plus Family through our own family groups, not Apple Family Sharing.** A one-time Plus Family purchase covers the buyer plus up to 5 people they invite by link or code, each on their own account and their own habits (private), on iPhone, Android and web. Plain Plus stays one person; owners can upgrade for the difference. Apple Family Sharing stays off on every product (it can't be turned off once on). Reviews: families without a family option stay free (18) rather than buy per person (3); 86 bought because of a family option; the failure to avoid is sharing with no in-app invite (Habit Tracker, 77 reviews, 2.6★) | [Research](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Family Sharing — Backlog 1.md>), 02 Billing §3.6 (was #1) |
