# 03. Backup and Restore

*Written by Claude (Claude Code), 26 Sep 2026. Topic 3 of the [architecture list](README.md). A proposal, not yet built.*

*Rewritten 27 Sep 2026 (was "Backup Without Our Account").*
- **Free users:** local-only, with no account. They are protected by on-device snapshots, the phone's own backup and export/import.
- **Plus users with an account:** also get automatic backup on our server.
- **Removed:** our own copies in iCloud (CloudKit) and Google Drive, the folder auto-export, the reinstall marker, and the "where should your backup go" card. Evidence: the "my own cloud" group is small and rarely pays ([Plus Scope and Account at Purchase §2](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus Scope and Account at Purchase.md>)).

**Scope:** how every user's data is protected on the phone and restored:
- local snapshots;
- the phone's own backup and transfer;
- export and import;
- restore.

The server copy for Plus is in [Data Safety §5](<Data Safety, Accounts and Sync.md>) and topic 6.

**How each rule is backed:**
- **Users show**: backed by review evidence.
- **Platform rule**: an Apple or Google requirement, with a link.
- **First principles**: reasoned from how the system works.

**Evidence:**
- **A fresh screen:** 1,487,223 reviews, 14 backup patterns in about 12 languages, 3,901 matches.
- **262 read in full and coded,** with quotes verified.
- **Reused:** the Data Safety report (section B) and ledger C020.
- **Files:** [`Backup Evidence/`](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/Backup Evidence/>).

## 1. The design on one page

1. **Automatic on-device snapshots for everyone:**
   - a compressed copy of all data, kept as 7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly copies, plus one before every risky moment;
   - no setup, and never a chore.
2. **The app always shows the honest state:**
   - free: "Saved on this phone · included in your phone's backup";
   - Plus with an account: "Backed up · 2 min ago";
   - a snapshot counts only once it has been read back and checked.
3. **Restore previews first and never destroys:**
   - it shows the date and what's inside;
   - it snapshots the current data first, so the restore can be undone;
   - the user chooses Replace or Merge;
   - it works from any app version.
4. **An open file format for export and import:** a zip with JSON + CSV and a documented manifest. It can be imported on iPhone and Android, and read in Excel. It is the free user's portable copy and the way to move iPhone ⇄ Android without an account.
5. **Plus with an account:** automatic backup on our server, on top of the snapshots, with no action needed (topic 5 and 6).
6. **Free, unlimited, no cooldowns:** snapshots, restore, export and import are never paid, never rate-limited, and never tied to a device.
7. **The phone's own backup and transfer must include us:** iCloud device backup, Quick Start, Android Auto Backup, device-to-device transfer and Samsung Smart Switch, with the device's identity excluded.
8. **No nagging:** there are no backup reminders. The only alerts are real failures (the phone is full, a snapshot can't be written).

## 3. The design in detail

### 3.1 Where copies live

| # | Copy | Free | Plus with account | Setup | Can we see its status? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Local snapshots** (on the phone) | ✅ | ✅ | none | ✅ |
| 2 | **Phone's own backup / transfer**: iCloud device backup and Quick Start; Android Auto Backup (25 MB), device-to-device transfer, Smart Switch | ✅ | ✅ | none (the user's phone settings) | ❌ no public API; we detect it *after* a restore (§3.5) |
| 3 | **Export file** (manual) | ✅ | ✅ | none | – |
| 4 | **Our server** (live copy, 30-day point-in-time recovery, nightly snapshots) | – | ✅ | the account step after purchase | ✅ |

### 3.2 What a snapshot and an export are
- **A file named `YYYY-MM-DD-HHmm.habits.zip`** containing:
  - `manifest.json`: format version, app version, platform, created at, record counts per table, and a SHA-256 of the data;
  - `data.json`: every habit, schedule version, check-in, note, part of day, setting, **archived and recently-deleted items**, and attachments;
  - `csv/`: one readable CSV per table, using **local calendar dates**.
- **The format is documented** in the repo and is **backward-readable forever:** every app version can read every older format. Tests keep a sample of each format.
- **No encryption beyond the phone's own.** A lost key would mean lost data (topic 1 evidence).

### 3.3 When snapshots run
- **After meaningful changes:**
  - check-ins trigger a snapshot at most every 6 hours;
  - habit edits and deletes trigger one within 5 minutes, because they are riskier.
- **Nightly:** a scheduled task (iOS `BGProcessingTask`, Android `WorkManager`) runs if nothing ran that day.
- **Before risky moments:** before every app update's database migration, before a restore, before signing in or out, and before "delete all data".
- **Retention:** 7 daily, 4 weekly and 6 monthly. Pruning never deletes the newest good snapshot.
- **Stored** in `Application Support` (iOS) or app files (Android), so the phone's own backup carries them (§3.7).

### 3.4 Showing the state
- **A snapshot ledger in the local database:** one row per attempt, with time, size, counts, checksum and result. A snapshot counts as good only after it is re-read and its checksum matches (users show: false "success", `P65#7648`).
- **Settings → Backup shows:**
  - Free: "Saved on this phone · last snapshot today 09:14 · 86 habits · included in your phone's backup (check your phone's settings)", with [Export a copy] and "Snapshots" (restore to any point).
  - Plus with an account: "Backed up · 2 min ago".
  - Plus without an account: "Backed up only on this phone · Turn on backup" (one tap, Apple or Google; topic 1).
- **Alerts only for real failures,** once each: "Your phone is full. Changes can't be saved.", "Snapshots can't be written". **Nothing is shown when things work** (users show: nag complaints, `A31#4681`).

### 3.5 Detecting that the phone's own backup restored us
- **The device ID lives outside backups:** Keychain "this device only" on iOS, the `no_backup` directory on Android. The database keeps its own copy.
- **On launch:** if the database exists but its device ID doesn't match this device's, the data came from a phone backup or transfer.
- **Then we show:** "Welcome back: your habits came across from your old phone (last change 3 Oct 21:40)." We register as a new device, keep all the data, and take a fresh snapshot.

### 3.6 Restore
1. **Pick a source:** a snapshot on this phone (listed by date), or a file.
2. **Preview:** the date, the device it came from, and counts, compared with what's on this phone now.
3. **A snapshot of current data is taken automatically:** "Undo restore" stays available for 30 days.
4. **Choose:**
   - **Replace:** the phone becomes exactly the backup;
   - **Merge:** combine by IDs; the newer edit wins; nothing is deleted.
5. **Validate before committing:** parse the file, check the checksum and counts against the manifest, and migrate old formats forward. **If anything doesn't match, stop. Nothing is changed** (users show: a partial restore then synced, `P84#10009`).
6. **Signed-in users:** the restored data syncs as ordinary edits only after the user confirms. A restore can't silently overwrite the server copy.
- **It works across devices, platforms and app versions.** Never paywalled.

### 3.7 The phone's own backups must include us (platform rule)
- **iOS:**
  - the database and snapshots live in `Application Support` (backed up, and kept on offload), never in `Caches`;
  - the device ID and tokens are excluded;
  - test Quick Start and an iCloud restore on every major iOS release.
- **Android:**
  - `allowBackup="true"`, plus `dataExtractionRules` with **both** `cloud-backup` and `device-transfer` sections including the database and the newest snapshot, and excluding the device ID and tokens (`no_backup`);
  - keep the total under 25 MB;
  - test on Pixel device-to-device transfer **and Samsung Smart Switch** (users show Smart Switch misses: `P84#8853`, `P126#11004`).
- **Low storage:** a write that fails for lack of space is reported ("Your phone is full. Changes can't be saved.") and **never** truncates or resets the database (users show: `N2#14395`).

### 3.8 Plus users with an account
- **Our server is primary** (topics 5 and 6): the live copy, 30-day point-in-time recovery, and nightly R2 snapshots.
- **Local snapshots keep running** as an independent net on every device.

## 4. Platform facts used

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| iOS backs up `Documents/` and `Library/Application Support/`, not `Caches/` or `tmp/`; `isExcludedFromBackup` excludes a file | [Apple: iCloud backup](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/foundation/optimizing-your-app-s-data-for-icloud-backup) |
| Android Auto Backup: 25 MB, about daily, only the latest copy kept, databases included by default; `dataExtractionRules` separates cloud and device-transfer | [Android Auto Backup](https://developer.android.com/identity/data/autobackup) |
| There is **no public API** to read whether iCloud device backup or Android Auto Backup is on, or when it last ran | First principles (none exists in either SDK). This is why §3.5 detects restores instead |

## 5. Test checklist
1. **Fresh iPhone, free:** a snapshot appears within 5 minutes of the first habit and is verified, and Settings shows it.
2. **Delete and reinstall, then restore the phone's iCloud backup:** everything returns, including archived habits and notes, and the "came across" message shows.
3. **Android device-to-device transfer and Samsung Smart Switch:** the data arrives, the "came across from your old phone" message shows, and it registers as a new device.
4. **Restore a snapshot or file from 3 app versions ago,** and import an iPhone export file on Android.
5. **A corrupted file:** a clear error and no change to current data. Replace, then Undo restore, returns exactly the prior state.
6. **Merge restore:** no duplicates; the newer edits win; nothing is deleted.
7. **Low storage during a write:** a clear message, and the database is intact after freeing space.
8. **Plus with an account:** Settings shows "Backed up · just now" after a check-in; turning off the network shows the last successful time, with no alarm for short outages.
9. **Free tier:** no backup reminder, card or cloud option appears anywhere.

## 6. Decided and removed
- **Decided 27 Sep 2026: no backup to the user's own cloud.** We don't build copies in iCloud (CloudKit) or Google Drive, a folder auto-export, a reinstall marker (iCloud key-value store, Block Store), or a "where should your backup go" card.
  - Why: the group that wants its own cloud is small and rarely pays. 87 refuse servers (6 say they paid); 131 ask for Drive (1 says they paid).
  - The phone's own backup plus export covers free users. The account covers Plus.
  - Evidence: [Plus Scope and Account at Purchase](<../Research/Research Reports/Business Model and Monetization/Plus Scope and Account at Purchase.md>), [Sign-in Prompts and the Backup Guarantee — Backlog 4](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/Sign-in Prompts and the Backup Guarantee — Backlog 4.md>).
- **Decided 26 Sep 2026: no CloudKit sync.** One sync system, ours ([iPad Sync and Server Trust — Backlog 5](<../Research/Research Reports/Data, Sync and Accounts/iPad Sync and Server Trust — Backlog 5.md>)).
