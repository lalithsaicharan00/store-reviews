## The questions

1. What do Android habit-app payers actually pay for? Cross-check the earlier machine counts (lifetime 17.1%, no ads
   6.6%, sync/devices 5.6% …) by reading payer reviews by hand.
2. Same method on the App Store, side by side: is it true iOS payers pay for Watch / iPad / devices more? (The earlier
   "Plus Scope" table, 4.3% devices and 1.7% Watch, was BOTH stores combined, 14,034 payers.)
3. "Would pay for X" statements on each store.
4. People who use both platforms: switching, paying twice, features missing on one platform (parity). How many, how
   angry, what they expect.
5. What Android habit apps sell in premium today, and at what price (web: Play listings / sites).
6. Market facts: Android tablets, Wear OS, Play vs App Store spending, Play billing rules (one-time products,
   cross-store entitlements, family sharing of in-app purchases).
7. So what is Android Plus, and how different can it be from iOS Plus without problems?

## Citation format

0-based line in its reviews.jsonl (checked: A1#272 = 14076814177, as in Plus Scope's appendix). Every cited ID is
