# Web facts checked 11 Oct 2026 (for the Android Plus report)

- **Google Play Family Library does not share in-app purchases.** Google Play Help: "You can't share in-app purchases
  and apps downloaded at no charge with your family members." (https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7007852).
  Only paid apps/games are shareable (developer agreement, 2016 coverage: androidpolice, gamesbeat). Subscriptions not
  mentioned on that page. → Plus Family (Apple: Family Sharing) has no equivalent on Play for a one-time in-app Plus.
- **Google does not require parity across platforms**: "We do not require parity across platforms. You can create
  different versions of your app to support different platforms, features, and pricing models." Consumption-only apps
  may let users "access content paid for somewhere else." Unlocking a purchase from another store inside an app that
  also sells via Play Billing is not addressed directly on the FAQ page → confirm at build time.
  (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818)
- **Apple 3.1.3(b) Multiplatform Services**: apps "may allow users to access content, subscriptions, or features they
  have acquired in your app on other platforms or your web site … provided those items are also available as in-app
  purchases within the app." (https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)
- **Store spending 2025 (Sensor Tower via Business of Apps, secondary)**: App Store $117.6B, Google Play $49.2B
  (≈70/30). Downloads split not found. Treat as estimates.
- **Smartwatches 2025 (Counterpoint, secondary)**: Apple 23%, Huawei 17%, Xiaomi 9%, Samsung 7%; Wear OS shipments
  declined YoY in Q3 2025 (Samsung −12% in 2025). No reliable Wear OS share found.
- **Competitors (not authorities, W2/Research CLAUDE.md)**: HabitNow (Android) premium is a one-time purchase (price
  unconfirmed, AlternativeTo says $11), premium = more capacity + cloud backup, restored by Google account.
  Habitify sells one purchase that unlocks Premium on every platform (App Store listing; iOS, Android, Web, Mac),
  lifetime $59.99–$89.99 depending on source/date; it runs its own accounts/servers.
