# Eden — Daily Routine Planner (App Store ID 1589243137) — Full Review Analysis

**App:** Eden: Daily Routine Planner — *Self care habit tracker, to do*
**Developer of record in the corpus metadata:** Millefeuille Agency · **Bundle:** `com.mfagency.nurture`
**Corpus:** 3,837 written reviews · 87 storefronts · 6 November 2021 → 6 September 2026
**Corpus mean rating:** 4.570 · **Extracted:** 8 September 2026 (`itunes MZStore userReviewsRow`)
**Analysis date:** 11 September 2026
**Business model as reviewers experience it:** free download, hard cap on free habits (reported as 3→5→6 over time), free garden growth ceiling at ~17%, then monthly / annual / lifetime in-app purchase. No ads. Model changed from one-time-unlock to subscription+lifetime in late 2022.

---

## How to read this

- Every quantified finding gives **count, percentage, denominator, scope, period, signal label, and review IDs**. Any claim traces back to the exact record via `review_id` in `reviews.jsonl`.
- Theme counts are **non-exclusive** — one review can carry several themes.
- Signal thresholds used throughout: `<0.1%` ignore · `0.1–0.5%` weak · `0.5–1%` emerging · `1–3%` meaningful · `3–5%` very strong · `>5%` high-priority.
- **Facts** are what reviewers wrote. **Interpretation** is labelled as such.

### ⚠️ Eight warnings before any number here is used

1. **This app rewards reviews with cosmetic unlocks, and reviewers say so in writing.** **13 reviews (0.34%, weak signal, mean 4.77)** state outright that they wrote the review to unlock a background, wallpaper, theme, song or flower: "they said if I write this review I'd get to change the look of my flower" (`12132143279`), "I'm just doing this to get the backgrounds 😋 I haven't even used the app at all" (`12272181443`), "they're encouraging me to do this so I can unlock backgrounds" (`13061382481`), "great (I'm doing this for rewards)" (`11882600620`), "i'm just doing dis for the wallpapers" (`11659462958`), "devo cuorarla per avere un certo colore di sfondo" (`13025206367`), plus `8304651472`, `11284478723`, `11717197486`, `12520666753`, `12984118431`. One reviewer pre-emptively writes "(I'm NOT a bot btw ❤️🫰)" (`13965948642`). **0.34% is the floor — only reviewers who volunteered the fact are counted.** This is a rating-inflation mechanism, and it is the single most important methodological fact in this corpus.
2. **Short reviews nearly doubled as a share of the corpus, and they are almost all 5★.** Reviews with a body of ≤25 characters rise from **20.9% of 2021–22 (87/416) and 16.5% of 2023 (90/547) to 35.0% of 2024 (532/1,521), 35.7% of 2025 (369/1,034) and 32.3% of 2026 (103/319)**. Between 83% and 91% of them are 5★ in every era. **Every aggregate below is therefore also restated for "substantive" reviews (body > 60 characters, n = 1,791), and the two diverge.** See Part 8.
3. **The improvement after 2023 is nevertheless real, not purely an artefact.** Restricting to substantive reviews only, mean rating still rises from **4.175 (2023, n=343) to 4.512 (2024, n=633)**, and monetisation friction still falls from **27.70% to 19.43%** of substantive reviews. Both the prompt effect and the product improvement are present; neither explains the other away.
4. **Theme rates are not equally comparable across languages.** The classifier is regex-based with Russian, Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Turkish, Vietnamese and Japanese patterns, but coverage is thin for Chinese, Korean, Thai, Arabic and Polish. Where a language's coverage is thin a theme is *under*-counted, never over-counted. The clearest artefact: `praise_aesthetic` reads 3.12% in China against 46–48% in Italy/Germany/Turkey — that is a pattern-coverage gap, not a real difference. **Country comparisons below lead with rating distributions, which are language-neutral.**
5. **Classifier error is measured, not assumed.** The free-tier-cap classifier was audited on a random 45-review sample: **3 false positives (6.7%)**. A recall probe on 35 unmatched reviews containing a paywall token found **~2–3 missed true mentions (~7%)**. Seven smaller negative themes (`flower_not_growing`, `all_or_nothing`, `no_backfill_midnight`, `no_stats_history`, `music_cannot_disable`, `support_unreachable`, `custom_habits_missing`) were **manually verified record-by-record across all matches**, and 32 false positives were removed. Counts for those seven are exact for the matched set; they remain floors with respect to recall.
6. **No `version` field exists in this corpus.** Release attribution is inferred from dates and from what reviewers say ("after the update", "с обновлением"). It is never asserted from metadata.
7. **The corpus is volume-uneven and has a collection gap.** 2024 alone is 39.6% of all records. December 2025 (13) and January 2026 (10) are anomalously low against neighbouring months (Nov 2025 = 67, Feb 2026 = 43). Treat late-2025/early-2026 rates as unreliable; do not read that dip as a product event.
8. **"Paid evidence" is self-selecting.** 72 reviewers (1.88%) state explicitly that they paid, bought or subscribed. That group skews toward both evangelism and transaction regret, and it is not a sample of paying customers. Never infer a conversion rate from it.

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**1. Eden is an aesthetics-and-calm product first, and that is an unusually dominant, unusually durable moat.**
**1,068 reviews (27.83%, high-priority, mean 4.64)** praise the design/aesthetic; **559 (14.57%, mean 4.68)** the music and ambience; **411 (10.71%, mean 4.70)** the simplicity; **229 (5.97%, mean 4.68)** the garden-growth metaphor as motivation. These four are the largest themes in the corpus by a wide margin and they are **stable across all five years** (aesthetic 28.4% → 28.9% → 26.0% → 27.8% → 34.2%). Evidence: `8788448563`, `9062177143`, `9945145056`, `10023340564`, `11064426818`, `12157437413`, `13024796099`, `13291395738`, `14068662243`. The recurring sentence is that other trackers are too busy: "A lot of apps in this area are too busy… This app is like a warm hug" (`9945145056`).

**2. The free-tier gate is the single largest negative fact, and it is mostly a *trial-design* problem, not a price problem.**
**436 reviews (11.36%, high-priority, mean 3.42)** carry some monetisation-gating theme; they are **63 of the 109 one-star reviews (57.8%)** and **196 of the 404 one-to-three-star reviews (48.5%)**. Within that: the numeric habit cap is **148 reviews (3.86%, very strong, mean 3.51)**, general paywall friction **281 (7.32%, high-priority, mean 3.55)**. But outright **price objection is only 29 reviews (0.76%, emerging)**, while **57 reviews (1.49%, mean 4.18) actively defend the price as fair or cheap** and **45 (1.17%, mean 4.49) praise the free tier as generous**. Evidence for the cap: `7996943615`, `8233610259`, `8505526778`, `9467741672`, `10682566057`, `11005479210`, `12298596229`, `12514072590`, `13253044306`, `13819382131`, `14371694907`. **Interpretation: reviewers are not saying Eden is too expensive; they are saying the free tier ends before they can judge it.**

**3. The free growth ceiling reads to users as a bug, not as a paywall — and that is a distinct, fixable reputational problem.**
**50 verified reviews (1.30%, meaningful, mean 2.96)** report the flower or garden not growing. **17 of them name the same number — 17%** — spanning **April 2022 → December 2025**, mean rating 3.00: `8554382470`, `8935137072`, `9316992889`, `9339556961`, `9543524696`, `9551243735`, `9634054918`, `9715657325`, `9791630979`, `9987971914`, `10424061096`, `11114333413`, `11311247050`, `11543624190`, `11697507657`, `13116410045`, `13464035283`. A further 10 name 2%/3%/6%. Reviewers file these as defects — "Glitches 🙄" (`9987971914`), "Awaria" (`13464035283`), "Bug" (`10370754063`), "Fake Flowers" (`13523628780`) — and several say support told them it was intentional (`8571919972`: "这是开发者回我的解释"). One reviewer states the confusion exactly: "I thought even with the free version you could grow at least 1 flower all the way" (`9791630979`). **Interpretation: the free tier silently stops a visible progress bar without explaining why. Users conclude the app is broken, then say so publicly.** A one-line in-app explanation converts a 1–3★ "it's broken" review into a legible upgrade prompt. This is the cheapest high-value fix in the corpus.

**4. The all-or-nothing reward rule is the sharpest *design* critique, and it comes from engaged users.**
**20 verified reviews (0.52%, emerging, mean 2.80)**: the garden only grows if *every* habit is checked that day, so partial completion earns nothing. The argument is consistently behavioural, not petulant: "If I miss only one out of 6 of my habits, nothing happens. Behaviourally this means people will say it's not worth trying to do any of the habits" (`12599605564`); "выполнение 90% задуманного должно поощряться, а не наоборот" (`10748238279`); "this goes against self growth as perfection isn't possible everyday" (`12023585537`); "No room for off days… I'm human" (`8885523614`). Evidence also `8807578550`, `10259102511`, `10573227883`, `10646722648`, `11911619291`, `12251741381`, `12811356742`, `12124622329`, `14317196415`. Note the counter-evidence: **25 reviews (0.65%, mean 4.76)** praise Eden precisely for being gentle and streak-free (`10056813574`, `12393951903`, `13781551382`, `13823192383`). **Interpretation: the gentleness is a positioning win; the all-or-nothing gate contradicts it.** Partial credit reconciles the two.

**5. A visible engineering turnaround happened in 2024, and the data supports crediting it.**
Rate per era, all reviews: **crash/launch failure 3.37% (2021-22) → 2.01% (2023) → 0.13% (2024) → 0.19% (2025)**; **"music can't be turned off" 2.88% (2021-22) → 0.00% (2023) → 0.07% (2024)**; mean rating **4.339 → 4.346 → 4.675**; 1★ share **7.0% → 4.9% → 1.5%**. The music complaint cluster is entirely 2022 (`8655424484`, `8807243801`, `8868778397`, `8899067436`, `8902349870`, `9116351698`, `9121762212`, `9241525509`, `9263941862`, `9300714052`) and then stops. A calendar/history feature also appears to have shipped around late 2024: a reviewer writes "Спасибо, что добавили календарь" (`12244017370`, Jan 2025) and a 2026 reviewer praises "аналитика прошлых привычек" (`14227614235`). **Interpretation: the 2022-era reliability and audio bugs were genuinely fixed. The monetisation complaints were not.**

**6. Paid reviewers' most common problem is losing what they bought.**
Among the **72 explicit payers (1.88%, mean 3.56)**, the top failure is **purchase lost or not delivered: 12/72 = 16.7% (segment rate; global count 24, 0.63%)** — `9091405863`, `9100759613`, `9110825123`, `9125973744`, `9171397848`, `9201257502`, `11035873886`, `11261841935`, `11704889459`, `11998240319`, `12455426263`, `12754805799`. Second is **trial/billing dispute: 6/72 = 8.3% (global 23, 0.60%, mean 1.48 — the lowest-rated theme in the corpus)**. The billing cluster names one mechanism repeatedly: a "3-day free trial" that charges immediately — `10909221823`, `11504294876`, `12370651672`, `12404082975`, `11234070366`, `13526453417`, `12128361462`. Two reviewers could not find any way to unsubscribe (`11617459927`, `11858690687`).

**7. The late-2022 repricing broke a cohort of lifetime buyers and they said so publicly.**
Price points in the corpus move from **$5–$7 / €6–€7 one-time (2021 → mid-2022)** to **$25 lifetime / $19.99 yr / $4.99 mo (from late 2022)** to **€40 (2026, `14074701122`)**. Seven reviewers in Sept–Oct 2022 report that the unlock they had already bought was withdrawn: "most of the features I had access to (which I paid for) were no longer accessible… now I would have to pay a subscription fee" (`9091405863`); "Mit den neuen App-Versionen und Preiserhöhungen wurde mir die erworbene Premiumversion wieder entzogen… Ich empfinde das als Vertragsbruch" (`9171397848`); also `9199616690`, `9100759613`, `9125973744`, `9126251428`, `9201257502`. The grievance recurs years later (`10780468727`, `12754805799`, `12116109329`). **Interpretation: this is a trust liability with a long tail, and it is why "already paid, asked to pay again" is the top paid-user complaint.**

**8. Russia is 19.2% of the corpus and behaves differently from every Western market.**
**736 reviews, mean 4.697, 85.3% 5★ — the highest satisfaction of any eligible market** — with monetisation friction at **7.34% against 14.29% (US), 19.83% (DE), 20.37% (FR), 18.52% (AU)**. But Russia supplies the corpus's highest rate of **missing statistics/history requests (2.58% vs 1.04% global)** and a distinct payment-rail problem: of the 16 "cannot complete purchase" reviews (0.42%), 8 are Russian and describe the buy button being unresponsive or cards being unlinkable (`10454824450`, `10635690558`, `11145405840`, `12774568544`, `13926278152`, `8478822922`, `10156327013`, `11704889459`). **Interpretation: Eden's largest non-US market is willing to pay and structurally unable to.** That is an addressable revenue leak, not a satisfaction problem.

**9. The biggest unshipped features, in evidence order:** statistics/history/calendar (**40 verified, 1.04%, meaningful**) → partial-credit growth (**20, 0.52%**) → backfill and a configurable day-boundary (**29, 0.76%**) → habit reordering / per-habit gardens (**18, 0.47%**) → flexible recurrence, e.g. "3× per week" (**12, 0.31%**) → Apple Watch (**8, 0.21%**) → multi-count-per-day for water/pages (**7, 0.18%**).

**10. Cheapest unshipped wins, in evidence order:** explain the 17% ceiling in-app → give partial credit for partial completion → make the trial-to-charge sequence explicit and put cancellation in-app → fix "already paid, asked to pay again" → allow backfill and a user-set day boundary → widen the free cap or time-box full access → fix the Russian/Chinese/Vietnamese payment rails → ship VoiceOver accessibility (`14231051461`).

# PART 1 — SCOPE AND METHOD

## 1.1 Files used

| File | Role |
|---|---|

## 1.2 Schema

Every record carries: `review_id`, `app_id`, `app_name`, `country`, `country_name`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `author`, `date` (ISO 8601 UTC), `vote_count`, `vote_sum`, `is_edited`.

Fields used in this report: `review_id`, `country`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `date`.
Fields deliberately ignored: `author` (personal data, no analytical value), `app_id`, `app_name` (constant), `vote_count`/`vote_sum` (only 91 records carry votes — too sparse to weight), `is_edited` (74 records; noted, not used).
**No `version` field exists.** No release-level attribution is asserted from metadata anywhere in this report.

**Every one of the 3,837 records was read.** Reviews were read in full in five passes: all 196 one- and two-star reviews; all 208 three-star reviews; all 1,174 four- and five-star reviews with a body over 80 characters; and all 2,259 remaining four- and five-star reviews. No sampling was used for reading.

## 1.4 Deduplication

**No records were removed.** Nine title+body pairs repeat across 38 records, but every repeated pair is generic short text ("good" ×6, "❤️" ×11, "❤️❤️❤️" ×7, "супер" ×3, ":)" ×3, "love", "bom", "nice", "yes") written by different authors in different storefronts on different dates. These are independent reviews, not technical duplicates. **The denominator for every global percentage in this report is 3,837.**

## 1.5 Classification method and measured error

Reviews were classified by a multilingual regular-expression taxonomy applied to `title + body`, lower-cased and HTML-unescaped. Themes are non-exclusive.

Validation performed:

| Check | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `cap_habit_limit` precision | Random 45-review sample, manual read | **3 false positives = 6.7%** |
| `cap_habit_limit` recall | 35-review probe of unmatched reviews containing a paywall token | **~2–3 missed = ~7% under-count** |
| 7 negative themes | **Exhaustive manual review of every match** | 32 false positives removed; counts now exact for the matched set |
| `adhd_neuro` | Re-specified after audit found bare `\badd\b` matching the verb "add" | 87 → **81 (2.11%)** |

The seven exhaustively verified themes are `flower_not_growing` (56→50), `all_or_nothing` (30→20), `no_backfill_midnight` (32→29), `no_stats_history` (46→40), `music_cannot_disable` (19→16), `support_unreachable` (14→13), `custom_habits_missing` (11→8).

**Known error risk:** all counts are floors with respect to recall, and thin-coverage languages (Chinese, Korean, Thai, Arabic, Polish) under-count in every theme. Precision for the large praise themes (`praise_aesthetic`, `praise_music_ambience`, `praise_simplicity`) was not exhaustively audited; those patterns are broad by design and their percentages should be read as "share of reviews mentioning this topic favourably", not as a precise sentiment measure.

## 1.6 Limitations

- App Store reviews are self-selected and skew to the delighted and the aggrieved. Silent churn is invisible.
- The review-reward mechanism (Warning 1) inflates the 5★ count by an unmeasurable amount.
- Dec 2025 / Jan 2026 volumes are anomalously low; rates for those months are unreliable.
- 73 storefronts fall below the 50-review threshold and receive no standalone conclusions.
- No external source was consulted. Every claim in this report is review-derived. Price points cited are what reviewers wrote, not verified store prices.

**4★ (13.94%).** This is the *"one thing away"* band and the most commercially informative one. The single most common reason for withholding the fifth star is the cap: **47 of 535 (8.8%)**. **1★ (2.84%).** **63 of 109 (57.8%) carry a monetisation theme.** The distinctive 1★ content is transactional, not feature-based: 17 billing disputes (mean 1.48 — the lowest-rated theme in the corpus), 12 crashes, 7 lost purchases. The highest-affect reviews in the corpus. ## 8.3 What changed — four defensible trends

**Trend 1 — the 2024 quality step-change is real (confidence: high).**
Mean rating rises 4.346 → 4.675 and 1★ share falls 4.9% → 1.5% between 2023 and 2024. The short-review share also jumps (16.5% → 35.0%), so part of this is the review prompt. **But the substantive-only mean rises too (4.175 → 4.512), and the defect themes fall independently of review length:** crashes 2.92% → 0.32% of substantive reviews, monetisation friction 27.70% → 19.43%. Both effects are present; the product improvement is not an artefact.

**Trend 2 — the background-music defect was fixed, and the fix is datable (confidence: high).**
All 10 "can't turn the music off" reviews except three fall in **2022** (`8655424484` May, `8807243801` Jun, `8868778397` Jul, `8899067436` Jul, `8902349870` Jul, `9116351698` Sep, `9121762212` Sep, `9241525509` Oct, `9263941862` Nov, `9300714052` Nov). The rate is **0.00% across all of 2023** and never exceeds 0.63% again. The three later mentions are the *inverse* problem — music won't play (`13163415628`, `13629469356`, `13817513437`). **A clean, closed defect.**

**Trend 3 — statistics/history was addressed around late 2024, then regressed or re-emerged as demand (confidence: medium).**
Complaints fall from 2.16%/1.83% (2021–23) to 0.46% (2024), and a reviewer thanks the team for adding a calendar in January 2025 (`12244017370`: "Спасибо, что добавили календарь"). A 2026 reviewer praises "аналитика прошлых привычек" (`14227614235`) and another can see "a yearly outline" (`14477621410`). **But the rate returns to 2.19% (all) / 4.26% (substantive) in 2026** — `13624817679` (can't reset statistics), `13837922277`, `14443983125`, `14468283183` (calendar hard to find), `14477621410`. **Interpretation: something shipped, and it is either insufficient or insufficiently discoverable.** This is worth a direct product check.

**Trend 4 — crashes returned in 2026 (confidence: low–medium, small n).**
Crash rate goes 0.13% (2024) → 0.19% (2025) → **1.25% (2026)**, and **2.13% of substantive 2026 reviews**. Only 4 records: `13776796955` (ES, 1★), `13917160052` (RU, 5★, "последнюю неделю цветок вообще не растет и не реагирует"), `13958209159` (RU, 1★, long-term premium subscriber, app crashes on the onboarding screen after reinstall, support unresponsive for months), `14150331209` (RU, 4★, crash on opening the habits view). **Three of four are Russian and two involve paying users.** n=4 cannot support a rate claim, but the cluster is recent, geographically concentrated, and hits paid users. **Flagged for verification against crash telemetry, not asserted as a trend.**

## 8.4 What persisted

Four themes show **no meaningful improvement across five years**: the all-or-nothing rule (1.20% → 0.63%), backfill/day-boundary (1.68% → 0.94%), trial/billing disputes (1.20% → 0.63%), and — after the 2024 improvement — monetisation friction, which has been flat at **19.43% → 19.00% → 18.44%** of substantive reviews for three consecutive years.

**Interpretation: Eden fixed its engineering problems and has not touched its design and commercial ones.**

## 8.5 One weakening positive worth watching

**Garden-motivation praise declines monotonically: 7.93% → 8.41% → 5.65% → 5.03% → 3.76%.** Aesthetic praise does not decline (it strengthens to 34.17%), so this is not a general affect drift. Corroborating qualitative evidence exists: reviewers report running out of gardens — "когда вырастил все сады, делать нечего. Хотелось бы новые сады" (`11848653184`); "when I plant all the gardens… I'll get bored if more aren't added" (`9501729733`); "YOU NEED MORE GARDEN DESIGNS!!" (`13393242871`); "j'ai peur qu'au bout d'un moment il y ait un manque de motivation si ce sont les mêmes fleurs déjà vues" (`12137473742`); also `9752337057`, `9988825300`, `11467616368`, `12263431554`, `13826711840`, `13272780198`.

**Interpretation: the core reward loop has a content ceiling, and long-term users are hitting it.** For a product whose entire retention mechanism is the garden, this is the most strategically important soft signal in the corpus.

## 9.3 Research questions (the corpus raises them but cannot answer them)

1. **What does the review-reward mechanism do to the rating?** 13 reviewers volunteer that they reviewed to unlock cosmetics (Warning 1), and the ≤25-char share doubled to ~35% when it appears to have been introduced. **The true 5★ inflation is unmeasurable from review text alone.** Check the prompt's trigger conditions and A/B its removal against rating and retention. There is also App Store policy exposure here that this report cannot assess.
2. **Do the impulse buyers (§5.3) retain?** Reviewers repeatedly describe buying within minutes on aesthetics, and payers rate a full point below the corpus. Cross-reference time-to-purchase against 30/90-day retention and refund rate.
3. **Why is payer satisfaction flat at ~3.5 across five years** while corpus satisfaction rose from 4.34 to 4.68 (§5.1)? The 2024 engineering fixes did not move it. Test whether entitlement failures (F2) are the whole explanation.
4. **Is the Russian market's low paywall friction a pricing effect or a payment-availability effect?** Russia has the highest satisfaction, lowest paywall friction, and half of all blocked-payment reviews. If users literally cannot reach the paywall, low friction is not a satisfaction signal.
5. **Did a statistics/calendar feature ship, and is the 2026 re-emergence a regression or a discoverability failure?** (§8.3, F7.)
6. **Would a rewarded-ad unlock path work for the under-18 segment** (§7.3, three explicit requests) without damaging the **7 reviews (mean 5.00)** that praise Eden for having no ads?

# PART 10 — EVIDENCE APPENDIX

## 10.1 Counting rules

- **Denominator:** 3,837 for every global percentage. Country percentages use that country's review count. Segment rates (explicit payers, one-star reviews) are labelled as segment rates and always show both the segment denominator and the global count.
- **Non-exclusive:** a review can carry any number of themes. Theme percentages therefore sum to more than 100%.
- **Unit of analysis:** one review = one record. A review mentioning the cap three times counts once.
- **Text scope:** `title` + `body`, HTML-unescaped, whitespace-normalised, lower-cased.
- **"Any monetisation gating"** = union of `cap_habit_limit`, `cap_garden_limit`, `paywall_general`, `price_objection`, `trial_billing_dispute`, `purchase_lost`. `cannot_pay` is deliberately **excluded** from that union — it is a payment-rail failure with a mean rating of 4.06, not a friction complaint.
- **Eras:** calendar years; 2021 (22 records) merged into 2021–22.
- **Substantive review:** `len(body) > 60` characters. n = 1,791 (46.7% of corpus).
