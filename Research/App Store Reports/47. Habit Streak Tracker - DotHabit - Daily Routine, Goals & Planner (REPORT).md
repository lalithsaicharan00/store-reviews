# Habit Streak Tracker: DotHabit (App Store ID 1439938837) — Full Review Analysis

**App:** Habit Streak Tracker: DotHabit — Japanese listing title *習慣記録で継続 DotHabit 運動・勉強・目標達成アプリ*
**Developer of record:** Mono Incorporated · **Bundle:** `com.supernora.DotHabit`
**Corpus:** 589 written reviews · 18 storefronts · 3 February 2019 → 26 August 2026
**Corpus mean rating:** 3.818 · **Extracted:** 8 September 2026 (`itunes MZStore userReviewsRow`)
**Analysis date:** 12 September 2026
**Business model:** Free download. From 2019 to early 2024 reviewers describe a **free app with banner ads** (a premium tier existed by January 2021, when three premium members report being reverted to free). In **May 2024** an update added **video ads on ordinary check-ins, put back-dated logging behind a video ad, and limited new habits for free users**. By **14 December 2024** a reviewer reports the video ads were replaced by banners (`12062387349`). From 2025 reviewers describe a **subscription with a trial, a free tier capped at 6 habits, and an "AI coach" (DotBuddy) that replies to check-ins**. The Japanese store listing (accessed 12 September 2026) lists seven in-app purchases from **¥600 to ¥14,800**, including *DotHabit プレミアム 年額* (annual) **¥1,990**, and describes premium as multiple-habit widgets plus the AI coach.

## How to read this

- Every quantified finding gives **count, percentage, denominator, scope, period, signal label and review IDs**. Every claim traces to an exact record via `review_id` in `reviews.jsonl`. **§9.7 is a complete 589-row index** — every review with its ID, storefront, star rating, date, verbatim title and assigned themes — and **§9.8 lists the full review-ID set for every one of the 96 themes.** Nothing in this report is unreachable from those two tables.
- Theme counts are **non-exclusive** — one review can carry several themes. All 589 records carry at least one theme (21 carry `meta_low_information`, most of them as their only theme).
- Signal thresholds: `<0.1%` ignore · `0.1–0.5%` weak · `0.5–1%` emerging · `1–3%` meaningful · `3–5%` very strong · `>5%` high-priority. The global denominator is **589**, so in counts: **1–2 reviews = weak, 3–5 = emerging, 6–17 = meaningful, 18–29 = very strong, 30+ = high-priority.** For the two eligible storefronts the denominator is that storefront's own count (Japan 369, Korea 92).
- **Facts** are what reviewers wrote. **Interpretation** is labelled as such.
- Non-English quotations are given in the original, with a translation beside them.

### ⚠️ Eight warnings before any number here is used

1. **This is a Japan corpus.** 369 of 589 reviews (**62.65%**) are from the Japanese storefront and 92 (15.62%) from Korea. **Only Japan and Korea clear the 50-review bar.** The United States contributes 7 reviews. Global figures are therefore mostly Japanese figures; Part 6 separates them.
2. **The corpus mean (3.818) is far below the store's displayed aggregate** (4.7 from about 17,000 ratings on the Japanese storefront, accessed 12 September 2026). Written reviews are roughly 2% of Japanese ratings. **Do not report 3.818 as this app's rating.**
3. **Eight months of 2024 dominate the negative evidence.** **63 reviews (10.70%) were posted 2 May → 28 December 2024**, right after the monetization update. Their mean is **2.38**; **39 of the 63 are 1★ or 2★ (61.9%)**, compared with 14.9–17.8% in every other era. Those eight months hold **39 of the corpus's 123 one- and two-star reviews (31.7%)**. Every record is kept in every denominator. Removing the window moves the global mean from 3.818 to **3.990 (n=526)**.
4. **No solicited-review burst was found.** Only 3 reviews complain about a rating prompt, and there are no exact duplicate reviews. The volume spikes are **incident clusters**: a sync hang (December 2019), a photo-attach crash (January 2021, 28 reviews in 21 days), the monetization update (May 2024, 19 reviews in one month, mean 1.89), an app that would not open (January 2026), and data loss after an update (August 2026). Each is dated and disclosed in Part 7.
5. **16 records (2.72%) have a star rating that contradicts their own text** (`meta_rating_text_mismatch`). **All 16 are 5★ reviews reporting an app that cannot be used**, for example `6859903726` (TW, 5★, *"但今天在習慣中加入照片後持續閃退 完全無法使用"* / "but today, after adding a photo to a habit, it keeps crashing — completely unusable"). Eight fall in the January 2021 crash window. They are all kept. **Star-only analysis understates the defect load of this app.**
6. **Classification was hand-curated, not blind-automated.** All 589 records were read individually, in full, in date order, in the original language: Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, English, Arabic and Vietnamese (`6686145926`, on the Japanese storefront, is written in Vietnamese). The map has **96 themes across 11 families, with 1,231 assignments (mean 2.09 per review)**. No sampling and no keyword filtering. Boundary rules are in §9.1.
7. **Purchase evidence is thin.** Only **13 reviewers (2.21%)** give first-person evidence of paying. Part 5 treats them as a qualitative segment. **No conversion rate is inferred or claimed.**
8. **Several product facts are dated by reviewer wording, not release notes.** The schema has no `version` field. Statements such as "backfill shipped by March 2020" or "video ads removed by December 2024" rest on the first review that reports them.

# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**1. The May 2024 monetization update is the defining event in this corpus. It cost the app eight months of one-star reviews, and it hit the longest-standing users hardest.**
Before May 2024 the era means are 3.99 (2019–2021) and 3.95 (2022–April 2024). **From May to December 2024 (E3, n=63) the mean is 2.38, and 61.9% of reviews are 1–2★.** Four themes appear only after the update:
- `mf_ad_overload_2024` — **24 reviews (4.07%, very strong, mean 1.46)**
- `mf_free_to_paid_backlash` — **23 (3.90%, very strong, mean 1.39)**
- `mf_backfill_paywalled` — **9 (1.53%, meaningful, mean 1.67)**
- `mf_free_habit_cap` — **7 (1.19%, meaningful)**

Together with the 2025 upsell prompts, the **monetization-shock union is 41 reviews (6.96%, high-priority, mean 1.68). 33 of the 41 sit in E3, where they are 52.4% of all reviews.** In the same eight months, **20 reviewers say they are deleting or switching** (`neg_stated_churn`, 31.7% of E3). **11 of the corpus's 23 self-described users of two years or more** wrote in these eight months. Representative quotes:
- *"改悪前は☆4-5くらいの日常使いでしたが、改悪後は☆1です。残念ながら違うアプリに乗り換えます"* / "Before it got worse it was a ★4–5 daily app; after, it's ★1. Sadly I'm switching" (`11265960114`, JP, 1★, two years of use)
- *"元々入力するのに1分もかからないのに広告も1分じゃ"* / "Logging takes under a minute, and then the ad is a minute too" (`11279074981`, JP, 1★)
- *"4年くらい使ってましたが、製作者の方が金儲け主義に走り、広告が導入されたので使用をやめました"* / "I used it for about four years, but the maker went money-first and added ads, so I stopped" (`11568978748`, JP, 1★)
- *"ERA DE GRAÇA AGORA É PAGO"* / "IT WAS FREE, NOW IT'S PAID" (`11455716256`, BR, 1★)

**The partial rollback worked.** By 14 December 2024 a reviewer reports *"現在は動画広告はなくなりバナー広告に変更されているので問題なく使用できるようになった"* / "the video ads are gone, replaced by a banner, so it's usable again" (`12062387349`, JP, 5★). **Ad complaints then drop to zero: 0 of 146 reviews from January 2025 to August 2026.** The E4 mean is back to 4.02. **Interpretation:** reviewers did not object to paying. They objected to losing things they already had — the uninterrupted check-in and the ability to fix a missed day. The word they use is 改悪 ("made worse").

**2. Logging a missed day is the product's oldest unmet need. Gating it was the single most resented monetization decision.**
The **backfill-demand union** has **52 reviews (8.83%, high-priority)**:
- `fr_backfill_past_days` — 22 (3.74%, very strong): let me check off a day after the fact.
- `fr_start_date_before_install` — 21 (3.57%, very strong): let me record days before I created the habit.
- `mf_backfill_paywalled` — 9: the 2024 gate.

The motive is protecting the streak: *"偶然拖到12点后记录，会断了之前的连续的数字，很焦躁"* / "if I happen to log after midnight it breaks my streak count — very frustrating" (`3824767302`, CN, 5★, 2019). Back-dating was shipped by 29 March 2020. The corpus's most-voted review (23 votes) opens with *"日付を遡ってチェックインできるようになってました！！！"* / "You can now check in for past dates!!!" (`5731143749`, JP, 5★). After that, `fr_backfill_past_days` falls from **7.1% of E1 reviews to 0.7% of E4**.

In May 2024 the same capability was put behind a video ad. *"前日の記録を入力すると言うマイナスをゼロに戻す作業に動画視聴を強要される"* / "you're forced to watch a video just to log yesterday — work that only takes a minus back to zero" (`11237013288`, JP, 1★). *"動画視聴が必須になったら使いません"* / "If watching a video becomes mandatory I won't use it" (`11231655490`, JP, 1★).

The pre-creation start date is still requested in 2026 (`14279904612`, KR, July 2026). One reviewer found it exists but is hidden: *"選択肢が空欄になっているので制限がかかっていると思っていました"* / "the options were blank, so I assumed it was restricted" (`14081256602`, JP, 3★, May 2026). **Interpretation:** back-dating is a trust feature — it is what makes the streak honest. Charging for it reads as charging to undo the app's own penalty.

**3. The value is simplicity plus a dot grid that fills in. That combination is why people pick this app over others.**
- `cp_simplicity` — **126 reviews (21.39%, high-priority, mean 4.61)**
- `cp_visual_progress` — **67 (11.38%, high-priority, mean 4.70)**
- `cp_design_aesthetic` — **54 (9.17%)**
- `cp_behaviour_change` — **44 (7.47%, mean 4.75)**
- `cp_best_of_many` ("I tried many; this one") — **33 (5.60%, mean 4.88)**

Representative quotes:
- *"シンプルイズベストを地で行くようなアプリ… マスが埋まっていくのを見るのが、地味に達成感を感じてとても良い"* / "It lives 'simple is best'… watching the squares fill gives a quiet sense of achievement" (`5501337301`, JP, 5★)
- *"やったところが色が塗られるので加点方式な感じがして好き"* / "What you did gets coloured in, so it feels like scoring points, not losing them" (`11675941548`, JP, 5★)
- *"このアプリのおかげでピアノ練習100日目到達"* / "Thanks to this app I reached day 100 of piano practice" (`13527160252`, JP, 5★)

Two reviewers state the design constraint outright. `11047099153` (JP, 5★) wanted weekly habits but concluded *"多機能を追加すると、今のシンプルさは壊れてしまう。ので、結論は今のままでいい"* / "adding features would break the simplicity, so leave it as it is". `11262616384` (JP, 2★) says the video ads destroyed *"サッと入力出来るシンプルな手軽さ"* / "the simple ease of logging in a second". **Interpretation:** simplicity is the product. Anything that adds taps to the check-in path — ads, upsell screens, prompts — is attacking the moat, not just annoying users.

**4. Reliability is the largest negative family, and it arrives in dated incidents rather than a steady drip.**
The **Reliability family covers 172 reviews (29.20%) and carries 63 of the 123 one- and two-star reviews (51.2%).** The crash / cannot-open union is **64 reviews (10.87%, high-priority, mean 2.84)**. The incidents:
- **December 2019:** sync hangs at 0%. 7 reviews in 9 days, `5256548642` → `5285259667`.
- **January 2021:** attaching a photo crashes the app, which then will not reopen. 10 photo-crash reviews, 9–24 January. 19 crash reviews in the window. 8 of those reviewers still gave 5★.
- **October 2020 → January 2024:** a white screen when typing a custom habit. **13 reviews, all Japanese, mean 1.54, 9 one-stars.** This is the **longest-running defect in the corpus — over three years.** Example: *"自分で入力しようとするとホワイトアウトしてしまい、アプリ自体全く使えない"* / "whenever I type my own habit the screen goes white; the app is completely unusable" (`7891739461`).
- **2019 → April 2021:** calendar weekdays off by one day. **14 reviews; 13 of them outside Japan and Korea** (CN, IT, FR, ES, AU).
- **February 2024 → May 2025:** a crash loop after logging in to the free backup. 6 reviews; logging out is the workaround (`11237073921`, `12050119923`).
- **1–8 January 2026:** the app will not open (`13584085462`, `13591584382`, `13599911832`, `13608086322`).

**5. Data loss is now the most severe live defect. It is concentrated in August 2026, in Japan, among long-term users.**
**`rel_data_loss` has 16 reviews (2.72%, meaningful, mean 1.94). All 16 are Japanese.** 10 of the 16 fall in E4, and **8 fall in 3–20 August 2026, after an update.**
- *"7年間使った結果がこれ… アプリ更新したら勝手にデータ上書きされて今までの記録消えた"* / "Seven years of use, and this: I updated and it overwrote my data; all my records are gone" (`14381253074`, 1★)
- *"登録していたメアドに復旧のための確認メールは来ない"* / "no recovery email ever arrives at my registered address" (`14382871183`, 1★)
- *"2022年から毎日つけていたトピックが消えていました"* / "habits I'd logged every day since 2022 were gone" (`14450610442`, 1★)
- *"内容が消えるとダメージが大きくなるので、やったことについてのメモや感想を記入することはやめています"* / "since losing entries hurts more the more I write, I've stopped writing notes" (`14432502077`, 2★, two years of use)

Two of the August reports say it was fixed within days (`14438246592`, `14397446556`). The **data-integrity union** (loss, wrong counts, scrambled order, stale widget) rises from **1.1% of E1 reviews to 13.0% of E4**. **Interpretation:** for a product whose entire output is an unbroken visual record, silently rewritten history is the worst possible failure. The August 2026 cluster should be treated as a live incident until the developer confirms root cause and recovery.

**6. The AI coach is the new differentiator. It is also the new paywall lever, and it breaks quietly.**
**`cp_ai_encouragement` — 13 reviews (2.21%, meaningful, mean 4.69), all from September 2025 onward.** The AI-coach union (praise, stopped comments, unwanted comments, paywall) is **18 reviews — 12.3% of E4**.
- *"そんなわけないだろ、AIだろ、って感じると思うけど意外に嬉しい"* / "You'd think 'come on, it's just AI' — but it's surprisingly nice" (`14326467058`, JP, 5★)
- *"AIに褒められてる感じがしなくて、承認欲求も満たされて"* / "It doesn't feel like being praised by an AI; it satisfies the need for approval" (`13833749330`, JP, 5★)

The friction is small but specific:
- Comments stop without explanation — `rel_ai_comment_stops`, 5: *"1週間くらいでAIのコメントが来なくなっちゃった"* / "after about a week the AI comments stopped coming" (`14205265209`, JP, 3★).
- Comments feel patronising — `ux_ai_comment_unwanted`, 3: *"1個記録つける度にAIがすごいすごい言ってくる。赤ちゃんか"* / "every time I log something the AI says 'amazing, amazing'. Am I a baby?" (`12894467546`, JP, 1★).
- A free user lost the feature — *"AIコメント機能が有料会員でないと使えなくなってしまいました… 褒めてくれるAIのコメントのおかげで、楽しく続けられていた"* / "the AI comments now need a paid membership… their praise is what kept me going" (`13151339965`, JP, 4★, September 2025).

**Interpretation:** this is the first premium feature in the corpus that reviewers praise unprompted. It monetizes by adding value, which the 2024 changes did not.

**7. Payers are few, rate the app lower, and mostly report something going wrong.**
**13 reviewers (2.21%, meaningful) give first-person purchase evidence. Their mean is 3.23, against 3.83 for everyone else.** **9** of them report a billing, entitlement or reliability problem. **8 reviews (1.36%, meaningful) describe a billing failure:**
- Premium status reverting to free, with ads back — three in January 2021 (`6828403002`, `6847937115`, `6879483949`)
- *"400円を2回も騙し取られた"* / "I got cheated out of ¥400 twice" (`11476471502`, JP, 1★)
- An unintended half-year subscription, cancelled in a panic (`13604118005`, JP, 1★)
- The app crashing the moment a trial payment went through (`12633545732`, JP, 3★, six years of use)
- *"アプリを開くと毎回「購入に失敗しました。」"* / "Every time I open the app it says 'purchase failed'" (`14340739070`, JP, 4★, July 2026)

**Price itself is rarely the objection:** only 4 reviews (0.68%) say the subscription is too expensive, and 1 says the lifetime price is (`14475596320`, *"高過ぎてワロタ"* / "so expensive it's funny"). Willingness to pay is real but old. **14 reviewers (2.38%) volunteer to pay, 9 of them before 2022, mostly for ad removal** (`fr_ad_removal_option`, 10, all 2019–2023): *"有料で良いので広告なし設定付けてください"* / "please add an ad-free setting, even a paid one" (`7109536051`, JP, 4★).

**8. In 2025 the friction moved from ads to upsell screens on the check-in path.**
**`mf_upsell_nag` — 4 reviews (0.68%, emerging, mean 1.25), all July–November 2025.**
- *"タスクを完了するたびに毎回有料版の導入を進めるページに移るのがうっとうしいから消した"* / "every time I complete a task it jumps to a page pushing the paid version — annoying, so I deleted it" (`12878957344`, JP, 1★)
- *"無料版で使わせる前提ではないのかもしれません"* / "maybe the free version isn't meant to be used" (`13147772741`, JP, 2★)

A paying user asks for the rating prompt to stop *"せめて課金してる人に対してくらいは"* / "at least for people who pay" (`14056892397`, JP, 3★, May 2026). **Interpretation:** the 2024 lesson — don't interrupt the check-in — has not fully carried over to 2025's prompts.

**9. Japan and Korea are two different stories.**
- **Japan (n=369, mean 3.54)** carries almost all of the monetization shock and data-integrity evidence: 20 of 24 ad-overload reviews, **all 16 data-loss reviews, all 13 white-screen reviews, and 12 of the 13 payers.**
- **Korea (n=92, mean 4.33)** is an early-era market. **65 of its 92 reviews date from 2019–2021, and only one from 2025.** It over-indexes on requests rather than complaints: back-dating (10, **10.87%** of KR), widgets (9, 9.78%), and "best of the many I tried" (12, 13.04%).

**Interpretation:** KR reviewers largely stopped writing after 2021. The corpus cannot say whether they left or stopped reviewing.

**10. The platform gaps are old and still open.**
- Widget requests — `fr_widget`, 20 (3.40%, very strong), every era 2019–2026. Since 2025 a widget exists, but it gets stuck at 0 or goes blank (`rel_widget_not_updating`, 5, all 2025–2026): *"ある日突然ウィジェットがずっと0表示になってしまいました"* / "one day the widget got stuck showing 0" (`13695033844`, JP, 3★).
- iPad — `ux_ipad_not_supported`, 11 (1.87%): the app runs at phone size.
- Apple Watch — `fr_apple_watch`, 6, all 2020–2022. The listing shows iPhone, Mac and Vision support only.

**11. The developer does ship what users ask for. The gap is in the support channel, not responsiveness.**
**`sup_fix_acknowledged` — 12 reviews (2.04%, meaningful, mean 4.42), 10 of them 2024 or later.** Shipped requests include:
- Photo multi-select within a week of the request (`12050119923`)
- Late-night check-ins no longer leaving a gap (`13652093408`: *"まさか本当に意見を取り入れてくださるとは"* / "I never thought they'd actually take my feedback")
- A Sunday week start (`13699643234`)
- Hiding non-scheduled weekdays (`14174277268`)

Against that, **7 reviews (1.19%) cannot reach support or get no reply**, and **5 (0.85%) cannot delete their account or email**: *"退会したいと何度かメッセージ送ってますがもちろん反応はないです"* / "I've messaged several times asking to delete my account; of course, no response" (`10396494379`, JP, 1★).

**12. The cheapest high-value moves, in evidence order:**
1. Treat the August 2026 data loss as an incident and ship a visible restore path. 16 data-loss reviews, 8 in 18 days, several from users of two to seven years.
2. Commit publicly that logging a missed day will never be gated. 52 reviews of backfill demand; the 2024 gate produced 9 reviews with mean 1.67.
3. Keep ads and upsell screens off the check-in path. The rollback took ad complaints from 24 in E3 to 0 in E4; upsell prompts have since produced 4 more one- and two-star reviews.
4. Fix entitlement recognition and the "purchase failed" loop. 8 billing-failure reviews, against only 13 identifiable payers.
5. Fix the stuck widget and AI comments that silently stop. 5 + 5 reviews, all in the last 16 months.
6. Add an in-app account-deletion path and a visible support contact. 5 + 7 reviews.
7. Only then, grow premium through additive features reviewers already name: the AI coach, multiple widgets, iPad and Mac layouts, and totals and stats (§8.4).

## 1.2 Schema

Fields present on every one of the 589 records: `review_id`, `app_id`, `app_name`, `country`, `country_name`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `author`, `date`, `vote_count`, `vote_sum`, `is_edited`.

**Fields used in this analysis:** `review_id`, `country`, `rating`, `title`, `body`, `date`.
**Fields inspected and set aside:** `author` (not analysed; no reviewer is named in this report); `app_id`, `app_name` and `country_name` (constant or derivable); `vote_sum` (differs from `vote_count` on 19 records; no finding uses it).
**Fields inspected and used only as an integrity check:**
- `vote_count` — 501 of 589 records (85.06%) have zero votes. The most-voted reviews are:
  - `5731143749` (JP, 5★, 23 votes — back-dating has shipped; a list of free features)
  - `8913596918` (JP, 5★, 14 — a high-schooler's diet log; *"動画を見れば使える色が増えます"* / "watch a video and you unlock more colours")
  - `12487255668` (JP, 2★, 11 — the longest review in the corpus, downgraded in April 2025)
  - `12062387349` (JP, 5★, 7 — video ads replaced by banners)
- `is_edited` — true on 12 records (2.04%). Where an edit changed the verdict it is noted in-line. Examples: `12487255668`, a 2023 review rewritten in April 2025 (*"大幅に変わったので評価下げます。もう使ってません"* / "it changed drastically, so I'm lowering my rating; I no longer use it"); `11955271097` and `14438246592`, both edited to say the bug was fixed. Full list in §9.3.

**Text handling:** HTML entities in `title` and `body` were unescaped before reading. Body length: median **63 characters**, mean 87, minimum 1 (`5516408123`, body "神"), maximum 1,834 (`12487255668`, JP, 2★). Titles often carry the verdict and bodies the detail: `6685962313`'s title is *"Apple Watchに対応してくれたら最高"* ("if it supported Apple Watch it would be the best"), and its body is *"です"* ("it is"). Title and body were therefore always read together.

## 1.5 Processing method

1. **Parse.** All 589 JSON lines were parsed and the schema verified field by field. HTML entities were unescaped, and records sorted by `date` into one flat working file (`Temp/47-all-reviews.txt`) with an index `[n]` per record.
2. **Reconcile.** The main file was cross-checked against all 18 `by_country/*.jsonl` files, `manifest.json` and `_state.json` on totals, per-country counts, rating distribution and record equality. All checks were exact (§1.3).
3. **Read.** Every record was read individually and in full, in date order, in its original language (Warning 6). No sampling, no keyword pre-filtering, no truncation, no stopping at a context limit.
4. **Classify.** The taxonomy was drafted from the full read. Themes were then hand-assigned to every record against its full text and recorded as an explicit `review_id → [themes]` map in `Temp/47-review-classification.py`. Each entry carries a comment with its working-file index and storefront. Assignment is non-exclusive: a review carries every theme it supports.
5. **Validate.** Programmatic checks over the map (`Temp/47-validate.py`): zero unknown review IDs, zero duplicate keys, zero unassigned records, zero records with no theme, zero duplicated themes within a record, zero undefined themes, zero orphan themes. All passed.
6. **Aggregate.** Counts, percentages, means, rating cross-tabs, family unions, per-country cuts, and per-era, per-half, per-year and incident-window cuts were computed from the validated map (`Temp/47-aggregate.py`, `Temp/47-trends.py`). The tables in §1.4, §3.1, §3.2, §4.6, §6.1, §7.1, §9.1, §9.7 and §9.8 are generated from the map directly, not transcribed (`Temp/47-tables.py`).
7. **Verify.** Every review ID cited anywhere in this report was checked to exist in `reviews.jsonl`. Every §9.7 row was checked against the map and the source, and every §3.1 count, percentage and mean was recomputed (§9.4).

## 1.6 Limitations and known biases

- **Japan-weighted.** 62.65% of records, and 76.6% (245 of 320) of records from 2022 onward, are Japanese. Global themes should be read as Japanese themes unless Part 6 says otherwise.
- **Selection bias toward writers.** Written reviews are about 2% of Japanese ratings. The corpus mean (3.818) is 0.88 below the displayed aggregate (4.7).
- **Incident clustering.** Large shares of some themes come from short windows (Warning 4). Era rates are therefore reported alongside the windows that drive them.
- **Contentless reviews.** 21 records (3.57%) carry no recoverable product content ("神", "좋음", "bom"). They are kept and never used as evidence for a product theme.
- **Price and entitlement boundaries are review-derived.** The corpus shows what reviewers say is free or paid when they wrote. It cannot show exact release dates for pricing or entitlement changes; those are stated as inferences.
- **No version field.** Defect clusters are dated, not versioned. Version numbers appear only where reviewers wrote them (for example `10978216170`: *"バージョン2.0で実装された無料バックアップ"* / "the free backup added in version 2.0", February 2024).
- **Storefront ≠ language.** At least nine reviews are in a language other than the storefront's usual one — English on KR (`5763529321`, `8181440831`, `8401212873`, `9167457657`), JP (`11242485127`, `13254924240`) and AR (`12865194480`) storefronts; Vietnamese on JP (`6686145926`); and a mixed Japanese/Chinese/English report on JP (`5270999481`). Storefront is used as the market unit because it is the field the data provides.
- **Sub-1% themes.** 39 of the 96 themes have fewer than 6 supporting reviews (<1%). They are reported at their honest signal label and never promoted.

## 3.3 Positive themes in detail

### 3.3.1 Simplicity — 126 reviews (21.39%, high-priority, mean 4.61) — present in every era

`cp_simplicity` is the largest theme in the corpus and in Japan (in Korea crashes outrank it), and the leading or joint-leading praise theme in every era. By era: **24.5% → 28.8% → 4.8% → 17.1%**. The praise is specific: few features, no tutorial needed, fast to log.
- *"打卡习惯类app没有什么高深技术含量，唯有逻辑和UI的精简再精简"* / "habit apps have no deep tech; all that matters is paring logic and UI down again and again" (`3801386782`, CN, 5★)
- *"다른앱들처럼 기능이 이리저리많고 복잡한거보다 좋아요 다른거 다삭제하고 이거잘쓰는중"* / "better than apps crammed with features; I deleted all the others" (`6271390705`, KR, 5★)
- *"チュートリアルも要らず、感覚的に使えてストレスがない"* / "no tutorial needed, intuitive, stress-free" (`10373785885`, JP, 5★)
- *"Todos os outros que eu testei eram cheio de funções que acabam atrapalhando"* / "every other one I tried was full of features that get in the way" (`11761509950`, BR, 5★)

It is also the frame reviewers use to judge change (`11047099153`, `11262616384`; Executive summary §3), and the reason the 2024 ads felt disproportionate. More IDs: `7547973874` `11075683835` `13598329974` `14426685768`.

### 3.3.2 The filling grid — 67 reviews (11.38%, high-priority, mean 4.70) — rising

`cp_visual_progress` runs **10.0% → 12.6% → 1.6% → 17.1%** by era. It is the mechanic users describe when they explain why they keep going:
- *"穴を開けたくなくて筋トレ１ヶ月以上続いてる"* / "I don't want a hole in it, so I've kept up strength training for over a month" (`6049067960`, JP, 5★)
- *"Dá gosto ir pintando o calendário e sentir um hábito ser construído de forma visual"* / "it's satisfying to paint the calendar and feel a habit being built visually" (`7119586754`, BR, 5★)
- *"Githubで「草を生やす」ように、習慣の達成も草を生やしたかった"* / "I wanted to 'grow grass' for my habits the way you do on GitHub" (`7744252519`, JP, 5★)

A small subset, `cp_non_punitive` (4, emerging, mean 5.00), values that **gaps do not erase what was filled**:
- *"連日の記録が途切れても、埋めた色は残っているから達成感ある"* / "even if the streak breaks, the colours I filled stay, so I still feel I achieved something" (`11046304560`)
- *"完璧じゃないくらいの習慣化の方が、本当の習慣って気がする"* / "an imperfect habit feels more like a real one" (`14354028854`)
- see also `11675941548`, `12683907851`

Against it: *"空枠が増えてモチベ下がる"* / "the empty squares keep growing and my motivation drops" (`10623893523`, JP, 3★), and `12487255668` (*"穴が空いても別に気にならない"* / "holes don't bother me"). **The same grid motivates most users and fails a few.**

## 5.2 What triggers a purchase — in buyers' and would-be buyers' own words

**Stated by payers:**
- **Accumulated value:** *"プレミアに入ったくらい重宝してます"* / "it's so useful I joined premium" (`11715444265`, 4★).

**Stated as conditional intent (non-payers):**
- **Ad removal** — the dominant 2019–2023 trigger: 10 `fr_ad_removal_option`. *"一度払い切りで広告を消すことができたら嬉しい"* / "I'd love a one-time payment to remove ads" (`8974995265`).

- **Supporting the developer:** *"광고란 없애는 것 있었으면 좋겠어요 조금이나마 도움되고 싶음"* / "I wish there were a way to remove the ad section — I want to help a little" (`5881887587`); `5729176001`, `8721860038`.

## 8.3 What not to change

- **The filling grid and its colours.** `cp_visual_progress` 67 (11.38%, mean 4.70), rising to 17.1% of E4. It is the mechanic behind the behaviour-change stories.

- **The memo-and-photo record on check-in** — the differentiator Korean and Japanese reviewers name (`5881887587`, `11428921232`).
- **The non-punitive framing** — filled colour stays when a streak breaks (`11046304560`, `14354028854`). Any "missed-day" marker (`fr_show_missed_days`, 2) should be optional.

## 8.5 Research questions this corpus raises but cannot answer

1. What caused the August 2026 data loss — which sync path, which versions, which users — and how many users lost history without writing a review?
2. Was the back-dating gate removed after June 2024, or did users stop mentioning it?
3. What share of the users who left during May–December 2024 came back after the rollback? One reviewer did not (`12487255668`, April 2025, *"もう使ってません"* / "I no longer use it").
4. Why did Korean reviews stop after 2021 — lower usage, lower review prompting, or a different storefront mix?

6. Do AI comments stop by design after a period, or is that the defect reviewers think it is?

**Boundary rules applied during classification:**
- **`cp_simplicity` vs `cp_design_aesthetic`:** simplicity is ease, minimal features and speed; design is the look (cute, clean, layout). A review can carry both.
- **`cp_visual_progress`** requires the filling / visible-progress mechanic, not just "motivating". **`cp_behaviour_change`** requires a stated sustained behaviour or outcome ("continued two months", "lost 3 kg", "no longer a 三日坊主").

- **`mf_ad_overload_2024` vs `mf_banner_ads_annoying`:** video or interstitial ads, or ads on every action, versus a static banner. The split was made on the described ad, not on the date; all overload reviews happen to fall in May–December 2024.
- **`mf_free_to_paid_backlash`** is assigned when the reviewer frames the change as a degradation of what they had (改悪, 以前は / 前は良かった, "was free, now paid") — not for a simple price objection.

- **`meta_rating_text_mismatch`** is assigned only when the star rating contradicts the text's severity (5★ plus "cannot use", "won't open", "completely unusable"). It is not assigned for a 5★ that carries only a minor request. One further case is included: `11209068138`, whose title says the reviewer lowered the rating to ★4 while the record is 5★.
- **`mp_paid_premium`** requires first-person evidence of payment or of being charged. Conditional intent goes to `mp_willing_to_pay`.
- **`neg_stated_churn`** covers deleted, stopped, switching, or actively looking for another app.
- **`seg_long_term_user`** requires an explicit duration of two years or more ("2年以上", "몇 년간", "1,145 days", "7年間").
- **`sup_fix_acknowledged`** requires the reviewer to report that their issue was fixed or their request shipped.
- **`meta_low_information`** is assigned when title and body together carry no product content; it is the sole theme only where nothing else applies.

## 9.4 Counting rules, denominators and reconciliation summary

- **Unit:** one review record = one `review_id`. 589 records, 589 unique IDs, zero duplicates, zero deduplication.
- **Global denominator:** 589 for every global percentage. **Country denominators:** Japan 369, Korea 92. **Segment rates** (per era, per star band, per payer group) always state their own denominator and are never used for the signal label.
- **Non-exclusive themes:** a review carries every theme it supports (1,231 assignments). Family and union counts count a review once.
- **Signal labels** are applied to the share of the relevant denominator, using the thresholds in "How to read this".
- **Reconciliation:** main file = 18 country files = manifest = `_state.json` on totals, per-country counts, rating distribution and record content (§1.3).
- **Verification performed before publication** (`Temp/47-verify-report.py`): every backticked review ID in this report exists in `reviews.jsonl`; every §9.7 row's storefront, rating, date and themes match the source and the map; every §3.1 row's count, percentage and mean match the map.
