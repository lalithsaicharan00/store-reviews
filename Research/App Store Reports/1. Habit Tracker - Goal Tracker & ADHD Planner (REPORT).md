# PART 0 — READ THIS FIRST: THE DATA IS DISTORTED

You cannot take the 4.67 average at face value. Three structural problems:

**1. China is 72% of the corpus and it is a review farm.**
- China = **40,991 of 56,653 reviews (72.4%)**, mean **4.82**.
- The app ran a *"leave a review → get 3–6 months free Premium"* campaign (评论送会员) from 2019 to ~2022.
- **1,340 Chinese reviews say out loud that they are only reviewing to get the membership** (3.3% of CN) — e.g. `8904637743`, `8910739685`, `8677766507`, `8513158580`, `8354830730`, `7966619964`, `7523226507`, `6642189102`, `5194031778`, `3916981057`. The real number is far higher; most just wrote "好用" and moved on.
- **21,751 Chinese reviews (53.1% of China) are under 12 characters**, mean rating 4.86. Pure filler.
- China 2021+2022 alone = **32,336 reviews at mean 4.85**. That single block is what holds the global average up.

**2. Globally, 39.1% of all reviews (22,136) are under 12 characters**, mean 4.86. Roughly four in ten reviews contain zero product information.

**3. Outside China the app is rated ~4.29, not 4.67 — and it has been falling hard.**

| Year | US mean | US 1★ rate | China mean | Non-CN mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019 | 4.61 | 1.6% | 4.81 | — |
| 2020 | 3.81 | 16.2% | 4.48 | — |
| 2021 | 4.30 | 7.5% | 4.85 | — |
| 2022 | 4.43 | 6.5% | 4.85 | — |
| **2023** | **4.54** | 5.4% | 4.68 | best year |
| 2024 | 4.14 | 11.7% | 4.66 | — |
| **2025** | **3.73** | **16.8%** | 4.66 | — |
| 2026 | 3.87 | **18.1%** | 4.37 | — |

- **Non-China monthly mean bottomed at 3.14 in Dec 2024** (65 one-star reviews out of 227) and has not returned to 2023 levels since.
- **The US 1★ rate more than tripled between 2023 and 2026** (5.4% → 18.1%).
- Two users independently documented **review-gating** — being routed to the App Store only if they rate highly, with low ratings swallowed by an in-app "thanks for your feedback" (`8836869044` UK; `6197402388`-era Polish user calls it an Apple ToS violation). One Chinese user documented the developer **deleting a critical review** (`12993356431`, Aug 2025). Total explicit gating/fake-review complaints: 9 (`12117289603`, `8836869044`, `8707927339`, `8423423589`, `8258057981`, `7611186789`, `4640019537`, `11067543951`, `13173072726`).

**What this means for you:** the honest baseline for a habit app of this quality is **~4.29 (non-China), trending to 3.8**. Design against the non-China numbers.

## 1.1 The model

- **One-time "Lifetime" purchase** is the headline product. Yearly and monthly subscriptions exist alongside it. A Family Lifetime plan launched ~mid-2024.
- Free tier = a **hard cap on number of habits**, plus most analytics locked.

## What produces 3★ and 4★ (the "almost" band)

3★ and 4★ are where **feature gaps** live, not anger:

- Every-X-days / custom frequency: 4★ lift **x4.2**, 3★ x3.9 — the classic "great app, but…"
- Mac / Windows / web version: 3★ lift **x4.8**
- Apple Health: 4★ lift x2.8
- Apple Watch: 3★ x4.7, 2★ x5.7
- Sub-tasks / folders / grouping: 2★ x2.7, 4★ x2.3
- Shortcuts / Siri / automation: 4★ x2.7
- iCloud sync: 3★ x5.0, 2★ x6.4
- Localization: 3★ **x5.5**

**Translation:** shipping custom frequency, a Mac app, better Watch support and grouping converts a large body of 3–4★ reviewers into 5★ reviewers. These people already like the app.

## 6.4 China — its own analysis

China needs separate treatment because it behaves nothing like the rest of the world.

- **40,991 reviews (72.4% of the corpus), mean 4.82, only 1.1% one-star.** Branded 习惯清单 (formerly Habify).

## Old vs new — what actually shifted

**2019–2020 (launch era)**
- Tiny volume, hand-crafted reviews, one very bad New Year (Jan 2020 report crash, 42 reviews in 3 days).
- Crash rate 17.32% of non-CN reviews in 2020 — the app was fragile.

**2021–2022 (the China boom + first paywall)**
- 32,336 Chinese reviews at 4.85 — the review farm at full throttle.
- Jan 2022 paywall regression; non-CN volume spikes to 429 that month.
- Outside China the app is quietly improving.

**2023 (peak quality)**
- **Best year in the app's history**: US mean 4.54, crashes down to 2.43%, scholarship program running, ADHD positioning at its strongest (4.07%).
- Free habit cap introduced — the only sour note (1.07%).

**2024–2026 (decline)**
- Apr 2024: widget paywalled + biggest crash incident on record.
- Mid-2024: Family plan launches broken and stays broken (0% → 1.94%).
- Dec 2024: year stats locked + minutes→hours bug → **worst month ever, mean 3.14**.
- 2025: billing complaints peak (0.90%), scholarship shut off, sync and data-loss complaints rise, localization demand peaks at 8.06%.
- 2026: partial recovery. Localization ships (8.06% → 3.02%), Japanese lands, free cap loosens back to 6, developer becomes responsive, mid-2026 monthly means climb back to 4.0–4.16.

**The story in one line:** the product got better at features and worse at trust. Quality of the *offer* fell (more paywalls, more billing errors, a broken family plan, no support channel) while quality of the *app* mostly held. Ratings followed the offer, not the app.

## Is anything AI-related happening? No.

- **Six reviews out of 56,653 mention AI at all — 0.011%, far below the 0.1% ignore threshold.**
- `10828370613`, `12071506651`, `12146111608`, `14398239845`, `14449350393` (uses "AI slop" as an *insult*), `14499683590` (the only genuine feature request: AI to build a schedule quickly).
- **Nobody in this category is asking for AI.** Users repeatedly ask for the opposite — "no bloat", "no fancy stuff", "not花里胡哨". Adding an AI feature to a habit tracker has zero demand evidence in 56,653 reviews. If you build one, it's a bet, not a response to users.

# PART 8 — WHAT TO BUILD (if you're designing the best habit tracker)

Ranked by evidence strength, not by effort.

### Non-negotiable (these determine your rating)
1. **Never take money incorrectly.** Billing errors are 21x over-represented in 1★ reviews. Single price shown, no trial-to-charge traps, refunds honoured, receipts clear.
2. **Ship an account system from day one.** No-account is the root cause of lost purchases, lost data, and failed multi-device sync — the top three complaints from people who paid. It's x12–16 over-represented in the paid cohort.
3. **Make sync actually work, and prove it.** Sync is the #1 differentiator buyers name (x9.8 lift) and the #1 thing that breaks. If it's the paid feature, it must be flawless.
4. **Harden the year-end report.** It crashed in Jan 2020, Dec 2020, Jan 2021, Dec 2024, Jan 2026. It's your best emotional moment and your most reliable outage.
5. **Never re-paywall a free feature.** x12 lift on 1★. Pick your free tier once and hold it.

### Pricing (this is what makes money here)
6. **Lead with a one-time lifetime purchase.** 22.4% of everyone who paid says the reason was "not a subscription". This is the strongest pricing signal in the dataset.
7. **Price it low and anchor against subscription competitors.** "Cheaper than a coffee" is a 4.79-mean-rating framing.
8. **Run a scholarship / hardship program and never stop it.** 91 of 101 mentions are 5★ with a perfect 5.00 mean. It also solves broken payment rails in Russia, Turkey, Argentina, Pakistan. Killing it in 2025 removed the app's best review source.
9. **Set the free cap generously (6+) and leave it alone.** Cap complaints carry x7.5 lift on 2★.
10. **If you ship a family plan, make it discoverable in-app.** This app's family plan has a mean rating of **1.65** across 52 complaints purely because nobody can find how to use it.

### Product (converts 3–4★ into 5★)
11. **Flexible frequency: every-X-days, specific weekdays, bi-weekly, quarterly, yearly, and custom total-over-a-window.** Highest 4★ lift in the dataset (x4.2). This is the #1 unmet functional need worldwide.
12. **Interactive widget check-off without opening the app.** x5.2 lift among buyers.
13. **Grouping / folders / multiple profiles** — me, my kids, work, pet.
14. **Cumulative totals** ("47 hours read this year"), not just streaks. Also offer *total days* instead of *consecutive days* to reduce streak anxiety.
15. **Editing a habit must never wipe its history.** This app fixed it in late 2025 and a user came back to raise their rating (`13449875017`).
16. **Apple Watch + Apple Health done properly**, including a Watch timer and two-way sync. Small volume, very high purchase intent (x4.2–x5.8).
17. **A Mac app.** 3★ lift x4.8; several users volunteered to pay extra for it (`8965466270`, `9503979717`).
18. **Shortcuts / Siri / URL scheme.** 4★ lift x2.7 — power users who will evangelise.

### Positioning
19. **Aim at ADHD and neurodivergent users.** 7.08% of US reviews, mean rating **4.79**. Highest-fit, highest-satisfaction audience in the corpus. Also students (mean 4.87) and people tracking medication/chronic illness.
20. **Stay minimal and ad-free.** "Simple/clean" appears in 32.2% of US reviews. "No ads" has the highest mean rating of any theme (4.91). Every request for more features is paired with "but don't make it complicated".
21. **Offer a design that isn't pastel/cute-by-default.** A recurring complaint from men and from users wanting a premium look.
22. **Localize early — it directly unlocks revenue.** Spanish (182 requests), Portuguese (162), French (48), Russian (44), Turkish (43), Arabic, Korean. Multiple users state plainly they would buy if it were in their language. Japan proves it: localization shipped mid-2026 and immediately produced 5★ reviews praising it.
23. **Don't build AI.** 0.011% demand. Build reliability instead.

### Do not do
24. **Do not run review-for-premium campaigns.** They gave this app 40,991 Chinese reviews, a #1 rank, a fake 4.8 average — and 0.86% purchase signal, a reputational backlash, and a dataset its own developer can no longer read honestly.
25. **Do not gate reviews.** Two users caught it, one called it an Apple ToS violation, and one documented a critical review being deleted.

## Appendix — method

- Every one of the 56,653 reviews was processed. All low-rated (1–3★) reviews and all substantive reviews were read individually; contentless reviews (39.1% of the corpus, under 12 characters) were aggregated by frequency rather than read one by one, and are counted in every total.
- 62 themes were built from the manual read, then applied programmatically across 20+ languages (English, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Polish, Dutch, Ukrainian, Thai, Swedish, Czech).
- Counts were computed against four denominators independently: US only, each rich market, each of the 45 countries with 50+ reviews, and all 56,653.
- Three regex false-positive classes were caught and corrected during validation: Chinese 卡 ("stuck") colliding with 打卡 ("check in"), English "add" colliding with "ADD/ADHD", and "ai" matching inside ordinary words. Corrected figures are used throughout.
