# ADHD App Reviews

Reviews of the ADHD apps, and of the wider apps ADHD users name, collected 7 November 2026 with `Tools/extract_reviews.py` and `Tools/play_extract.py`. Read-only.

**Alarmy on Play:** its `reviews.jsonl` is over GitHub's 100 MB limit, so git holds it in 3 parts and ignores the whole file. To put it back, run this inside `Play Store/69. Alarmy - Alarm Clock & Sleep/`:

`cat reviews.part-*.jsonl > reviews.jsonl`
