# Apple Design Awards — Complete Winner Catalogue, 2016–2026

Written by Codex, 15 October 2026.

The census covers every winner in the ten completed award years **2017–2026: 113 winner instances**, plus all **12 winners from the 2016 baseline**. Apps and games are both retained. Repeated products in different years remain separate award instances. Finalists are excluded from these totals; selected adjacent finalists are studied separately in the strategy report.

Read with [the detailed research and strategy](<Apple Design Awards — Why Apps Win and Our 2027 Strategy.md>). The short rationale is our paraphrase of Apple’s published explanation, not a complete jury scorecard. Codes are our descriptive interpretation; no code predicts winning. “General” means the older ceremony did not use today’s six category names. The 2016 student prizes remain labeled separately.

## Codebook

- **clarity:** Explicitly approachable controls, hierarchy, reduced effort or onboarding.
- **platform:** Meaningful Apple-device adaptation or named system/tool integration.
- **inclusion:** Access across disability, language, identity or ability explicitly addressed.
- **delight:** Playfulness, rewarding feedback, sound or emotional enjoyment.
- **visuals:** A coherent or unusually crafted visual/animated presentation.
- **innovation:** A distinct mechanism, new capability or reframed familiar activity.
- **impact:** An explicit human, social, ecological or community benefit.
- **performance:** Performance, low latency or graphical adaptability explicitly praised.

These codes can overlap. Absence means the reviewed summary did not establish that theme; it does not mean the product lacks it. Relevance labels prioritize our reading and are not App Store categories. All entries below were read individually against the annual source.

## Audit notes

The old developer-site paths for 2017–2019 are unreliable: a year path can redirect to the current awards. This catalogue instead uses dated Apple Newsroom articles for those years. 2016 is explicitly an archived transcript of Apple speakers, not a newly available Apple-hosted page. Obsolete 3D Touch, Glances and similar references are historical context rather than implementation recommendations.

Machine-readable [winner census](<Evidence/winner_census.json>), [counts](<Evidence/pattern_counts.json>) and [source register](<Evidence/source_register.json>) preserve each year/name/type/category and the coding scope. Raw source HTML and competitor imagery are excluded from publication (D11).
