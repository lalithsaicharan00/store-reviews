# Following the Money: Which Apps in Our Lists Earn the Most

Written by Claude (Claude Code), 8 November 2026, at the user's request: "out of everything, see which is the app that is
earning the highest … I don't care about the categories … I'm purely talking about these all of the apps that we have in
our lists. Whether it's in Play Store or from App Store."

**Scope:** every app in our review folders (App Store Reviews, Play Store Reviews, ADHD App Reviews: 341 corpora, 339
store IDs), whatever its category. Where only one store's copy is in our folders, the app's other store is counted too,
because the question is what the *app* earns. The 21 October revenue report (Codex) covered habit apps only; this one
covers everything we hold.

**No exact figures exist.** Private companies don't publish them. Everything below is an estimate, labelled with where it
came from, and the ranks are an educated guess, not a measurement.

### In one line each

- **Finch is the clear number one in the app stores**, and every source agrees.
- **Alarmy is probably the biggest business**: about $32M a year with over 40% profit, and the only figure here a
  company published itself.
- **The other big earners aren't habit trackers.** They're an alarm clock, a gym log, to-do lists, a screen-time
  blocker, a day planner and focus tools. The best pure habit trackers (Life Reset, Me+, InnerGrow, Grit) make about
  $50K–200K a month.
- **ADHD-specific apps are small in the stores**, apart from Tiimo (~$0.2M) and Focus Friend. The coaching apps (Shimmer,
  Inflow) charge on the web or charge little.

## 2. How the numbers were got

1. **Sensor Tower's public app pages** give a downloads band and a revenue band for "last month" (October 2026),
   worldwide, for each store. One source, one month, one definition: the best basis for comparing. Read on 8 November
   2026, for 66 store copies (65 of the store IDs in our folders plus Finch's Android copy) of the apps most likely to matter, chosen by recent review volume (step 3) and by name. Read
   one page at a time with pauses, because the site started refusing requests (HTTP 429) when read faster. The bands
   are wide (for example $100K–500K), so the bands give the order and the second source narrows them.
2. **ScreensDesign's app pages** give one monthly figure per iPhone app. Where it falls inside Sensor Tower's band, it's
   used as the point. Where there's no second figure, the point is the band's geometric middle ($100K–500K → ~$220K;
   $50K–100K → ~$70K; $5K–50K → ~$16K), which is an assumption. Forest is the one app where the two sources disagree
   (Sensor Tower $100K–500K, ScreensDesign $50K).
3. **Screening the rest.** For all 341 corpora, reviews posted in the 90 days before collection were counted
   (`Research/Temp/revenue-velocity.csv`). 188 apps had fewer than 20 reviews in those 90 days. Spot checks of apps with far
   more reviews than that came back under $5K, so a few dozen reviews a quarter points to a small business. The 274 store
   copies not read on Sensor Tower are judged small this way, **not measured**. A paid-up-front app or one selling on the
   web could still surprise.
4. **Company figures,** where they exist: Delight Room (Alarmy) published its 2025 revenue; Doist's (Todoist) are
   third-party estimates; Shimmer's $1M a year is from April 2024; Appfigures' Focus Friend figure is from September 2025.

All revenue here is store sales before Apple's and Google's cut. Not profit, and not what the developer receives.

## 3. What it means for us

- **The money is in apps people open every day for a reason that isn't "tracking"**: waking up (Alarmy), a pet to look
  after (Finch), gym sessions (Hevy), the day's tasks (TickTick, Todoist, Structured), not touching the phone (Opal, Forest,
  Focus Friend, one sec). A habit tracker earns more when it becomes one of these daily reasons.
- **Cross-platform task managers earn on the web**, where no store takes 30% (Todoist, Any.do, Sunsama, Amazing Marvin).
  That matches our plan of selling Plus sync across web, desktop and tablet.
- **The biggest single company figure is Alarmy's** ($32M in 2025, 40%+ margin), and Alarmy is a free app with
  subscriptions and ads. We've ruled out ads, so its ad income isn't a model for us.

## 4. What can't be known from here

- Exact numbers. Sensor Tower's bands are wide, and its full figures need a paid account.
- Android may be under-counted: Sensor Tower puts some well-known paid Android apps (HabitNow, Focus To-Do) under $5K,
  and the 21 October report found the same pattern.
- Web sales (Todoist, Any.do, Brain.fm, Sunsama, Amazing Marvin, Shimmer, Inflow), ads (Alarmy, Forest) and other
  platforms (Things for Mac and iPad) aren't in store figures.
- To settle the order properly: one paid export for one month, worldwide, by store, with its revenue definition stated.
