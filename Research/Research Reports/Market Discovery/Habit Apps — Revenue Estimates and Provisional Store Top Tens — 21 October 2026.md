# Habit Apps — Revenue Estimates and Provisional Store Top Tens

**Finding:** Finch has the clearest evidence of leading this broad habit/routine shortlist: public Sensor Tower overviews show roughly **$1M/month on the App Store and $700K/month on Google Play**. The remaining order is substantially less certain. These are **ten-name working shortlists sorted by reference estimates**, not a verified worldwide league table. Most Android amounts are explicitly modeled scenarios. **Life Reset's substantial public revenue estimate has unresolved store scope and is kept outside the iOS table.**

## What is being compared

The target is worldwide monthly **store revenue**, in USD, from paid downloads and in-app purchases/subscriptions. Advertising, web checkout, enterprise sales and other company income are excluded. A broader app's whole store revenue is counted, not only revenue attributable to its habit feature. This includes Finch, Fabulous, Life Reset and Habitica because recurring habits/routines are a central use case. General calendars, task managers, fitness/workout logs and meditation-only products are outside this shortlist; TickTick, Structured, Tiimo, Calm and Strava are therefore not ranked here.

**The period is each source's displayed last month/last 30 days, not a common September or October export.** Several providers do not reveal exact dates. App Store or Google Play storefront metadata is often US-specific even when a revenue card says worldwide. Where a source does not explicitly state geography, it remains undisclosed; it has not been silently upgraded to worldwide coverage.

The analyst scenarios below target gross store cash sales before fees, taxes and refunds. Provider figures retain their source definition: a consistent gross-versus-net basis was not established for all of them. They are therefore only directionally comparable. This is **not profit, developer take-home, accounting revenue or verified MRR**. An annual payment is booked as a sale when charged in this model; it is not divided by 12 and then called that month's cash receipts. No annual totals or combined-store totals are derived from these incompatible snapshots.

## Review coverage and candidate discovery

The complete local App Store and Play Store `reviews.jsonl` inventories were parsed record by record as a **machine census**. All manifest counts matched: **70 App Store apps / 337,331 records**, and **146 Play Store apps / 901,453 records**, for **1,238,784 review records**. Country/language duplicates in subsidiary files were not counted again. Eleven Native Store corpora were excluded because they are not commercial habit-app candidates.

The saved 90-app iOS and 146-app Android keyword inventories were inspected for identities and scope, and all 70 existing App Store reports were screened by their monetization headings. The monetization sections for twelve relevant reports were inspected more closely (1, 4, 5, 7, 9, 10, 13, 18, 24, 25, 49 and 50). Broader web discovery added missing cross-platform candidates, including Finch on Android, Life Reset and TeddyCare.

**This is not a fresh individual hand-reading of all 1.24M reviews.** The user's literal all-reviews reading step remains unfinished. Existing app-level analyses are attributed prior research, and their classifications/totals were not re-audited. No review-derived conversion rate, payer count or churn rate is claimed. The census establishes what files exist, not review completeness or unique people.

The original inventory score is weighted keyword-search position, **not a revenue ranking**. A top search result, cumulative install band, star rating or percentage of reviews mentioning payment is not a revenue measure. Reviews inform monetization context; they cannot supply the missing subscriber base, renewal rate or receipts.

### How the Android scenarios are calculated

For subscription-oriented apps (**S**):

`monthly cash-sales scenario = recent monthly downloads × paid conversion × blended first payment × renewal multiplier`

| Assumption | Low | Reference | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Downloads converting to a paid charge | 1% | 2% | 3% |
| Average first charge after regional pricing, discounts and plan mix | $15 | $20 | $30 |
| Total charges including existing-cohort renewals, divided by new-customer charges | 1.3× | 2× | 2.5× |
| Implied monthly cash sales per recent download | $0.195 | $0.80 | $2.25 |

These are **analyst-selected assumptions**, not measured app-specific conversion, transaction or renewal data, and not empirical category benchmarks. They describe a plausible mature subscription business; a shrinking or growing app may not fit. $20 is a blended initial charge, not an asserted US annual subscription price. The multiplier represents cash charges from older customers; it does not annualize a new user's lifetime value.

For lower-price utility IAP apps (**U**, including the Habit Diary row), reference cash yield is `4% × $6 = $0.24/download`, with cases `2% × $4 = $0.08` and `8% × $10 = $0.80`. There is no assumed recurring renewal multiplier. This is a conservative utility proxy, **not confirmation that the app sells only one-time purchases**. Habit Diary's exact plan mix was not verified; if subscriptions dominate it should be remodeled and its rank may rise. Ads remain excluded.

Example: Me+ has an AppBrain observation of **280K recent downloads**. Reference: `280,000 × 2% × $20 × 2 = $224,000/month`; cases **$54,600–$630,000**. TeddyCare's **39K** downloads produce **$31,200**, with **$7,605–$87,750** cases. Both download figures are observed provider estimates; every monetization input is assumed.

Habitica's reference is `sqrt($19,000 × $130,000) ≈ $49,699`. It is an analyst-selected geometric midpoint within one provider's range, not independently observed revenue.

## Important conflicting evidence

- **HabitKit:** the founder's 2025 business figures establish a real business but not today's iOS/Android split. FocusKit contributes a small disclosed amount to the business total. Neither the full $602K nor the full $28K MRR belongs to either individual store. A 2025 annual average is not October 2026 MRR.
- **Finch:** AppRank's larger $2M/$800K platform figures have a January revenue-record collection date; current storefront metadata does not refresh that record. The Sensor Tower overview references are chosen for the working table, without implying exact reporting dates are known.
- **InnerGrow:** $100K in the Sensor Tower overview versus $400K on AppRank. The difference is a source conflict, not evidence of 4× growth.
- **Grit:** $65K header, $90K showcase body and $200K NicheMetric app estimate. Publisher/niche totals cannot substitute for app monthly revenue.
- **Routinery:** $40K Peekly headline versus $70K AppRank; neither exact reporting period is established here.
- **Productive:** $70K AppRank versus $40K NicheMetric. Their displayed download estimates also differ.
- **Me+:** the $100K Sensor Tower overview is lower than $250K/$300K ScreensDesign header/body figures. Rating-velocity models produce much wider estimates again; averaging all providers would manufacture false certainty.
- **Paid apps:** Streaks' public $6K revenue / 7K downloads / $5.99 price combination does not establish that every counted download was a fresh full-price paid sale. Multiplying every download by the list price would be unsupported. It remains a candidate, not an omitted assumed $42K business.
- **Regional/undercovered apps:** ShineDay, Ultiself, Habio, bondavi's Habit and HabitYou have missing or poorly scoped current store receipts. They are unranked unknowns, not proven smaller businesses. Web-led subscription funnels can also make total company revenue much larger than store revenue.

## What the numbers support

Finch is the strongest large-business lead in this research. Me+, Life Reset, InnerGrow Habit Tracker, Productive, Grit and MyRoutine merit further competitive research. HabitKit has unusually useful direct historical financial evidence. Those are useful prioritization conclusions; the exact ranks below the leading group are not established.

To turn this into a defensible current top ten, obtain one provider export for **the same completed month, worldwide, separated by Apple ID/package, with the revenue/fee definition stated**, for the entire candidate set. Resolve Life Reset's store scope, the Habitify tie and unpriced regional candidates before calling the ranking complete. A complete fresh manual reread of the review corpus is a separate unfinished requirement and would not resolve these financial gaps.
