# Me Plus Lifestyle Routine — Organic Download Growth

Written by Codex, 13 October 2026. Research order: 6 in the saved iOS list. Work began after the user-selected localization example was committed and verified on main.

**Finding:** no verified download spike with an actionable organic explanation was established. Public brand habit guides and an individual family recommendation exist. Their incremental installs are unknown. A matched-app creator brief offers paid compensation, so routine-style social videos cannot automatically be classified as organic.

[Me+ Lifestyle Routine](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/me-lifestyle-routine/id1596403446), Apple ID **1596403446**, seller **ENERJOY PTE. LTD.**, bundle **alarm.smart.awake.sleep.health**. Fresh [Apple lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1596403446&country=us), checked 13 October 2026, records first release **7 January 2022**, `2022-01-07T08:00:00Z`.

This is the first release of the store ID. The historical alarm-related bundle and the current listing's AlarmMe purchase label mean this date should not automatically be called the launch of the present Me+ product/name; no exact rebranding date was established. ## Organic lead 1: individual word of mouth

On **16 August 2024**, App Store reviewer **Hattie_11** describes their use of Me+ and states:

> “I got my siblings to use this as well” — [original UK App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/me-lifestyle-routine/id1596403446?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews).

The exact store ID matches. This is one qualitative account of family adoption, not a measured referral campaign, install count or historical peak. The review does not disclose an incentive; sponsorship was not independently audited. Its request that relatives leave reviews does not establish that those reviews occurred. This was a targeted source check, not a whole-corpus review audit.

## Organic lead 2: brand habit guides, outcome unknown

The public profile [@meplus_app](https://www.instagram.com/meplus_app/) was read in the browser. A creator brief links the same handle and exact App Store ID; the profile's public collaboration contact uses enerjoy.life. On 13 October 2026 it displayed approximately **304K followers**, which is an audience count, not downloads or verified organic reach.

An original post, [How to Have a Mindful, Productive Day](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dc0Mk5LEgpP/), offers a small set of daily actions and asks readers which habit they want to try. Its public timestamp is **3 September 2026**, `2026-09-03T06:44:08.000Z`; the profile image's alternative text says September 2. Preserve the machine-readable timestamp rather than assuming those presentations are identical.

At inspection it showed **254 likes** and two visible reaction comments, neither reporting an install. This confirms useful public brand content, with no disclosed attributable installs, historical spike or paid-boosting status. It may be a content-format lead, but it does not meet our stronger standard for an organic download-growth example. The image stays outside the repository (D11).

## Paid evidence that prevents calling all social content organic

The original [Back to School with Me+ creator brief](https://homefromcollege.com/gigs/back-to-school-with--89374) offers **$100 one-time** compensation for ambassadors/influencers/UGC creators. Suggested formats include room resets, to-do lists, study tips and daily routine vlogs. It requests natural-looking content and links to [Apple ID 1596403446](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/me-lifestyle-routine/id1596403446), @meplus_app and [TikTok @mepluss](https://www.tiktok.com/@mepluss).

The poster is named **EWP PTE. LTD.**, whereas Apple's seller is Enerjoy; the exact app link establishes the product, not the corporate relationship. The page says the application period ended, but provides no verified campaign year/start date or resulting installs. It does not prove that the inspected Instagram guide was paid. Its significance is narrower: creator content that looks personal can be commissioned.

[KPI Media's own case study](https://www.kpimedia.co/blog/performance-marketing-for-the-health-wellness-industry-in-singapore) describes paid search account restructuring across **Me+, JustFit and ShutEye**. This is portfolio paid-acquisition context, not an organic cause or app-specific install effect; ROAS is not downloads.

## Leads not promoted into findings

The [Growth Hacking Lab breakdown](https://thegrowthhackinglab.com/case-studies/me-lifestyle-300k-month/) claims large Discord/Instagram/TikTok audiences, extensive search-keyword rankings and effective review prompts. It supplies no original Discord link, scoped install export or attribution test for these claims. Its monetization/onboarding discussion describes users after installation and cannot by itself explain acquisition. Repeated claims in [a Reddit marketing breakdown](https://www.reddit.com/r/iOSAppsMarketing/comments/1n5q9cu/this_wellness_app_is_quietly_doing_300kmonth/) are not founder testimony or independent verification. The current Instagram profile was checked separately; a follower count still does not establish downloads.

A mirrored Android changelog mentions a July 2024 Life Tips Spotlight contest. Original rules, incentives, entries and attributable installs were not obtained; it is an unverified lead. Publicly listed support for several languages does not prove a localization-triggered surge. No matched-app Reddit challenge with a verified participant download was found in this pass.

## Actionable-shortlist result

**Do not add a new proven marketing example for Me+ at this stage.** There is an individual family-adoption account and an observed brand guide format. Neither demonstrates meaningful organic download volume or explains a verified spike. Paid creator and portfolio search evidence are excluded from the user's organic-only shortlist.

This does not establish that Me+ has no organic acquisition. It means the public evidence reviewed did not quantify it or expose a comparable, outcome-supported tactic. Keep causes unknown under W2.
