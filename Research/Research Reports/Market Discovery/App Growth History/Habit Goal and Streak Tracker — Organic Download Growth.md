# Habit Goal and Streak Tracker — Organic Download Growth

Written by Codex, 13 October 2026. Research order: 5 in the saved iOS list, after HabitKit. Organic acquisition and actions we could actively use.

**Finding:** a dated, actionable localization example exists. bondavi released a 32-language update in **July 2023**, localized App Store screenshots, and subsequently reported gradual overseas download growth. It also publishes original habit-related reports that have received news coverage. Neither tactic has a disclosed attributable install count. A separate **2020 two-month download rise of 51%** is reported and associated with pandemic demand; it is not evidence of the 2023 localization effect.

The user excluded HabitKit's unsolicited creator recommendation from the actionable marketing shortlist. Its factual research remains preserved; this study prioritizes actions under a developer's control (W1/W2).

## Which app?

**[Habit: Goal & Streak Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/habit-goal-streak-tracker/id1120239484)**, seller **bondavi Inc.**, Apple ID **1120239484**, bundle **info.todice.habit**. In Japan, the same ID is **継続する技術｜ダイエットも筋トレも記録・習慣化で目標達成**, commonly shortened to **継続する技術**. Fresh US/Japan [Apple metadata](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=1120239484&country=jp) matches the identity and first release `2016-06-18T22:52:44Z`: **18 June 2016 UTC / 19 June in Japan**. The company's historical June 19 release date is consistent with that local date.

## Actionable example 1: localize the product and store presentation

**Original source:** [bondavi announcement, 9 August 2023](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000054.000066061.html), also available as the [company's original PDF](https://bondavi.jp/news/2308_global.pdf).

It explicitly identifies **継続する技術**, dates the update to **July 2023**, and describes:

- Support for **32 languages**, with App Store screenshots for each language.
- Changes for differing text lengths and right-to-left languages, including Arabic and Hebrew.
- Cultural adaptation of the app's distinctive encouragement messages.
- Gradual overseas download growth after the rollout.

A [1 November 2023 company PR account](https://note.com/bondavi/n/n3cd1e79fe4a4) adds that English and Chinese were localized by a native-speaking contributor instead of relying on automatic translation. It also reports gradual download growth, but discusses the company's apps together. Its August completion wording differs from the earlier app-specific July update date; do not invent one exact rollout day. Its Product Hunt link is for **Focus**, so it does not establish a Habit launch or installs there.

**What this supports:** developer-controlled localization followed by reported overseas growth. It does not establish a sudden spike, a percentage lift or which language performed best. This is app/store localization, not evidence of multilingual website landing pages. The company asserts no paid promotion; that historical claim is not an independent advertising audit.

**Proposed adaptation for Often Enough:** choose a small set of languages using available demand evidence, translate the product and store copy, create matching screenshots, and have fluent readers check meaning and layout. Compare country/storefront impressions and first-time downloads before and after each rollout. The mechanism is broader comprehension and reach; the effect on our app must be measured.

## Actionable example 2: publish useful research that can become a news story

On **26 December 2023**, bondavi published a report about New Year's goals and which goals users stopped tracking within four days. It names its app, explains the analysis and provides material journalists can cite. The stated dataset is **552,692 records**, covering **1 December 2022–30 November 2023**, across 20 goal types. The headline's two million is not the analytical sample size. [Original company report](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000057.000066061.html).

On **31 December 2023**, **Daily Sports / よろず〜ニュース** published a newsroom article naming bondavi and the app and reporting the same study. This confirms a concrete publication-to-coverage sequence. It does not prove clicks, installs, an unpaid commercial arrangement or a download peak. [Original newsroom coverage](https://www.daily.co.jp/gossip/subculture/2023/12/31/0017183070.shtml).

**Proposed adaptation:** publish a useful habit guide or a transparent voluntary survey around an event such as New Year, then offer it to relevant writers. Public research and voluntary responses can supply material without collecting users' private habit histories, consistent with Often Enough's D16 privacy model. This is a proposed content/PR experiment, not a demonstrated install campaign. Paid press-release distribution, if used, is a distribution cost rather than proof that subsequent editorial coverage was bought.

## What not to infer

The company's [business retrospective](https://bondavi.jp/story/donate-2) describes store search as the earlier main discovery route and says later media coverage did **not** bring sudden download growth, although it broadened awareness and trust. It also says a later rise in donations occurred without a large download increase. These statements concern the portfolio, including Focus; donation growth cannot be used as this habit app's acquisition lift.

The 2020 **51%** comparison, 2023 localization trend and December 2023 editorial coverage are different events. None supplies a verified current organic channel share. Portfolio totals of five million or more are not this app's downloads, and historical search placement is not a current ranking guarantee. No direct Reddit acquisition example was verified for this matched app.

**Keep for the actionable shortlist:** localized app/store presentation with qualitative growth evidence; original useful content with verified editorial pickup. Exact install attribution remains unknown.
