# HabitKit — Organic Download Growth

Written by Codex, 13 October 2026. Research order: 4, after Habit Tracker in the saved iOS download-supported list. Organic acquisition only.

**Finding:** there is a concrete creator recommendation which the founder directly attributes to a download surge: The Studio's **14 December 2024** app roundup. Organic store search is also a founder-reported acquisition channel. Exact incremental installs, the daily peak and the video-versus-New-Year split remain unknown.

## Concrete organic lead: The Studio recommendation

**Original video:** [Apps You'll ACTUALLY Use — What's On Our Phones (iOS & Android)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vC9N7PgIvEM&t=187s), The Studio, published **14 December 2024**. The founder links this identical video in his retrospective and [personal site's appearances section](https://www.roehl.dev/).

The original YouTube page and its English auto-generated transcript were inspected. At approximately **3:07–3:46**, Adam selects HabitKit for productivity. He describes the GitHub-like contribution grids, his journaling/meditation tracking and interest in reviewing long-term patterns. This is a personal-use explanation of a recognizable product feature, rather than a feature inventory. The transcript may contain transcription errors; timestamps are approximate.

The video **does contain a sponsor segment**: Epidemic Sound is explicitly named at approximately **7:11** and promoted in the description. No HabitKit sponsorship was identified in the inspected description or transcript. Therefore, classify the HabitKit segment as **apparently earned editorial coverage**, supported by the founder's account, rather than declaring the whole video unsponsored. Contracts and paid amplification were not independently audited.

In his **31 December 2024** retrospective, the founder explicitly connects the feature to the download surge and says he had not approached the team beforehand. He guesses that Adam discovered him on Threads. That initial discovery route is **unconfirmed**. [Original founder account](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/2024-my-indie-app-business-year-review-sebastian-r%C3%B6hl-kyybe).

A **16 October 2025** interview adds detail: he discovered the feature after unexpected sales notifications and a follower's post, then thanked the editor afterward. He reports approximately **$1,000 revenue in an hour**; this is a recollection about sales, not 1,000 downloads. The same interview acknowledges that winter timing confounds the numbers. [Happy Bootstrapping #143, original transcript](https://happy-bootstrapping.podigee.io/143-von-5-000-auf-30-000-dollar-mrr-sebastian-von-habitkit-zuruck-nach-2-jahren-143).

**Attribution strength:** original recommendation verified, founder-reported causal effect, apparently organic placement. No independently verified attributable install total or controlled comparison.

## Sustained organic acquisition: store search

In his **20 October 2024** [ASO strategy](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/my-app-store-optimization-strategy), the founder describes putting the primary search phrase at the beginning of **Habit Tracker - HabitKit**, using **Streaks & Accountability** in the subtitle, collecting native ratings after a first habit completion, replying to customers and sharing development publicly. He reports early UK top-ten keyword placement and continuing organic installs. These are his observed practices and explanations, not proof of Apple's ranking algorithm.

The same article discloses a **$100/month Apple Search Ads budget** and a shown 40 paid installs at €2.50 each. Its ranking effect is explicitly unknown. HabitKit's entire historical growth cannot therefore be labeled exclusively organic.

By **19 January 2025**, he reports top-five positions for the **habit tracker search keyword** in the US, UK, Germany, France and Australia. These are keyword positions, not overall store/category rankings. Version 1.11's unlimited tracking was still upcoming; that later feature cannot explain the preceding December surge. [Contemporaneous log](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/building-an-indie-app-business-56).

On **2 February 2025**, he reports US keyword position four and says that keyword supplies most downloads/revenue. On **8 June 2025**, he reports US Google Play top-three keyword placement and describes store search as his main acquisition channel. The latter is Android evidence, not an iOS rank. [February log](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/building-an-indie-app-business-58), [June log](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/building-an-indie-app-business-67).

For the initial 2023 breakthrough, the founder says in [Launched #84](https://launchedfm.com/episode/84-habitkit-sebastian-rohl) that there was no contemporaneous viral post or metadata update and he still does not know why the ranking suddenly changed. The acquisition channel is known; its initial trigger remains unknown. The episode's relative-year wording is not used to date the independently anchored December 2024 video.

## Other organic leads and limits

On **8 December 2024**, he reports a first-place Uneed launch and **900 Google Search clicks to habitkit.app “this month.”** The reporting interval is ambiguous and clicks are not app installs; no attributable download lift is disclosed. [Weekly Indie Log #53](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/weekly-indie-log-53).

His 2025 review also credits a **Starter Story interview** with another download increase and better rankings. His site dates the appearance to June 2025 and links [How I Built It: $15K/month Mobile App](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gpn1jEoWT4A). The original video's disclosures were not inspected; this remains a founder-reported lead, not a second verified unpaid placement.

The review reports **$112,000 January 2025 revenue** and attributes the strong start to both the video and New Year's demand. That money figure does not measure installs or the video's isolated contribution. Multiple posts/interviews repeat one founder's account; they are not independent analytics corroboration. [2025 retrospective](https://sebastianroehl.substack.com/p/2025-the-year-that-changed-everything).

## What is useful for our marketing research

Keep this as an **earned creator recommendation plus store-search acquisition example**. A creator who actually uses the app explained its visual value to viewers; the founder reports meaningful downstream growth. Public development posts may make a product discoverable, but the specific Threads origin is not verified. Search metadata, genuine customer feedback and continued product improvement are reported ongoing practices, with no isolated lift for each tactic.

The exact peak date/count, incremental iOS installs, geographical lift and organic channel percentages remain unavailable. Do not turn the narrative into a numeric graph or claim that a particular feature update caused the surge.
