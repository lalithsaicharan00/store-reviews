# Streaks — Organic Download Growth

Written by Codex, 15 October 2026. Research order: 7, the final app in the saved download-supported iOS list.

**Finding:** a historical download rise coincides with the 2016 Apple Design Award. Award publicity is a credible explanation, but its incremental installs and organic share are unknown. The founder also documents localization and preparing new Apple-platform features; neither has a measured acquisition result in the sources reviewed. No new proven marketing example is added to the shortlist.

## Identity and release

[Streaks](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/streaks/id963034692), Apple ID **963034692**, seller **Crunchy Bagel Pty Ltd**, bundle **com.streaksapp.streak**. The fresh [Apple lookup](https://itunes.apple.com/lookup?id=963034692&country=us), checked 15 October 2026, records first release **1 June 2015**, `2015-06-01T02:53:36Z`. Quentin Zervaas and Isaac Forman created the app; [Apple's developer spotlight](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=67dti69d) identifies the collaborators. The developer's [2016 retrospective](https://crunchybagel.com/apple-design-awards-2016/) describes the original Crunchy Bagel / Triplezero collaboration.

This is distinct from **Streaks Workout** and the same-named Android app by Shobhit Bakliwal. The [YourStory account of 23,000 downloads](https://yourstory.com/2016/07/seinfelds-productivity-hack-app-streaks-daily-habit-tracker) concerns that other Android developer and is excluded.

## Download rise checked first

[Sensor Tower's June 2016 study](https://sensortower.com/blog/apple-design-awards-effect) estimates **US App Store** installs. Its [individual-app chart](https://images.ctfassets.net/vfkpgemp7ek3/1457087462/646d80690ce2fe7914bf705c14917580/apple-design-awards-2016-download-ranking.png) shows Streaks at **below 500 for June 5–12**, then **roughly 3,000–4,000 for June 13–20**. These are visual approximations from unlabeled bars, not exact published totals. The two displayed windows each span eight calendar dates; retain the provider's labels. No exact Streaks daily peak or long-term daily series was obtained.

The headline **16×** and the daily peak **31,600** describe the award-winner cohort, not Streaks. Also, the chart marks Streaks as not receiving Apple featuring, while the article's prose implies non-student winners received hero banners. This inconsistency is unresolved: **do not claim a Streaks hero banner caused the rise**.

## What happened around that rise?

Streaks received the Apple Design Award at WWDC in June 2016. In the developer's [7 July retrospective](https://crunchybagel.com/apple-design-awards-2016/), Quentin describes subsequent radio/newspaper attention, Apple Store wall placement, preinstalled store-demo iPhones and a changed App Store listing appearance. Those are concrete exposure opportunities. Their exact dates and acquired installs are not disclosed, so later retail exposure cannot automatically explain the June comparison.

**Interpretation:** the timing supports award-associated publicity as a plausible contributor. It does not isolate the award's causal lift, identify which placement converted, or establish that all downloads were organic. Winning an award also gives us little direct control over repeating this result.

One tempting recommendation must be excluded. The retrospective quotes John Gruber's praise, but the [original Daring Fireball post, 3 June 2016](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2016/06/03/streaks), explicitly thanks Quentin for sponsoring the RSS feed to promote Streaks. **It was paid sponsorship.** It predates the award; no attribution data separates its delayed effects from subsequent attention.

## Practical lead: localization workflow, outcome unknown

**Follow-up, 15 October 2026:** the separate [award research](<../../Apple Design Awards/Apple Design Awards — Why Apps Win and Our 2027 Strategy.md>) recovered Apple's 2016 ceremony rationale: simplicity, visual design and Apple Watch execution. The archived presenters also mention 25 languages already in 2016. That establishes presence at award time, not a localization rollout date, download effect or selection weight. The later process interview below remains useful without establishing causality.

In the [original 13 October 2017 Ray Wenderlich interview, now on Kodeco](https://www.kodeco.com/357-creator-of-streaks-and-full-time-indie-ios-dev-a-top-dev-interview-with-quentin-zervaas), Quentin explains using I Can Localize, generating screenshots for each language/device with Fastlane Snapshot, and sending screenshots for contextual translation review. He also describes generating/uploading store screenshots. [Page 2](https://www.kodeco.com/357-creator-of-streaks-and-full-time-indie-ios-dev-a-top-dev-interview-with-quentin-zervaas/page/2) says Streaks supported **25+ languages** and recommends relevant languages, accessibility, Apple Watch and new platform capabilities to improve the chance of Apple exposure.

The useful operational idea is to review translations inside real screens and automate screenshot production across languages. This is evidence of the founder's process and advice. **No rollout date, country-level lift or before/after installs are supplied.** The later interview cannot establish localization as the cause of the June 2016 rise. It also describes localized app/store assets, not a multilingual website SEO campaign.

## Launch coverage: qualitative only

In a [July 2022 founder interview](https://syntopikon.com/interviews/quentin-zervaas/), Quentin recalls immediate success and high-profile website coverage after the June 2015 launch. He supplies no launch download count, outreach sequence, referral attribution or sponsorship breakdown. Treat this as qualitative launch history, not a verified press-outreach playbook or measured organic spike.

## Result for our marketing research

**No additional outcome-supported organic tactic established for Streaks.** There is an award-associated estimated rise, and a concrete localization workflow we can learn from. There is no measured download result for that workflow or the feature-development advice. Keep the approved bondavi localization example separate: Streaks does not independently validate its growth effect.
