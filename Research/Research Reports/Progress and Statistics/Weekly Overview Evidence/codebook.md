# Weekly overview card — codebook (2 Oct 2026)
Prefix: + praises having it · ? asks for it / misses it · - complains about it. One review can carry several codes.
PCT    one overall percentage / completion rate across all habits
DAYNM  each day's score: "8 of 10 today", a ring or % per day
FULL   perfect / full days (everything done), crowns, medals
FSTRK  a streak of perfect days / overall streak across habits
HAB    every habit side by side; which habits are strong or need attention
PREV   compared with last week / last period; "am I improving"
TREND  one overall graph or trend over time
TOT    totals: time spent, amounts, number of completions
WEEKG  the week grid of all habits at a glance (habits x days)
RPT    a weekly / monthly / yearly report or summary as such
CONF   the combined number is wrong or can't be understood (bugs included)
NONDAILY  weekly / flexible / unscheduled days or goal changes miscounted in the combined number
EXCL   wants some habits kept out of the combined number (track-only, optional, bad habits)
QUIT   quit / bad habits and how they fit the combined number
VAGUE  one combined figure alone hides the story (which habits, which areas)
CAT    by category / area / group
PERIOD a recent period (week, month) is more motivating than all-time
SHAME  the combined number discourages / pressures
SIMPLE wants fewer numbers, praises few
PART   partial credit counts in the combined number
GEN    generic "overview / stats are good", no specific number
NA     not about a combined view
HONEST  a summary that flatters or hides what was missed is disliked
WEIGHT  wants habits weighted by importance in the combined number
FORGIVE a forgiving measure (a miss doesn't wipe it) is praised or asked
PAT     patterns: best day, weekday, which days are productive, correlations
