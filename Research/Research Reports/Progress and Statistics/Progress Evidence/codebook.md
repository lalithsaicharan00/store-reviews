# Progress-page review codebook (hand-coding of refined2 hits)
Format in cls/bNNN.txt: `idx:CODE,CODE` one per line. Plain code = praise/positive mention of having it; `?CODE` = asks for it / misses it; `X...` = complaint. `NA` = not about progress/stats/history.
CAL per-habit calendar / month history view | YEAR year grid, heat map, GitHub, year in pixels | WEEK week view / weekly grid
CHART charts, graphs, trend over time | PCT completion/success %, rate, score, grade | STRK streak count, best/longest streak, streak history
TOT totals, cumulative counts/hours, averages, amounts over time | ALL overall/all-habits overview, daily score, all habits in one view
RPT weekly/monthly/yearly report, summary, year in review | PAT patterns/comparison (weekdays, habits vs habits, why missed, mood/correlation)
GRP per-category/group stats | QST quit stats (time since, relapse/reset history, attempts, best/average run, money/units avoided)
DAYD tap a day for detail / notes shown in history | LONG long-range / all-time / multi-year history, look back far | EXP export of data/stats
MILE milestones, achievements, celebration | VIS seeing progress visually motivates (chain, filling, colours) | GEN generic "see/track my progress" with no specific feature
PART partial progress / over-achievement shown | FORG forgiving, non-punitive view (no shame, skips neutral, not reset to zero)
XGATE stats/history paywalled | XWRONG stats/streaks/dates wrong | XTHIN stats too basic / missing | XFIND can't find stats / too many taps
XCONF can't understand numbers or marks | XSHAME stats/streaks demotivate, guilt, pressure | XBUG stats crash/slow/won't load | XLOST history lost/reset
XNONDAILY weekly/flexible/skip/non-scheduled days wrongly counted | XREMOVED stats feature removed | XCLUTTER too many stats/too complex
Added after batch 0: STAT stats/charts praised with no specific form | BF back-dating/editing past days as part of keeping history true
Added during coding: CONF marks and scoring made clear or configurable (distinct missed / not-logged marks, week start, day-end hour, a plain explanation of how a number is computed); ?CONF asks for it
