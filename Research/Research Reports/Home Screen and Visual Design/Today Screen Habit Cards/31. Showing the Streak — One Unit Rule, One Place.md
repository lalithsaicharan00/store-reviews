# Today Screen, Question 31: Showing the Streak — One Unit Rule, One Place

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 2. This report **revises the streak part of Question 28**.

**The feedback.**
- "58 days" on one card and "4 weeks" on another read as two different things.
- The streak moves: it sits first on some cards and second on others, after the time.
- What happens past 100 days (three digits), or past a month or a year? If we switch to months or years, the word gets longer.
- How do people prefer streaks shown?

**Basis.**
- **Counted:** how reviewers state a streak's length, across 876,180 habit-app reviews.
- **Read in full:**
  - 52 matches on weekly, monthly or yearly streaks;
  - 12 on where the streak is shown.
- **Reused:** the Notion cards C024 (streaks), C038 (the number has to be right) and C095 (never shame).
- **External:** Habitify's and Strava's help pages; guidance on tabular numbers.

---

## What to do

1. **The streak counts the habit's own period:**
   - **days** for anything done daily, including several times a day;
   - **weeks** for a weekly goal, like "3× a week".
2. **It never switches to months or years.**
   - 400 days stays "400 days", and 1,200 days is "1,200 days", with a thousands separator.
   - Months and years belong to milestone moments ("1 year!"), not the counter.
3. **Always the word, never a letter:** "58 days", "4 weeks". No "58d" or "4w".
4. **One fixed place on every card:** line 2, at the start, in a slot of fixed width.
   - Nothing sits before it, so it never moves.
   - What follows it (the frequency) starts at the same point on every card (Question 33).
5. **Digits are tabular (fixed width),** so the numbers line up down the list.
6. **The slot is empty below 2,** and when someone turns streaks off (C157). It never says "0".
7. **The weekly unit is explained by its neighbour.** A weekly habit's frequency ("3× a week") sits right after its streak, so "4 weeks" reads as "4 weeks in a row at 3 a week".

**What it looks like, with the width budget:**

| Habit | Streak slot | Next slot |
|---|---|---|
| Water, daily | 🔥 23 days | |
| Meds, 3× a day | 🔥 58 days | 3× a day |
| Call family, 3× a week | 🔥 4 weeks | 3× a week |
| A long one | 🔥 1,234 days | |

The slot is sized for "🔥 9,999 days" (27 years), so nothing realistic overflows it.

## Key findings

**1. People count long streaks in days, even in the thousands.**

| How reviewers state a streak's length | Reviews | Without Finch |
|---|---|---|
| "N-day streak" | 574 | 180 |
| "N-year streak" | 24 | 4 |
| "N-week streak" | 15 | 13 |
| "N-month streak" | 9 | 2 |

- Of the day-count mentions, **54 are 365 days or more,** and 163 more are 100–364. People write "my 515 day streak" `A10#3703`, "a streak of 1089 DAYS" `A10#28466`, "a 1,986 day streak" `A3#7180`.
- **Months and years appear when people speak loosely** ("I had almost 2 years streak" `A10#20322`), not when they read a counter. Finch accounts for most of those.
- **So three and four digits aren't a problem.** People already read, and are proud of, large day counts.
- Converting to "1 year" would also hide progress: day 366 would look the same as day 700.

**2. Weekly habits need a weekly count, but it must be clear.**
- **Wanted:** 4 reviews ask for weekly goals to count weeks.
  - "if I want to track exercising 3 times per week, I want to know how many weeks I've achieved that rather than how many consecutive days" `P3#10486`
  - "when you try to apply 4 x week, the display should follow the weekly streak goal not daily" `A43#334`
- **Confusing when unexplained:** 9 reviews find weekly streak counts confusing or wrong.
  - "I'm on a 12 week streak for a habit I started 8 weeks ago." `A7#723`
  - "The weekly streak counter is a cool idea but confusing" `P69#200`
- **External:** Habitify counts a weekly habit's streak as "the number of continuous weeks you complete your goal". Strava's streak is weekly by design.
- **So the unit follows the goal, and the goal is printed beside it.** Your confusion at "4 weeks" came from seeing it without "3× a week" next to it.

**3. The streak must stay on the list, always in one place.**
- **9 reviews** want the streak number on the main list.
  - "Why did they take away the streak number on the main page? That was my favorite part." `A31#3272`
  - "I wish streaks would also somehow be shown on the main page for 'countable' habbits, just like for yes/no habits." `P3#901`
- **The last one asks for the same treatment on every habit type.**
- **Notion C024:** removing the streak from the main list drew 52 complaints in one app, and "the streak display never moves or disappears once shipped".
- **1 review** asks to hide it on specific habits (`A50#134`). C157's off switch covers that.

**4. Numbers that sit in a column should be tabular (external).**
- With proportional digits, "11" is narrower than "88", so the units don't line up.
- Tabular (fixed-width) digits keep every "days" at the same x.
- Apple's type guidance recommends them for numbers that change or align in columns.

---

**Limits.**
- **Mostly one app:** the day-count dominance is 394 of 574 from Finch, which shows days. Without Finch it is still 180 days against 19 for weeks, months and years combined.
- **Test it:** whether "4 weeks" beside "3× a week" is understood at first glance.
