# Today Screen, Question 36: Quit Habits — A Live Counter, and Why Cutting Back Is Different

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 4, part 2. This report **revises the quit card and the "Coffee" example in Question 35**. The section placement (a "Quitting" section at the top of Today) stands.

**The feedback.**
- Is a quit habit just a clock counting the time since you stopped? Is the live counter the main thing?
- Are there different kinds of quit habit?
- What exactly goes on the card: start date, reset date?
- "Coffee 1/3 cups" isn't a quit habit. Explain it or drop it.
- Find the best quit app, take its best parts (combining several apps is fine), and fit them to our app.

**Basis.**
- **Reports reused:**
  - Days Since (App Store 3): 10,621 reviews, mean 4.77, 19,073 US ratings at 4.82. It is the best-rated quit counter in the corpus.
  - Quit Bad Habits (App Store 90).
  - The Quit Habit Decision memo and the Notion cards C019 (Quit mode), C095 and C101.
- **Read in full, across 876,180 habit-app reviews:**
  - 172 on live or second-by-second counting;
  - 52 on start and reset dates;
  - 145 on cutting back on a vice;
  - 31 on logging each time.
- **Screenshots** of Days Since, Quit Bad Habits, Quitzilla, I Am Sober and Nomo (Question 35).

## The short answer

1. **You're right: a quit habit is a live counter.** It shows the time since you stopped, counting up every second. That is what people praise most, after simplicity and the widget.
2. **There are two kinds of "doing less", and only one is a quit habit:**

   | Kind | What it tracks | Example | Where it goes |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | **Quit** | time since the last time | Smoking, alcohol, doomscrolling | **Quitting** section, live counter |
   | **Cut back** | a daily or weekly count with a **maximum** | "No more than 2 coffees a day" | a normal count habit in its day section, marked **max** |

   The "Coffee 1/3 cups" card in Question 35 was a cut-back habit, placed in the wrong section. It moves out of Quitting.
3. **The quit card shows three things, and has no button:**

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [icon]  Smoking                      12 days 04:23:07 │  ← live, ticks every second
│         Since 12 Sep     Best 45 days                 │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

   - **Live time since**, right-aligned, tabular digits: "12 days 04:23:07". Under a day it shows just the clock, "04:23:07".
   - **Since [date]**: the date it counts from, whether that's your quit date or your last slip.
   - **Best [n] days.** When the current run is your best, it says **"Your best"**.
   - **No card fill.** A quit habit has no daily goal to fill up. The ticking clock is the progress.
   - **No button.** The rare action, **Log a slip**, lives inside the habit.

## The habit's own screen (tap the card)

- **The big live counter:** "12 days 04:23:07".
- **The same time in every unit:** 292 hours · 1.7 weeks · 0.4 months. This keeps the benefit of Days Since's unit switch without a setting.
- **Since 12 Sep, 21:40.** Tap to edit, for example when you quit before you installed the app.
- **Milestones:** 1 day ✓, 7 days ✓, **30 days next**, 90 days, 1 year.
- **Best 45 days · Average 19 days · Slips 4**, then the slip history with its notes.
- **Money saved,** if the habit has a cost set.
- **Show on Today · Pause · Log a slip.**
  - Log a slip asks when it happened (just now, or an earlier time) and for an optional note, then Save, with Undo.
  - The counter restarts from that moment. History and best stay.

## Key findings

**1. The live, second-by-second count is what people love.**
- **44 reviews praise counting to the second or in real time; 1 prefers days only.**
  - "the best part is it counts to the very second" `A3#7275`
  - "it’s encouraging to watch even the seconds count up from your last bad habit" `A3#9037`
  - "i love how it does seconds and minutes, so you feel accomplished even if you just quit something" `A3#8442`
  - "Having a real-time counter helps me remember what I’m fighting for on a daily basis" `A3#4922`
  - Against: "Wish I could turn off the hours, minutes and seconds. I just need the days since." `A3#5296`
- **They also want the seconds on the always-visible surface:** "Would be great if you added minutes and seconds as well on the widget. Every second counts!" `A3#8515`
- **So the card itself ticks.** Today is our always-visible surface.
- **One setting:** with the system's Reduce Motion on, the card updates each minute and hides the seconds (reasoned: motion on a list draws the eye, and some people need that off).

**2. A quit habit is "time since", and time since is the streak.**
- Every quit app examined headlines the time since:
  - Days Since: "13 days", "5 hours";
  - Nomo: "187 days";
  - Quitzilla: "45 days, 18 hr, 33 min";
  - I Am Sober: "Sober for 23 days".
- **9 reviews praise counting up itself:** "The only app I could find that counts up." `A3#1176`
- Days Since's own list puts one line under each counter, "Started on 10 Oct" or "Reset on 14 Dec". We use one neutral word for both, **"Since"** (C095). The date matters to people: 10 reviews speak of their "clean date" or quit date, e.g. "This app is great for keeping track of my clean date." `A3#5573`

**3. Cutting back is real, but it isn't a quit habit.**
- **22 reviews ask for a limit** ("no more than", "maximum").
  - "my target is to limit my caffeine intake to 2 coffees a day. I should be able to log each coffee I drink into the app" `A13#4441`
- **6 praise it where it exists:** "days with fewer than 2 coffees" `A48#55`
- The Notion card C019 adds 23, 21 and 15 requests from three more apps.
- **It is a daily count with a ceiling, not a clock.** You log each coffee, and the day succeeds if you stay under the maximum.
- **So it sits with the other counts,** in its day section, and reads like one: **"1/2 cups max"**, then "3/2 cups max" in red when over. It never shows a ✓, since reaching the limit isn't the goal. Its streak counts days kept under the limit.
- **The word "max" is not optional:**
  - "I created a NO MORE THAN goal but it shows up as an AT LEAST goal." `A55#1140`
  - C019 notes 12 more reviews confused by an inverted tick in another app.
- **Some people cut down using a quit counter instead:** 11 Days Since reviews ("It absolutely helped my cut down drinking to a more healthy level." `A3#2414`). Both kinds serve "doing less". We offer both and let the person pick.

**4. The dates must be editable, and the slip time chosen.**
- **15 ask to backdate the start or edit a reset date,** because they quit before finding the app:
  - "for the very first entry, I would like to be able to backdate it" `A3#10490`
- **6 praise choosing when the reset happened:**
  - "finally an app that’ll let me change the reset date!!!" `A3#9447`
- Choosing the time is also the safety net against mistakes: "it’s good for if you slip up and press the reset accidentally! you can just cancel" `A3#10200`

**Limits.**
- **Mostly one app:** 42 of the 44 second-by-second reviews are about Days Since. It is the category's reference app, but the praise may partly reflect that app's style.
- **Reasoned, not reviewed:**
  - hiding the seconds under Reduce Motion;
  - dropping the card fill on quit cards.
- **Test:** whether a ticking clock on Today feels motivating or distracting.
- **Days Since's other audience:** it is also used for chores, as "days since I last did X" (443 reviews). That is a good-habit use of the same counter, noted here but not designed.
