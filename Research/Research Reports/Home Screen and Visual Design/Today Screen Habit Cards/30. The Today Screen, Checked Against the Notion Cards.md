## Open: not yet designed or tested

1. **"What's next" (C183).**
   - Now is marked on the section, but nothing marks the next undone habit inside it.
   - Proposal: within a section, habits with a set time sort by that time. The others keep your own order (C073). Then the next one is simply the first undone card after the ones already done. This needs no new badge (reasoned).
2. **Done habits "get out of the way" (C183).**
   - Done cards stay where they are by default. **Hide completed** (board J) is the option for people who want them gone.
   - Reasoned: a card that jumps away right after you tap it makes undo harder, and breaks "same place every time".
   - Keep this default, and test it.
3. **The whole-day finished look (C101).**
   - The day's ring in the strip fills up, but a finished day has no finished look.
   - C101 says design the plain completion state first, before any celebration.
4. **Large text (C171).**
   - At the largest Dynamic Type sizes, the detail line must drop to two lines, or cut only its last (goal) item.
   - The time inside a timer ring needs checking at those sizes.
5. **The feel of the tap (C069, C229).**
   - A haptic on each tap, and a fill animation on the ring, are specified but can't be shown in wireframes.
