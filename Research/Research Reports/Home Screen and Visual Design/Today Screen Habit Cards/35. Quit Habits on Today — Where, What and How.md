# Today Screen, Question 35: Quit Habits on Today — Where, What and How

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

> **Revised by Question 36:** the quit card now shows a live counter ("12 days 04:23:07"), "Since [date]" and "Best [n] days", with no fill and no button. "Cut back" habits (the Coffee example) are counts with a maximum and move out of the Quitting section into their day section.

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 4.

**The question.**
- People can also track habits they are quitting.
- Should those appear on Today? If so, in which section, since the day is split into times and a quit habit has no time or reminder?
- What should the card show? For a quit habit the time since the last slip and the streak are the same number. Do we need both?

**Basis.**
- **Settled already:**
  - the Quit Habit Decision memo and Notion card C019 ("Quit mode"): time since the last slip, not a streak; a slip is logged and never wipes history; best-ever beside current; allowances as well as zero; milestones 1, 7, 30, 90 days;
  - C095 (never shame a missed day).
- **Read in full:**
  - 34 reviews on where quit counters are shown;
  - 30 on accidental resets;
  - 115 on what a quit counter should display (every quit-app review in the set was read).
- **Counted:** the words people use for this kind of habit.
- **Screenshots examined** (listing images, current):
  - Days Since (App Store 3) and Quit Bad Habits (App Store 90);
  - Habit Tracker (App Store 1) and Strides (App Store 48);
  - I Am Sober, Nomo, Quitzilla.

Competitor screens were used as a map of options. Each choice below is backed by reviews or labelled as reasoned (CLAUDE.md rule).

---

## What to do

**1. Yes, show them on Today, in their own section at the top: "Quitting".**
- It sits above Anytime and has no time. It hides itself when there are no quit habits.
- It collapses like any section. It isn't part of the day's count (5/11), because nothing in it is a to-do.
- Habits you're **cutting back** on (for example "no more than 3 coffees") live here too.
- Each quit habit has a **Show on Today** switch, for people who find the number a trigger.

**2. A quit habit uses the same card, and each place keeps its meaning.**

| Place on the card | Normal habit | Quit (none at all) | Cutting back (a limit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card fill | today's progress | progress to the next milestone | how much of today's allowance is used |
| Line 2 | 🔥 streak | **Best 45 days** | 🔥 days kept within the limit |
| Right text | 5/8 glasses | **12/30 days** (time since / next milestone) | 1/3 coffees; over the limit, "4/3 coffees" in red |
| Round button | ✓ / + / ▶ | **none** | + |

**3. One number, not two.**
- For a quit habit, the time since the last slip **is** the streak, so it appears once, on the right, where every card shows "how far".
- Line 2 shows the one different number worth having: the **best ever**. C019 asks for it beside the current count.

**4. Units follow the streak rule (Question 31), with one addition.**
- Under a day, the count is in **hours**, against the first milestone: "5/24 h".
- After that, **days**, which never switch to months or years: "12/30 days", "400/540 days".
- Changing the unit (weeks, months) is a setting inside the habit, not on Today.

**5. Milestones set the goal:** 1, 7, 30 and 90 days, then 180 and 365, then every year. The fill runs from one milestone to the next.

**6. No slip button on the card.**
- A slip is logged from inside the habit: **Log a slip**, then when it happened, an optional note, and **Save**. An undo bar follows.
- The count restarts; the history and the best stay.

**7. Words.** The section is "Quitting", never "Bad habits". The button in the habit says "Log a slip", never "Reset".

## Key findings

**1. Seeing the number every day is the whole mechanism, so it belongs on Today.**
- 24 Days Since reviews say that seeing the counter every day is what keeps them going:
  - "It is so nice to see the counter on my Home Screen as a daily reminder of all the hard work I’ve put in so far." `A3#9408`
  - "“Days Since…” is the first thing I see when I unlock my phone. 240 days later and I’m still going." `A3#7508`
- The quit reports agree. In Days Since, the widget was the second-largest praise theme. In App Store 90, a widget is the top request, "because without one you can forget the app is installed".
- Today is our equivalent of that glance, so the counter should be there.
- **The opposite need is real too:**
  - "seeing the longest streak and average streak ruins the progress if I keep comparing myself to the past" `A3#9621`
  - A Days Since user doesn't want "a visual cue to stop being sober" (report 3).
  - Hence the per-habit **Show on Today** switch (C157, every pressure has an off switch).

**2. One list, but its own section (evidence plus reasoning).**
- **Evidence for one list:**
  - Notion C019: Done's "build and quit habits in one list" is praised by 96 reviewers, and Way of Life's good and bad habits in one grid by 73.
  - "good to see good habits and bad habits together" `P22#1292`
- **Only 2 prefer them apart:**
  - one asks for "A second tab for bad habits" `P3#11615`;
  - one prefers "the main screen of reaching daily goals" `A23#5593`.
- **So:** the same screen, not a separate tab.
- **Why its own section (reasoned from first principles):**
  - Day sections answer "when do I do this?". A quit habit has no when: it runs all day, every day.
  - Putting it in Morning or Anytime would make it look like a task to tick, and it would inflate the day's count with things that can't be "done".
  - A section that sits first, above the timed day, reads as "what I'm keeping up all day", then "what I'm doing today".

**3. Time since and streak are one number; the best is the useful second one.**
- **Every quit app examined shows the time since as the headline:**
  - Days Since: "13 days", "5 hours";
  - Nomo: "187 days";
  - I Am Sober: "Sober for 23 days";
  - Quitzilla: "45 days, 18 hr, 33 min".
- None shows a separate streak beside it.
- **The longest streak is loved:** 17 reviews praise it. "the best part imo is the ‘longest streak’ feature" `A3#2427`
- So the card shows time since and best, never time since and streak.

**4. Why "12/30 days" and a fill toward the next milestone.**
- **Screens:**
  - Quit Bad Habits' list shows each habit with a ring toward its "Current goal 7 days".
  - Quitzilla shows a bar toward "3 months 50%".
  - Nomo shows a ring around the day count.
- **Reviews:**
  - App Store 90's step-up goals are a theme there (25 reviews on goals, including 15 on rigid steps: "after 7 days straight to 30").
  - Milestones are where the Days Since corpus is happiest (decision memo).
- **Reasoned:**
  - "amount/goal + unit" and a filling card are already how every other card reads (Question 34), so a quit card needs no new way of reading.
  - The milestone gives the fill a meaning. A bare "12 days" would leave the fill unexplained.
  - The steps start close together (1, 7, 30), because 7 → 30 is the gap reviewers call too far.

**5. Hours at the start, days after that.**
- 4 reviews want hours early:
  - "sometimes I last 1 day 23 hours without addiction and it‘s gonna show “1 day” only" `A3#4585`
  - Report 3 calls hour-to-year flexibility "what makes day 1 survivable" (117 reviews).
- Showing hours under a day also avoids the "day 0" problem. "when you start or reset a counter instead of saying day 1, it’s day zero" `A3#8629`
- Past day 1, the card keeps days, like every streak (Question 31).
- Some people switch units as they go ("I moved from showing hours, then days then weeks and now I am using months" `A3#2376`), so unit switching lives in the habit's settings.

**6. Never a one-tap slip.**
- 15 reviews describe resetting by accident with a single button:
  - "I accidentally reset my timer so much because it’s the only button on the widget" `A3#716`
  - "it is too easy to accidentally hit" `A3#7929`
  - "there wasn’t a confirmation on resets and I accidentally reset my sobriety time a few times" `A3#7349`
- Report 3 counts 33 accidental widget resets.
- A confirmation step is praised where it exists: "if you slip up and press the reset accidentally! you can just cancel, so crisis averted" `A3#10200`
- **Screens:**
  - Quit Bad Habits, Nomo and Quitzilla all keep the relapse action inside the habit.
  - Days Since's one-tap widget reset is the source of the complaints.
- So the quit card has no button. The rare, heavy action takes two deliberate steps and is undoable.

**7. "Quitting", not "Bad habits".**

| What reviewers call these habits | Reviews |
|---|---|
| "bad habits" | 2,716 |
| "sober / sobriety" | 498 |
| "quitting" | 446 |
| "breaking habits" | 416 |
| "cutting back / down" | 98 |

- "Bad habits" is the common word, but as a heading on someone's own day it is a verdict.
- C095 (never shame) and C019 ("I’d rather not see TO DO next to bad habits") point to neutral words.
- "Quitting" is the most common neutral term, and it says what the person is doing (reasoned).
- "Log a slip" rather than "Reset" follows C095 and the memo's "a relapse must not wipe history".

---

**Limits.**
- **Section placement is reasoned:** "Quitting" first, above Anytime. Test whether people expect it at the top, and whether the name fits limit habits.
- **"12/30 days" needs a first-read test.** It must read as "12 days, next milestone 30", not "12 of 30 days".
- **The 24 "daily glance" reviews mostly describe the phone's home-screen widget,** not an in-app list. Today stands in for that glance by inference.
