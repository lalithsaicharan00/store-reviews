# Today Screen, Question 33: Fixed Places on the Card — Time, Streak, Frequency

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

> **Revised by Question 34:** the frequency words are dropped; line 2 is streak then time (fixed slots). The time moves from the top right to line 2.

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round 2. This report **revises the layout part of Question 28**.

**The feedback.**
- Line 2 was one flowing line (time · streak · goal), so each item landed in a different spot depending on what came before it.
- Each kind of information must live in the same place on every card:
  - reminder time with reminder time;
  - streak with streak;
  - habit type and frequency with habit type.
- "Twice a day" beside "3 times a day" beside "3× a week" is three grammars for one idea.
- There is still too much on the card.

**Basis.**
- **Reasoned from first principles:** what the eye does when scanning a list.
- **Reused:** Question 28's time evidence (10 reviews ask to see a habit's time on the list) and Questions 31–32.
- **External:** guidance on scanning, and on numerals.

## What to do

**A fixed grid, the same on every card:**

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [icon]  Meds                        14:00  ┌─────┐ │
│         🔥 58 days    3× a day             │  ✓  │ │
│                                            └─────┘ │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
          └ streak ─┘ └ frequency ┘  └time┘  └button┘
```

| Slot | Where | Shows | When it is empty |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Title** | line 1, left | the habit's name; cut with "…" if long | never |
| **Time** | line 1, right, beside the button | the habit's next set time today, like **14:00** | no set time, or all of today's times have passed |
| **Streak** | line 2, left, fixed width | **🔥 58 days** or **🔥 4 weeks** (Question 31) | under 2, or streaks turned off |
| **Frequency** | line 2, always starting at the same x after the streak slot | **3× a day**, **3× a week**, **Mon Wed Fri**, **Every 2 days** | the habit is every day |
| **Button** | right edge | amount/goal and unit, or ✓ (Question 32) | never |

**The rules:**
1. **Every slot keeps its place when another is empty.**
   - A missing time leaves a gap; it doesn't pull anything across.
   - A missing streak leaves its slot blank, so the frequency still starts at the same x.
   - That is what lets the eye run straight down one column.
2. **One grammar for frequency:** a numeral and "×", then the period: "2× a day", "3× a week", "1× a month". Named days use three letters: "Mon Wed Fri".
   - Never "twice", "three times" or "thrice".
   - "Every day" isn't printed: an empty slot means daily. Most habits are daily, and the same words on most cards would be noise.
3. **The goal and unit live only in the button** ("5/8 glasses"). They are not repeated on line 2.
4. **Removed from the card:** the goal text on line 2, the group dot and the note icon (Question 28).
5. **Tabular digits** for time, streak and button values, so the numbers line up down the list.

## Key findings

**1. Columns are what make a list scannable (external, and reasoned from first principles).**
- Scannability guidance says to keep each item in the same position and layout on every row.
- A flowing line breaks that as soon as one item is optional: on one card the streak comes first, on the next it comes after a time.
- Fixed slots turn the list into a quiet table. To check your streaks, you look down one column; to check times, another.

**2. Why the time goes top right (reasoned from first principles).**
- The time answers "when?", and the button answers "done yet?". People read them together ("14:00, not done"), so they sit side by side.
- Right-aligned against the button, every time ends at the same x, and tabular digits make "08:00" and "21:00" line up exactly.
- The time is shown when set. Question 28 found 10 reviews asking for it and none objecting.

**3. Why the streak goes first on line 2.**
- It is the one number almost every card has.
- Anchoring it under the title makes it the most stable column after the titles themselves.
- Notion C024 says the streak display "never moves or disappears once shipped".

**4. Why numerals, and one grammar (external).**
- NN/g: "Write numbers with digits, not letters (23, not twenty-three)." Its eye-tracking found that numerals stand out to peripheral vision.
- A single pattern ("N× a period") lets the eye compare 2× a day with 3× a week without reading.

**5. The card now carries at most five things, and most carry three.**
- A typical daily habit shows its title, its streak and its button.
- Time and frequency appear only on habits that have them, and always in the same place.

**Limits.**
- **Reasoned, not reviewed:** the grid is built from scanning principles, not from reviews of this layout. Test:
  - whether the empty frequency slot reads as "every day";
  - whether a top-right time reads as the habit's time.
