# Today Screen, Question 29: One Button for Every Habit — Progress and Logging Together

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

> **Revised by Question 32:** the ring became one box that always shows amount/goal and unit (a timer shows "8/20 min", a walk "5.2k/8k steps"), with one look for done. No ▶ or +1 icons.

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Day cards round.

**Screen.** The right side of the card changes with the habit type:
- a ring on its own;
- a ring with "+1" in it;
- a ▶ button **outside** the ring, next to an empty ring;
- a done disc.

The timer's ▶ takes extra width, and for count and timer habits the progress sits in one place and the action in another.

**The feedback.**
- Can progress and logging be one button?
- Fold the timer into the circle.
- Every habit type should work the same way, with logging always in the same place: "if I look here, I always find X".

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 13 matches on tapping to add one to a count, and 2 on starting a timer from the list.
- **Reused:** the Notion cards C143, C048, C264, C240, C229, C069, C223, C066 and C043, and the Day Structure decision on steps.
- **Reasoned from first principles:** where the reviews are silent (timer, checklist and skip states).

---

## What to do

**One rule:** the button on the right always shows **today's progress**, and tapping it always does **the next step**.

| Habit type | Before | In progress | Done | One tap does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yes / no | empty ring | — | filled ✓ | done; tap again to undo |
| Count (Water 8) | ring, "0/8" | ring fills, "5/8" | filled ✓ | +1, and keeps counting past the goal |
| Timer (Read 20 min) | ring with ▶ | ring fills, time left "12:40" | filled ✓ | start or pause; ticks itself at the goal |
| Steps (Skincare 3) | ring, "0/3" | ring fills, "1/3" | filled ✓ | ticks the next step |
| Times a week (Call family 3×) | empty ring | — | filled ✓ | today done; "2 of 3 this week" is in the detail line |
| Several times a day (Meds 3×) | one card per time, each a yes/no, in its day section | — | filled ✓ | done for that time |
| Skipped | dashed ring with "–" | — | — | set from the hold menu; the detail line says "Skipped" |

**The rest of the card:**
- **Tap anywhere else on the card** to open the habit: its history, notes, steps and edit.
- **Press and hold the button** for a menu: Undo, Skip today, Enter a number, Mark all steps done, Add a note.
- **After every tap,** a small bar appears for a few seconds, like "Water 6/8 · Undo". Every habit type undoes the same way.
- **The button is 40 pt to the eye and 44 pt to tap,** and it sits on the same edge on every card.

---

## Key findings

**1. People ask for exactly this: tap to add one, with no window.**
- **8 of the 13 matches** ask to increment with a single tap.
  - "tapping a progress bar should increment it, not necessarily complete it" `A1#45243`
  - "the option to simply tap a habit to increment it, rather than having to bring up a new window and type in a number" `P2#2567`
  - "It would be easier if I could just tap the habit to increase the count by one." `A1#56112`
- **One reviewer also names the rest of our rule:** "A simple tap adds the increment. Tap and hold can open each habit to its" own screen `A23#4826`
- **Notion "Tap again: 3 of 8" (C143)** says each tap adds one, shows "3 of 8" with a filling mark, and can undo one. **"Numbers, not just ticks" (C048)** says partial isn't failure and there's no ceiling.

**2. Nothing may sit between the tap and done.**
- **Notion C264 ("Not one more tap") and C240 ("Nothing between the tap and done")** say one gesture logs, nothing opens, and the extras are optional.
- The hold menu and the tap-the-card behaviour keep every extra off the main path.
- **Notion "Let them take the tap back" (C223)** asks for a visible undo, and big targets away from Info.
  - The undo bar is visible.
  - The button's target is kept apart from the card's open-habit area by the button's own 44 pt.

**3. The timer belongs inside the ring (Notion C066, and first principles).**
- C066 says: the timer lives on the habit, finishing ticks it, it counts past the goal, and nothing is added for non-timer habits.
- **Reasoned from first principles:**
  - A ▶ outside the ring is a second control with a second meaning, and it takes width from the title.
  - The ring already means "how far through today's goal". For a timer, that is time done over the goal.
  - So ▶ goes inside the ring before starting. The ring fills as it runs, and the time left shows inside.
- Only 2 reviews mention starting a timer from the list, both about bugs. That is too few for evidence either way.

**4. One place, one meaning, for every type (reasoned from first principles).**
- **Someone scanning Today asks two questions per card: "is it done?" and "what do I do?"** If the answer is always in the same spot, in the same form, they learn it once.
- **Separate +1 and ▶ buttons break that.** Each type would then teach its own control.
- **Every type differs only in what "the next step" is:**
  - done, for a yes/no;
  - +1, for a count;
  - start, for a timer;
  - the next step, for steps.
- **Times-a-week habits:** the day's action is still yes/no. The week's progress is a count, so it goes in the detail line's goal slot ("2 of 3 this week").
  - Showing week progress in the button would make one card's ring mean "this week" while every other ring means "today".
- **Several-times-a-day habits with set times** (Meds at 08:00, 14:00, 20:00) appear as one card per time, in their day sections.
  - The Day Structure decision already says morning and evening occurrences are checked separately.
  - Water, with no set times, stays one count card.

**5. Steps: tap for the next one, hold to finish them all.**
- The Day Structure decision asks for steps collapsed under the habit ("2 of 3 steps") and a **Mark all done** shortcut.
- A tap ticks the next step, so the ring works like a count. The hold menu offers Mark all done.
- Tapping the card opens the named steps, to tick them out of order.

**6. Skipped is its own state.**
- **Notion "An empty square means three things" (C256)** says done, skipped, missed and not-yet-logged must look different.
- A dashed ring with "–", plus "Skipped" at the end of the detail line, keeps a skip from looking like "not done yet".

---

**Limits.**
- **Reasoned, not reviewed:** the timer, steps, skip and times-a-week behaviour have little or no direct review evidence. Test them:
  - whether people find the hold menu;
  - whether "tap = next step" feels right for steps;
  - whether the time left fits inside a 40 pt ring at large text sizes.
- **Hold vs. tap:** a press-and-hold to complete is praised in the Notion cards C229 and C069, and one reviewer found hold glitchy (`A23#4826`). Tap stays the main gesture. Hold opens the menu.
