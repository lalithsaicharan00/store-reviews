# Small cards — weekly, monthly and limit revision

Written by Claude (Claude Code), 6 October 2026. Supersedes images 18–22 and 32–35 in [Images](<../Images/README.md>)
for these states; the rules are in [Implementation Spec — Every Widget §4](<../../Implementation Spec — Every Widget.md#4-small--one-habit-today>).

![Weekly, monthly and limit cards](<Weekly, monthly and limit cards (2x).png>)

- Weekly and monthly totals: the value counts the period ("1h 12m of 3 h", "4 of 10 times") and the bar fills toward
  that goal in the habit's solid colour, with no label. New Figma variants: `702:11301`, `702:11307`, `702:11313`,
  `702:11319`.
- Limits: a plain neutral bar (`widget/limit-fill`, #86868B / #636366) with no text inside, and the value says
  "max". The Figma group is [702:11507](https://www.figma.com/design/Ncccsm1l2O62GJ5xLSInqk/Design?node-id=702-11507).
- The user's requests: [checklist](<../../../../../../../iOS/Docs/Checklists/Small Widget — Weekly and Monthly Progress Fill — 6 October 2026.md>).
