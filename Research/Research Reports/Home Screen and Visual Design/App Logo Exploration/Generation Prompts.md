# App Logo Exploration — Generation Prompts

Written by Codex, 11 October 2026. Built-in image generation, four separate concepts. These are exploratory raster assets, not finalized brand marks.

## A — Open Orbit

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single 1024 by 1024 square raster app icon concept for Often Enough, a calm, native habit tracker. Brand idea: Habits grow through repetition. You choose how often is enough. Minimal, simple, exceptionally memorable, custom designed graphic silhouette. Flat warm-white (#FAF9F6) symbol on an opaque edge-to-edge charcoal (#242424) square background. Center symbol in about 58 percent of canvas width with generous clear margins. Sharp clean antialiased edges and consistent optical balance. No text, letters written as typography, words, labels, watermark, mockup, border, outer rounded corners, shadows, gradients, textures, 3D, glass, check marks, arrows, calendar, flames, leaves, standard infinity sign or generic loading spinner. One icon only, not a concept sheet. Concept A / Open Orbit: a bold almost-circular O-shaped band made of two broad curved pieces. The longer lower-left C-shaped piece gently rises to meet a shorter top-right curved segment which is slightly displaced radially outward. Two deliberate spacious breaks, one upper-left and one lower-right. A subtle echo in the two repeated curved parts, distinctive offset silhouette. It should suggest returning to a habit with breathing room, not a progress indicator or power button. The outer form is softly asymmetric, its center is clean and open. Only two solid white pieces.
