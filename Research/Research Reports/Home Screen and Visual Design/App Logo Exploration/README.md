## Round 02 — meaning before form

| Direction | Meaning defined before generation | Design risk to check |
|---|---|---|
| **Growth rings** | Repeated small increments create lasting growth; the circular form also relates to Often's O | Avoid a target, fingerprint or loading indicator; keep gaps wide |
| **Building blocks** | Small repeated actions add up to something stable | Avoid a generic statistics chart or a menu; keep a compact constructed silhouette |
| **Woven strands** | Repeated small strands create strength together | Avoid a complicated knot, generic infinity symbol or fragile tiny crossings |

The woven generation read as a camera shutter and was set aside. Its replacement, **Built Arch**, starts from the idea that repeated small blocks form a lasting whole. The arch's open circular outline also retains a connection to Often's O. This is a concrete constructed metaphor, not a letter trick added after generation.

These are design hypotheses from the accepted brief, not proven meanings or measured memorability. No logo is finalized until the user chooses it. The Round 02 building-block edit improved the lengths, but the bars are still not exactly equal: precise module geometry remains an explicit limitation. Both the spiral and the arch have possible other associations, which the user's review should test.
