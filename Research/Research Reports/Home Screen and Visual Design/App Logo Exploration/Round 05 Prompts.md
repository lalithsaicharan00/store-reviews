## Initial A — Joined Steps

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Often Enough iOS app icon, a single 1024x1024 square raster concept. Agreed meaning: Small actions, repeated often enough, build something lasting. Client rejected generic growth rings, unfinished arches, detached progress bars and OE letter monograms. Now design a memorable construction symbol from a few repeated building blocks. It must feel like a considered adult brand mark, calm and dependable, with a clear distinctive silhouette and large open negative spaces at 29 pixels. Build the identity from how equal small pieces fit together into a stable whole. No letters or numbers anywhere. No arrow, graph, check, ring, spiral, infinity, camera shutter, calendar, house or pictorial toy bricks. No illustration or 3D. Exactly two solid flat colors: warm white #F6F5F2 mark and perfectly uniform charcoal #262626 canvas. Absolutely no gradient, texture, shadow, bevel, light bloom or background lighting. Single centered mark occupying around 55% of width and height, generous margins, crisp geometry, consistent corner radius and proportion. No icon border, no rounded canvas mask, no explanatory text. Output one finished-looking symbol, not a sheet of sketches.
A — Joined Steps. Construct a compact emblem from exactly THREE identical stout rounded-square building tiles arranged on a rising diagonal from lower-left to upper-right. Each tile overlaps and locks into the next with a broad shared joint; do not leave them floating or merely touching at a corner. Reduce the union to a strong stepped outline, with two short generous seam notches that hint at the three pieces while leaving the whole structurally connected. The three square units, their equal size and their joining should be immediately visible. Use squarish tiles, not horizontal bars. A shared one-unit geometry gives the outer outline a distinctive three-step rhythm. Make the symbol compact, optically centered and stable, with restrained corner softening and a silhouette that can be drawn from memory. Avoid making a thin diagonal chain, a pixel staircase, a road or an up-right arrow.

## Connectivity refinement — shortlisted soft version

Use case: precise-object-edit, logo-brand. Edit the supplied three-tile mark for Often Enough. The crucial change is CONNECTIVITY: merge the THREE diagonal square building units into ONE cohesive solid white symbol. Keep their same diagonal lower-left to upper-right placement and overall compact stepped outline, but close the two large gaps by expanding broad structural bridges between each pair. Bridges must be at least half a tile's width, not thin necks. Leave only TWO short inset diagonal charcoal seam notches along the edge, not gaps crossing through the symbol. Preserve generous margins and one 1024x1024 full square master. No letters, text or arrowhead. Meaning: small repeated building units create one lasting whole.

## Curation and observed limitations

Initial A did not connect its three tiles as requested; the connectivity edit did. Initial B remained a literal four-brick arrangement and was set aside by the agent, not declared rejected by the user. The two final studies share the connected stepped construction and two diagonal notches. The tighter version reduces rounding but does not guarantee the requested exact eight-pixel radius. Subtle tonal variation remains despite flat-color instructions. Both remain concepts for user review.
