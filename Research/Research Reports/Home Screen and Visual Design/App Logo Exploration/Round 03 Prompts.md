# Round 03 — Building Blocks Refinement Prompts

Written by Codex, 11 October 2026. Built-in image generation; the Round 02 Building Blocks PNG is the starting reference. The brief and rejections are recorded in README.md.

## A — Stepped E

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Often Enough iOS app icon concept, one 1024x1024 square raster image. Brand meaning agreed with the client BEFORE form: 'Small actions, repeated often enough, build something lasting.' Refine the supplied Building Blocks study into a distinctive proprietary mark, with more deliberate typography and optical balance. Supplied image is a starting reference only: retain the idea of small building units, monochrome near-white ink and a deep charcoal flat square background; redesign the mark. Adult, calm, exact, professional identity work. Single centered flat symbol occupying around 55% of the square with generous margins. Large clear counters and deliberate module construction that reads at 29 pixels. Use absolutely flat solid colors, clean sharp raster edges, no texture, shading, highlights, shadows, bevels, gradients, presentation mockups, borders or text. Do not produce a generic chart, hamburger menu, wifi, stairs alone, growing rings, spiral, arch, checkmark or infinity symbol. One icon only, no explanation labels.
Direction A — Stepped e: Design a custom lowercase e, built from three thick horizontal building courses linked on the left into one compact solid glyph. Its horizontal courses rise gently from lower-left to upper-right through one-unit offsets, retaining the rising rhythm of the reference. The overall silhouette must still read as an e, never as detached dashes. Give the e a large open right-side aperture and a rectangular internal counter. The construction should feel like three small actions fitted into one stable letter. Use a shared square module, constant thickness, precisely repeated spacing and modest softened outside corners. The distinctive identifying feature is a single staircase cut inside the e's open mouth, echoed by the stepped outside edge. Keep it bold, balanced, minimal and immediately drawable from memory.

The three pieces should meet to form one stable whole while two broad horizontal charcoal seam cuts reveal their repeated construction. Offset the three pieces very slightly like brick courses; shape their right endpoints into one deliberate diagonal stepped cut rather than three separate equal-ended bars. The identifying form is an e assembled through repetition, not a font letter with arbitrary decorative lines. Constant weight, same corner treatment, precise spacing and optical centering. Keep broad recognizable solid mass and roomy negative spaces at tiny size.

## B — Small-size seam refinement

Use case: precise-object-edit; logo-brand refinement. Edit this supplied Often Enough modular E icon. Keep the exact three-piece geometric E silhouette, two left corner curves, diagonal-cut right endpoints, relative widths, centering and overall size. Change only the two horizontal separation seams and surface rendering: increase both seams to a consistent roughly 40 pixels on a 1024 square master so the three repeated building pieces remain perceptible at 29 pixels. Preserve the counter and aperture shapes. Make the background a perfectly uniform solid #262626 and the mark perfectly uniform #F6F5F2; remove every gradient, shadow, texture and highlight. Crisp flat logo, not a 3D object. Single full square 1024x1024 opaque raster master, no rounding of the canvas, no extra text, no border, no other elements.

## Observed output limits

The generator drew uppercase E-like silhouettes despite the lowercase-e specification. The initial B was superseded by the wider-seam edit. Both final candidates retain subtle tonal variation despite the flat-color request; they are exploratory rasters, not exact geometric/color specifications.
