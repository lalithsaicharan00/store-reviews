# Today Screen, Question 23: Deleting Day Sections and Starting From None

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

> **Revised by Question 25:** there is no "Set a time" switch (Anytime is the only untimed section). Only the latest section has an end, and the first timed section must have one.

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3 follow-up.

**The feedback.**
- Tapping a section in the Day sections editor must open a screen with **Delete**. It's missing.
- Should the defaults (Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening) be deletable at all?
- If someone deletes all of them and then adds a first section, they should be able to set a start *and* an end time, or no time at all. Can end times fill in by themselves?

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 31 new matches on deleting, removing or hiding sections.
- **Reused:** the Day Structure reports (125 hand-read reviews on customising sections, 88 on hidden and whole-day views, and the product model).
- **External:** Habitify's time-of-day help.

---

## What to do

1. **Tapping a section opens Edit section:**
   - **Name**;
   - **Set a time** (on/off), then **Starts** and **Ends**;
   - **Show on Today**, which hides the section without deleting it;
   - **Delete section**, in red, at the bottom.
2. **Deleting asks once:** "Delete Morning? Its 4 habits move to **Anytime** *(change)*. Their history stays."
3. **Morning, Afternoon and Evening can be deleted. Anytime can't.**
   - Anytime is the home for habits with no time, and for habits whose section was deleted.
   - It can be renamed, and it hides itself on Today when it's empty.
   - When Anytime is the only section left, Today is one plain list with no headers. That is the same as turning sections off.
4. **Starting from none.** With only Anytime left, the editor explains: "Split your day into sections, like Morning or Before work." Then **+ Add section**.
   - The first section can have **no time**, or **both a start and an end**.
5. **Times fill themselves in, but stay editable:**
   - **Starts** defaults to where the previous section ends.
   - **Ends** defaults to where the next one starts, or to when the new day starts if nothing follows.
   - Changing a time that overlaps a neighbour moves the neighbour's edge. A line says so: "Afternoon now starts at 13:00."
   - A gap is allowed. During a gap no section is marked **Now**, and habits are unaffected.
6. **Untimed sections sit at the top or bottom,** like Anytime, since they have no time to sort by.

## Key findings

**1. People do want to remove defaults they don't use.**
- "it's kind of annoying to not be able to turn off the afternoon routine. I don't need it" `A24#37029`
- "I can't get rid of the Afternoon Area." `A33#3193`
- "Is there a way to disable the morning/noon/evening times alltogether?" `P33#1859`
- Day Structure: 17 want the time-of-day split switched off entirely, 3 want to turn off one section, and 47 want more or different ones.

**2. The leading app already allows it, but keeps one block.**
- Habitify: "You can delete any time block you no longer need, including the default ones (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)."
- It also "requires you to have at least one Time of Day block at all times".
- Our Anytime plays that role, and makes the fallback for habits explicit.

**3. Start and end are both expected, and time is optional.**
- Habitify has you "Set the Start Time and End Time" for a block.
- Day Structure: 28 want to change the start and end of each section.
- The product model says "A preferred time window is optional. 'After work' can stay untimed."
- Automatic ends (the next section's start) avoid most gaps and overlaps. Editable ends cover the rest.

**4. Deleting must never lose habits.**
- Round 1 found 13 complaints of accidental changes.
- The Day Structure report's rule for groups ("Deleting a group preserves its habits and history") applies equally to sections.
