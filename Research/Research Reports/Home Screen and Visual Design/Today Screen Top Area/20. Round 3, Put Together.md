## What stays from round 2

- The two-row header: the title (now a date picker only), the View button, and +.
- The week strip in Day view, the "Today" jump when away, and the active-filter chips.
- The View button now uses a **sliders** icon. It still fills and shows a count only when a filter is on, because a layout choice isn't a filter.
