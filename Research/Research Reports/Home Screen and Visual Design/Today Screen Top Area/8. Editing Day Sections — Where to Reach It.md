# Today Screen, Question 8: Editing Day Sections — Where to Reach It

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** Home is one list split into day sections: Anytime, Morning 06:00, Afternoon 12:00, Evening 18:00. Each header shows a collapse chevron, the name, the start time, a count ("1/2") and a Start button. Nothing on the screen says how to change them.

**The question.** People can add sections, change their start times, set when the day starts, and turn sections off. Where should the way in be, and what should it look like?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for edit / delete / rearrange / "how do I change", time of day, day start, long press and edit mode.
- **Read in full:** all 672 habit-app matches. 286 relevant.
- **Earlier evidence:** the Day Structure report (how sections should work) and the Organization report (visible reorder), plus Question 1's midnight finding.
- **External:** Apple's guidance on context menus, gestures and lists.

---

## What to do

1. **One place holds the whole day: a "Your day" sheet.**
   - It lists the sections in time order, each with its start time. A section ends where the next one starts.
   - It also holds **Day starts at 04:00**, **+ Add section**, and a switch, **Show sections on Today**.
   - Sections sort by start time, so there is no separate reorder for them (Day Structure report).
2. **Give it four ways in, and put none of them in the top bar.**
   - **End of the list:** one quiet row after the last habit: **Reorder · Edit day**. Anyone who scrolls to the bottom sees it, and it takes no room at the top.
   - **Section header, long press:** a short menu with **Edit Morning…**, **Add section** and **Reorder habits**. "Edit Morning…" opens the sheet at that section.
   - **Filter menu (All ▾):** its last item is **Edit sections and groups…**. People thinking about what the list shows will look there.
   - **Me → Settings → Your day:** the permanent home, for people who look in settings first.
3. **Show the day-start setting when it matters.**
   - Between midnight and the day-start time, the period line reads **"Still Thursday · until 04:00"**, and tapping the time opens the setting.
   - If someone with the default midnight start logs a habit shortly after midnight, show a one-time tip: **"Up late? Let your day end at 3:00. Change"**.
4. **For habits:**
   - **Tap the circle** to complete. That is the only way to complete.
   - **Tap the row** to open the habit, with a visible **Edit** button.
   - **Long press the row** for a menu: Edit, Move to section, Skip today, Delete.
   - **Hold and drag** to reorder. Dropping a habit in another section moves its time of day.
   - Long press **never** completes a habit.
5. **Teach the hidden gestures once.** The first time someone has three or more habits, show one line: "Hold a habit to move it or edit it." After that, the visible **Reorder · Edit day** row does the job.
6. **No global Edit button at the top.** The top area stays as lean as Question 5 left it.

---

## Key findings

**1. The biggest editing problem is not finding it at all.**
- 174 of the 286 relevant reviews say the person could not find how to edit, delete or rearrange something. They come from 46 apps, with a mean rating of 3.33★.
- "Edit and delete should be as easy to find as the big plus button." `P11#5753`
- "took me a while to figure out that the features I was looking for were hidden in settings" `P2#11332`
- Being in settings is not the problem. Being *only* in settings is: "Allows to rearrange order and archive habits but it" is "a bit hidden in the settings menu" `A1#2380`.

**2. People want to shape their day, and many can't find where.**
- **39 reviews ask for it**, from 17 apps:
  - 22 are about when the day starts or ends: "You should add some settings like 'Day Starts At' and 'Day ends at' for people having entirely different schedule" `P8#9485`.
  - 17 are about sections or categories themselves: adding, renaming, retiming or removing them.
- **18 couldn't find it.** 15 of those come from Me+ and Fabulous, where time of day is buried inside each habit's settings:
  - "it just doesn’t appear for me under the repeat section. This means all my tasks just appear in one long list" `A4#18502`
- **Some apps had the setting and people still missed it:**
  - "It would be perfect, if I could configure that my day starts at 03:00" `A23#3485`. The same reviewer later found that "there is an option for that in settings already".
- Question 1 found 154 midnight-rollover reviews in 44 apps. That is the moment the day-start setting matters, so the hint belongs there.

**3. Long press is wanted, but only as a shortcut.**
- 30 reviews ask for a long press, split three ways:
  - 9 to drag and reorder: "changing the poisition of habits from top to bottom by long press and drag like we can do with apps on mobile home screen" `P7#330`
  - 9 to open edit options: "an option to edit the category in the long-tap menu would be nice, right now you have to go into a habit first" `P9#160`
  - 9 to guard completion against mistakes: "I like that you can choose long press to ckeck the day so you won't do it by mistake" `P3#5867`
- 16 complain about hidden press-and-hold controls. 9 of them are Streaks users:
  - "A mess of hidden UI elements behind presses and long presses. Just use buttons." `A23#3300`
  - "My instinct is to long press the task itself to edit it but, of course, that triggers the task to be marked as done." `A23#4809`
  - "inform users reordering needs a long press, so there's no confusion." `P9#1037`
- So long press should open a menu and start a drag, like the iPhone home screen. It must never complete a habit, and every action it offers needs a visible twin.

**4. People expect to edit the thing itself.**
- 12 want editing to start from the item on Home:
  - "It would be better if you put it while pressing the goal itself since we users are used to that." `P65#6115`
- Only 5 ask for an explicit Edit mode. That is too few to justify an Edit button in the top bar.
- 13 describe changing or ticking something by accident. Editing and completing must not share one tap:
  - "the edit button could be more accessible (the habit gets tapped "done" and I go chasing it to reactivate it)" `P11#5674`

**5. Apple's guidance says the same.**
- **Context menus:** a context menu is "hidden by default, so people might not know it’s there". Apple adds: "Always make context menu items available in the main interface, too."
- **Gestures:** a custom gesture should be "Not the only way to perform an important action".
- **Lists:** "People appreciate being able to reorder a list".
- That is why every long-press action above also has a visible way in: the end-of-list row, the filter menu and Settings.

---

**Limits.**
- **Findability is inferred from complaints.** People who found the setting easily rarely write about it. Test the four ways in with first-time users before launch.
- **Two apps dominate the "can't find it" group:** Me+ and Fabulous supply 15 of the 18.
- **The long-press split is small:** 9, 9 and 9. Treat it as showing a direction, not as a ranking.
