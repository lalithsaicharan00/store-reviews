## Feedback checklist

1. Keep tapping Today to open a calendar for date navigation.
2. Remove Day / Week / Month / Year from that calendar: these change habit-card layouts.
3. Give card-layout selection an intuitive, discoverable home without returning rarely used controls to a full permanent row.
4. Keep the filter primarily about choosing which habits appear.
5. Put the day-section Edit action beside its own heading/options.
6. Put the Groups Edit action beside its own heading/options.
7. Research an in-context Add group action, including the first-group state.
8. Research an in-context Add day section action, including custom sections.
9. Revisit “Time of day” / “Times of day” copy using the full Home Screen and Day Structure research, not isolated phrase frequency.
10. Make filtering, editing and creating distinct, understandable actions.
11. Explain what each number counts: scheduled on the selected date versus all habits assigned to the group/section; avoid confusing either with completion progress.
12. Decide how to represent an existing group/section with no habits scheduled today, and distinguish that from a genuinely empty group/section.
13. Provide a route from the filter context to assigned habits that are not due on the selected date.
14. Place and explain the start-of-day setting coherently with day sections.
15. Ask for only the day-start boundary; explain that earlier entries belong to the previous day, with no redundant end-of-day input.
16. Carry consistent terminology and entry points through the duplicated screens, including active filters, long-press shortcuts and the end-of-list action.

## Research questions

| ID | Question | Evidence needed / what must be resolved |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Where should the card-layout selector live? | Existing view preferences versus switching frequency; distinction between view and date; discoverability of a compact labelled control versus an overflow action. |
| Q2 | What should the section concept be called? | How sections behave (custom names, flexible boundaries, Anytime, day rollover), not just how often a phrase appears. Compare “Time of day”, “Day sections”, “Your day”. |
| Q3 | How should Filter contain Edit and Add without ambiguity? | Local requests for discoverable organization; visible versus hidden actions; contextual creation/editing patterns; separate filter selection from management navigation. |
| Q4 | What should filter counts mean? | Scheduled-date counts, total assigned inventory, completion counts; selected-date semantics away from Today; effects of combined filters and Hide completed. |
| Q5 | Should zero-match groups/sections remain visible? | Vanishing/hidden items, empty versus temporarily unscheduled categories; large-list clutter; preserving a selected zero-result filter and a recovery path. |
| Q6 | How can users access all assigned habits from this context? | An explicit management/browse route; due and not-due separation; no accidental changes to the Today schedule or progress denominator. |
| Q7 | Where should Day starts at live and how should it read? | Night-shift/midnight reviews; one boundary, prior-day attribution, default versus customized examples; relationship to sections and Anytime. |
| Q8 | Which edge states and examples are required in Round 3? | Calendar-only sheet; layout chooser; default/active filters; zero scheduled versus zero assigned; first group; add/edit flows; all-habits browsing; day-start boundary. |

## Evidence rules

- Previous reports are evidence sources, not automatically correct conclusions. Record conflicts and superseded Round 2 recommendations.
- Do not turn keyword counts into user preferences or claim representative frequency from a targeted sample.
- Preserve exact review IDs, original text and source paths for cited examples; label targeted retrieval scope.
- Reviews may support a need without proving exact UI placement or wording. Label the latter as design inference and identify what still needs usability testing.
- Maintain a requirement → evidence → decision → Figma board mapping, then verify the preserved Round 2 and the delivered Round 3.
