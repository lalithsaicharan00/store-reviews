# Today Screen, Question 3: The Date Strip (Fixed Week or Movable)

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** Row 2 is a strip of seven day cells, Fri 18 to Thu 24. Each cell has a progress ring, and today is filled.

**The question.** Do people need this strip at all? If they do, should it be a fixed seven days, or should they be able to move around (earlier weeks, later days, any date)? And which seven days should it show: the last seven, ending today, or the calendar week?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for day-navigation wording (move to past or future days, date strips, going back to today) plus "yesterday / previous days / tomorrow" wording in habit apps. 3,086 matched.
- **Read in full:** all 295 strong matches (strip, swipe or tap between days, today button, future days, week start), plus a random 300 of the 2,158 weaker habit-app matches (mostly "let me log yesterday").
- **Relevant:** 254 (145 strong + 109 from the sample).
- **External:** Nielsen Norman Group on gestures, and the 49-app Home census.

---

## What to do

1. **Keep the strip, and keep it compact:** one row, with the weekday letter, a ring with the date, and today highlighted.
   - It is the visible way to reach recent days, and reaching yesterday is the most common job after ticking today.
   - Never hide it behind a toggle.
2. **Show the calendar week** (Mon–Sun, or Sun–Sat by locale, changeable in settings). Today is highlighted wherever it falls.
   - Later days in the week appear muted. Tapping one opens a **read-only preview** of what's due, with no ticking ahead.
   - This matches the Week view and the "2 of 3 this week" counts.
3. **Three ways to move, from easiest to find to fastest:**
   - **Tap a day** in the strip. This is the visible control.
   - **Swipe the strip** to go to the previous or next week.
   - **Swipe the habit list sideways** to go back or forward one day. This is a shortcut only, and it needs a firm horizontal swipe so it doesn't fire while ticking.
4. **Jump anywhere:** tapping the date line (Question 1) opens a month calendar. **Put no limit** on how far back you can log.
5. **A "Back to today" chip** appears whenever you are not on today (Question 1).
6. **On another day, it must be obvious** that you're not on today: the title changes, that day is outlined in the strip, and the rows show that day's state.
   - Past days need that deliberate tap first. Nothing on Today should tick a past day by accident.

---

## Key findings

**1. Going back is the strip's real job.**
- **Wanting it:** in the random sample, 78 of 300 habit-app matches (26%) want to see or log earlier days, usually yesterday. Scaled to the full 2,158, that is roughly 560 reviews. The reasons are forgetting, or finishing after midnight.
- **Removing it:** when an app takes the swipe away, reviewers are harsh. After Done's 2023 redesign replaced swipe-between-days with a calendar picker, at least 20 reviews complained, for example:
  - "Before you could go to the previous day with a simple swipe. Now you have to click the calendar, click the date, then click confirm. It’s a bad change." `A31#3258`
  - "No longer being able to swipe between days, alone, makes it unusable." `A31#3224`

**2. Tapping a day on the strip is the visible path; swiping is the fast one.**
- 55 reviews ask for swiping or praise it, and 22 more complain that moving between days is slow.
- Habitify hid the strip behind a disclosure triangle and was criticised for it: "I used to be able to instantly tap on any other day of the week in the top bar, and to also swipe left or right to choose days even further ahead or behind." `A33#3440`
- Swipes also misfire (4 reviews): "the screen constantly goes to the next page instead of marking the goal" `P24#12507`.
- NN/g: gestures "are invisible and people have trouble remembering them", so always pair them with a visible control. The day cells are that visible control.

**3. People want to look ahead as well as back (65 reviews; 2 don't).**
- "I want to see what I am doing tomorrow, because yesterday’s tasks can’t help me plan." `A13#16266`
- Productive, Me+, Habitify and Finch users all ask for it. Me+ users are angry that future days can't be edited ("let's focus on today").
- The two against: "I really like that it doesn't show you the Habits you have to complete on upcoming days." `P112#2`

A muted, read-only preview serves both groups: nothing to tick, nothing forced.

**4. Calendar week or last seven days: both have fans.**

| Preference | Reviews |
|---|---|
| Calendar week, or a chosen week start | 8 |
| Last seven days | 5 |

- **For the week:** "great improvement to show the calendar week instead of the last 7 days! I track things by calendar week" `A34#713`
- **Against:** "It‘s a little sad to see everything unchecked at the start of every week." `A34#138`

The calendar week wins for us for three reasons:
- it lines up with the Week view and the weekly goals;
- it leaves room for the future days people want to preview;
- on a Monday, yesterday is still one swipe away.

**5. Time limits on going back are resented (28 reviews).** For example:
- Productive's 7-day editing limit: "At the moment app allows to mark habits only for the previous 7 days" `A13#14957`
- Streaks' 1-day limit
- Loop's 4-day limit

People also ask to jump straight to a date rather than step back day by day.

**6. Coming back to today must be one tap (9 reviews), and the other day must be obvious.**
- "Also a button for 'today' if we move to a different date in next week or next month." `P2#10369`
- One app was praised because it "doesn’t allow to accidentally modify past days, if you want to change something in the history you have to be intentional" `A41#708`.

**7. A minority find the strip takes space (Productive, after its 2019 redesign).**
- "I hate the calendar at the top which takes up too much space and isn’t my focus." `A13#13733`

The fix is a single slim row with no decoration, not removing the strip. Removing it causes the problems in findings 1 and 2.

## What other apps do

- **Strips are common:** 24 of the 49 competitor Home screens have a day strip.
- **What the strips show:** most show weekday and date; 7 put a ring in each day.
- **Goal Streak** shows a floating "Today" button when you leave today.
- **Habit Check Calendar and MyRoutine** open a month picker from the month name ("July 2026 ▾").
- **Past days:** Streaks and Awesome Habits offer a "yesterday or today?" prompt after midnight. This is praised, but it is no substitute for the day-start setting (Question 8).

---

**Limits.**
- **Sample, not full read:** the "log yesterday" count (≈560) is scaled from a random sample of 300, not read in full. The real figure could plausibly be anywhere from about 460 to 670.
- **Small counts:** the calendar-week vs last-seven-days split is small (8 vs 5), so the choice also rests on consistency with our Week view.
- **One coder:** Claude coded every review. Codes are in the evidence folder.
