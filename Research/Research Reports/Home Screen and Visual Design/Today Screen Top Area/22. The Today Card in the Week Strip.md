# Today Screen, Question 22: The Today Card in the Week Strip

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3 follow-up.

**Screen.**
- Today is a black rounded card holding the weekday and a ring with the date.
- "5 of 11" floats **below** the card, outside it.
- The other six days reserve an invisible gap for a caption they never show.

**The feedback.** The card looks odd, especially where "5 of 11" sits.

**Basis.**
- **Reused:** round 1's progress evidence (Question 2, 1,255 reviews read).
- **Census:** the 49-app census of week strips.
- **External:** NN/g's common-region principle.

---

## What to do

1. **Put the count inside the card.** The selected day becomes one tidy unit, top to bottom:

   ```
   ┌─────┐
   │ Thu │
   │ (24)│   ring with the date
   │ 5/11│
   └─────┘
   ```

2. **Use "5/11" in the card,** at the same size as the weekday. The longer "5 of 11" stays in the thin bar that appears on scroll, where there is room.
3. **All seven cells are the same height,** so the row doesn't jump. Only the selected day shows a count.
4. **Selecting another day moves the count into that day's card:**
   - the outlined card shows its own count, "9/11";
   - today stays filled, without a count.
5. **Nothing below the strip.** The first section header follows straight after.

---

## Key findings

**1. People want the number with the ring, once.**
- Round 1:
  - 105 value seeing the day's progress;
  - 47 want a number;
  - 15 want the ring and the number together;
  - 19 want to see how many are left.
- It also found "5 of 11" clearer than a percentage for a day in progress.

**2. Things read as belonging together when they share a boundary.**
- NN/g: "items within a boundary are perceived as a group". Also: "Using a border or background color to create a container for related items helps users quickly and effectively understand the UI's structure".
- Outside the card, "5 of 11" reads as a separate label hanging in space. Inside, it belongs to Thursday.

**3. No competitor hangs a count under a strip day.**
- In the census, 7 apps put a ring in each day of the strip. None writes a number beneath one day.
- Apps that show a day count put it in its own card or bar: Blossom's "TODAY 4 of 6", and a progress bar under the strip.
- Inside the selected card is the closest to both: the number stays with its day, and no extra row is added.

---

**Limits.**
- **Tight fit:** "5/11" fits a 48 pt card at 11–12 pt. Counts like "12/15" need checking at the largest text sizes.
