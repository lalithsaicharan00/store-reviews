# Today Screen, Question 13: The Filter Sheet — Time of Day, Group, Completed and Editing

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** An **"All ▾"** button opens a small popup. It lists:
- All habits;
- Due now · Afternoon;
- Hide done;
- the groups;
- "Edit sections and groups…".

Combining choices gives long, odd button text ("Due now · Health …"), and the last item isn't clear.

**The questions.** These were asked as two questions, but they are about the same control:
- How often do people use groups and filters?
- What does this control need to hold?
- How should it read, in users' own words?
- How should combined choices show?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for "hide/show completed", "filter / sort / group / view by", remembering filters, and group-filter use.
- **Read in full:** all 343 matches in habit and to-do apps.
- **Counted:** the wording people use across the whole corpus.
- **Reused:** the Day Structure report (2,228 group reviews) and round-1 Questions 5 and 6.
- **External:** Apple's menu and sheet guidance, NN/g on bottom sheets, Apple Mail's filter button, and Todoist's display options.

---

## What to do

1. **One filter button, an icon, next to +.**
   - It fills in and shows a count when anything is filtered: **⚲ 2**.
   - It opens a **bottom sheet** titled **Filter**, not a popup.
2. **The sheet has four short parts, all in users' words:**

   | Part | Choices | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Time of day** | All day · Anytime · Morning · **Afternoon · Now** · Evening | Replaces "Due now". The current part of the day is tagged *Now* |
   | **Group** | All groups · ● Health 4 of 7 · ● Mind 0 of 2 · … | Shown only when groups exist |
   | **Completed** | **Hide completed habits** (switch) | A set-once preference, so it isn't counted as a filter |
   | **Edit** | **Times of day ›** Morning 06:00 · Afternoon 12:00 · Evening 18:00 · day starts 04:00; **Groups ›** 4 groups | Replaces "Edit sections and groups…" |

3. **Show what's filtered as chips, not as button text.**
   - Under the strip, only while something is on, a row reads **[Afternoon ✕] [Health ✕] Clear**.
   - Each chip removes one filter. Clear removes all.
4. **Remember the last filter.** People ask for this, and the chip row keeps it visible, so no habit is ever silently hidden. This revises round 1's "always open on All".
5. **When completed habits are hidden,** one row at the end of the list says **"5 completed · Show"**, the way Reminders does. Nothing vanishes without a trace.
6. **Rename "Your day" to "Times of day"** (Question 8). It is the phrase people use.

---

## Key findings

**1. None of these is used every minute, so none earns a place on screen.**
- **Groups are optional,** and wanted mainly to tidy a long list.
  - 933 of 2,228 group reviews ask for groups.
  - Only 260 talk about looking at one group at a time, and 93 of those describe a filter (Day Structure report).
  - In round 2, filter praise and filter complaints were a handful each (3 and 2).
- **Hiding completed habits is a setting people choose once.**
  - 71 want completed items hidden or moved down: 33 in habit apps, across 23 apps.
  - "I love that you can turn on a setting to hide completed tasks." `P84#14960`
  - "it'd be great to have an option to hide completed habits. My list is getting long" `A7#880`
- **Filtering by time of day is valued when it's there:**
  - "The ability to set and filter by time of day makes the app clean and easy to see what should be completed next." `A33#3588`
  - "filter by time doesn't work (iOS 17) which is one of the most crucial feature" `A1#49150`

**2. The wording should be the users' own.**

| People say | Count | Instead of |
|---|---|---|
| "hide completed" | 68 | "hide done", "hide finished", "hide checked": 10 in all |
| "time of day" | 472 in habit apps | "parts of the day": 174. "Sections" is our internal word |
| "due today" / "what's left" | 26 / 20 | "due now": 2 |
| "filter" | 291 in habit apps | — |

- So the labels are Hide completed habits, Time of day and Filter. "Due now" goes.

**3. People want the filter kept, and they want to see it.**
- "it defaults to showing all habits which IMO defeats the purpose if I have to go back and reapply the filter" `A1#55065`
- In Habitica, "The filter in daily always resets back to the option all" `P8#13267`
- 7 reviews in 3 apps ask for the filter to be remembered.
- The Day Structure report's warning was about habits hidden *without a sign*. Visible chips remove that risk.

**4. Burying a *frequent* toggle is punished.** Microsoft To Do users who re-show completed items all the time (grocery lists) complain: "Now that it takes 3 extra clicks it's super annoying." `N10#21578`

For a habit list, hiding completed items is set once, so a sheet is fine. But the "5 completed · Show" row keeps one-tap access where it's needed.

**5. Familiar patterns.**
- **Apple Mail:** a filter button that you "tap … to turn off all filters", and a "Filtered by" list for the criteria.
- **Todoist:** a Display menu grouping layout, sorting, grouping and filters in one place.
- **Apple's menus:** use one toggled item for show/hide, and "consider using a checkmark to show that an attribute is currently in effect".
- **NN/g on bottom sheets:** they hold "contextual controls" and suit cases where users "need to refer to the main, background information". They should "include a visible a Close (or X) button".
- **Apple's sheets** can rest at a medium height, so the list stays visible and updates behind.

---

**Limits.**
- **Mixed corpus:** much of the show/hide wording comes from to-do apps (Tasks, Reminders, Microsoft To Do). Habit apps alone give 33 hide requests and 4 show requests.
- **Frequency is inferred:** how often groups are filtered is inferred from how people describe them. Measure it after launch.
