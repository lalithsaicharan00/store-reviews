# Today Screen, Question 11: Period Titles — One Rule for Every View

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** The titles today follow four different patterns:

| View | Title now | What's wrong |
|---|---|---|
| Day | "Today · Thursday 24 September" | Long. The strip below already says Thu 24 |
| Week | "This week 18–24 Sep" | Two labels for one thing. The grid below already shows 18–24 |
| Month | "September 2026" | No relative word, unlike the other three. The year is obvious |
| Year | "This year Oct–Sep" | "This year" but a rolling Oct–Sep window: the label and the data disagree |

**The question.** How should each view's title read so the four are consistent, short and never repeat what's on screen?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** every review for the words people use for periods ("this week", "last 7 days", "due today"), and for date and title complaints.
- **Read in full:** all 94 habit-app matches.
- **Reused:** the round-1 title findings (1,151 read).

---

## What to do

**One rule.**
- **Relative words for now and the one before.**
- **A short date otherwise.**
- **Add the year only when it isn't this year.**

| View | Now | One back | Further back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day | **Today** | **Yesterday** | **Tuesday** (this week) / **Tue 15 Sep** (earlier) |
| Week | **This week** | **Last week** | **7–13 Sep** |
| Month | **This month** | **Last month** | **July** / **Aug 2025** |
| Year | **This year** | **Last year** | **2024** |

1. **Every view's "now" reads "This …".** Day's is "Today", because that is what people say.
2. **Weeks, months and years are calendar periods,** matching the calendar-week strip from Question 3.
   - The Year view stops using a rolling Oct–Sep window.
   - "Last 7 days" belongs in Progress, not here.
3. **The title never repeats what sits below it.**
   - On today the strip shows "Thu 24", so the title says only **Today**.
   - In Week view the grid shows the dates, so the title says only **This week**.
4. **Words go before numbers.** The title is the thing people scan first, so it leads with the word they would say: Today, This week.
5. **After midnight, before the day starts** (default 04:00), the title reads **"Still Thursday"**. That is the day the ticks count for (round 1, Questions 1 and 8).

---

## Key findings

**1. People talk in calendar periods and relative words.**
- Across habit-app reviews, "this week" appears 281 times and "last 7 days" 37.
- "Due today" appears 26 times; "due now" appears only twice.
- A calendar week is what people expect when they count: "the app checks if this habit was done twice in the last 7 days and not in the calendar" week `P3#3453`.

**2. The complaints are about which day is shown, not about missing detail.**
- In round 1, 28 reviews were unsure which day they were logging, and 154 were caught out at midnight.
- Widgets that kept saying "Today" on another day drew complaints `P2#1767`.
- The rule fixes this: the word Today appears only on today.
- One reviewer wants the full date instead of Today: "Can we get the date at the top instead of Today or Yesterday?" `A31#3625`.
  - That is a minority view (1 review), and the strip already shows the weekday and date right under the title.

**3. Repetition costs space and scanning time.**
- The current Day header says the day three times: in the title, in the strip and in the "5 of 11" caption.
- Round 1 found people punish tops that push the habits down: 68 reviews in 25 apps.
- The rule above says each fact once.

---

**Limits.**
- **"Last month" / "Last year":** these are inferred by symmetry with the Day and Week wording. No reviewer asks for them by name.
- **Locale:** date order and the week start follow the user's locale.
