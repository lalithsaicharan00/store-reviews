# Today Screen, Question 24: Group Order — Manual or Automatic

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3 follow-up.

**The feedback.** Is manual reordering of groups worth having? Groups appear only in the View filter, in the habit form, and in Progress. Maybe A–Z or by count is enough.

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 63 new matches on ordering groups, categories and tags.
- **Reused:** the Day Structure report's 474 grouping-friction reviews.

---

## What to do

1. **Default: A–Z.** Predictable, and zero effort.
2. **Keep drag to reorder in the Groups editor** for people who want their own order.
   - Dragging switches the order to "Your order".
   - A small **Sort A–Z** action switches back.
3. **Never sort by count.**
   - Today's counts change every day, so the chips would jump around and break muscle memory.
   - Totals change as habits are added.
4. **One order, used everywhere:** View chips, the habit form's group picker, and Progress.

---

## Key findings

**1. Order matters because it decides what's first to reach.**
- The chip row is scanned left to right, so the first group is the quickest to tap.
- "It does not allow you to delete or organize the groups on the top row which makes my top priority habit groups get randomly organized" `A1#50937`
- "I wish there was an option to manually rearrange the groups on the home page." `A1#49737`

**2. Both manual and alphabetical have supporters. Random order has none.**
- **Manual, 6 reviews:** besides the two above:
  - "I will like the ability to rearrange group orders" `A31#3128`;
  - "I'm not able to reorder the tags" `A1#2029`;
  - "Can't reorder tags so my tasks show daily, monthly, then weekly" `P4#17811`;
  - Habitify's areas order not saving `A33#3193`.
- **Alphabetical, 2 reviews:** "consider alphabetizing the tags and categories. I find randomly ordered lists hard to read" `A85#2057`, and a request to sort tags `P4#15088`.
- **The Day Structure report:** 70 of its 474 grouping-friction reviews are about group order, "sorted alphabetically or at random" (keyword floor).

**3. The cost is low.** Groups are a short list, and the editor already has rows. Drag handles plus one Sort A–Z action is all it takes.

---

**Limits.**
- **Small numbers:** 8 direct reviews this round. The Day Structure figure is a keyword floor.
- **Why it's worth keeping:** it is a small feature that prevents a known, specific annoyance.
