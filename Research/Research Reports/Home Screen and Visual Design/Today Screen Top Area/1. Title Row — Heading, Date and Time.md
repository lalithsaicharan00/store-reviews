# Today Screen, Question 1: The Title Row (Heading, Date and Time)

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** The default Today screen in List view (Figma board 18:4). Row 1 currently reads: **Today** · *Thu 24 Sep · 14:30* · progress ring *5/11* · **+**.

**The question.** The tab bar already says "Today". Do people need a "Today" heading as well? Do they need "Thursday 24 September"? Do they need the time 14:30? Where should each item sit, and how do we cut the clutter in this first row?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews, in two keyword passes: date and time wording, and top-of-screen, wasted-space and midnight-rollover wording.
- **Matched:** 1,331. Of these, 1,151 were read in full. The other 180 were coded by a rule: 103 Google Calendar "show the date on the app icon" reviews, and 77 where "top" meant ranking ("top of my list").
- **Relevant:** 565.
- **External:** Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, Nielsen Norman Group's heuristics, and a census of the Home screens of 49 habit apps, taken from their App Store screenshots.

Citations such as `A13#13107` point to line 13107 (counting from 0) of `App Store Reviews/13. …/reviews.jsonl`. `P` means Play Store and `N` means the native apps.

---

## What to do

1. **Remove the time (14:30).** Nobody asks for a clock inside the app. The phone's status bar already shows the time, so just keep the status bar visible.
2. **Keep the date, but as a small line above the title** ("THURSDAY 24 SEPTEMBER"), not squeezed in beside it.
   - The date strip already shows "Thu 24". The line adds the month, and it tells you which day your ticks will count for.
3. **Keep a title, and make it say which day you are on.** It should read "Today", then "Yesterday", then "Tue 22 Sep" as you move back. Offer a **Back to today** chip whenever you are not on today.
   - A title that changes with the day is no longer a copy of the tab label.
4. **Put only the title and the + button in row 1.** The progress ring moves out of this row (see Question 2). Row 1 then has 2 items instead of 4.
5. **Nothing else goes above the habits.** No banners, greetings, quotes, avatars or illustrations. Reviewers punish exactly this.
6. **Follow the day-start setting.** At 01:30, with the day starting at 04:00, the title still says "Today · Wed 23 Sep". The date on screen must always be the day the ticks count for.
7. **On scroll,** the large title shrinks into a small bar that still shows "Today" and the + button (standard iOS behaviour).

```
Now:     Today  Thu 24 Sep · 14:30              ◔ 5/11   (+)

Better:  THURSDAY 24 SEPTEMBER
         Today                                           (+)

On Tue:  TUESDAY 22 SEPTEMBER
         Tue 22 Sep            [ Back to today ]         (+)
```

## Key findings

**1. Nobody asks for an in-app clock.** Of 1,151 reviews read, the only time requests (6, from 3 apps) came from apps that **hide the phone's status bar**. Those users wanted the phone clock back, not a second clock.
- "i can’t see the time at the top of my screen in the app. i use finch to try to HELP my time blindness" `A10#19827`
- "It blocks your phones clock at the top" `A10#26076`
- None of the 49 competitor Home screens shows a clock.

**2. People do want to know which day they are logging (28 reviews, 15 apps).**

Some ask for the date outright:
- "Can we get the date at the top instead of Today or Yesterday?" `A31#3625`
- "It would be great to have a calendar view or even just the date at the top of the screen." `A43#253`

One uninstalled the app because there was no date: "日付が表示されないため" (because the date isn't shown), they lost track of which days they had logged `A70#510`.

Others logged the wrong day by mistake: "I accidentally pressed the wrong day, and there is no way for me to edit it" `A1#1641`.

**3. The date matters most when you are not on today.** When you move to another day or week, the header must name it.
- "That’s not so bad if you are dealing with NOW, but if you are planning out a couple months, you can’t tell what date you are on." `N8#22470`
- Several widget reviews complain that the header kept saying "Today" while showing another day (HabitNow, `P2#1767`).

**4. Midnight is where "which day?" really bites (154 reviews in 44 habit apps).** People log after midnight, and the ticks land on the wrong day.
- "The widgets show the new date at night, whereas for the owl it is the late evening of the previous day, not the new day." `P3#10590`
- "chose today - but it was a few minutes after midnight so I realized afterwards I had made a mistake" `A23#5243`

Apps with a "day starts at" setting are praised: 17 reviews, mean rating 4.94★. So the title must show the day being logged, not the calendar date.

**5. The top of Home is where clutter hurts most (68 reviews, 25 apps, mean 2.63★).** The complaints are about big graphics, banners and extras sitting above the habits. None is about a one-line date.
- "I DON’T WANT OR NEED A PICTURE OF THE SUN’S POSITION IN THE SKY TAKING UP HALF THE SCREEN" `A13#13744`
- "The advertisement create a visual clutter and reduces the amount of screen space for the goals" `A31#4520`
- 11 reviews praise habits sitting right at the top: "The habits or my routines should be right at the top." `A24#6783`

**6. The strongest "remove it" review is about one crowded header.** After a Productive redesign, one user wrote that the new screen "cuts off half of my tasks" and asked for the Today label, the date and the time-of-day row to be taken "OFF of the screen" `A13#13107`.

That is the risk our row 1 carries now. The answer is one light title block, not no title at all. Findings 2 to 4 show people still need to know the day.

## What the guidelines and other apps say

**Apple HIG, toolbars:**
- "A title helps people confirm their location as they navigate your app … If titling a toolbar seems redundant, you can leave the title area empty."
- Keep the title under 15 characters, and never use the app name.
- A large title "transitions to a standard title as people begin scrolling".
- On clutter: "Minimize the number of groups … aim for a maximum of three."

**Nielsen Norman heuristics:**
- #1, visibility of system status, is the case for showing the selected day.
- #8: "Every extra unit of information in an interface competes with the relevant units of information". That is the case for removing the time and the duplicate ring.

**What 49 competitor Home screens put at the top:**

| Element | Apps |
|---|---|
| Title "Today" | 16 |
| Full date or weekday near the title | 8 |
| Month only | 8 |
| Clock time | **0** |
| Day strip at the top | 24 |
| Overall progress in the header | 16 |

Two apps pair a small date with a title, as recommended above:
- Blossom: small "WEDNESDAY, MAY 20" above the heading.
- Dear Me: "Today" with "Thursday, 16th" beside it.

Goal Streak shows a floating **Today** button whenever you leave today.

**Limits.**
- **Few reviews about headers:** people rarely write about headers, so the direct counts are small (28 for the date, 6 for the time). The midnight group (154) and the top-clutter group (68) carry most of the weight.
- **Screenshots are marketing:** the competitor census uses App Store screenshots, which are staged. Real screens may differ.
- **One coder:** Claude coded every review. The codebook and per-review codes are in the evidence folder.
