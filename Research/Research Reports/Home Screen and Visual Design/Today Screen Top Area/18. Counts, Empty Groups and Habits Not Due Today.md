# Today Screen, Question 18: Counts, Empty Groups and Habits Not Due Today

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3.

**Screen (round 2).**
- Group chips read "Health 4 of 7". That mixes two ideas: done today, and due today.
- A group can hold 12 habits while only 5 are due today.
- The chips don't say which number is which, what happens when a group has nothing today, or how to see the rest.

**The questions.**
- What should the number mean?
- Should a group or section with nothing today still show? How should that differ from a group that is truly empty?
- How do people reach the habits in a group that aren't due today?

**Basis.**
- **Read in full:** 132 new matches on not-due habits and seeing all habits, and 15 on empty groups.
- **Reused:** the Non-Scheduled Days report (479 relevant reviews).
- **External:** Baymard's filter research.

---

## What to do

1. **One number per chip: how many habits it will show on the selected day.**
   - **Health 5** means tapping it shows 5 habits.
   - Progress ("done") stays where it already is: the day ring and the section headers ("1/3").
   - A small line under the sheet title says what the numbers mean: "Numbers show habits due Thu 24".
2. **Three states, three looks:**

   | State | Chip | Tapping it |
   |---|---|---|
   | Has habits today | **● Health 5** | Filters to them |
   | Has habits, none today | **● Mind 0**, quieter | Filters, then shows "Nothing from Mind today", its **Not due today (4)** row, and a way back to All |
   | Empty: no habits at all | **○ Reading –**, last in the row | Opens Edit → "Add habits to Reading" |

3. **Reaching the rest of a group.** When a group or section is filtered, the list ends with the **Not due today (n)** row from the Non-Scheduled Days report, filtered the same way. Tap it to see and log those habits.
4. **Totals live in Edit.** The group editor lists every group with its full count ("Health · 12 habits") and its habits. That is the "inventory" view, kept apart from today's filter.
5. **Hide completed doesn't change the chip numbers.** It hides rows, not habits. The "5 completed · Show" row keeps them one tap away.

---

## Key findings

**1. People want a filter to show what's due now, not everything in the group.**
- "Why the tags show all goals even when it's not due today. It is more organize to show only due goal on each goals." `A48#1567`
- "I see that habit there, like if I should do that on the wednesday. Would be nice just see the habits that you need to do TODAY." `P24#8062`
- The Non-Scheduled Days report found 104 reviews wanting habits that aren't due kept out of Today's list.

**2. They also want to reach the rest.**
- "would be nice to see all habits at all times in the app, instead of only the habits active for the day" `P37#257`
- "Why can I not edit my habits that are not due today?" `P4#26370`
- 13 reviews this round, and 82 in the Non-Scheduled Days report, say that hiding off-day habits completely costs them.
- The closed **Not due today** row answers both sides. Filtering it by group gives the "rest of this group" route.

**3. A count must say what it counts, and a zero must not be a dead end.**
- Baymard: "Displaying the number of matching products next to each filter option (e.g., 'Blue (34)') gives users confidence before they click."
- Baymard on empty results: "showing them an empty page, is a dead end". The page needs "a clear recovery path".
- So "Mind 0" stays tappable, and its empty screen offers the Not due row and All.

**4. A group with nothing today is not the same as an empty group.**
- "Mind 0" is a real group resting today. "Reading –" has no habits at all.
- Empty groups annoy when they crowd the real ones: `A1#50937` in Question 17.
- So an empty group goes last and leads to adding habits, not to a filtered list.

---

**Limits.**
- **Inference:** the exact chip wording ("5", "0", "–") is a design choice and needs testing.
- **Future days:** counts follow the selected day, so on a future-day preview the chips count that day's due habits.
