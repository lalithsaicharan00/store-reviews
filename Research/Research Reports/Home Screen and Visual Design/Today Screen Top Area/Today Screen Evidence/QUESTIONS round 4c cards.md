# Day cards — round 3 feedback (24 Sep 2026)

User's proposal:
- Take the core idea of "Habit Tracker" (App Store 1, Inner Grow): the card itself fills with colour as progress.
- "Done" (minimal app): the fill, and at the end just an icon, with no boxed button.
- So: the card is the progress. Text such as "5/8 glasses" sits vertically centred near the end, then the button (icon).
- Is an icon alone enough, or does it need a small circular tappable shape around it?
- Reminder time: maybe beside the streak. HabitHub has three lines, which is probably not needed.
- Drop "3× a week / 3× a day / 2× a day"?
- Two layouts to compare:
  - (a) progress text under the habit name, with the streak moved beside the check;
  - (b) keep the current arrangement.
- Progress is conveyed twice (card fill + text). Is that right? Which matters most to the user?
- As minimal and clean as possible. Research on the internet and in reviews.

Questions:
- F1: whole-card fill as the progress: evidence from App Store 1 reviews (praise, problems: accidental taps and drags, readability of text over fill).
- F2: icon only vs icon in a circle: affordance, tap target, evidence.
- F3: where the progress text goes, and where the streak goes (layout a vs b vs the user's centred version).
- F4: reminder time placement.
- F5: drop frequency? What replaces it for weekly habits and several-a-day habits?
- F6: two progress signals (fill + text): redundant or complementary?
