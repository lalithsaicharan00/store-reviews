## The user's points

1. **Rename "Layouts" to "Progress"?**
   - The user's assumption: each layout shows progress over a period (the day, the week, the month, the year).
   - Verify it. Use it only if the evidence supports it.
2. **The day card shows the whole week's history dots.**
   - With a Week layout available, this feels redundant.
   - The Day card should show today's progress.
3. **Time on the card.**
   - The user remembers a Notion card saying the habit's reminder or scheduled time should show on the card.
   - Check the Notion cards: right or wrong?
4. **Clutter.** Water shows "5 of 8 glasses", "23d", "4/7", history dots and a +1 ring.
   - Keep what's essential: icon, title, progress, streak, time if it's important.
   - Cut the rest.
   - Consider combining progress and logging in one button.
5. **Text order: no dangling text.**
   - Put the fixed-format items first (time, streak) and the uneven-length text last.
   - Research this rule and its principles.
6. **The timer card (Read):**
   - The play button sits outside the circle and wastes space.
   - Consider putting the timer, the goal and the log in one button.
7. **Consistency.**
   - Every habit type (yes/no, count, timer, checklist, multi-dose, x times a week) uses the same card anatomy.
   - The same place always holds the same kind of information, and the log control is always in the same spot.
8. **Cross-check the whole Today screen against every relevant Notion card** (Home, cards, progress), before or after the card redesign.
