# Day cards — round 2 feedback (24 Sep 2026)

User's points (verbatim intent):
1. **One progress model for every type.** Water shows 5/8 and Skincare 0/3, but Read (timer) shows ▶ and Walk (8,000 steps) shows ✓. The same representation everywhere.
2. **Fixed slots.** Time and streak take turns at the start of line 2, so the streak moves. Each kind of information must always sit in the same place on every card: habit type/target, streak, reminder time, multiple-times-a-day.
3. **Streak format.** "58 days" beside "4 weeks" is confusing. Edge cases: 3+ digits (100+ days), months, years ("months" = 6 letters). Research on the web how people prefer streaks shown: days only, or days → weeks → months → years? Be consistent.
4. **Type wording.** "twice a day" vs "3 times a day" vs "3× a week": one grammar, one place.
5. **Still too much.** Buttons plus subtext plus progress is a lot. Improve; no restriction on button shape or layout, just good UX.
6. Research extensively: reviews, then internet. Mental model and scanning must be consistent. "This time, make sure you get things right."

Questions:
- D1 Streak: which unit(s), how big numbers display, weekly habits, where on the card.
- D2 Progress: one format for yes/no, count, timer, steps target, checklist, x/week, several-a-day; where; the done state; how the action is signalled.
- D3 Slots: which items get a fixed position; what happens when an item is absent (does anything shift?).
- D4 Frequency/target wording: one grammar.
- D5 Time: which time (reminder / next occurrence), its slot.
- D6 Density: what can go, to keep the card calm.
