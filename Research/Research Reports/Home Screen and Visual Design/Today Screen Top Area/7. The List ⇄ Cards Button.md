# Today Screen, Question 7: The List ⇄ Cards (Grid) Button

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.** A square **▦** button sits next to "Now". Tapping it switches Home between compact rows and bigger cards.

**The question.** What is this button for? Do people need it on Home? If they do, where should it go?

**What it does today.** It is the **density option** from the Home Screen Cards and Widgets report.
- That report found layout preferences split: most people want a compact list, but a minority love big, calm cards (15 praise big cards; 25 complain about them).
- It concluded that "a toggle serves both groups".
- The wireframe put that toggle on Home.

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for compact / list / grid / card / tile layouts, bigger or smaller rows, and "too much scrolling".
- **Read in full:** all 198 habit-app matches. 82 relevant.
- **Earlier evidence:** the Home Screen report's density findings (381 density requests).
- **External:** Apple's toolbar guidance.

---

## What to do

1. **Remove the ▦ button from Home.** Layout is a choice people make once, not a control they use every day. The icon also doesn't say what it does.
2. **Move the choice to Me → Appearance.** It sits next to theme, icon style and completion mark (Figma board "Appearance").
   - It is labelled in words: **Row size: Compact · Comfortable · Cards**.
   - It shows a live preview of the real Today list, as the theme picker already does.
3. **Default to Compact.** Every due habit should be visible without scrolling on a normal phone (Home Screen report).
4. **Optional shortcut:** pinch the habit list to step between sizes, as the Photos app does with its grid.
   - Only as a hidden extra; the setting is the real control.
5. **Keep "List" out of the view names.** As Question 4 recommends, the views become Day · Week · Month · Year, so the word "List" never competes with the layout setting.

---

## Key findings

**1. The need is real, and mostly one-sided: people want more habits on screen.**

| What reviewers ask for | Reviews | Apps |
|---|---|---|
| A more compact layout (smaller rows, icons, circles; everything on one screen) | 51 | 29 |
| An option or toggle between layouts | 23 | — |
| Bigger cards or tiles | 8 | — |
| Tired of scrolling | 8 | — |

- 13 of the compact requests are from Streaks users stuck with six big circles a page:
  - "I would love the option to have a compact view to see all my streaks on one page." `A23#5224`
- Habitica's boxes after an update: "Boxed are too BIG!!!" `A85#1372`
- The big-card minority is real too, and asks for it *as an option*:
  - "Great app just wish it had a large tile based up option in the settings too" `P106#437`
  - "Card layout would be more reachable in my opinion. Atleast an option to have it." `P2#11002`

**2. People describe layout as a preference they set, not a switch they flip.**
- Every request is phrased as "an option", "a mode" or "a setting":
  - "Would love an OPTION for a customizable view - like a list view or smaller icons" `A23#4153`
  - "I want the option to choose the old list format, not this new one item at a time format." `P49#257`
- None of the 82 describes switching layouts back and forth during a day. Compare the view switcher, where one-tap switching is the praised part (Question 4).
- So the layout control belongs in settings, and the Home top keeps only what is used daily (Question 5).

**3. Where competitors put it.**
- **Settings:** Atoms and Evoday expose compact/list vs tiles in settings (Home Screen report).
- **Home:** Atoms also has a "Compact View" switch on its Home, one of only two of the 49 census apps with a layout control on Home.
- **Tab bar:** Dots and DotHabit put list/grid/calendar in the tab bar, because for them these are *views*, not densities.

**4. An unlabeled square icon breaks the guidelines.** Apple: "Make sure the meaning of each control is clear. Don't make people guess or experiment." ▦ reads as "grid view", "calendar" or "apps".

A labelled row in Appearance, with a preview, removes the guesswork.

---

**Limits.**
- **One app dominates:** Streaks supplies 13 of the 51 compact requests. Without it the direction is the same, and 28 other apps contribute.
- **Frequency is inferred:** "set once" comes from how people phrase their requests, not from usage data. Check it with analytics after launch.
