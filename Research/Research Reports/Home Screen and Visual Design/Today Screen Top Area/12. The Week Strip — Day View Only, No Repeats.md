# Today Screen, Question 12: The Week Strip — Day View Only, No Repeats

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**Screen.**
- **Day view:** the strip shows Mon–Sun with a ring per day.
- **Old Week board:** the strip also appears there, above a grid whose columns are the same seven days (F S S M T W T, 18–24). So the week is shown twice.

**The question.**
- Do people value the strip?
- What do they use it for?
- Which views need it, and where should it sit?

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** every review for day strips, "calendar at the top", and switching between days.
- **Read in full:** all 21 habit-app matches.
- **Reused:**
  - round 1's 595 strip reviews (Question 3);
  - 1,255 progress reviews (Question 2).

---

## What to do

1. **Keep the strip, in Day view only.**
   - Week view's grid columns *are* the week.
   - Month has its calendar and Year its heatmap.
   - In those three views the strip would only repeat them.
2. **Because it's Day-only, it sits under the Day title,** not above the view control. It belongs to the day you're looking at.
3. **It does three jobs, and nothing else:**
   - **today's progress:** today's ring, with "5 of 11" under the selected day only;
   - **reaching recent days:** tap a day, or swipe the strip for another week;
   - **a glance at the week:** one ring per day, and later days muted.
4. **No repeats around it.**
   - The title says "Today", not the date (Question 11).
   - The count appears once, under the selected day.
   - The month name lives in the calendar sheet (Question 10), not in the strip.
5. **Never hide it behind a toggle.** Keep it one row, about 70 pt.

---

## Key findings

**1. Moving between recent days is the second most common job after ticking today.**
- Round 1 sampled 300 of 2,158 day-navigation matches: 78 wanted to go back to log or see past days. That is about 560 across the corpus.
- Other day-navigation counts: 55 want to swipe between days, and 23 say moving between days is slow.
- In round 2, 11 more say the same: "one can't easily switch back and forth between days anymore" `A31#969`.

**2. Today's progress is what people want to see at a glance.**
- Round 1: 105 reviews value seeing the day's progress, and 23 want it per day in the strip or calendar.
- One reviewer: "The updates on week at the landing screen has helped me alot!" `P5#1755`

**3. Hiding the strip is punished, and so is making it huge.**
- **Hidden:** Habitify put its day bar behind a disclosure triangle and drew "Now I have to tap a disclosure triangle to show the top bar first." `A33#3440`
- **Huge:** Productive's oversized version drew "Ugly dominating days at top which prevents an easy overview of tasks" `A13#6094`. That app had 3 such 1★ reviews.
- So the strip stays, but compact.

**4. The Week grid already is a strip.**
- The old Week board shows the same seven days twice: once in the strip, once as grid columns.
- People ask for the dates on the week header ("can you add the date on the top of Monday, Tue..." `P130#175`), and the grid header can hold them.

---

**Limits.**
- **Few direct reviews:** only 21 reviews mention the strip itself. Its value is shown through what people do with it: go back to days and watch progress.
- **Productive:** its complaints are about one oversized design, not strips in general.
