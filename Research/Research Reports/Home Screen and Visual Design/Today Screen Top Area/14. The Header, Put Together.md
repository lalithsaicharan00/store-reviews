# Today Screen, Question 14: The Header, Put Together

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion.

**The question.**
- On a past day, the round-1 header squeezed the date, "Back to today" and "All" into one line.
- What is the smallest header that keeps everyday things up front, puts rare things one tap away, and repeats nothing?
- This report applies Questions 10–13 together.

**The rule used.**
- **Every day** (tick today, see today's progress, go to a recent day): visible, no taps.
- **Sometimes** (change the view, jump far back, filter, hide completed, edit times of day): behind one clearly labelled control, in words people use.
- **Never twice:** each fact appears once on screen.

---

## The header

```
Today                        (⚲) (+)
Mon Tue Wed Thu Fri Sat Sun
 ◔   ●   ◔  [◔]  ○   ○   ○
            5 of 11
────────────────────────────────────
ANYTIME …
```

- **Row 1:**
  - **Title**, which is also the view control ("Today ⌄", Question 10).
  - **Filter** button (Question 13).
  - **+** to add a habit.
- **Row 2:** the **week strip**, in Day view only (Question 12).
- **Row 3, only when a filter is on:** **[Afternoon ✕] [Health ✕] Clear**.
- **Round 1 had three rows,** one of them a full-width switcher. This has **two**.

**On a past day:**

```
Tuesday ⌄              [Today] (⚲) (+)
Mon [Tue] Wed Thu …
    11 of 11
```

- The title names the day (Question 11): Yesterday, Tuesday, or Tue 15 Sep.
- A **Today** button appears in row 1. It is the same idea as Google Calendar's "Jump to today" at the top right.
- There's room for it because the title is now one short word.
- Re-tapping the **Today** tab does the same (Question 9).

**In the other views** (the rule applied; not drawn here):
- The title reads **This week ⌄**, **This month ⌄** or **This year ⌄**.
- There is **no strip**: the grid is the calendar.
- The filter and + stay.

## What each round-1 item became

| Round 1 | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Day · Week · Month · Year segmented row | Inside the title's sheet | Chosen once, then kept (Q10) |
| Period line "Today · Thursday 24 September" | The title "Today" | The strip already shows Thu 24 (Q11) |
| Tap period line → calendar | Same sheet as the views | Both are "go to another period" jobs, both rare (Q10) |
| "All ▾" popup | Filter icon → bottom sheet | Options are occasional, so they are tucked away but clearly named (Q13) |
| "Back to today" chip in a crowded line | Today button in row 1, only when away | Space freed by the short title |
| "Due now" / "Hide done" / "Edit sections and groups…" | Time of day · Hide completed habits · Edit Times of day / Groups | Users' own words (Q13) |
| "5 of 11" under the selected day | Unchanged | Shown once (Q12) |

---

## Checks this passes

- **Everyday jobs cost no taps:** ticking, today's ring and count, and yesterday (tap Wed, or swipe the list).
- **Rare jobs cost one tap, and the control says what it does:**
  - the title with a chevron opens views and dates;
  - the funnel icon, which fills with a count when active, opens filters and editing.
- **Other ways in:** editing times of day can also be reached from a section header's long press, the "Reorder · Edit times of day" row at the end of the list, and Me → Settings (Question 8).
- **Nothing is hidden silently:** active filters show as chips, and hidden completed habits leave a "5 completed · Show" row.
- **Scanning:** read top-left to right, the header gives one word for where you are, then state (the ring and count), then actions.

---

**Limits.**
- **Test the icon:** a funnel icon alone may be read as "sort". Pair it with the count when active, and test with first-time users. If it tests poorly, use the word "Filter" as its label.
- **Tappable title:** that the title can be tapped needs the chevron and a first-run hint. Measure how many people find the views.
