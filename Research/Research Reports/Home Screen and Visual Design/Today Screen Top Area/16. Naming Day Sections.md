# Today Screen, Question 16: Naming Day Sections

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 24 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. Round 3.

**Screen (round 2).** The filter's heading and the editor are called **"Time of day" / "Times of day"**.

**The question.** Round 2 picked that name from how often the phrase appears. What name fits what these things actually are?

**Basis.**
- **Reports:**
  - the Day Structure decision report (the product model and its naming);
  - the plain-English Day Structure report (125 hand-read reviews on customising sections);
  - the Organization report.
- **New count:** 1,791 habit reviews that mention both morning and afternoon/evening, checked for what people call the parts.

---

## What to do

1. **Call them "Day sections".** In the filter heading, the editor title and the "Add section" action.
   - This is the Day Structure report's own model: "One primary container: Day sections", managed as "Day structure", created with "Add section".
2. **Under the heading, one helper line explains it:** "Morning, Evening, or your own, like Before work."
3. **Keep "routine" for the Start run.** People often say "morning routine" for the whole morning. But in our model, "routine" is the optional guided way to *do* a section. Naming the section itself a routine would blur the two.
4. **Leave the section names people see on Home unchanged:** Anytime, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, plus their own.

---

## Key findings

**1. The things being named are not all times of day.**
- The Day Structure model lets people rename sections and add their own: "Before work, After work, School run, Wind down, Night shift".
- **Anytime** has no time at all.
- 125 reviews ask to customise sections. The largest group, 47, want **more or different** ones (lunch, before bed, commute).
- "Time of day" describes Morning/Afternoon/Evening, but not "School run" or "Anytime". "Section" describes all of them.

**2. People describe them as divisions of their day.**
- "I like being able to divide tasks into groups (default morning, afternoon, evening, but you can rename them)" `P12#38292`
- In the 1,791 reviews that name both morning and a later part of the day, people say:

  | Word | Reviews | Notes |
  |---|---|---|
  | "routine" | 863 | Often for the morning *set of habits*, but also for daily life in general |
  | "time of day" | 74 | |
  | "section" | 45 | |
  | "part of the day" | 8 | |

- None of these is a settled user term. So the label should match what's on screen: the list really is split into sections, each with a header.

**3. Round 2's reason was too narrow.** "Time of day" (472 mentions overall) won on phrase frequency alone. That count mostly reflects apps that use the phrase for fixed Morning/Afternoon/Evening blocks, like Habitify's "Time of Day". Our model is broader.

---

**Limits.**
- **Unproven:** "Day sections" is the reports' proposed term, not something reviewers say themselves (the Day Structure report calls it "not empirically proven superior wording").
- **Test it:** run a quick card-sort, asking what people would call the Morning / Evening / Before work headers.
