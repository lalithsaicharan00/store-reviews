# Today Screen, Question 45: A Check Button and a Bigger Icon on Every Tile

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 25 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. This revises Question 44's tile and its tap model.

**The feedback on Question 44.**
- The compact tiles are good.
- But there is **no check mark to tick a habit off quickly.** Logging needs a visible button.
- **Give the icon more emphasis.** Make it a little bigger, because icons are what people recognise fastest.
- Rework the tile so it has room for the button.

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for three things:
  - icon size ("bigger icons", "icons too small", "huge icon");
  - recognising habits by their icon ("at a glance", "tell apart");
  - ticking tiles, circles or grids, including by accident.
- **Read:** all 106 matches. **39 relevant.** Every quote was checked against its review by script.
- **Reused:**
  - Question 34 (a round button beats a bare icon; don't make the whole card the tap target);
  - Question 29 (one button, and one tap does the next step);
  - Question 44 (tile density).
- **Measured on our board** (390 × 844): about 600 pt between the week strip and the floating tab bar.

Citations such as `A23#3441` point to line 3441 (from 0) of `App Store Reviews/23. …/reviews.jsonl`. `P` means Play Store and `N` means the native apps.

---

## The short answer

- **Each tile gets two rows:**
  - **Top: the icon on the left, the round button on the right.** What it is, and how to do it.
  - **Bottom: the name, then the progress on the left and the 🔥 streak on the right.** How far you've got.
- **The icon grows** from 18 pt, bare, to **22 pt inside a 32 pt rounded chip**. It is now the first thing the eye lands on, but the name is still the largest text.
- **The button** is a 30 pt circle with a 44 pt tap area, and its symbol says what one tap does:
  - ✓ ticks;
  - \+ adds one;
  - a triangle starts the timer, which shows ❚❚ while it runs.

  When the habit is done, the button turns solid.
- **Only the button logs.** Tap anywhere else on the tile to open the habit. Hold the button for another amount, Skip or a note. Every log shows Undo.
  - This **reverts Question 44's "the whole tile logs"** and goes back to Question 34's rule.
- **It costs 8 pt of height per tile** (66 → 74). With Question 43's folding rule, the morning view still fits **about 23 habits with no scroll.** With every part open, it fits about 10.

```
┌──────────────────┐
│ [💧]        ( + )│  ← what it is · how to do it
│ Water            │
│ 3/8 glasses  🔥23│  ← how far · streak
└──────────────────┘
  fill behind = today's progress · dark when done
```

## 1. Why a button, and not the whole tile

**Users show that a visible circle is how people expect to tick.**
- 4 reviews call tapping a circle the satisfying part:
  - "the satisfying circle graphic to tick of your items" `A23#2605`;
  - "it is more satisfying to click on a circle and have color pop in" `P2#11912`.
- 2 ask for a grid where each habit is a simple thing to tick: "a grid option where each habit/task is a big button" `P2#11426`.
- **Without a visible control, people don't know how to log.** Question 34 found 9 Done reviews like "Icons mean nothing" `A31#5135`, and 5 that can't find how to tick at all.

**Users show that whole-area ticking goes wrong.**
- 9 reviews (4 apps) tick something by accident, and most of them then struggle to undo it:
  - "when I scroll my screen with my left thumb and click on a circle accidentally" `N10#13567`;
  - "I accidentally clicked on the icon and it marked as I completed it" `A54#351`.
- Question 34 found 4 more who tick while scrolling (`A10#17251`).
- Question 44 made the whole 114 × 66 tile a tick target, which is a far larger area to hit by mistake than a 44 pt button. A button limits accidental ticks to that small area, and Undo covers what's left.

**Small check controls are also a complaint**, so the button can't be tiny:
- "click on a teeny tiny icon in a row of other icons" `A26#3540`;
- "move to a small icon the bottom of the screen to check in" `A86#1363`;
- "Make the selection icons bigger and easier to use" `N6#6687`.

The 44 pt tap area fits inside the tile's top-right corner: from x = 69 to 113 and y = 2 to 46 on a 114 × 74 tile.

**Why top right** (reasoned from first principles, with Question 34):
- **Same place on every tile.** You learn it once, and the buttons form columns you can run down with your thumb.
- **Away from the numbers.** Question 34 found that a circle placed beside a flame and a number reads as one confusing cluster. On top, the button pairs with the icon ("what is it · do it"), and the numbers keep the bottom row to themselves.
- **The name keeps the full width.** A button in the bottom right would cut the name to about 60 pt, or about 7 characters.

*Considered and rejected:*
- **The icon itself as the button** (tap the icon to tick, Streaks-style). One symbol would do two jobs, naming the habit and acting on it, and nothing shows what a tap will do until you learn it. That is the "Icons mean nothing" problem again.
- **Whole-tile tap (Question 44).** It causes accidental ticks, as shown above.

## 2. How big should the icon be?

**What reviews show: bigger than tiny, but not huge.**

| What they say | Reviews | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Icons too small, want them larger | 5 | "Too small icons and buttons" `A1#44627`; "expecting large icons" `P3#14190` |
| Icons help recognise habits at a glance | 4 | "the icons are neat allowing you to recognize tasks at a glance" `A13#18050` |
| Icons too big (Streaks' redesign and others) | 4 | "the icons for tasks are now too big" `A23#1932`; "too big icon on habit list" `P98#3176` |
| Want smaller icons so more fit on a page | 4 (all Streaks) | "Use smaller icons so users have the option of seeing everything on one page" `A23#5213` |
| A huge icon drowns out the streak | 2 | "a tiny number under a huge icon for the habit" `A23#3441` |
| Icons alone get ambiguous as habits grow | 1 | "as the number of habits grow icons alone make it hard to tell what is what" `A41#688` |

**What this means for the tile:**
- **Make it bigger.** 18 pt bare was the size of a text glyph. 22 pt in a tinted 32 pt chip gives it a clear shape and weight, and it is the first thing the eye lands on.
- **Don't let it take over.** Streaks' big icons cost it both density (6 per page) and the streak's visibility, and that is where its complaints come from. So the chip is the same height as the button, and the streak keeps its own slot at a readable 11 pt SemiBold.
- **Always keep the name.** Icons speed up recognition, but they don't replace words once people have many habits (`A41#688`). A large icon next to a small word is what one reviewer praises (`A46#2050`).
- **Next step:** each habit's own colour in the chip (Question 44's open point) would speed up recognition further. The wireframes stay greyscale for now.

## 3. Capacity: what the button costs

| Tile | Height | All parts open (6 rows) | Morning at 8:10, Question 43's rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Question 44 (no button, 18 pt icon) | 66 | ~12 | ~25 |
| **Question 45 (button, 22 pt icon)** | **74** | **~10** | **~23** |

- The button needs its own row alongside the icon, which adds 8 pt to every tile.
- In practice, Question 43's rule is what applies: later parts fold. So the usual morning is board K3 (12 habits, with half the screen spare) or board L3 (23 habits, no scroll).
- **The limit is rows, not habits.** A seventh Anytime habit starts a new row and pushes the last folded line under the tab bar. Past that, the page scrolls, or the user folds Anytime.
- *Considered and rejected:* squeezing the streak into the top row between the icon and the button to save height. It leaves about 28 pt, which is too little for "🔥 4 wk" or three-digit streaks, so the streak would change place for some habits.

## 4. Every type, same four places (board P3)

| Type | Not started | In progress | Done | Button does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yes / no | "Not yet", ✓ outline | — | dark tile, solid ✓ | tick |
| Count | "0/8 glasses", + | fill 5/8, + | dark, solid ✓ | +1, and keeps counting past the goal |
| Timer | "0/20 min", triangle | "12:40 left", ❚❚ | dark, solid ✓ | start / pause |
| Weekly | "0/3 this wk", ✓ | fill 1/3 | dark, solid ✓ | today done |
| Cut back | "0/2 max", + | fill 1/2 | **over**: red tint "3/2 max", + stays | +1, honestly past the max |
| Quit | live "12d 04:23:07", "Best 45d" top right | — | — | **no button**; a slip is logged inside the habit |
| Skipped | "Skipped", dashed circle with – | — | — | set from the hold menu |

- Weekly now reads "this wk" so the progress and "🔥 4 wk" fit on one line at 3 across.
- The quit tile has no button, because its counter is the point (Question 43). Its best streak moves to the top right, so the live counter gets the whole bottom line.

## What this does not settle

- **Usability test:**
  - do people read the triangle as "start the timer", and + as "one more"? (open since Question 34);
  - do they find the hold menu?
- **Left-handed use:** the button is always on the right. One left-handed reviewer ticks the *left* circle while scrolling (`N10#18008`). A mirrored layout could be an Appearance option later, reasoned from first principles.
- **Large text:** at large Dynamic Type the tile drops to two across (Question 44's rule), and the button keeps its 44 pt tap area.
- **Habit colour in the icon chip** is not drawn yet.
