# Today Screen, Question 44: Compact Habit Tiles — The Whole Day Without Scrolling

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 25 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. This builds on Questions 42 and 43.

**The feedback on Question 43.**
- If nothing is hidden, people with many habits still scroll. We are back at square one.
- Does each habit have to be a full-width list row? Find a compact card: clean, no horizontal or vertical scrolling.
- It must still show the streak and the progress ("0/8 glasses", "0/20 min").

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for two things:
  - grid, tile, column and circle layouts;
  - names being cut off or truncated.
- **Read:** all 125 habit-app matches; **29 relevant**, with quotes checked by script.
- **Reused:**
  - Question 42 (153 want the whole day on one screen; 68 want denser rows);
  - the Home Screen Cards report (tile and grid layouts liked; circles polarising: 92 praise and 47 complaints, the complaints about density; big cards and one-per-screen criticised).
- **Space measured on our own board** (120:4, 390 × 844): about 600 pt of list between the week strip and the floating tab bar.

---

## The short answer

- **Make each habit a small tile, three across,** instead of a full-width row. Each tile keeps four things in fixed places:
  - the **icon** and the **streak** on top;
  - the **name**;
  - the **progress** ("0/8 glasses", "12:40 left", "12d 04:23:07").
- **The tile fills as you go and turns dark when done.**
  - The light tiles are what's left, so done habits don't need to move or hide.
- **Tap the tile to do the next step** (tick, +1, start the timer). **Hold it to open the habit.** Every tap shows Undo.
- **Capacity with no scrolling:**
  - **12 habits with every part open;**
  - **about 25** when later parts fold to their one-line summary (Question 43's rule).
- A full-width list fits about 7.

---

## Why rows can't get there (measured)

| Layout | One habit takes | Habits that fit in ~600 pt, all parts open | …with later parts folded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today's card row | 358 × 64 + gap | ~7 | ~9 |
| Compact row | 358 × 52 + gap | ~9 | ~11 |
| **Tile, 3 across** | **114 × 66** | **~12–15** | **~25** |
| Tile, 4 across | 84 × 70 | ~16–18 | ~30 |

- A row spends its full width on one habit, but a habit's content (icon, a short name, "0/8") needs about a third of it.
- Three across uses that width.
- Four across fits more, but the names break. At 84 pt a name holds about 9 characters, so "Call fami…", "Brush te…", "2/3 this we…" (board M).

**What this adds up to:**
- **Density:** people ask for density.
- **Circles vs tiles:** circles are loved for looks but waste their corners. Six per page was Streaks' biggest density complaint (Home Screen Cards report). Rounded tiles give the same "tap a shape" feel with room for text.
- **Streak:** it has to stay on the tile.
- **Names:** they must not be cut so short they can't be read. That rules out 4 across as the default.

## The tile

| Place | Shows | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Top left | icon (18 pt) | recognition at a glance: icon and colour are how people tell habits apart (Home Screen Cards report) |
| Top right | 🔥 streak ("23 days"; "Best 45 d" for quit) | must be on the main screen (5 reviews, all 2–3★); unit rule from Question 31 |
| Middle | name, one line (about 14 characters at 3 across) | readable; longer names end in "…" and show in full when the habit is opened |
| Bottom | progress: "0/8 glasses", "12:40 left", "3/3 this week", "12d 04:23:07" | the number people want (Questions 32 and 34) |
| Background | fills left to right with today's progress; dark when done; red tint when a cut-back is over its max | "the card is the progress" (Question 34), kept |

**Checked for every type and state (board P):**
- Types: yes/no, count, timer, weekly, cut back and quit.
- States: not started, in progress and done.
- Every tile uses the same four places.
- A quit tile has no tap action: its bottom line is the live counter, and a slip is logged inside the habit (Question 35).

**Tap model: this changes Question 34** ("only the round button logs").
- The tile has no room for a separate button, so **the whole tile is the button.**
- At 114 × 66 it is well above the 44 pt minimum. Every tap shows Undo (C223). Hold opens the habit.
- This is the model of Apple's Home app tiles (reasoned precedent).
- **Risk: accidental ticks while scrolling.** With no scrolling in the common case that risk is smaller, but test it.

---

## Rules that keep it honest

1. **Three across is the default.**
2. **The column count follows text size, never habit count.** At large Dynamic Type, tiles drop to two across and grow taller.
   - That respects C171 (accessibility) and the 7 truncation complaints (`A54#361`: text cut off with larger text settings).
   - The layout never changes because you added a habit (no hidden state).
3. **Question 43's folding rule stays:**
   - open: Quitting, Anytime, the Now part, and earlier parts with habits left;
   - folded to one line: later and finished parts.

   With tiles, all parts stay open up to about 12 habits.
4. **Done tiles stay in place and turn dark.** This settles the "hide done or keep it" split from Question 42 (85 vs 14). What's left is visible at a glance by colour, without anything jumping away from under your thumb.
5. **Parts start a new row.** A part with one habit leaves two empty slots. That is the price of keeping the day's order visible. *Considered and rejected:* flowing parts into each other without breaks, because it hides where a part starts.

---

## What this does not settle

- **Past about 25 habits with most of them open** (for example 12 in Anytime), even tiles need a scroll, or Anytime folded by the user.
- **The tap model change** (whole tile vs a button) needs a usability test for accidental ticks.
- **Tile colour:** the user's own colour per habit (Home Screen Cards report: colour is loved when it's the user's) is not drawn yet. The wireframes are greyscale.
- **The list layout could stay as an option** in Me → Appearance (Question 7), for people who prefer rows (3 reviews ask Streaks for a list). Whether to offer both is open.
