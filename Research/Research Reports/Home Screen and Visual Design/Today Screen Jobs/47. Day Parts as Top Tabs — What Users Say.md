# Today Screen, Question 47: Day Parts as Top Tabs — What Users Say

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 25 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision. Data only; nothing is designed here.** Decisions live in Notion.

**The question.**
- Some habit apps show the parts of the day as **tabs in the top header** instead of stacked sections: Productive, Habitify, Me+.
- Google Tasks shows its lists as top tabs, and people want to **swipe left and right** between them.
- What is the user experience of each?
- The idea on the table combines the two: **day-part tabs you can swipe between.**
- Quitting and Anytime have to fit in too.

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** every habit-app review (App Store and Play) plus Google Tasks (native iOS) for four things:
  - a part of the day next to a control word (tab, filter, swipe, page, slider);
  - a part of the day next to "hidden", "disappeared" or "can't see";
  - "only shows the current part";
  - swiping between lists, tabs or pages.
- **Read by hand:** all **341 matches**. **119 are relevant.** Every quote was checked against its review by script.
- **Reused:**
  - Question 42 (153 want the whole day on one screen);
  - Question 43 (Anytime and Quitting);
  - the Google Tasks review report;
  - the navigation report.

Citations such as `A13#13733` point to line 13733 (from 0) of `App Store Reviews/13. …/reviews.jsonl`. `P` means Play Store and `N` the native apps.

---

## The short answer

1. **Seeing only one part of the day is liked as an option and hated as the default.**
   - Productive shows the natural experiment. Before December 2019 it let you focus on the current part or slide back to "the entire day", and people praised that.
   - Its 2.0 update (21 December 2019) made the parts into **separate swipe pages** that open on the current part. **Nine complaints came in within ten days**, mostly 1–2★.
2. **What goes wrong with day-part tabs, in order of how often people say it:**
   - no single list of the whole day;
   - the app jumps to the current part, even when it's empty;
   - habits from other parts are hidden: a missed morning habit, all-day habits;
   - you swipe to get anywhere;
   - swiping and ticking collide.
3. **Swiping between lists is wanted, a lot:** 27 reviews ask for it (Google Tasks 11, Streaks 8, the Tasks to-do app 4).
   - Where it exists, it's liked (5), but **it collides with swipe-to-delete** (6 in one app).
   - With **many** lists, horizontal top tabs get their own complaint: "requires repeatedly swiping left and right to find a specific list".
4. **Horizontal swipe is already claimed** by a different job: moving between days. Eight reviews ask for it; one app that removed it got 20+ complaints (Question 42, C010).
5. **Anytime and Quitting are the hardest fit.** Hiding all-day habits behind the current part is the worst-rated failure in this whole area (Productive, 2.11★ in Question 43). Quit counters only help when they're seen.

## 1. Productive: the natural experiment (23 reviews, 2016–2024)

| Period | What the app did | What reviewers said |
|---|---|---|
| **2018–mid 2019** | Showed the current part of the day, with a **slider at the top to switch between the current part and the entire day** | **Liked:** "By only showing one of applicable time frames, it helps keep you focused" `A13#15613`; "much, much less intimidating than showing them all at once" `A13#14549`; "the new slider at the top to switch between the current part of the day versus the entire day" `A13#14645` |
| **21 Dec 2019 (2.0)** | Parts became **horizontal pages you swipe between**, and the app **opens on the current part** | **9 complaints in 10 days**, mostly 1–2★ (quotes below) |
| **2020–2024** | Same structure | Still asking for "All day" as the default page (`A13#12292`, `A13#1766`, `A13#5406`) |

**What 2.0 reviewers said:**
- "Rather than having morning, afternoon, evening and all day tasks in one list like before, I now have to scroll sideways from morning list to all day" `A13#6092`
- "auto focusing on time of day even though I have zero habits for this time" `A13#13733`
- "I want to be able to see my “anytime” habits all of the time" `A13#13723`
- "I hate having to swipe to see all the habits. At least let the default view be “all day”" `A13#13702`
- "I don’t want to swipe left and right a thousand times (while accidentally marking tasks as completed or skipped because it’s the same motion)" `A13#13744`
- "It keeps opening into the morning section instead of the full day" `A13#13651`
- "not one overview of all habits/tasks and you end up swiping between morning/afternoon/etc." `A13#9058`

**What changed between the liked version and the hated one:**
- **Liked:** one list with a *filter*. The whole day was one slide away and stayed that way.
- **Hated:**
  - *pages* with no single list;
  - opening on the current part every time;
  - a sideways swipe to reach the rest of the day, the same gesture as ticking a habit.

**Across the corpus:**
- **Negative experiences with tabs:** 20 reviews. That counts the dislike, jump-to-current, hidden-habit, missing-habit and collision rows, with no review counted twice; mean 2.45★.
- **Positive:** 8 like tabs and 6 ask for them, mostly as an *option* or a *filter*.
- **Bigger context:**
  - Question 42: 153 reviews want the whole day on one screen, and 82 are overwhelmed by a flat list.
  - The Day Structure report: time-of-day sections are the most-praised structure (656 reviews).
  - The same report: for **groups**, filter or tab switching is asked for more often than headers (260 against 158). Tabs are wanted for groups, not for parts of the day.

## 3. Streaks' pages: a close cousin (19 reviews)

Streaks shows 6 habits per page, with up to 4 pages, switched by dots at the bottom.

| What they say | Reviews | Mean ★ | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use pages as parts of the day** | 7 (+1 HabitKit widgets) | 4.75 | "I have my morning, afternoon, and evening habits on their own page" `A23#4780` |
| **Want to swipe between pages** (only tapping is possible) | 8 | 3.9 | "I constantly try to swipe left/right between the pages of the tasks" `A23#1544` |
| **Tired of flipping pages; want one page** | 4 | 3.75 | "I find it tiring to swipe between all pages" `A23#3151` |

- People like **grouping** by part of the day, and expect **swipe** when there are pages.
- A minority still wants everything on one page.

## 4. Google Tasks and other list apps: swiping between tabs (47 reviews)

| What they say | Reviews | Mean ★ | Apps | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Want swipe left/right between lists or tabs** | 27 | 3.96 | Google Tasks 11, Streaks 8, Tasks 4, others | "I wish I could just swipe left or right to go to my other list" `N6#2133` |
| **Like swiping between lists** where it exists | 5 | 3.40 | Tasks 4, Habitica | "Swiping sideways to see sublist is awesome" `P84#32239` |
| **Swipe to switch collides with swipe to delete** | 6 | 4.00 | Tasks | "try to swipe to switch between lists and youll remove an item from your list" `P84#29536` |
| **Horizontal top tabs get painful with many lists** | 2 (+3 from the navigation report) | 2.0 | Google Tasks | "the lists are displayed as horizontal tabs at the top, which requires repeatedly swiping left and right to find a specific list" `N6#4541` |
| Want to see several lists at once | 1 | 5 | Google Tasks | "view a few lists concurrently instead of just one at a time" `N6#4419` |

**From the Google Tasks review report:**
- Lists became top tabs in 2021, and praise for moving between lists grew from 0% to 3.6% of reviews (71 reviews, 4.65★).
- A Japanese reviewer: tabs "definitely got easier to use".
- The 2026 redesign removed the side menu and left only scrolling tabs. Complaints followed: "I absolutely despise that the little hamburger menu was taken away to flip through the many lists I have" `N6#4827`.

**So, for tabs:**
- Top tabs plus swipe work for **a handful of lists you use one at a time**.
- They fail when there are **many** tabs, or when you need to see **across** them.

## 5. Two gestures competing for the same swipe

| Job | Evidence it needs a horizontal swipe |
|---|---|
| **Move between days** (Today → yesterday) | 8 ask for it here (HabitNow 5, Done, Habitify, Evoday): "make swiping right/left in the Today's tab to switch between other dates" `P2#10421`. One app that removed it: "removed the ability to just swipe to go back and forth a single day" `A31#3310`. Question 42: 20+ complaints in Done (C010). |
| **Move between day parts or lists** | 27 want it (section 4) |
| **Log or delete on a row** | Productive's swipe-to-tick and Tasks' swipe-to-delete both collided with swipe-to-switch (8 reviews, sections 1 and 4) |

- One horizontal swipe can only do one of these.
- The corpus shows collisions every time two share it.

## 6. Where Anytime and Quitting sit in a tabbed day (from Questions 43 and 36)

- **Anytime:**
  - 48 reviews say they have habits with no set time.
  - Hiding all-day habits behind the current part is the worst-rated failure found: Productive 2.0, 2.11★.
  - One reviewer's request covers it: "“Anytime tasks” should show up all of the time like they did before" `A13#13723`.
- **Quitting:**
  - 24 reviews say seeing the counter every day is what keeps them going; 44 praise the live counter.
  - The typical user keeps 3 counters.
  - A counter on a tab you're not on is not seen.
- **Habitify's answer:** an **"All Habits" tab**, plus a time-of-day bar that **switches automatically** to the current block. Its reviews show:
  - one "filter hid my habits" confusion (`A33#1164`);
  - two asks to turn the categorisation off (`P37#359`, `P37#702`);
  - one praise (`A33#3588`).

---

## What this does not settle

- **Counts are small.** Reviewers rarely talk about this pattern: 119 relevant reviews. Productive's 2019 switch is the clearest evidence, because the same users saw both versions within days.
- **Where tabs or filters are liked, it is always as an option on top of an all-day view.** No review praises an app that *only* offers one part at a time with no single list.
- **Swipe demand is mostly for lists and pages, not day parts.** Only 1 review asks to swipe between morning, afternoon and evening in a habit app (`A1#53099`).
