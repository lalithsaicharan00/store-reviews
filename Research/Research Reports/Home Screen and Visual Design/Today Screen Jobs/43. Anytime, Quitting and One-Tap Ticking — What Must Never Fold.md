# Today Screen, Question 43: Anytime, Quitting and One-Tap Ticking — What Must Never Fold

> **Written by Claude (Claude Code)**, 25 September 2026. Authorship of every report is listed in the [Research Reports index](<../../README.md>).

**Status: research, not a decision.** Decisions live in Notion. This revises Question 42.

**The feedback on Question 42.**
- Folded parts with icons and "N left" do the morning glance well.
- But folding breaks **"tick it off in one tap"**. Anytime has no time, so it would never be the Now part and would never open.
- **Quit habits** only help when the live counter is seen. Folding them away defeats the point.
- **Containers are inconsistent:** a folded part sits in a white box; an open part has no box.
- The first question to settle: **do users really have "anytime" habits, or do we not need Anytime at all?** One habit can already appear in several parts.

**Basis.**
- **Screened:** all 1,487,223 reviews for two things:
  - wording about habits with no set time: "any time of day", "all day habits", "no specific time", "forced to pick a time";
  - wording about keeping several quit counters: "multiple counters", "3 addictions", "more than 2 streaks".
- **Matched:** 291 untimed and 261 counter reviews. All 552 were read. **143 were relevant.** All quotes were checked against the review text by script.
- **Reused:**
  - the Day Structure verification (Productive's time-of-day focus hid all-day habits);
  - Questions 35 and 36 (quit habits: 24 say seeing the count daily is what works; 44 praise the live counter).

Citations such as `A13#13723` point to line 13723 (from 0) of `App Store Reviews/13. …/reviews.jsonl`. `P` means Play Store and `N` means the native apps.

---

## The short answer

1. **Yes, we need Anytime.** Many habits have no time of day. Forcing a time is one of the worst-rated changes in the corpus.
   - It is not a time of day, though, so it must never fold by itself.
2. **Quit counters never fold by themselves either.** The live number is the whole point.
   - Most people keep 2 or 3. About 1 in 4 keep 5 or more.
   - Each has a "Show on Today" switch (Question 35).
3. **The folding rule becomes: whatever you can act on right now is open.**
   - Open: Quitting, Anytime, the Now part, and any earlier part with habits left.
   - One line each: later parts, and parts that are all done.
   - Every habit you might tick right now is one tap away.
4. **One container rule for every part, folded or open: no box.**
   - A part is its header line plus its cards.
   - Folded, the header line also shows the part's icons and "N left".
   - The line looks the same in both states; only the chevron and what follows the name change.

## 1. Do users have "anytime" habits? (143 relevant reviews)

| What they say | Reviews | Apps | Mean ★ | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **They have habits with no set time** | 48 | 23 | 3.98 | "a lot of anyone's habits aren't strictly for one time of day" `A13#12697` |
| **Being forced to give every task a time** | 24 | 11 | **2.75** | "I'm forced to set the time on each task to 11:59pm to mimc an all day task" `P126#17564` |
| **All-day habits hidden** behind the current part | 9 | 2 | **2.11** | "auto focusing on time of day even though I have zero habits for this time … So I open the app and see zero content" `A13#13733` |
| **Want untimed habits always in view** | 10 | 4 | 3.60 | "I want to be able to see my “anytime” habits all of the time" `A13#13723` |
| …but also **want time parts** (not everything "any time") | 22 | 6 | 3.14 | "All my tasks go into “any time of day” which makes the app useless to me" `A4#1172` (Me+) |
| Like seeing only the current part | 2 | 1 | 3.50 | "By only showing one of applicable time frames, it helps keep you focused" `A13#15613` |

**What this shows:**

- **Both kinds exist, and people want both.**
  - When Me+ removed its parts and put everything under "any time of day", users complained (22).
  - When a to-do app made a time compulsory, its ratings fell (24, mean 2.75): "Please make the time element optional again" `P126#18262`.
  - Weekly and x-per-week habits are untimed by nature: "4x a week anytime of day" `A13#13673`.
- **"Appears in several parts" does not replace Anytime.** It covers habits done *more than once* at set parts (teeth AM and PM). It doesn't cover a habit done *once, whenever* (read, walk, call family, 3× a week gym). Forcing those into a part makes them look late or early for no reason.
- **The failure to avoid has already happened to our model.** Productive 2.0 opened on the current part and hid the all-day list. Its highest-ranked reviews in that period are 1★ and 2★ reviews saying exactly that (`A13#6092`, `A13#13733`, `A13#13744`, `A13#13723`). Question 42's "fold what isn't Now" would have repeated it.

**Other apps:**
- **Apple Reminders:** untimed items sit above Morning, Afternoon and Tonight in Today.
- **Things 3:** Today, with "This Evening" at the bottom.
- **Habitify's focus mode:** shows the current part *together with* anytime habits (`A33#3693`).

None of them folds untimed items away.

**So Anytime stays, and it:**
- sits at the top, under Quitting;
- is always open unless the user folds it (then it stays folded until they open it; remembered);
- keeps its place all day.

*Considered and rejected:* letting untimed habits move into whichever part is Now. It keeps them open, but a habit would change place three times a day. That breaks "same place, every time", reasoned from first principles.

## 2. Quit habits: the counter has to be seen

- **Evidence from Questions 35 and 36:**
  - 24 reviews say seeing the count every day is what keeps them going;
  - 44 praise a live counter.
- **In this pass:** 15 Days Since reviewers say they put the counter on the Home Screen so they see it without opening the app: "I've broken multiple bad habits just by adding this as a widget on my phone" `A3#6495`.
- **Folding the counter away removes what works.**

**How many counters?** 25 reviewers state a number:

| Counters | Reviewers |
|---|---|
| 2 | 10 |
| 3 | 5 |
| 4 | 1 |
| 5–6 | 5 |
| 8–12 | 3 |
| 20+ | 1 |

- The median is 3. Many apps' free limit of 2 is a common complaint: "more than 2 streaks" `P6#65`.
- Examples:
  - "I'm tracking 5 bad habits" `A3#2066`;
  - "I've 8" `A3#3277`;
  - "around 12 different counters referring to 3 or 4 habits" `A3#3283`.

**So Quitting:**
- never folds by itself;
- keeps the Question 36 card (live "12 days 04:23:07", Since, Best);
- takes Compact rows (48 pt, one line: icon · name · live time) when the user picks Compact;
- has **Show on Today** per counter, for anyone who wants a counter off the home screen (Question 35);
- can be folded by the user; its folded line then still shows "12 days" for the longest counter.

This revises Question 35 ("It collapses like any section"): **by the user, yes; by the app, never.**

## 3. One tap for everything you can do now

The folding rule, restated so it serves both the glance and the tick:

| Part | Open? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Quitting | always | the live number is the point |
| Anytime | always (user may fold) | due all day; Productive shows what hiding it does |
| The Now part | always | where most ticks happen |
| An earlier part with habits left | open | still doable now, so one tap. "Catch-up" is a Today job (step 1 board) |
| An earlier part, all done | folded: "All done" | nothing to do; it's also the finished look (Question 30) |
| A later part | folded: icons + "N left" | the morning plan, without spending the screen |

- **Ticking a habit from a later part** (doing the evening walk at noon) takes two taps: open, then tick. The part then stays open for the day.
- *Reasoned:* this is rare, and the icons on the folded line show it's there.
- *Considered and rejected:* making the icons on a folded line tappable ticks. They are 24 pt, below the 44 pt minimum, next to each other, and a tick has no confirmation step. Accidental ticks are already a known complaint (Question 34, C223).

## 4. One container rule

**The problem.** In Question 42, a folded part was a white box and an open part was bare. A box that disappears when you open it makes the same object look like two different things.

**Two consistent options:**

- **A. No box, ever (recommended).**
  - Every part is its header line plus its cards.
  - The header line is identical folded and open: chevron, name, then the Now badge and Start when open, or the icons and "N left" when folded.
  - Each habit is already its own card, and the card fill is the progress (Question 34). A box around cards would put cards inside a card.
- **B. A box around every part.**
  - A tinted panel holds the header and, when open, its cards.
  - This groups strongly (common region) but adds a layer of edges, and uses about 16 pt more height per part.

**Recommendation: A.**
- It keeps the page flat and matches Apple Reminders and Things, whose Today headers are bare.
- A folded line still reads as tappable through its chevron and icons.
- B is drawn in Figma for comparison.

## What this does not settle

- **The later parts' lines can drop below the fold, and sooner than expected.**
  - In Figma board F (12 habits: 1 quit counter, 3 Anytime, 4 Morning), the Afternoon and Evening lines already sit just below the fold at 8:10.
  - At 25 habits with 8 in Anytime, they are a full screen down.
  - The user can fold Anytime (remembered), and Compact rows help.
  - Rule 1 (everything tickable now is open) and "the whole day on one screen" pull in different directions once untimed habits get past about 6. We chose one tap.
- **Whether a user-folded Anytime should reopen each new day.** Reasoned default: no, it stays folded until they open it.
- **Quitting with 8+ counters** is a long block at the top. "Show on Today" is the escape hatch. A two-column tile layout was considered and rejected, because it would be the only place the card changes shape (the same-model rule).
