# Q45 codebook
ICON_BIG_WANT         wants habit/task icons (and buttons) larger
ICON_TOO_BIG          says the habit icons are too big (mostly Streaks' redesign)
ICON_SMALLER_DENSITY  wants smaller icons so more habits fit on one page (Streaks' 6-per-page grid)
ICON_OVERSHADOWS      a large icon crowds out the streak number
ICON_RECOG            icons help recognise habits at a glance, or asks for an icon to identify each
ICON_ALONE_AMBIG      icons alone become ambiguous as habits grow; names needed
TINY_CHECK            the check-off control is too small or far away
BIG_PRESS_GO          misses a big press-and-go target replaced by a small play icon
ACCIDENTAL_CHECK      ticks a circle by accident (often while scrolling) and can't easily undo
CHECK_SATISFYING      tapping a circle to tick is satisfying / easy
GRID_BIG_BUTTON       wants a grid where each habit is a big, simple button to tick
