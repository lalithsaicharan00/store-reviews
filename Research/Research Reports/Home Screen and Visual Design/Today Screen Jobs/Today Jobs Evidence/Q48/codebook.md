# Q48 codebook — how many quit habits and anytime habits people keep
QN=n          exact number of quit counters or habits the reviewer tracks or has quit (a range counts as its midpoint; "a couple" = 2)
QV=FEW|SEVERAL|MANY|MULTI   more than one quit habit, with no exact number
MIX           the review also mentions build (good) habits in the same app
QOF=q/t       q quit habits out of t habits in total
MULTI_PRAISE  praises that the app allows multiple counters (feature praise; not counted as the reviewer's own number)
Screened: App + Play habit and quit apps. Families: Q1_COUNT 1,008 (464 after removing "N habit trackers/apps" noise), Q2_QUITN 58, A1_ANYN 3, A2_SPLIT 15; 540 unique, all read.
Merged with Q43's QN statements (one new review added). The exact counts come from 74 people; 89 more are vague.
Anytime: reviews give no per-person counts (18 matches, none usable). The anytime figures in the report are a labelled model: the Q46 habit-count distribution × the share of habits that are anytime, taken from Loggd's top-40 habit names.
