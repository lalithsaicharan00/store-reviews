# External sources (fetched 2026-09-24)
- Habitify Help, "See the Progress of a Good Habit": consistency score does not count Off-Mode days or "Non-Scheduled Days"; "all averages are calculated based only on the days the habit was active". Calendar marks: solid circle = success, incomplete circle = partial, right arrow = skipped, black cross = failed. https://intercom.help/habitify-app/en/articles/6113616-see-the-progress-of-a-good-habit
- Habitify Help, "Learn about Streak": Mon/Wed/Fri habit — "Skip on the scheduled days or check in on other days won't change the streak." https://intercom.help/habitify-app/en/articles/6113621-learn-about-streak-in-habitify
- Loop Habit Tracker FAQ (GitHub discussion 689): unfilled checkmarks are added automatically for non-daily (x per period) habits; no fixed weekdays — frequency only. https://github.com/iSoron/uhabits/discussions/689
- Streaks site: "Some tasks aren't for every day. Set the days so you don't break your streak!" (specific days or N days/week). https://streaksapp.com/ . Reviews show non-due tasks are displayed dimmed on the grid (A23 reviews).
- Habitica wiki / issue 494: Dailies not due today are shown grey; "Due" and "Grey" filters. https://github.com/HabitRPG/habitica/issues/494 ; https://habitica.fandom.com/wiki/Dailies (402 on fetch; via search snippet)
- HabitKit Help: no specific-weekday schedules ("no way to choose particular weekdays"); no rest-day feature; missed days show as gaps; weekly goal streak adds qualifying days. https://habitkit.app/help/habits-and-streaks/how-streaks-are-counted ; https://habitkit.app/help/habits-and-streaks/take-a-day-off-without-breaking-your-streak
- Apple Fitness (iOS): goals can be set per day of week; rings can be paused up to 90 days without breaking award streaks. https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/adjust-your-activity-ring-goals-iph9a08e004e/ios
- WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.1 Use of Color: "Color is not used as the only visual means of conveying information..." https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html
