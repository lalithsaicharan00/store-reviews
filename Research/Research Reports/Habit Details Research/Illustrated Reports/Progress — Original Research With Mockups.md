## Original Year in pixels

Twelve month columns and 31 date rows show a portrait year with exact date inspection, larger calendar access and an adjacent legend accordion. This geometry is a research proposal, not proven accessible merely because it fits. Preserve unknown, zero, partial, met, skip, pause, future and impossible-date distinctions.

## Quit event record example

A recorded interval and saved slips are meaningful facts; no slip recorded is not verified abstinence. Preserve past intervals after a slip and label restart/pause semantics. Do not reuse build-habit completion percentages or invented medical/money benefits. This original example is not a fully populated quit-specific revision.
