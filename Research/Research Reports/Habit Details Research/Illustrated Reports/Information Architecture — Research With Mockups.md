# Habit Details Information Architecture — Research With Mockups

Written by Codex, 3 October 2026. Illustrated companion; the original research text is preserved separately.

**Mockup status: schematic layout and content study only. Habit names, dates, values and note text are dummy/illustrative data. These are not final visual designs. Final visuals need substantial polish: typography, spacing, hierarchy, colors, chart labels, accessibility, native interaction and all states. No usability test or wired interaction prototype is implied.**

**Version status:** Three jobs with separate History / Notes / Progress tabs are accepted. The first board documents the architecture; its calendar-first History and coarse Progress placeholders are earlier studies, superseded by the focused History and revised Progress companions.

[Unchanged original research report](<../Original Reports/Day Structure and Organization/Habit Details — Information Architecture Research.md>) · [Full Figma study](https://www.figma.com/design/Ncccsm1l2O62GJ5xLSInqk/Design?node-id=317-133).

A habit visit can mean correcting a record, retrieving written context, or understanding progress. Keep common context small: habit name, actual goal/schedule, status and management menu. Preserve destination intent: a general habit visit opens History; a Progress link opens Progress and its relevant period; a note/day link opens its matching context. The three-tab choice is a design response to those jobs, not a review-derived preference percentage.

## Habit management menu

Edit habit configuration, Pause/Resume, Archive/Restore and Delete belong in the top-right three-dot menu. Editing an entry stays with History; editing a note stays with the note. Archive retains history; destructive deletion needs explicitly scoped confirmation. The pictured menu is placement/content guidance, not a finished native menu.
