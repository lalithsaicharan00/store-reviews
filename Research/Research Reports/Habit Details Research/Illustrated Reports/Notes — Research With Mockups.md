[Unchanged original research report](<../Original Reports/Day Structure and Organization/Habit Notes — Research and Recommended Experience.md>) · [Full Figma study](https://www.figma.com/design/Ncccsm1l2O62GJ5xLSInqk/Design?node-id=337-276).

Keep notes independently useful: add notes on incomplete, skipped or note-only days; read them without changing progress. History and Notes open the same habit/day note. Standing habit instructions belong in Edit habit. One note per habit/day is the current-model baseline, not a proven universal preference.

## Browse notes for one habit

Show visible Search notes and Add note, latest tracking date first, month/year headings and short previews. Omit empty dates and mandatory titles. Search is within this habit’s text; show matching passage/date and a clear empty/no-match state. Editing an old note must not move it to Today’s chronological position.

## Read the full note

A row opens a reader with habit, date and full text. Keyboard stays closed; Edit note is explicit. View day opens the matching History day. Delete note is scoped to this habit/date and preserves progress. Reader geometry remains a proposal.

![Schematic Read the full note](<../Mockups/Notes/Read Full Note.png>)

[Editable Figma source](https://www.figma.com/design/Ncccsm1l2O62GJ5xLSInqk/Design?node-id=337-328).

## Add or edit a note

Use comfortable multiline writing with reachable Save/Cancel. Add defaults to Today, or the day from the originating context; an existing note opens its text. Preserve unsaved drafts across interruption and distinguish draft from saved state. The System keyboard block is an intentional wireframe placeholder, not the keyboard design. Validate active-line visibility, scrolling and large text.

## Full study board

![Full research board](<../Mockups/Notes/Study Board.png>)

For short capture retain the keyboard-docked NoteBar, with Expand carrying the same draft/date into a larger editor. Required titles, prompts, rich text, attachments and unlimited entries/day are not established baseline requirements. The 1,000-character cap is an existing constraint, not a research-validated ideal. The original report and 24 verified originals retain all evidence, caveats and pending checks.
