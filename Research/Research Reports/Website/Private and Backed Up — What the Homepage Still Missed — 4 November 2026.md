## What the page said before this section

Every computer frame's text was searched on 4 Nov 2026.

- **Nowhere on the page:** offline, internet, back up, restore, export, spreadsheet, new phone, privacy, Android, iPhone, dark, note.
- **All the page said about safety:** "No account. No ads. Your habits stay private." in the hero, "No time limit and no account." and "Backup to your own Google Drive or iCloud" as one row of the Free list.

## What users show

A machine scan of 372,850 English reviews of habit trackers. **These are keyword counts, not hand-coded, and they are noisy:** "lost my money" and "reinstall" for a crash both land here. Use them as a ranking, never as exact numbers.

| Worry | Mentions (4–5★ / 3★ / 1–2★) | Share of reviews | 1–2★ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lost data | 225 / 96 / 485 | 0.22% | 60% |
| New phone, reinstall | 413 / 226 / 953 | 0.43% | 59% |
| Privacy | 448 / 95 / 654 | 0.32% | 54% |
| Forced account | 364 / 79 / 519 | 0.26% | 53% |
| Backup or restore | 828 / 224 / 655 | 0.46% | 38% |
| Between iPhone and Android | 113 / 32 / 85 | 0.06% | 36% |
| Offline | 243 / 57 / 119 | 0.11% | 28% |
| Export | 591 / 57 / 112 | 0.20% | 14% |

Few reviews mention these, but they are among the angriest. Forty random samples were read by hand, ten from each of four groups, and the worry is real:

- Users show the loss happens on a change of phone: "Lost all progress when switching to new device."
- Users show updates and restores that failed, and backups they thought they had.
- **Of ten new-phone reviews read, about as many were about a paid plan not carrying over as about habits.** Ten is a small sample, but it matters for the Plus page: say that Plus comes back with the store.

Rulebook D2 already records the same thing from the wider study: an update that wiped data is the most damaging failure in 1.5 M reviews, at 53.9% 1★.

## Search

The [Website Reset](<Website Reset — Pages, SEO and AI Search — 4 November 2026.md>) plan lists *private habit tracker*, *offline habit tracker* and *habit tracker without account* to test (none measured). The page had no text for them, and a page can't rank for words it doesn't hold. The new heading carries them word for word.

## Why no Questions section

- Only 2 of the 11 usable homepages read for [After the Hero](<After the Hero — What People Want to Know Next — 4 November 2026.md>) have one.
- Google ended FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026, so it brings no special result. Its text still answers questions, but it doesn't need its own section to do that.
- After this section nearly every likely question is answered on the page, in the section where it belongs. A list would repeat them and make a long page longer.
- Question wording goes where it fits ("New phone? Your habits come back."), and the rest belongs in Often Enough facts and Support.

This is reasoned from first principles. No review talks about a website's FAQ.

## The section

Facts come from the Android code and the Rulebook (D1, D5, D10, D15, D16), never memory.

**Heading:** Private, offline and backed up.
**Lead:** No account and no ads. Your habits stay on your phone, work without internet and back up to your own cloud.

| Card | Copy | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Backed up to your own cloud | Connect your own Google Drive, or iCloud on iPhone. It backs up by itself as you go, and we never see it. | The Backup & Sync page |
| New phone? Your habits come back. | Restore your backup on the new phone and your habits, streaks and history are all there. You can undo a restore for 30 days. | The question Restore asks |
| No account | Nothing to sign up for and no email to give. We run no server, so your habits never reach us. | Icon |
| Works offline | Log, get reminders and use widgets with no internet. Your backup catches up when you're back online. | Icon |
| No ads, no tracking | No ads, no analytics and no trackers in the app, on the free plan too. | Icon |
| Take your data with you | Save one backup file that opens on any phone, iPhone or Android. Or export a spreadsheet to use your history anywhere else. Both are free. | Save a backup file, Export a spreadsheet (CSV) |

**Link:** Read our privacy policy, to `/privacy`.

**What the copy is careful about:**

- **Never "syncs".** Android backs up; syncing between devices isn't built there.
- **Never that backup is on from the start on Android.** Someone connects their own Drive first, so the card says "Connect".
- **"Your own cloud" in the lead, with the names in the card.** Google Drive and iCloud both stay on the page; the lead just stays two lines.
- **"Restoring more habits than the free limit needs Plus" (A30) stays off the homepage.** It belongs in Support and the Plus page.
