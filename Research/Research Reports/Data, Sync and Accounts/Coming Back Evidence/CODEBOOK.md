# Coming back — codebook (14 Oct 2026)
One code per review (the main point), a short exact quote.
DRIVE_WANT    asks for (automatic) Google Drive / cloud backup or sync
DRIVE_PRAISE  praises an app's Google Drive backup or sync being there
DRIVE_OK      got habits back or moved them through Google Drive
DRIVE_FAIL    Google Drive backup or restore failed, errored, or couldn't be found
LOST_NO_BACKUP  phone lost, broken, reset or changed: data gone because nothing backed up by itself
MANUAL_HASSLE moving by export/import or a file by hand; a hassle or a workaround
X_WANT        wants the app on the other platform, or iPhone and Android together
X_PAID        a purchase didn't follow to the other platform
X_DATA_LOST   switching between iPhone and Android lost the data
X_DATA_OK     switching between iPhone and Android kept the data
X_NOSYNC      iPhone and Android copies don't sync
CODE_OK       a phone-to-phone transfer (code, QR, nearby, Wi-Fi) worked or is praised
CODE_FAIL     a phone-to-phone transfer failed, needed both phones, or confused
CODE_WANT     asks for a way to move between phones
PHONE_TOOL    the phone's own transfer (Smart Switch, Quick Start, Switch to Android, Google restore) and this app's data
WRONG_ACCT    a different Google / Apple / email account: data not found, empty, or a new account
WHICH_BACKUP  an old or wrong backup restored; choosing a backup
PAID_LOST     a purchase didn't come back on a new phone, reinstall or another account (same platform)
LOST_OK       phone lost or broken, but the habits came back (account, cloud, backup)
MERGE_MESS    signing in or restoring on a device that already had data: duplicates, mixed or overwritten
OFF           off topic
