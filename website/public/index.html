Often Enough

A calm habit tracker for iPhone. Tick off what you did, see how often you do it, and keep going.

  • Free, without an account. Five habits on one device, with reminders and widgets. Your habits stay on your phone.
  • Backed up without thinking about it. Into your own iCloud, or into your account if you make one.
  • Plus, bought once: unlimited habits, your iPad and Apple Watch, and the same habits on every device.
