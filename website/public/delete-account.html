YOU ARE IN CONTROL

Delete your account
and data.

An Often Enough account is optional; the app works fully without one. Account details, device records, private-cloud copies and store purchases have separate removal controls.

Before deleting anything

Use iCloud & Backup → Save a Backup File or Export a Spreadsheet (CSV) if you want a copy. Save outside the app’s own folder. Read each confirmation; deletion can be permanent.

Delete your optional account

The optional account service is being prepared and is not live in the current release build. When available, open Plus & Account › Delete my account in the app and review the confirmation.

This removes your account, including its sign-in identifiers, shortened email, purchase/refund details, family membership and cloud-location details. If you own a Plus Family, it removes the family too.

Your purchases stay with Google or Apple, so signing in again brings Plus back. Deleting your Often Enough account does not delete your Google or Apple account, cancel a store purchase or request a refund.

Your habits, tasks, logs and notes are never in this account. They remain on your devices and in your own iCloud or Google Drive. Use the separate controls below to remove those records.

1. Remove this device’s records

Open Often Enough → menu → iCloud & Backup → Erase All My Data, then review and confirm. This clears local records and local recovery copies on this device, not your private-cloud or exported copies.

Deleting the app also removes local app data, but does not remove iCloud data. Offload App on iPhone keeps documents and data.

2. Remove Often Enough data from iCloud

Open iCloud & Backup → Delete My Data From iCloud. This removes the app’s synced records and backup files in your iCloud while keeping records on this device. Cloud sending stays off until you deliberately enable it again. Use both controls to remove local and cloud data.

You can also review app storage in Apple’s iCloud settings. We cannot perform private-cloud deletion remotely. Other devices and exported files may still hold copies; review them separately.

3. Google Drive and exported files

If you used a build with Drive backup enabled, disconnect Often Enough in your Google Account and use Google Drive’s app-management controls to delete hidden app data where offered. Revoking permission alone does not necessarily delete stored copies.

Remove backups from Files, Drive, email attachments, device backups and other places you saved them. Android is in development; Android-specific in-app instructions will be published before release.

4. Purchases and usage data

Erasing app records does not delete your Apple or Google account or the store’s purchase history. A one-time Plus purchase has no subscription to cancel. Ask the store about refunds.

Turn off Privacy & Security → Share Usage to stop optional collection and clear pending events. Contact support@oftenenough.com about support messages or usage information already received. We may need proportionate details to verify a request about data we hold. Never send your password.

Need help without the app?

Email support@oftenenough.com with the subject “Often Enough data deletion”. We can help you find the correct controls and handle support correspondence. This static website cannot sign into your device or cloud and does not claim to complete deletion online.

For help deleting your optional Often Enough account, include your app version and build number so support can identify the available controls. The account service never holds your habits, tasks, logs or notes.
