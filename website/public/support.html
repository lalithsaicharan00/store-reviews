Support

Write to support@oftenenough.com. We answer within one working day.

Backups

Where is my backup?

Open Settings → Backup & Sync. It says where your backup is and when it last worked. Without an account it's in your own iCloud (or only on your phone, if iCloud is off for the app); with an account it's on our server.

Something's wrong with my backup

The app tells you as soon as it knows, with a card on Today and a red line in Settings, and offers the fix. Your habits are always safe on your phone in the meantime.

A new phone or tablet

  • With an account: install the app, choose "I've used this before" and sign in.
  • Without one, same Apple Account: open the app on the new device and choose "I've used this before"; your iCloud backup is waiting.
  • Without one, any other device: on the old one, Settings → Backup & Sync → Move to Another Device, and send the file (AirDrop, Files, email). On the new one, open the file, or choose "I've used this before" → Import a File.

Restoring

Settings → Backup & Sync → Restore shows what a backup holds before anything changes. Replace makes this device exactly the backup; Merge adds what's missing and keeps everything else. You can undo a restore for 30 days.

Sync

Sync keeps the same habits on all your devices, through your account. It's part of Plus. On the free plan each device keeps its own habits, and you can copy them from one device to another once.

Plus

Plus is bought once. To get it back on a new device, sign in to your account, or use Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Your account

Delete it in the app (Settings → Backup & Sync → Your account → Delete Account) or on the delete account page. Your habits can stay on your phone if you like.
