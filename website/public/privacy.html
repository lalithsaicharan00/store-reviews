Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026.

Often Enough is a habit tracker. This page says, in plain words, what we keep about you, where, why, for how long, and how to get it or delete it. Questions: support@oftenenough.com.

The short version

  • Without an account, your habits never reach us. They stay on your device, in your device's own backup, and in your own iCloud if you use it. We can't see any of it.
  • An account is optional. If you make one, we keep a backup of your habits so a new phone only needs a sign-in, and with Plus we keep them in step across your devices.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • You can delete everything, any time: in the app, or on our delete account page.

We never collect: your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number, advertising ID, age or gender, or anything from other apps.

Where it's kept, and who helps us

Our servers run on Cloudflare. If you're in the European Economic Area, your account's data is stored in the EU. These companies process data for us, only to provide the service:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage and backups for accounts.
  • Apple and Google: signing in, and purchases through the App Store or Google Play.
  • Resend: sending the purchase confirmation email.
  • PostHog (EU): anonymous usage counts, if you leave them on.
  • Sentry: crash reports.

Your own iCloud or Google Drive, if you keep a backup there, belongs to you and Apple or Google; we can't see it.

Why we're allowed to

We keep account data because you asked us to provide the service (backup and sync). We collect anonymous usage counts with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. Habit names can say something personal, so we treat everything you write in the app as sensitive: it never goes into analytics, logs or crash reports.

Your choices and rights

  • Get a copy: Settings → Backup & Sync → Export a File gives you everything, readable in any spreadsheet.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app.
  • Delete it: Settings → Backup & Sync → Your account → Delete Account, or our delete account page. What's on our server is deleted at once; every copy, backups included, is gone within 30 days. Deleting the app deletes what's on your device.
  • Ask us: write to support@oftenenough.com for access, correction, deletion or a copy of your data. We answer within one month.
  • If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Often Enough isn't made for children under 13 (16 in the EU), and we don't knowingly keep data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here with a new date, and say so in the app when the change matters to you.
