YOUR HABITS STAY YOURS

Privacy policy

Updated 15 October 2026 · Pre-release verification version

This policy covers the Often Enough app under development and this website. Privacy enquiries: support@oftenenough.com.

The short version

  • Your habits, tasks, logs, notes and goals are stored on your devices. We do not run a server that receives or stores these records.
  • Backup and sync, when enabled, use your own cloud.
  • Signing in with Google or Apple is optional. An optional account keeps Plus, family membership and which cloud you use, never your habits. The account service is being prepared.
  • We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers.
  • Optional usage sharing is separate from habit records. This website contains no analytics or advertising scripts.

Your records and your cloud

The local database lets you log habits offline. Supported Apple builds use your private iCloud database for sync and separate files in iCloud Drive for dated backups. We cannot browse those private habit records. Apple processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

Google Drive backup and Android sync are being prepared. They require your explicit Google permission when available. The current iPhone build keeps the Drive option disabled pending configuration. A connection is for the app’s own data, rather than general access to your documents.

iCloud and Google Drive do not automatically sync with one another. Export and Import move a copy between platforms. Exported or shared files go to the destination you choose.

Your optional account

Signing in is optional, using Google or Apple only. The app works fully without an Often Enough account.

The account service is being prepared and is not live in the current release build. When available, if you create an account, we keep only:

  • An account ID and the identifier of the Google or Apple account you signed in with.
  • A shortened form of your email, such as l•••••@gmail.com, never your full email.
  • Your Plus purchase: product, store and date, checked with Google or Apple, and refunds.
  • Who is in your family, if you use Plus Family.
  • Which cloud holds your habits (iCloud or Google Drive), that cloud account’s private ID, and when a device last synced.

Never your habits, tasks, logs or notes. They stay on your devices and in your own iCloud or Google Drive.

We use these account details to keep Plus and your family on all your devices and both stores, and to point you back to the right cloud. Providing them is optional; they are used for account management and Plus functionality.

The account server is a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare processes these account details on our behalf to run the service. They are encrypted in transit.

Plus & Account › Delete my account removes your account and, if you own a family, the family too. Purchases stay with Google or Apple, so signing in again brings Plus back. Deleting this account does not delete your habit records: see Delete account and data for the separate controls.

Optional usage sharing

Where offered, Privacy & Security → Share Usage controls feature counts and estimated active screen time, associated with a random installation identifier. Eligible production delivery uses PostHog’s EU endpoint. App/build version, OS major version and release-channel information can accompany events.

Habit and task names, notes, goals, logged values, account details and advertising identifiers are excluded. Turning sharing off stops collection, clears pending usage data and rotates the local analytics identity. Queued records expire after seven days. This data is not used for cross-app advertising.

Production delivery is gated separately from consent. The production configuration and provider retention will be reviewed and published before public collection is enabled. Crash sharing is not available in the current build; we do not claim a crash-reporting service is active.

Purchases and device permissions

Apple or Google processes store purchases and payment details. The app reads entitlements to unlock Plus and restore purchases. We do not receive your full card details. Stores may retain transaction records for legal obligations.

Notifications and supported integrations use permissions you grant on your device. They do not grant us access to unrelated photos, contacts, recordings or location. Change permissions in system settings.

This website and support

Cloudflare hosts this website and may process IP address, requested URL, request time, browser headers and security information to deliver and protect it. Cloudflare may use essential security cookies. The static site does not create accounts, take payments, upload habit data or set its own analytics cookies.

Support receives your email address and information you choose to send. Avoid sensitive habit details unless needed. Correspondence is used to answer your request and kept only as long as needed for support, security or legal obligations. Ask us to remove it when no longer needed.

Retention, export and deletion

Local records remain until you erase them or delete the app. Your own cloud retains records and rotating backups according to app settings and provider storage rules. Exported copies remain where you saved them until you delete them there.

Use Save a Backup File or Export a Spreadsheet (CSV) in the app. Local erasure and cloud deletion are separate: see Delete account and data. Deleting the app alone does not remove cloud backups.

We cannot remotely retrieve or erase private records on your device or in your own cloud. Contact us about information we hold, such as support correspondence or optional usage data. Depending on local law, you may have rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to your data-protection authority. A request may require proportionate verification.

Children and policy changes

Often Enough is not directed to children under 13. Where local law requires parental consent at a higher age, that requirement applies. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

We will update this policy to reflect actual services, including before public release, and communicate material changes where required.

Provider policies: Apple, Google, Cloudflare, PostHog.
