Reminders

Why is my reminder late or missing?

Your phone may have put the app to sleep to save battery. In Settings, open Keep reminders coming. It shows what your phone needs and takes you to each switch. On iPhone, check that notifications are on for Often Enough in your phone’s Settings.

Widgets

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty part of your home screen, choose Widgets, then find Often Enough. Pick a size and what it shows.

Why has my widget stopped updating?

Open the app once, which wakes it up. If it keeps happening, your phone may be putting the app to sleep, so follow the steps in Keep reminders coming.

Backup and your data

How do I back up my habits?

In Settings, open Backup & Sync and connect your own Google Drive. It backs up by itself as you go. On iPhone, your backup goes to iCloud.

How do I get my habits on a new phone?

Install the app and restore from Google Drive, iCloud or a backup file when it asks. On a phone already in use, it’s Backup & Sync, then Restore from a backup.

How do I move between iPhone and Android?

In Backup & Sync, choose Save a backup file on the old phone, and send it to yourself. Then open it on the new phone with Restore from a backup.

I restored the wrong backup. Can I go back?

Yes, for 30 days. In Backup & Sync, choose Undo last restore.

Why does my restore ask for Plus?

The free plan has five habits, so a backup with more needs Plus. Your backup stays as it is until then.

How do I delete my data?

In Backup & Sync, Erase all my data clears this phone, and Delete Google Drive backup removes your backup. Already deleted the app? On a computer, open Google Drive’s settings, then Manage apps, and choose Delete hidden app data beside Often Enough.

Plus and payments

I paid, but I don’t have Plus.

Plus stays with the Google or Apple account you bought it with. Use that account in Google Play or the App Store, open the app, and Plus comes back by itself. If it doesn’t, email us.

Will I be charged again?

No. Plus is one payment, with no subscription and nothing to renew.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds come from the store you paid. Ask Google Play or Apple. If Google Play sends you to us, email us.

I moved between iPhone and Android. What about Plus?

Plus stays with the store that sold it. Email us and we’ll move it for you.

Using the app

How do I undo a log?

Tap Undo under the habit’s row on Today. For a tick, you can also tap the ✓ again.

How do I skip a day?

On Today, swipe the row to Skip today, or touch and hold it and choose Skip today. Skipped days don’t end your streak.

How do I pause a habit?

On Today, touch and hold its row and choose Pause.

I stay up past midnight. Which day counts?

In Settings, set New day starts at to a later hour, and a late night still counts as the day before.
