Privacy policy

Your habits stay on your phone and in your own cloud. We never see them.

Updated 4 November 2026

What do you collect?

Nothing through the app. There’s no account and no server of ours, so your habits never reach us.

Who can see my habits?

You. They’re on your phone, and in your own Google Drive or iCloud if you back up there.

Do you sell my data?

No. We have none to sell, and the app has no ads, analytics or trackers.

How do I delete it?

Open Settings, then Backup & Sync, to erase this phone or delete your backup. Here’s how to delete your data.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Often Enough app on Android and iPhone, and this website, oftenenough.com. Often Enough is made by Koyalakonda Lalith Sai Charan. When we say “we”, that’s who we mean.

What stays on your phone

Your habits, tasks, logs, notes, reminders and settings are saved in the app on your phone, and everything works without internet. There are no accounts and we run no server, so none of it is sent to us.

Google Drive backup on Android

Backing up is your choice. If you connect Google Drive, you pick the account on Google’s own screen and agree there.

What we ask for
Only the app’s own hidden folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission). The app can’t see any of your other files.
What goes there
A copy of your habits, tasks, logs, notes, reminders and settings. It goes straight from your phone to Google over an encrypted connection, never through us.
Your Google address
The app reads it to show which account is connected. It stays on your phone.
Who else gets it
Nobody. Google stores it in your account, under Google’s privacy policy.
Turning it off
Delete Google Drive backup in Backup & Sync removes it. You can also remove Often Enough’s access in your Google Account.

Often Enough’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We never use it for ads, and we never sell it.

iCloud backup on iPhone

On iPhone, your backup goes to your own iCloud. Apple stores it in your account, under Apple’s privacy policy, and we can’t read it.

Backup files and spreadsheets

When you save a backup file or export a spreadsheet, it goes only where you choose. A backup file also holds the name of the phone that made it. These files aren’t locked with a password, so keep them somewhere safe.

Buying Plus

Plus is paid for through Google Play or the App Store. They handle your payment, and we never see your card details. The app asks the store what you own, and the store keeps its own record of the purchase.

The app never asks for your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or health data.

Ads, analytics and crash reports

The app has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn’t use your phone’s advertising ID. Google Play and the App Store show us overall numbers, such as installs and crashes. They don’t tell us who you are or what you track.

This website

Cloudflare hosts this website. To deliver and protect it, Cloudflare handles your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser’s details, and it may set a security cookie. We run no analytics here. One small script picks which store button to show. It runs in your browser and sends nothing.

When you email us

If you write to support, we get your email address and what you send. The app starts its email with the app’s version, your phone’s model and its Android version, and you can delete those before sending. We keep emails only as long as we need them to help you, and we’ll delete yours if you ask.

Your rights

Everything you track is yours and on your phone, so you can see it, export it and delete it yourself. We hold no habit data to hand over.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over personal data we do hold, such as an email to support: to see it, correct it, delete it or move it, to object, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we’ll help.

Children

Often Enough isn’t made for children under 13. If you think a child has sent us personal information, tell us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its date. If a change affects how your data is used, we’ll tell you in the app first.
